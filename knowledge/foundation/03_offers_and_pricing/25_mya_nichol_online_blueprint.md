## 1. THE 5-OFFER ECOSYSTEM

A sustainable business needs multiple offers at different price points.

| Tier | Price Range | Purpose | Income Engine Example |
|------|-------------|---------|----------------------|
| Free | $0 | Lead generation, build trust | Skool Community, Domain Burn Audit PDF |
| Low-Ticket | $7-$97 | First purchase, tripwire | Infrastructure Starter Kit ($47) |
| Mid-Ticket | $100-$500 | Core transformation | VIP Membership ($197/year) |
| High-Ticket | $500+ | Deep transformation | Infrastructure Private Deployment ($497) |
| Recurring | Monthly/yearly | Predictable income | Future Mastermind ($5k+/year) |

### Why Multiple Offers

| Reason | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|
| Different budgets | Not everyone can afford high-ticket |
| Different buying stages | Some ready now, others need trust first |
| Different learning styles | DIY (courses) vs hand-holding (1:1) |
| Revenue stability | If one dips, others keep cash flowing |
| Customer ascension | Move clients from low to mid to high over time |

## 3. E-PRODUCTS

### 3.1 What is an E-Product?

A digital product that can be downloaded immediately. No ongoing work after creation.

**Examples:** Ebooks, templates, checklists, presets, guides, scripts.

### 3.2 7-Day E-Product Sprint

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 1 | Idea |
| 2-6 | Creation |
| 7 | Add to platform, set up checkout, get the link |
| Post | Share in community for feedback and initial sales |

### 3.3 Where to Sell E-Products

| Platform | Pros | Cons |
|----------|------|------|
| Gumroad | Free to start, easy | 10% fee on every sale |
| Kajabi | All-in-one, 0% transaction fees | Monthly subscription |
| Stan Store | Link-in-bio style | Starts at $29/month |
| Flodesk Checkout | Integrated with email | $38/month, 3% Stripe fee |
| Etsy, Shopify | Marketplace exposure | Fees, competition |

## 4. 1:1 COACHING SYSTEM

### 4.1 Complete Automated Workflow

| Step | Action | Tool |
|------|--------|------|
| 1 | Post about 1:1 on IG Stories + link in bio | Instagram |
| 2 | Prospect fills out application | Typeform / Google Forms |
| 3 | Accept or reject application | - |
| 3a | If reject: send email, refer to another offer | Email |
| 3b | If accept: send email with booking link + contract | Calendly + HelloSign |
| 4 | Customer checks out | Kajabi / Stripe |
| 5 | Welcome email sent automatically | Kajabi |
| 6 | Customer messages on Voxer | Voxer |
| 7 | Carry out container | - |
| 8 | Close container: send off-boarding form + extension offer | Google Forms |

### 4.2 Application Form Questions

- Name, email, Instagram handle
- What is your biggest struggle right now?
- What have you tried so far?
- What is your budget for coaching?
- Why do you want to work with me specifically?

### 4.3 Onboarding Form Questions

- What are your top 3 goals for our time together?
- What does success look like at the end of our container?
- What are your biggest fears or hesitations?
- What is your preferred learning style?
- Any relevant background information?

### 4.4 Voxer Welcome Message Template

> Yay!! Welcome to our voxer chat!
> 
> There are a few quick housekeeping things to get us started:
> 
> 1. Please keep an eye out in your email for your coaching agreement. Please review & sign prior to our first coaching session.
> 
> 2. Here is the link to schedule our first session!! [Calendly link]
> 
> **Please note: our coaching container (including voxer messaging) will not officially begin until the date of our first call.**
> 
> 3. Please fill out this form before our first call: [Onboarding form link]
> 
> 4. How to get the most out of our voxer coaching:
>    - We will use voxer for quick & potent communication between calls!
>    - Please keep voice memos to no longer than 2 minutes.
>    - I check voxer usually once a day between 9am and 5pm MDT M-F - please allow up to 24 hours to respond.
>    - You are welcome to send messages outside that window but I may not respond until the next day.
>    - Nothing is off limits, but please keep communications quick & potent.
>    - Please send all coaching questions here, not on Instagram.

### 4.5 Closing Voxer Message Template

> Hey ___! Just wanted to send the reminder that I am closing down this chat at the end of the day today, so if you need to grab anything out of here please do so soon!
> 
> Here is a link to an off-boarding form, please fill it out when you have some time: [Off-boarding form link]
> 
> It was such a pleasure working with you over these last ___ months and I'm excited to keep following you online and see what you do next!

### 4.6 Off-Boarding Form Questions

- How was your experience working with me?
- What was the biggest result you achieved?
- Would you recommend me to a friend? Why or why not?
- Any feedback on how I can improve?
- Would you be interested in extending our container? (Yes/No)

### 4.7 Price Increase Script

> Hey! I hope you're having a great Friday!
> 
> No response necessary here - I just wanted to send over my updated rates for private coaching as they have shifted recently.
> 
> My [length] container is now $_____, with the same structure of [calls + Voxer + access to programs].
> 
> For your reference, our current container ends [date].
> 
> That's it! If you have any questions let me know.

### 4.8 1:1 Coaching Checklist

- [ ] Make it CLEAR what your 1:1 includes and doesn't include
- [ ] Have a contract signed BEFORE the call
- [ ] Set boundaries for when client has access to you
- [ ] Make sure client knows where and how you will communicate
- [ ] Send reminders before calls
- [ ] Have a clear cancellation or missed call policy

## 5. PROGRAMS

### 5.1 What is a Program?

A mix of live trainings and pre-recorded content, usually 1+ weeks long. Students get live access to you.

### 5.2 Program Operational Checklist

- [ ] Create the Facebook group (if using)
- [ ] Post polls to find best day/time for lives
- [ ] Schedule posts
- [ ] Create events
- [ ] Create guides
- [ ] Upload recordings after lives
- [ ] Encourage interaction with questions, challenges, homework
- [ ] Collect testimonials throughout and at the end

### 5.3 Program Communication Checklist

- [ ] Make it CLEAR how long the program is, how many calls, if calls will be recorded
- [ ] How long will students have access to recordings? Lifetime? Limited?
- [ ] Have terms and conditions at checkout
- [ ] Set boundaries for when and how people have access to you
- [ ] Make sure clients know where and how you will communicate
- [ ] Send reminders in FB group and/or email
- [ ] Know the main trainings so you can advertise them
- [ ] Know the outcome students will achieve

## 6. COURSES

### 6.1 Course Creation Wisdom

- **Don't include everything.** You can bundle courses later.
- **More value doesn't mean more videos.** Longer videos don't mean more value.
- **Your trainings don't need to be perfect.**
- **Leave some trainings out** and add them as updates later, then raise the price.
- **Courses = little to no access to you.**
- **Focus on micro-offers.**

### 6.2 Pros & Cons

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Great for passive income | No access to you |
| Students work at their own pace | May take longer to build |
| Great signature offers | Information overload if large |
| If created well, can last years | Not great for topics that change frequently |

## 7. MEMBERSHIPS

### 7.1 Types of Membership Structures

| Type | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Classic | Pay monthly, get immediate access to all content | Netflix |
| New Month New Content | Access only current month's content | Some subscriptions |
| Same Start Date + New Monthly Content | Everyone starts same day, gets new content monthly | IG University |
| Combo / Tiered | Different access levels based on price | Basic vs Premium |

### 7.2 Membership Access Decisions

- Do you have a minimum commitment (3 months, 6 months)?
- Can they cancel anytime?
- Do they get lifetime access to content they accessed while subscribed?
- If they unsubscribe, do they lose access?
- Do you offer a free trial?

### 7.3 Membership Operational Checklist

- [ ] Make it CLEAR what is included with each tier
- [ ] Make canceling process clear
- [ ] If minimum time commitment, add checked box at checkout
- [ ] Focus on customer experience for retention
- [ ] Structure in a way that is sustainable

## 9. 10-STEP PACKAGING PROCESS

| Step | Action | Description |
|------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Decide where the offer fits | Look at offer suite (low, mid, high) |
| 2 | Write the big idea | Overall concept, outcome |
| 3 | Brain dump | Everything that comes to mind |
| 4 | Find themes | Group related ideas into categories |
| 5 | Organize by category | List trainings under each category |
| 6 | Ask your audience | Survey to fill gaps |
| 7 | Decide the offer type | E-product, course, program, etc. |
| 8 | Start creating | Outline, script, record, edit |
| 9 | Finalize pricing + name | Set price and name |
| 10 | Build sales infrastructure | Sales page, checkout, links |

## 10. PRICING FRAMEWORK

### 10.1 Factors to Consider

| Factor | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|
| Investments | Degrees, certifications, courses, coaching you've taken |
| Years of experience | Your expertise level |
| Unique technique/process | Is your method proprietary? |
| Depth of knowledge | Basic or deep? |
| Time commitment after creation | More time = higher price |
| Your failures | Lessons learned the hard way add value |
| Outcome/transformation | Bigger transformation = higher price |
| DFY vs DIY | Done-for-you commands higher price |
| Time/money saved | Does your offer save significant time or money? |

### 10.2 Price Psychology

| Type | Examples |
|------|----------|
| Angel Numbers | $333, $1111, $555, $444 |
| Price Psychology | $347, $467, $697, $997 |

### 10.3 Pricing Philosophy

- Pricing is a personal journey
- Don't compare your price to others
- Confidence grows with sales and experience
- Raise prices from a place of "want", not "need"
- You are in charge. Prices can always be raised.

## 11. PAY IN FULL VS PAYMENT PLANS

### 11.1 Pros & Cons

| | Pay in Full | Payment Plans |
|--|-------------|---------------|
| Pros | Nice cash injection | Recurring income, more comfortable for buyers |
| Cons | Not everyone can afford | Risk of failed payments, more admin |

### 11.2 Key Considerations

- Raise the price a little on payment plans
- They must pay off payments before getting access (optional)
- You can offer both PIF and payment plans
- Make payment plans very clear in terms of use

## 12. FAILED PAYMENT SOP

### Step 1: Automated Emails

**Subject:** "Oh no! Your Payment Failed"

**Body:**
> Hi {{ member.name }}! We were unable to process your payment for {{ offer.title }}. Don't worry. We'll try your payment again over the next few days! To make sure you don't lose access to {{ offer.title }}, you may need to update your payment details:
> 
> [Update Payment Link]
> 
> If you have any questions, please contact support. Hope to see you back inside soon!

Set up Stripe multi-retry: 4 attempts over 7 days.

### Step 2: Personal Outreach (after 2 failed attempts)

> Hey __! Just bumping this up in your inbox! You should have received several automated emails with instructions on how to update your payment.
> 
> As per your contract you are free to cancel at any time. Here are a few options:
> - **Pause payments** for up to 3 months and remove access
> - **Pay the current balance** and keep access
> - **Get an extension** on payments (e.g., 3-month to 6-month)
> - **Buy out** of the contract
> 
> Please let us know which option works best for you! If we do not hear back within 2 days these options will expire.

### Step 3: Final Warning (after 3 failed attempts)

> Hey __! Our records indicate your payment is 3 days past due.
> 
> **How we move forward:**
> - You will have until [date] to complete the current outstanding payment.
> - If not completed, outstanding payments will be forwarded to collections and access revoked.

### Step 4: Collections

If no response, send to collections.

## 13. CROSS-SELLING VS UPSELLING

| | Definition | Example |
|--|------------|---------|
| Upselling | Get customers to purchase more expensive items or upgrades | "Buy a cake and I'll offer a better one for $25 more" |
| Cross-selling | Sell additional products to customers | "Buy a cake and I'll throw in 10 cupcakes for $10" |

**Statistics:**
- Upselling can increase revenue by 10-30%
- Cross-selling can account for up to 35% of revenue increase
- Probability of selling to existing customers is 60-70% higher than new customers
