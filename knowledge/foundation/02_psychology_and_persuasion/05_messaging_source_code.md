## 7. APPLICATION TO INCOME ENGINE

### For Manifestor Types
- Lead with bold, polarizing statements
- "I burned 17 domains so you don't have to"
- Inform your audience - don't ask permission

### For Generator Types
- Share what genuinely lights you up
- Your enthusiasm is your marketing
- Talk about the joy of automated campaigns

### For Projector Types
- Don't chase - create content that positions you as expert
- Deep case studies, insightful breakdowns
- Wait for the right people to invite you deeper

### For Reflector Types
- Take your time
- Share observations about industry trends
- Let your rare perspective be your magnet

## 8. COMBINING WITH OTHER FRAMEWORKS

### Dan Lok Script + Soul Style
