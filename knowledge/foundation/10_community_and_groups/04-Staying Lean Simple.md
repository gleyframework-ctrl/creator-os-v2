ABOUT THIS LESSON
If you have a social media account.. You literally have everything you need to start having success online..

Seriously.. Our first $100k individually online was using our Facebook profiles, content on them + conversations to
send an affiliate link.
Keep your business to the least amount of moving parts as you can for as long as possible.
Even to this day, our 7-figure business consists of: 1. Short-form content to create attention
2. Long-form content in groups to build/accelerate relationships
3. DM’s & PDFS to convert sales..
