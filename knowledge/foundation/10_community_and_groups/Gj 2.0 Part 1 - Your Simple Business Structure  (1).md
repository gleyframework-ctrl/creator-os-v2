## **Strategic Moves for Massive Momentum out of the Gate**

> **Most importantly -** Understand that it goes both ways, Momentum can
> build up and slingshot your business or it can also take you back &
> tank everything you're doing.

**#1 and without getting too philosophical -** your mindset, the biggest
struggle you will face it will come and go in waves, while it can\'t
give you a quick fix, i will share some things that will help with this.

**Avoid Burnout**

> ● **Do Less -** most who're just starting are still working jobs, and
> have tons on their plate, they keep piling it on, overcomplicating
> things, and it feeds itself. You're not getting results because you're
> doing 30 different things and trying to keep up with the success
> everyone around you is having.
>
> And I know this is very basic, and you've probably heard it a dozen
> times. But it is extremely easy to add more & more onto what you're
> doing without realizing you're pulling focus From what's important for
> your business.
>
> **● You Don't Owe Anyone A Response -** this is extremely prevalent
> when you're promoting value ladder products, so often I see newer
> affiliates diving into these deep conversations, building these huge
> amounts of rapport with prospects who haven't jumped into a lower
> ticket training.
>
> This will lead to burnout extremely fast, feeling like you're doing
> the most putting in a ton of effort, you're helping these people, and
> providing insane amounts of value for virtually nothing.
>
> A way you can help eliminate this is by being transparent, and tell
> them "Hey I'd be more than happy to help you get going, but I only
> work with people who're serious about X"
>
> Now, I'm not saying to be rude or don't answer any questions, but you
> have to put a barrier on yourself and your time.
>
> Being this way will repel some people & that's fine because they
> likely aren't a good fit for your brand anyways, and it will always
> position you as a leader/if they buy or don't buy it doesn't mean
> anything to you... Which will alternatively make people gravitate
> towards you (feel like they're missing out)
>
> **● Make More Offers / Stop solving all their problems - i**f you're
> engaging in DM's (which i would highly encourage you to be) Go into
> every conversation understanding that you're not there to solve all of
> their problems
>
> You're there to ask questions, help them realize they have a problem,
> and present the solution which would be the product you're promoting.
>
> Do not get in the habit of giving value value value in hopes that
> they're going to ask to buy something from you, it will do the
> opposite, they will get confused when you make an offer because they
> will be used to you giving them everything for free
>
> You can give mad value while preselling at the same time.
>
> ● **Ride The Waves -** You'll go through phases of feeling fired up
> and times where you feel like doing nothing, make the most of them.
>
> ○ Batch create content
>
> ○ Repurpose old content

**Picking Up Momentum From Ground Zero**

> Understanding that it's a snowball effect, everything you do in this
> online space will compound on top of itself.
>
> Making sales & getting new clients as the ball is rolling is 100X
> easier than it is getting your first few.
>
> **● Throw Together An INSANE OFFER -** when you're at ground zero
> maybe you have a couple of sales or none This one thing can really
> kickstart everything and put things in motion.
>
> You could be horrible at copywriting, your content might sub-bar, but
> if you have products and bonuses that are so enticing the rest of the
> shit really doesn't matter.
>
> If you're promoting a product that pays out \$1000 , spend \$500 of
> that on bonuses. Get them a Chromebook, AirPods, Books, something that

>
> Yes, it'll eat your profit margins, but the compound effect is where
> everything will set in motion.
>
> You'll have other people posting and talking about what they got from
