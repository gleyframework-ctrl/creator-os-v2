Before we build anything, make ONE decision:

**Which community is the center of your business?**

## **Why Your Own Group Changes Everything**

**1. It creates a completely different angle.**

Instead of \"Hey buy my product thousands of other people are selling
the same way\" --- it becomes: Join this place where we\'re going
deeper.

**2. It creates security LONG TERM.**

The product you\'re selling might not be around in 12 months. Your own
group creates a movement deeper than your offers. People buy into the
culture (which means they buy again and again).

**3. It creates leverage and momentum.**

Most people are building alongside a full-time job. A group focus
compounds onto itself. You build deep relationships with multiple people
at once.

**4. It drastically shrinks your sales process.**

We\'ve closed 4,000+ sales with products up to \$9k+ without a single
sales call in years. When people can binge on YOU, sales become low
pressure and easy.

**5. It\'s YOURS.**

We\'ve watched people grow someone else\'s group, the owner changes
direction, and those people get left starting over. Your group is your
house. Your rules. Your members. Your culture.

## **What Your Group Removes From Your Business**

**Funnels, tech, and automation (to start):** Your community can BE your
funnel. Could be up and ready by end of day. Focus on what matters ---
marketing and content.

**Lack of clarity in \"what to post\":** Your community becomes a
breeding ground for content ideas. Something happens in your group →
that something becomes content → that content grows your group. Loop.

**Sales calls, cold outreach, or \"targeted lead gen\":** This flips the
entire process. Folks come to YOU. Wanting what you have. Knowing what
you teach. Ultimately coming to you TO BUY.

**Feast or famine:** No more chasing your next sale. You create a stream
of people coming into your world, living in your ecosystem, becoming
customers. We have clients that pay and stay for YEARS because of
groups.

## **Who Your Group Serves**

This is as deep as we\'ll ever get into \"dream client exercises.\"

We see folks going crazy with this and creating fake personas.

**Your customer avatar isn\'t a person.** It isn\'t Sally who lives in
Nebraska and drives a minivan.

**Your customer avatar is a PROBLEM someone is having.**

A problem you overcame --- 5 years ago, 1 year ago, 6 months ago, last
week --- is who you\'re speaking to.

### **Your Community Mission Statement**

****Helping (specific audience) break free from (specific struggle)

so they can (achieve meaningful transformation).

This community is about (your big picture vision).

**Example:** \"Helping online entrepreneurs escape burnout and build a

### **Who You\'re Helping**

****I help (specific type of person / previous version of you)

who is struggling with (specific challenge you overcame)

by showing them (the plan that got you through).

**Example:** \"I help service-based entrepreneurs who are tired of
chasing clients finally create a community that brings leads to them on

### **A Micro Pain Point (To Start)**

**Go narrow and deep in the beginning.**

When we launched Group Juice, it was a narrow problem: \"How to grow
your audience on Facebook without adding friends, posting in other
people\'s groups, or doing cold outreach.\"

For 2 years it was centered around: The 4 steps to growing and
monetizing a Facebook group without \[insert pain\].

As time went on, it got broader. But it started MICRO.

Helping (previous version of you) go from (specific struggle)

to (specific win) with (what you did to overcome it).

**Example:** \"Helping new group owners get their first 100 engaged
members without ads, funnels, or spammy outreach.\"

### **Rules & Boundaries**

It\'s YOUR house. You set the tone.

**What\'s Allowed:**

-   Asking questions & engaging with posts

-   Sharing wins & lessons learned

-   Giving value & supporting others

**What\'s NOT Allowed:**

-   Spamming or pitching offers

-   DMing members without permission

-   Posting irrelevant links

## **The 6 Non-Negotiables**

Every profitable community has these:

**1. A Single Outcome**

Your community removes ONE specific pain. Not \"value.\" Not
\"support.\" One measurable outcome.

Vague communities attract vague people who never buy.

**2. A Clear Entry Frame**

New members instantly understand:

-   What this place is for

-   What they should do first

-   That paid opportunities exist

No surprises. No bait-and-switch feeling later.

**3. Proof Stays Visible**

Your wins. Student wins. Screenshots. Progress.

If it\'s happening, it\'s public.

Hidden proof = hidden certainty = longer sales cycles.

**4. Content as Documentation**

Not a content calendar. A living record of what you\'re doing, testing,
learning.

**Treat your community like a living, breathing journal.**

The true power is when people watch you overcome challenges they\'re
facing and see they can do it too.

**5. Conversation Triggers Built In**

Your community surfaces hand-raisers.

Repeated engagement + \"what\'s next?\" questions = bridge to DM.

**6. Simple Linear Flow**

1.  Join

2.  Read welcome post

3.  Consume 1-2 pinned assets

4.  Engage

5.  Move to DM when ready

Complexity kills conversion.

## **What To Post Inside Your Group**

Most people struggle with content. Not because they don\'t have ideas
--- because they overthink it.

**Treat your group as your living, breathing journal.**

The goal: Make them feel like you know EXACTLY where they\'re at. Show
them you really do know how to get them out of it.

### **The 5 Buckets of Group Content**

**1. Who You Are (Beyond Business)**

Life, story, journey, missions, morals, ethics. Be yourself 100%. People
are buying YOU.

**2. Plans, Process, Model**

What you\'re doing RIGHT NOW to get the result they want. What you did
in the past that stopped you.

**3. What You DON\'T Do**

What they\'re probably doing that you stopped. This creates contrast.

**4. Offers (Not Links to Checkouts)**

What\'s in it for them? What problem does it solve? Where will they be
after?

**5. ALL Wins**

Big, small, yours, students, community members. Every win. Public.

3️⃣ Makes selling a breeze --- after you help with X, you sell the

in it for THEM.

**Go narrow and deep in the beginning.**

### **Narrow Problem Examples**

**Health & Wellness:**

-   Struggling with late-night cravings → How to stop snacking at night
    > without feeling deprived

-   Low energy & fatigue → How to boost energy naturally without relying
    > on caffeine

**Wealth & Business:**

-   Getting first clients without ads → How to land high-paying clients
    > without spending money on ads

-   Content burnout → How to create engaging content without posting
    > every day

**Relationships:**

-   Building self-discipline → How to stay consistent and follow through

(Creates curiosity. \"Am I making those mistakes?\")

**Cold audience =** Lead away from pain

**Warm / Hot audience =** You can lead towards pleasure / outcome

# **Part 2: The Workshop Blueprint**

## **Why Workshops Change Everything**

Your workshop is a lead magnet on steroids.

But more than that --- it\'s a **belief installation machine.**

**The goal isn\'t to teach.** The goal is to shift beliefs until buying
becomes the obvious next step.

When deployed right:

-   People come READY to buy (no objection handling)

-   No sales calls needed

-   No convincing required

**The flow:**

Content → Free Community → Workshop → Ready-to-Buy DMs

By the time they finish, they should be thinking: \"Okay, how do I work
with this person?\"

## **The Psychology of Why This Works**

People don\'t buy because they understand your offer.

**People buy when:**

1.  They believe the OPPORTUNITY is real (this could work)

2.  They believe the VEHICLE is right (this approach makes sense)

3.  They believe they\'re CAPABLE (I can do this)

4.  They believe NOW is the time (I can\'t wait)

Your workshop systematically installs these four beliefs.

Miss any of them, and people stall. Hit all four, and they ask how to
pay.

## **Workshop Length**

**3-7 days is the sweet spot.**

The longer the runway, the easier sales become.

They\'ve gotten wins. They trust you. By the time you invite them to
buy, resistance is gone.

**But here\'s the key:** Each day should build on the last. Not random
value --- a SEQUENCE that leads somewhere.

## **The Workshop Architecture (6 Parts)**

### **Day 1: The Origin (Why Listen To You)**

Before they\'ll believe anything you say, they need to believe YOU.

**This isn\'t a bio dump.** It\'s a strategic story.

**Structure:**

1.  **Where you started** (relatable struggle --- same place they are)

2.  **What you tried that failed** (validate their past attempts)

3.  **The moment everything changed** (your epiphany)

4.  **Where you are now** (proof it worked)

5.  **Why you\'re sharing this** (your mission)

**The goal:** \"This person was where I am. They figured it out. Maybe
they can help me.\"

**Key:** Be specific. \"I struggled\" is weak. \"I was \$47,000 in debt,
posting 3x a day for 8 months with nothing to show for it\" is strong.

### **Day 2: The Opportunity (Why This, Why Now)**

Now you shift their belief about what\'s POSSIBLE.

**Your job:** Show them the OLD way is broken and there\'s a NEW way
that actually works.

**Structure:**

1.  **The Old Way** (what everyone teaches, why it\'s flawed)

2.  **Why it doesn\'t work** (their experience confirms this)

3.  **The New Opportunity** (your approach)

4.  **Why it works** (the mechanism)

5.  **Proof it works** (results)

**The goal:** \"The reason nothing has worked isn\'t ME --- it\'s the
approach. This new way makes sense.\"

**Example framing:**

\"The old model was: build a funnel, run ads, do sales calls, grind
forever.

The new model is: one community that does the selling for you. No calls.
No complex funnels. No daily lives.

Here\'s why it works and here\'s what it looks like\...\"

### **Day 3: The First Belief Shift (Internal)**

Now we handle: \"I don\'t think I can do this.\"

**This is about THEM, not you.**

**Structure:**

1.  **Name the internal doubt** (what they secretly think)

2.  **Validate it** (I felt the same way)

3.  **Reframe it** (here\'s what\'s actually true)

4.  **Prove it** (show someone like them who did it)

5.  **Give them a win** (something they can do TODAY)

**The goal:** \"Maybe I CAN do this after all.\"

**Tactical win idea:** Give them something they can deploy and get a
result from.

-   Set up their profile

-   Write their first post

-   Create their group

-   Clarify their offer

**This is critical.** When they take action and see a small result,
belief skyrockets.

### **Day 4: The Second Belief Shift (External)**

Now we handle: \"This won\'t work because I don\'t have X.\"

**Common \"external\" objections:**

-   \"I don\'t have a big audience\"

-   \"I don\'t have time\"

-   \"I don\'t have money for ads\"

-   \"I\'m not tech savvy\"

-   \"I don\'t have a product yet\"

-   This can also be noise they hear from other people

**Structure:**

1.  **Name the excuse** (what they think they need)

2.  **Break it** (show why it\'s not true)

3.  **Prove it** (show someone without that thing who succeeded)

4.  **Reframe** (what you ACTUALLY need)

5.  **Show the path** (how to do it without the thing)

**The goal:** \"I\'ve been using X as an excuse. I don\'t actually need
it.\"

**Example:**

\"You think you need a big audience. I did my first \$100k with under
1,000 followers. Here\'s how\... and here\'s a student who did \$50k
with 400 people in their group.\"

**Bonus:** If you can REMOVE something they hate from the process, this
is where you hammer it.

> We built a \$1M offer showing how to close deals WITHOUT calls,
> outreach, or daily lives. This became our core positioning.

### **Day 5: The Third Belief Shift (Speed/Timing)**

Now we handle: \"I\'ll do it eventually\" or \"Now isn\'t the right
time.\"

**Structure:**

1.  **Name the delay pattern** (what they tell themselves)

2.  **Show the cost of waiting** (what they lose)

3.  **Reframe timing** (why now is actually ideal)

4.  **Give another win** (build momentum)

5.  **Seed the offer** (hint at what\'s coming)

**The goal:** \"If I don\'t do this now, I\'m just going to stay
stuck.\"

**Tactical win:** Look at what naturally comes NEXT after their Day 3
win.

-   Day 3: Set up their group

-   Day 5: How to get their first members

Momentum creates urgency better than any countdown timer.

### **Day 6: The Invitation**

Now they\'ve experienced:

-   Your story (trust)

-   Your framework (belief in the opportunity)

-   Internal belief shift (they can do it)

-   External belief shift (they don\'t need what they thought)

-   Momentum (they\'ve taken action and seen results)

**The only thing left is the invitation.**

**Structure:**

1.  **Recap the journey** (what you covered, what they accomplished)

2.  **Name the next problem** (what they\'re about to hit)

3.  **Position your offer as the solution** (not a pitch --- a path)

4.  **Explain what they get** (clear, specific)

5.  **Handle the fence** (who it\'s for, who it\'s not for)

6.  **Call to action** (exactly what to do next)

**Key reframe:** You\'re not \"selling.\" You\'re offering to continue
the journey together.

\"You\'ve done X. The next step is Y. That\'s what we do inside
\[Offer\].\"

## **Two Invitation Styles**

**Option A --- Next Step Frame:**

\"You\'ve got \[result from workshop\]. The natural next challenge is
\[problem your offer solves\]. That\'s exactly what we help with inside
\[Offer\].\"

**Option B --- Implementation Frame:**

\"Over the last 5 days, we mapped out the whole system. Now the question
is: do you want to implement this alone, or do you want help? \[Offer\]
is where we build it together.\"

## **Volume vs Quality Trade-Off**

**Want more conversations?** Leave out price. Optimize for DMs. More
volume, more qualifying needed.

**Want easier sales?** Explain everything including price. Less volume,
but hyper-qualified.

Pick based on your capacity.

# **Part 3: Workshop Angles & Install**

## **Where To Put It**

**Option 1: Facebook Group**

-   Guides section

-   Replay playlist

-   Pinned in welcome flow

**Option 2: Skool**

-   Classroom module

-   First thing new members see

-   Linked from welcome post

Pick one. Don\'t overcomplicate.

## **The Content Loop**

Connect everything:

-   Welcome post links to Day 1

-   Day 1 links to Day 2

-   Day 2 links to Day 3

-   And so on\...

**No dead ends.** People follow the path without thinking.

## **Promoting Your Workshop**

**Give yourself 3-10 days to build curiosity.**

**What to post:**

-   Teaser of what you\'ll cover

-   The transformation they\'ll get

-   Behind-the-scenes of you building it

-   Who it\'s for (and not for)

**Where to post:**

-   Profile

-   Group

-   Stories

-   Content

-   Emails

**Goal:** Maximum attendance for the live run.

## **Delivering It**

**Best case:** Run it LIVE first.

Why?

-   Real-time feedback

-   See what lands, what doesn\'t

-   Energy is different (scarcity, momentum)

-   You can adjust on the fly

**Aim for 20-40 minutes per video.** 20-30 is the sweet spot.

**Options:**

1.  Live over 6 days in your group

2.  Live via Zoom, replays in group

3.  Pre-recorded, released daily

Live first. Evergreen later.

## **After Delivery: The Autopilot Machine**

Once it\'s done, it lives there 24/7.

**The automation:**

1.  New member joins

2.  Welcome DM links to Day 1

3.  They go through workshop

4.  They message you ready to buy

**If using email:**

-   Email 1: Welcome + join group

-   Email 2: Day 1

-   Email 3: Day 2

-   Continue through Day 6

Workshop runs whether you\'re there or not.
