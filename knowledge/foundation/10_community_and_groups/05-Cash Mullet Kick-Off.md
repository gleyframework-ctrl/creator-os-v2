Cash Mullet Kick-Off

Free Training To Build Goodwill & Drop Tons Of Value + Extend Invitation To Offer On The Back
(Can be very similar to offer itself / a 1000ft overview of the process in general)
Works well because naturally, you’ll have a bit more volume attending live because it’s a “free thing”
The goal would be to actually give them a tangible thing on the free training - so they could actually go implement
something afterward (makes working with you even more appealing / reinforces proof of concept & that you know
your shit)
       Going Live (Emotional Buyers Jump In) If you can snag a couple sales off the live it’ll really set the tone for the
       whole promo / enhance everything else) (1-3 Days)
       What they get
       How to get it
       How much it is
       Bonuses
       # of spots
       Timing etc (Can leave this vague/open if you’re running the free training early in the promo / you don’t wanna
       shoot yourself in the foot saying this closes down in 72 hours if you don’t get any traction in that timeframe)

Curiosity / Hype Phase

Hammer content pumping curiosity and what is to come with your free training (Go into the free training with the
goal of actually giving them takeaways & over-delivering on the free info)
Feed the upcoming live event for 2-7 days - depending on audience size - would lean into shorter, punchier content to
build up as much hype/attention as possible / so you can get as much attention to the thing you’re doing
(Good rule of thumb - kick off any promo with a shorter content style - get hand raisers / interest / stack momentum
from start of promo)
Aim for 45-90 minutes for the free training (anything much longer seems to be hard to maintain retention)
Temp check throughout live - prompt for engagement / bait people for questions “Drop me a 1 if you’d would be
interested in eliminating cold dm’s from your business?”
(can do this with different portions of the offer to build interest in the offer itself)
Can tease about the offer throughout training - “If you stay until the end, I have something special for a select few”
Extend an invitation to check out the offer doc / have a chat / briefly break down an offer on the live depending on
how long you ran
       Hype building/teasers (3-7 Days)
       Results that your new thing has achieved
       Testimonials & credibility building
       Why it is different from what people have seen before
       Who it is for & who it isn’t for
       When it is going live & how to get it
       Any relevant scarcity (time or quantity)

Pre-Training DM’s

Hit up everyone you’ve chatted with previously about an offer, who’s shown interest in what you’re doing that’s been semi responsive at one point - Let
them know that you have an upcoming training covering X & lob them an invite.


“Hey Name, I know it’s been a while since we chatted but I’ve got this super cool training planned for X date that’s
going to break down exactly how I was able to -insert outcome- if that’s something you wanna check out.. “Let me
know - Join me here - link to group”

Power Of This

        Running the training in itself is PRESELLING your offer - especially if you can embed wins/results into the
        strategy
        It’s a leveraged thing that you can continue to push traffic to - even after the live for weeks/months -
        depending on how long you’re running a promo or however long you want to keep it inside the group
        The Sales on the back end are usually VERY LOW pressure - They’ve spent X amount of time with you already
        & lit up about the fact that you’re dropping nuggets on them - Getting them into Full-offer is a breeze
        Can work really well to kick off the momentum of a full-promo cycle. Snag a few sales right out of the gate & it
        makes all the other components spicier
                Limited Availability/proof of concept because they’ll see other people tipping in or even interested to
                join
                Step Further Away from DM - Resuming your regular programming posting content & running promo / In
                dm people ask questions about X - you can tag them on the training & have them circle back with you
                after

Post-Free Training Content

*Test a few different short form content angles / copy to get even more people into it post training*
        Slicing & Dicing (Logical Buyers Pushed Off The Fence (3-14 Days)
        Objections
        False Beliefs
        Stories
        Results
        Why It Will Work For Them
        Best case scenario - you close a spot or a few off the back end of the live - that is going to be one of the first
        things you take to your content (further feeds reason for them to act now - especially if you talk about limited
        spots)
        Heavy on proof of concept
        Takeaways people had from the free training, the strategy your offer breaks down as a whole
        Client / your own results that are directly related to the strategy taught inside the offer
        What it’s done for you / what it’s done for someone else
        The plan as a whole - who it is and isn’t for
        Reasons to act meow - Why they shouldn’t wait
Cycling through a rotation of parts of the offer while feeding/pointing people back to the free training
(this will help maintain goodwill while selling unison - ultra important if you’re running a longer promo & it will give
your audience time to acclimate to what you’re doing)
Ex - We covered 5-different components - I would then lean into content on each of them while pointing back on a
training that breaks it down in higher detail/anchor an outcome
pre-selling. Your offers. Ideas. Your Brand as a whole.
The better job that you can do with your content & inside of it.. The shorter & easier conversations become.
Especially when running an offer/promo that is qualifying people inside of your content. “That rapport-building
phase” is virtually skipped because by the time you’re in the DMs.. They know why they’re there.
Can you position what you’re selling to eliminate their pains & give them what they want in unison (Recipe for cash)

Post Training DM’s

Follow up with EVERYONE who watched/lurked on the free training you ran. Can also tap into the ones you
messaged previously who said they were going to check it out / were going to jump on with you.
