BONUS: Your DM Playbook
The exact conversations that move community members to clients without pressure or scripts.




BEFORE YOU USE THIS
This is not a script book. This is a decision guide.

The goal is simple: Move the conversation to a point where presenting your offer feels natural.

If you ever feel weird sending a DM, it's because:

The signal wasn't there

The timing was off

The ecosystem hasn't done its job yet

Use this playbook to handle different situations. Not to sound robotic.

Sales is about:

Trust and rapport

Finding the real problem

Recommending a solution

Guiding them to yes

If you're ever lost, remember these four steps. Keep it calm.




SCENARIO 1: THE LAYUP
They ask about a specific offer.

The Signal: They DM you requesting info on a specific product or service.

The Move:

"Hey [Name], appreciate you reaching out about [Offer]. Before I drop the details, mind if I ask
one quick question to make sure it's actually a fit?"

"This is best for people who [Key Qualifier]. Does that sound like where you're at?"

"Awesome. I've got everything laid out in a short breakdown. Want me to send it over?"

Then:

"Great. Once I drop it over, just let me know if you're in, out, or have questions. Cool?"

This is the honor code. It gets them to commit to responding and makes "no" an acceptable
answer.

Send the link.

From here, they respond with yes, no, or questions.

If yes, enroll them.

If no, ask what made them reach out in the first place and dig into fit.

If questions, answer them and keep moving forward.




SCENARIO 2: THE CRY FOR HELP
They want the outcome, not the product yet.

The Signal: They ask for help or describe a struggle your offer solves.

The Move:

"Hey [Name], can definitely help with [Thing]. Can you tell me a bit more about your situation so
I can point you in the right direction?"

Let them explain. Some give a short answer. Some give a life story. Either is fine.

You need to understand:

Their situation

Their goals

Their challenges

What they've tried before

Reflect it back:

"So it sounds like you're trying to [Goal] without [Friction]. Is that fair or am I missing anything?"

Then:

"One final question. When are you looking to start [Outcome] and stop [Struggle]? Is this a
sooner or later thing?"

If sooner:

"Awesome. Based on what you've told me, I think [Offer] would help with this. Want me to send
you a breakdown so you can see if it's a fit?"

If later, dig into why. Then decide if it's worth moving forward.

Once they say yes:

"I've got all the details here. Do you have a few minutes now to check it out so we can decide if
it makes sense or not?"

Send the link.

Handle yes, no, or questions the same as Scenario 1.




SCENARIO 3: THE THANK YOU / BELIEF DM
They've been engaging. You acknowledge it.

The Signal: Consistent engagement, advocacy, or clear belief in your approach.

The Move:

"Hey [Name], just wanted to say thanks for being active around here. How are things going with
[Topic]?"

Let the conversation flow. Be normal.

Ask about:

Their pains and roadblocks

Their goals and wants

Reflect it back when it feels right:

"So it sounds like you're trying to [Goal] without [Friction]. Is that fair?"

If there's a fit:

"Forgive me for being direct, but based on what you shared, I might have something that could
help with [Problem]. Want me to send details?"

If they say yes:

"I've got all the details here. Do you have a few minutes now to check it out?"

Send the link.

Don't force this one. If they say no thanks, leave it alone.




SCENARIO 4: THE HAND RAISER
They comment on a post or answer a group question asking for info.

The Signal: They comment asking for details or raise their hand publicly.

The Move:

"Hey [Name], saw your comment on my post asking about [Goal]. Appreciate you raising your
hand on this one."

"Best place to start is if you can tell me a bit more about your current situation and we can take
it from there."

From here, it's the same as Scenario 2.

Understand their situation.

Reflect it back.

Ask about timing.

Make the recommendation.

Get the breakdown in their hands.

SCENARIO 5: THE SEQUEL SALE
Previous customers moving to the next level.

The Signal: They've bought from you before and you see a fit for the next step.

The Move:

"Hey [Name], really enjoyed working with you inside [Previous Program]. Wanted to touch base
and see how everything was going?"

Let them respond.

Then:

"You know [Name], typically with folks who've been through [Previous Program], the next
challenge they face is [Next Problem]. Sounds like you might be at that point now. Is that fair?"

If they say yes:

"It's quite common. We're actually working with a few folks right now helping them overcome
that by [What You Do] so they can [Outcome]. Would you be interested in some details about
that?"

If yes:

Send the breakdown. Handle questions. Close the deal.

Don't rush this one. Let it happen naturally.




SCENARIO 6: THE INVITATION
You invite them into what you're building.

The Signal: Existing relationship (past customer or active community member).

The Move:

"Hey [Name], been really impressed with [Thing You've Seen Them Doing]. Permission to send
over an invitation to [Program] which I think would really help accelerate [Outcome]?"

SCENARIO 7: THE CPR SALE
Reviving old conversations.

The Signal: Old conversation that died but they were qualified and interested.

The Move:

"Hey [Name], it's been a while. Are you still looking to [Outcome]?"

If yes:

"I understand. I thought of you because I'm putting together a few folks this month to [Thing
They Want] with [Your Solution] so they can [Outcome]. Would you like to be part of that?"

If they say yes:

"I've got all the details here. We're looking to get started soon, so once you take a look, just let
me know if you're in, out, or have questions. Cool?"

Send the link.

Handle yes, no, or questions.




FOLLOW-UP: THE 3-TOUCH RULE
Follow-up is not chasing. It's closing the loop.
                                         imarketing.courses



Touch 1 (24-48 hours):

"How'd you go with that doc, [Name]?"

Touch 2 (48-72 hours):

"Just bumping this to the top of your inbox."

Touch 3 (72+ hours):

"Hey [Name], totally cool if this isn't for you. Just drop me back a quick 'no thanks' so I don't
keep bugging you."

Or:

"Should I close the file on this one?"

Stop after three touches. Don't endlessly nudge.




FINAL REMINDER
If your DMs feel heavy, awkward, or full of resistance, the DM is not the problem.

Go upstream. Fix:

Entry Positioning

Visible Proof

Ascension Clarity

When the system works, the DMs are just the paperwork.
