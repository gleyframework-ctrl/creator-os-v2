### Diagnosing in Messenger 

Getting a convo started can be easy and hard depending it is an inbound
message (they reach out to you) or an outbound message ( you reach out
to them)

What we want to do in either case is disarm them. Everyone has their
guard up these days due to so many quick pitchers and unethical
marketers.

This really gives us a chance to really wow folks by understanding them
on a deep level.

Inbounds are best and that\'s what we teach lol, in some cases outbound
will be necessary.

" Hey name, thanks for reaching out. I just want to make it clear that I
don't work with just anyone as what I have to offer isn\'t for everyone.
I like to actually get to know folks to make sure I can help them first.
Is it cool if I ask you a few questions to see if I can truly help you?"

Get your answer, if yes then you can proceed. If not, you can dig a bit
more into why they reached out.

What we really wanna do here as our main goal is simple

1\. Get a super clear understanding of where they are now and where they
want to be.

2\. Help them see their struggles so they understand what has to happen
for things to change. Really dig into those pains that are keeping them
stuck from getting what they really want.

3\. Pour a cup of salt and alcohol into the cuts we just opened so it
burns & dig into their pains (without pain there is no purchase)

We don't do this to be assholes, but we do this to be polarizing as we
get ready to show them the plan and solution.

**Questions to kick this shit off:**

"So tell me a little bit about what you've done in the affiliate
marketing space?"

"Can you tell me a bit about you and where you\'re currently at in
business or if there\'s anything you\'re looking to change right away?"

"Tell me, why are you really wanting to make money from home?"

"Can you tell me what you do online right now and what your expertise
is?"

"What are you currently doing to make money online?"

If the person doesn\'t respond well with genuine or real answers they
may not be a good fit, still early to tell. But you can **make sure they
are comfortable.**

"Hey, if this is a bad time I can give you a shout at a better time for
you, I dont wanna waste your time"

This can shock folks because you\'re completely detached (Powerful)

You shouldn\'t care what kind of answers you get whether it\'s short or
long, but just enough to fuel the convo as we go. Pay close attention to
their answers as we will be bringing them back up.

Test different questions of your own to see what works best for you

Once we are super clear on where they want to get to in their business
and why, we can help them see the bigger picture long-term as well as
what is stopping them from getting it

Its pretty powerful to figure out what they make in total money each
month or year so we can leverage that in the convo

Not just online but offline as well. This is a temp check as I like to
call it. Helps you find out if they actually have money to invest
without bringing up the price as a whole.

Again, we want to make them comfortable and feel good about sharing info
and talking with us.

Itll make it easier to transition to why they want to achieve (insert
want or desire)

By having profitable online business

Asking them the hard question is next

"So how serious are you about making this happen"

"So you mentioned earlier that you really wanted to do xyz, how serious

This is where we can really use what they have shared with us the whole
time.

Once you've found out theyre a good fit we can look to start talking

every single one of them is going to be the same

Things that make the most significant difference in how easy or the
length of the conversations you're having with people.

-   How much content they've consumed from you

-   How deep they feel like they know about who you are and what you do

-   The belief that you're the one that has the ability to help them fix
    > what they're struggling with

-   To an extent - an internal belief in themself that they actually
    > have what it takes / confidence or enough pain in where they're
    > currently at

If you want to make more sales in the DMs here are the 4 main things
that will make a huge difference for you

1.  Building trust and rapport via the relationship

2.  Finding out what their FOR REAL problem is that they have (could be
    > multiple)

3.  Getting to a point where you can softly share the solution ya have
    > that you think would be best for them based on #2

4.  If it\'s a good fit and they qualify you are just helping them
    > **make the best decision for themselves** and leading them to the
    > sale

The two main things that will put you miles ahead in convos:

1)  The ability to read the convo & become aware of the prospects
    > responsiveness

    a.  If someone is extremely short with you, there is a
        > problem/something isn't clear to them / internal issue -

Being able to read these 2 separately quickly will give you the ability
