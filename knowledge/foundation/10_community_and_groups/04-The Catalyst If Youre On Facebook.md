ABOUT THIS LESSON
Facebook Reels - we first started testing around with these when they first came out about a year ago - and have
regularly used them to explode our groups. (I have stopped due to tooo much traffic)
(videos that didn’t perform well on other platforms were getting 1M+ views) - we’ve had a handful of clients take
single FB reels & turn them into 1,000+ group requests.
Why it’s powerful:
        It’s linear - meaning it’s on the platform - Pushing to a group / a conversation isn’t going to negatively impact
        the performance of the content because you’re not attempting to redirect people off the platform
        It allows you to tap into a broader audience (instead of having to add friends or rely on outreach/building your

        friend's list - It will attract people to you & usually a lot of people who’re not bombarded by others in your
        space)
        Increase the reach & impact from your actual profile content (because you’ll build a bigger following beyond
        the 5,000 friend cap)
Now one thing you need to know about creating short-form content is with those quick wins & viral traffic, you do
need to nurture these leads more than other platforms or strategies.
The goal of these videos is to fuel your Group, so you can build rapport quickly and identify people who would be
potential buyers.
The main goal is to leverage the virality of the short-form traffic sources to set your community on fire.
Leverage the attention from short form video to get more eyeballs on your LONG form content that will nurture -
Presell & build the deeper connections with your audience.
Created Layers In Our Business (something we picked up from working with Jacob Caris)
Profile / short form Becomes like a highlight reel - snippets of life & whats going on in business & talking about what
we’re doing inside our communities.
Group is where we drop the “Goods” - shedding light on anything & everything inside our business & how we can help
people / what’s actually in it for them.
