## MODULE 1: SELL YOUR COACHING

### 1.1 The Core Philosophy

Offer unlimited 15-minute coaching calls with a **homework rule** for a flat annual fee.

**Why It Works for Clients:**
- Access for entire year at low monthly cost
- As many calls as they want (if they do homework)
- Unlimited email support between calls

**Why It Works for You:**
- Homework rule = most clients only have 2-3 calls/year
- Upfront payment = no chasing payments
- Perfect down-sell for higher-priced offers

### 1.2 The Pricing Model

| Element | Recommendation |
|---------|----------------|
| Price | $997 for one year |
| Duration | 1 year (or 2 years at $1,997) |
| Payment | Full upfront, no payment plans |
| Calls | Unlimited 15-minute calls with homework |
| Guarantee | First-call guarantee (1 in 60-70 refund rate) |

**Key Insight:** People care more about monthly payment than total price.

Always anchor to the monthly equivalent.

### 1.3 The Trust Framework

Trust is the single most important factor.

| Element | Application |
|---------|-------------|
| Know, Like, Trust | Trust is most critical |
| Your Voice | Emails in authentic voice |
| Nurture Before Selling | 30 days nurture before promoting |
| Be Authentic | Don't use phrases you don't normally use |

### 1.4 Packaging Your Coaching

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|

### 1.5 The 3-Day Email Campaign

| Day | Email Type | Purpose |
|-----|------------|---------|
| Pre-Day 1-2 | Nurture (optional) | Two pure value emails |
| Day 1 | Announce | Introduce offer with excitement |
| Day 2 | Social Proof | Show others are buying/engaged |
| Day 3 | Scarcity | Create urgency - offer ends soon |

**Key Observation:** Day 3 emails convert better than Day 2.

## MODULE 3: DELIVER YOUR COACHING

### 3.1 The Roadmap

A one-page visual document showing the client's journey.

**Why It Prevents Refunds:**
- On first call, show where they are on the roadmap
- Confirm you understand their situation
- They feel heard and confident

**Example Roadmap for CPA Operators:**

| Step | Description |
|------|-------------|
| 1 | Lead Magnet (Domain Burn Audit PDF) |
| 2 | Nurture Sequence (7-email trust building) |
| 3 | Deep Nurture (Telegram community) |
| 4 | Email Campaigns (VIP launch broadcasts) |
| 5 | Automated Webinar (optional) |
| 6 | Traffic (targeted outreach) |

### 3.2 The Pre-Call Question

Ask in scheduler before every call:

> "What do you hope to accomplish on this call?"

- Tells you their current priority
- You can prepare and deliver exactly what they want
- Builds trust and reduces refund risk

### 3.3 First Call Outline (30 minutes)

| Step | Action | Why |
|------|--------|-----|
| 1 | "Tell me about your situation" | Let them talk |
| 2 | Active listening | They feel heard |
| 3 | Confirm you understand and can help | Builds trust |
| 4 | Show where they are on roadmap | Clarity = confidence |
| 5 | Explain homework rule | Set expectations |
| 6 | Assign easy first homework | Ensure completion |
| 7 | Magic phrase to end | Professional close |

**Magic Phrase:**
> "I'll send you a recording of our call, confirmation of your homework, and a link to schedule your next call once your homework is complete."

### 3.4 Normal Call Outline (15 minutes)

| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| Before | Glance at notes: goal, previous homework |
| 0:00 | "How can I support you on today's call?" |
| ~9:00 | If 6 minutes left: "Let's make sure we figure out your homework" |
| ~11:00 | If 4 minutes left: "Do you mind if we confirm your homework?" |
| 13:00 | Ask: "What would you like your homework to be?" |
| 14:00 | Use magic phrase to end |

### 3.5 Between-Call Support

| Support | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Unlimited email support | Offer it - no one abuses it |
| Video replies | Use Loom - plants seed for video coaching upsells |
| Homework flexibility | If homework no longer serves, set aside and create new |
| Manual scheduling | If no spots available, schedule manually |

### 3.6 Upsell Psychology

**The "Apply What They've Spent" Principle:**

> "We'll apply the $997 you already invested toward this new program. So it's like you got the laser coaching for free."

**Early vs Late Relationship Upsells:**

| Timing | Strategy |
|--------|----------|
| Early (first few months) | Keep laser coaching - add something on top |
| Late (many months, low usage) | Replace laser coaching with new offer |

### 3.7 Upsell Examples for Income Engine

| Current | Upsell | Offer |
|---------|--------|-------|
| VIP ($197) | Infrastructure ($497) | "Apply your $197 - only pay $300 more" |
| VIP ($197) | VIP Weekend ($1,997) | "Apply $197 + 2 extra years of VIP" |
| VIP ($197) | Mastermind ($5,000+) | "Apply $197, pay rest over 12 months" |
