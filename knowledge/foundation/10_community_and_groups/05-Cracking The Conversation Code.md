ABOUT THIS LESSON
Getting a convo started can be easy and hard depending it is an inbound message (they reach out to you) or an
outbound message ( you reach out to them)
What we want to do in either case is disarm them. Everyone has their guard up these days due to so many quick
pitchers and unethical marketers.
This really gives us a chance to really wow folks by understanding them on a deep level.
Inbounds are best and that's what we teach lol, in some cases outbound will be necessary.
“ Hey name, thanks for reaching out. I just want to make it clear that I don’t work with just anyone as what I have to

offer isn't for everyone. I like to actually get to know folks to make sure I can help them first. Is it cool if I ask you a
few questions to see if I can truly help you?”
Get your answer, if yes then you can proceed. If not, you can dig a bit more into why they reached out.
What we really wanna do here as our main goal is simple

       Get a super clear understanding of where they are now and where they want to be.
       Help them see their struggles so they understand what has to happen for things to change. Really dig into
       those pains that are keeping them stuck from getting what they really want.
       Pour a cup of salt and alcohol into the cuts we just opened so it burns & dig into their pains (without pain there
       is no purchase)

We don’t do this to be assholes, but we do this to be polarizing as we get ready to show them the plan and solution.
Questions to kick this shit off:
        “So tell me a little bit about what you’ve done in the affiliate marketing space?”
        “Can you tell me a bit about you and where you're currently at in business or if there's anything you're looking
        to change right away?”
        “Tell me, why are you really wanting to make money from home?”
        “Can you tell me what you do online right now and what your expertise is?”
        “What are you currently doing to make money online?”
If the person doesn't respond well with genuine or real answers they may not be a good fit, still early to tell. But you

can make sure they are comfortable.
        “Hey, if this is a bad time I can give you a shout at a better time for you, I dont wanna waste your time”
This can shock folks because you're completely detached (Powerful)
You shouldn't care what kind of answers you get whether it's short or long, but just enough to fuel the convo as we
go. Pay close attention to their answers as we will be bringing them back up.
Test different questions of your own to see what works best for you
Once we are super clear on where they want to get to in their business and why, we can help them see the bigger
picture long-term as well as what is stopping them from getting it
Its pretty powerful to figure out what they make in total money each month or year so we can leverage that in the
convo
Not just online but offline as well. This is a temp check as I like to call it. Helps you find out if they actually have
money to invest without bringing up the price as a whole.
Again, we want to make them comfortable and feel good about sharing info and talking with us.
Itll make it easier to transition to why they want to achieve (insert want or desire)

By having profitable online business
Asking them the hard question is next
        “So how serious are you about making this happen”
        “So you mentioned earlier that you really wanted to do xyz, how serious are you about bringing that to life?”
This is where we can really use what they have shared with us the whole time.
Once you’ve found out theyre a good fit we can look to start talking about the offer that you think is best for them
based on what theyve told you.
This little smidget below is a JC framework, credit to him on this piece
Your Own Results To Back Up How You Can Help
        “Okay - it’s awesome you’re super committed to making this happen. I definitely think I can help you with that.
        Would you like me to tell you about how that would work?”
        “Okay - I can definitely help you achieve [goal / desired outcome]. Would you like me to tell you about how that
        would work?”
        “Okay - I can definitely help you achieve [goal / desired outcome]. Would you like me to tell you more about
        that?”
        “Okay - I can definitely help you achieve [goal / desired outcome]. Would you like me to tell you more about
        what I do?”
No Results Of Your Own (Or Even If You Have Results & Want To Leverage The Communities Success)
        “Awesome - well I’m part of a community where we have people making $X in X time period by following a
        simple system on FB. Want me to tell you more about that?
        “Awesome - well I’m part of a community where we have people making $X in X time period with affiliate
        marketing. Want me to tell you more about that?
Main thing honestly guys is to be emotionless to what happens.
At the end of every day we still have the solution and they still have a problem
