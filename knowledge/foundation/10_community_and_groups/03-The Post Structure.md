ABOUT THIS LESSON

Post Structure
0 - 5 seconds - The Hook - “How we Did 500k+ In 13 Months Without Sales Calls”
5 - 45 - Body / Solution / Reveal - “Short-form video content pointing to a Facebook group, where you’re then
leveraging long-form content to build connects & provide a deeper level of value, then kick-starting conversations off
the long content & leveraging a google doc showcasing an offer to close the sales”
X - 45 - Wrap Up CTA - “Now if you want a completely free training breaking down how you can leverage this same
process in your own business, i ran a training inside my group giving it away in high detail, (Tap link in bio, Drop a
comment)”
Always have some kind of CTA (call to action) in every video. Whether its to Follow you, comment, share, check out
the link in your bio, smash the heart button. (If you’re pre 1,000 followers - which is needed for link - go heavy on
“Follow or tap the plus)
I grew my   first account to over 40,000 followers in about 6 months and then TT deleted my account.
With what I knew I started a new account and grew it to 20,000 followers in 30 days and Monetized it almost
immediately.
What I focused on was my EEM Formula which stands for Entertain, Educate and Motivate.
One Of THE BIGGEST THINGS WITH SHORT FORM.. TEST TEST TEST
        Different styles, lengths, variations
Content that performs well for me, might not for you & vice versa. You have to FIND YOUR Content
One of the best aspects of leveraging short-form videos Like Tiktok is that you can also leverage one piece of
content on multiple social media platforms, Establishing omnipresence for your brand without having to do any
additional work
You can create 1 piece of content & Put it onto 5 different platforms.
        Tiktok
        Instagram Reels
        Facebook Reels (Insane For Organic FB marketers)
        Pinterest Stories
        Youtube Shorts
Even if you have a video that doesn’t do well on one platform, it still has the ability to on another platform.. Meaning:
        More Eyeballs, Leads, And Sales
