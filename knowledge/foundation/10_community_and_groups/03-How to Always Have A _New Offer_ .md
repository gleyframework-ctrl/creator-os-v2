**Promotion Dynamics &**

**How To Always Have A "NEW OFFER"**

It's not news that everyone likes new shiny stuff.. And changing the
dynamics of your promo will allow you to have a fresh face/coat of paint
on the same ol' thing you've already been selling.

If you notice, from the outside looking in on my business, I very rarely
promote the product im selling on the front end.

It's always some subsection of my stacked offer. One Component or a
fraction of that entire offer will be the face of what I'm selling.

This allows me to regularly Add physiological buyer triggers into my
offers constantly while still selling my evergreen thing.

Adding Time Sensitive, Limited Spots, X amount of bonuses.

It adds scarcity & that combined with "New" Is a recipe for a fatter
wallet..

You're basically Preselling one piece of your offer inside of your
content.. That becomes the face of your promo.. Which then also allows
you to do the same thing.. Next month.. The month after.. And so on.

**Ways you can effectively do this:**

-   An existing offer in place - The more in-depth your offer you have,
    > the different dynamics you can approach promotion

    -   IE - if you have 3 -weeks of coaching as a part of your offer,
        > going heavy in your content about 1 on 1 support or creating
        > lead gen content built around that aspect

    -   Case Study Group - For example, I rolled out a case study group
        > for exploding group growth and getting results on FB, but I
        > was selling the same thing that I was for over a year.

    -   Live launch/workshop - Similar to Group Juice & the way we
        > rolled it out.. But in the future, or if you're selling an
        > affiliate product, you're selling the "launch" on the front
        > end & monetizing with the affiliate product.

    -   Assets - Using assets you have, training you've done, videos you
        > have, as the face of your promo. Whether that\'s one single
        > video that over-delivers on video, or a compliation of many.

    -   Any other bonuses you're offering - Coaching, DFY, Set up,
        > anything can virtually become the face of your promo & the
        > type of content you're creating.

> Side note: Don't be afraid to roll out lower ticket promos, Lower cost
> things, yeah they might not yield those big results in the front end..
> But it will allow you to pick up on the ones who're a better fit for
> your higher ticket offers or find your rockstars that are going to
> crush it & inadvertently help your brand grow & testimonials stack.

-   Product - If you are promoting a product that covers a wide range of
    > things, you can promote one subsection of it as your front-end
    > offer.

    -   Ie - if you\'re selling a masterclass - you can take on a
        > subsection of the entire program, and use that as the fuel for
        > pre-selling inside of your content.

    -   Youre selling something dives deep into mindset - run a promo on
        > 4 Week mindset mastery - Course & combination of coaching

    -   You're selling something that Covers 4 Pillars of Marketing -
        > You now have 4 different offers that you can run.. 4 months of
        > a "brand new" Opportunity with scarcity. Time limits / Limited
        > spots.

**How to do this regularly every month -**

**One of the reasons that this can be very effective is because you can
add different layers to it every month like:**

-   **Time-sensitive -** Offer is closing in 2 weeks

-   **Limited spots -** offer is only open to 3 people

-   **The two combined -** Having a time exclusive offer & limited
    > availability lights a fire under people and really encourages them
    > to get in.

Now when you do this, you want to make it known that you closed the
doors on the offer, Spots filled, or time is up and the offer is no
longer available.

This will help in the future in your audience when you push something
that has a time-sensitive aspect to it because they know its not a false
scarcity.

Post about it everywhere - Countdowns. Tagging People in the posts when
they jump in.

ANNOUNCE IT WHEN YOU CLOSE THE DOORS! This will light a fire under
people when you do it again.. Because it's not a false scarcity promo.
(obviously don't add layers of scarcity if you\'re not changing
anything)

Be ethical.. If you're running a promo on X amount of coaching.. Pull
some off or add some more when the doors close.

\*\*Change the offer\*\*

**Changing the dynamics of Promo -**

The beauty of having your own offer is you can change things out on a
regular basis (which I would encourage you to)

1.  Pulling out portions or adding coaching & support

2.  Adding or removing physical bonuses

3.  Adding or removing relevant resources (Lives, training, Assets you
    > already have)

4.  Promoting the Evergreen offer from a different perspective (Making
    > one component of the product you're selling the face of your
    > promotion)

**Why This is very effective -**

1.  It plays into the Shiny object syndrome and lets you monetize.
    > People are always looking for something new, flashy, hot
    > opportunities that will eliminate all of their problems (obviously
    > be ethical, But it is a reality of the online space, so might as
    > well use it to your advantage)

2.  It allows you to layer in scarcity, without really having to do much
    > or change things. Time-sensitive, Limited availability

3.  You're going to stand out because on the outside looking in you're
    > selling something of your own, a component that\'s yours, it's
    > unique to you and your brand, so theoretically no one else has it,
    > there isn't any competition

4.  It will allow you to open up different types of convos with people,
    > giving you a better gauge on where they're at or what they're
    > doing to see if you can sell them something even if they're not
    > interested in your main offer

    a.  For example if im selling my 6-step fast start process and i
        > start having a conversation with someone & they're interested
        > in building out a blog, or a youtube channel instead of going
        > with the organic FB route\
        > \
        > I can change out the actual product I'm selling with ease, and
        > plug in a relevant product to what they want to do. Since they
        > inquired about my 6-Step Fast Start Program, I can just change
        > the product im monetizing with to something thats more
        > relevant to what they want to do.

5.  You can get hyper-specific in your content - Instead of posting
    > content and generic things like "you're going to get access to a
    > masterclass that will show you how to start an online business"\
    > \
    > Your content can become more pain point driven and future paced
    > (would do this either way but promoting like this makes it easier)
    > "You're going to learn the exact strategy that you can use to
    > eliminate your competition and get red hot leads raising their
    > hand because you have a compelling offer of your own"

6.  Can make stacking momentum and results in a lot easier, because
    > you're focusing on 1 subsection. Making it easier for you to get
    > results for the people you're working with & have a catalog of
    > testimonials & exploding your brand

    a.  Example - If your running a promo, that\'s about having your
        > first 1k day online or working with people while they build
        > their online business. It can be prolonged & quite sometime
        > before you're able to deliver those results to your students.\
        > \
        > But if you're running a promo on Offer Creation. Every time a
        > person creates an offer, that\'s a case study/testimonial.\
        > \
        > You're running a promo on setting up your Organic FB system,
        > every time someone sets up their system, That\'s a result to
        > leverage.
