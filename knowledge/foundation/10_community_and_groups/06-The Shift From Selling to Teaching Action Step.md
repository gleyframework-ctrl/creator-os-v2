This session isn't about selling harder.

It's about removing friction.

When you only push your offer, you're asking people to jump too far.
(You also drain your audience)

When you give clarity first, the offer becomes easier to say yes to.

The workshop works because it makes success with your product feel
simpler before money is involved.
