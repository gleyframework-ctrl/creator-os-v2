# **Your Group Positioning**

How clear outcomes create natural ascension without pressure.

## **WHAT THIS LESSON IS REALLY ABOUT**

Entry Positioning is not about getting people into your group.

It\'s about designing the path out of the group.

Most people think selling starts when they pitch.

In reality, selling starts the moment someone enters your world.

If your entry positioning is clear, ascension happens automatically.

## **THE CORE PROBLEM MOST PEOPLE CREATE**

Most communities are positioned as \"free value,\" \"support,\" or \"a
place to hang out.\"

This creates no direction.

When there\'s no direction:

People stay comfortable

Momentum stalls

Paid offers feel random

Selling feels awkward

You end up needing launches, pressure, and urgency tactics---not because
your offer isn\'t good, but because your group has no designed outcome.

## **THE SHIFT THAT CHANGES EVERYTHING**

When your community is targeted at a specific outcome, three things
happen:

1.  People join with intent

2.  Progress becomes visible

3.  The next problem becomes obvious

Once someone solves Problem A inside your group, Problem B naturally
appears.

Your paid offer is simply the solution to Problem B.

That\'s ascension by design.

## **WHY THIS REMOVES PRESSURE FROM SELLING**

When the group outcome is clear, you don\'t need to convince, chase, or
run constant lives.

People already know:

What you help with

How you think

What it feels like to be inside your process

The sale becomes a continuation, not a decision.

## **ENTRY POSITIONING: THE 4 COMPONENTS**

These four elements make selling inevitable.

### **1️⃣ A Problem-Based Entry Frame**

Your group must be anchored to one outcome---not your whole business or
your full framework, but one clear problem you help remove.

Narrow entry allows people to self-identify faster and makes the next
step obvious.

### **2️⃣ A Visible Process (Proof of Motion)**

People don\'t buy promises. They buy patterns.

When your group shows you solving problems live and others making
progress, people start asking: \"Okay... what\'s next?\"

That question is ascension knocking.

### **3️⃣ A Clear \"This Leads to More\" Signal**

Do not hide your paid offers. Do not shove them in faces.

Openly state: \"This group helps you fix X. When you\'re ready to fix Y,
there\'s a next step.\"

This builds trust and frames selling as a service.

No bait-and-switch.

### **4️⃣ A Natural Problem Ladder**

Every good community solves one problem now and reveals the next one
naturally:

Clarity → Implementation

Awareness → Execution

Fixing one leak → Scaling

Your paid offer exists because the next problem cannot be solved at the
free level.

That\'s not pressure. That\'s honesty.

## **WHAT ASCENSION LOOKS LIKE WHEN DONE RIGHT**

You will notice:

DMs start with \"I think I need...\"

People reference specific posts or wins

Pricing resistance drops

Sales calls feel unnecessary

You\'re not selling. You\'re confirming readiness.

## **WHY THIS SCALES WITHOUT YOU**

When ascension is designed into the environment, the system works
without your constant presence.

The group warms the buyers

Proof does the selling

Conversations replace pitches

This is how income stops resetting monthly.

You didn\'t work harder. The environment did the work for you.

## **ENTRY POSITIONING CHECKLIST**

Before moving on, confirm:

-   \[ \] Does my group have one clear outcome?

-   \[ \] Is progress toward that outcome visible?

-   \[ \] Is the \"next problem\" implied or stated?

-   \[ \] Does my paid offer solve that next problem?

-   \[ \] Does selling feel like alignment instead of effort?
