## **The Step Before the Sale**

**What Your Audience Needs Before They Are Ready to Buy**

Most people think customers say \"no\" because of price, timing, or
trust. That is rarely the real issue.

People do not buy because something is missing *before* your offer ever
enters the conversation. Until that missing piece is resolved, no amount
of explanation works. This is where most businesses waste effort.

### **1. Buyers Do Not Start at the Offer**

Nobody wakes up ready to buy your solution. They start much earlier in
the process.

They start confused.

They start unsure.

They start with the wrong explanation for why they are stuck.

If you speak to them as if they are already ready to buy, you lose them.
This model accounts for that gap.

### **2. The Concept of the \"Pre-Problem\"**

Every offer solves a problem. But before that problem, there is a
**Pre-Problem**.

The Pre-Problem is the specific obstacle that keeps someone stuck long
before they are ready to look for a solution. It acts as a barrier. If
they cannot get past this, they cannot understand why they need your
help.

**Common examples of Pre-Problems:**

-   **Lack of Words:** They cannot explain what they sell clearly.

-   **Wrong Focus:** They think their content is the issue, when it is
    > actually what they are selling.

-   **False Beliefs:** They believe they need more people to see their
    > work, rather than fixing how they talk to the people already
    > there.

-   **Feeling Lost:** They feel busy but have no direction.

-   **Self-Blame:** They blame their discipline instead of their lack of
    > a plan.

Until you fix this specific issue, offering them a solution feels
premature.

### **3. The Workshop Solves the Pre-Problem**

This is the real job of the workshop in this system.

It is not teaching just for the sake of teaching.

It is not overwhelming them with information.

It is not a sales pitch with slides.

**The workshop exists to do one single thing:**

Move someone from confusion into clarity.

It proves to them that they were focused on the wrong thing, and shows
them the right thing. Once they have clarity, buying becomes the logical
next step.
