The real power in the content creation game is behind how long you’ve been doing it consistently, and how much
your audience has consumed from you. Minimal consumption, interaction, “New to you” = harder & longer sales
process
Lots of consumption, interaction “Not New To YOu” = Easier, Quicker & Faster Sales Process
Blogging & Youtube

Both take MASSIVE amounts of content creation & prolonged periods before you can start to see traffic from them..
But once you do.. You'll likely be getting it for along time
