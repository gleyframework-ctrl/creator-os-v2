## 4. THE STORIES TO SALES METHOD

### 3-Step Formula

| Step | Name | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| 1 | The Hook (Story) | Agitate pain, deliver CTA |
| 2 | The Automation (DM) | Capture email, deliver freebie |
| 3 | The Nurture (Email) | Build relationship, sell |

### Step 1: The Hook (Story Template)

**Slide 1:** Emotional intro hook
- "Are you tired of burning domains?"

**Slide 2:** Simple Yes/No question
- "Do you want to know why?"

**Slide 3:** Branded photo with solution and CTA
- "I burned 17 domains before I figured this out. Reply 'BURN' and I'll send you the fix."

### Step 2: The Automation (DM Flow)

**Message 1:**
> "Thanks for the reply! Here's the free guide you requested: [link]. Quick question - do you want to get notified when I open spots for my Infrastructure program? Reply 'YES' and I'll add you to the priority list."

**Message 2 (if they reply YES):**
> "Awesome! What's your best email? I'll send you the details as soon as spots open."

### Step 3: The Nurture (Email Sequence)

Once email captured, they enter automated sequences.

You now own the relationship and can sell repeatedly.
