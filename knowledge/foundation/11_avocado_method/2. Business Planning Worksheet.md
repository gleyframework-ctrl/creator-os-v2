**[NICHE 1-]{.underline}**

**Passion**

๏ Would you help these people for free?

๏ What do folks tell you you're good at?

๏ What comes easy to you?

**Problem**

๏What do you believe is the explicit need?

๏What problems are you equipped to help them solve?

๏Which problems do you want to help them solve?

๏Can you solve it better, cheaper or faster?

**Profit**

๏Can they afford to get help?

๏How big is this niche?

๏What are the benefits if they were to solve these problems?

๏What do you think you can charge?

Like to charge? 3-6k What if you doubled that?

**Presence**

๏How will you be seen as the "go-to" provider?

๏How competitive is the niche? (quantify)

๏What messaging is being used by competitors in the market?

**Pathway**

๏How easily can you target online?

๏Which social channels dominate?
