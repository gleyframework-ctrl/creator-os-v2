Pitfalls To Avoid
          Latest trends
-   They come & go, some won’t be here in a few months even
          Not building a strong foundation
-   Nothing great was ever built without a strong foundation
-   The only thing that will stop everything crumbling when there’s an earthquake
-   If your mind is not right it will always be an uphill battle

-   Focus on building skills
          Trying to go it alone
-   Waste too much time compiling information
-   Have nobody to ask questions when you get stuck
-   Easy to get distracted or told something that isn’t helpful
-   Lots of bad advice floating around out there
          Getting overwhelmed with tech
-   Don’t need funnels to start with
-   If you can use Facebook, you can do affiliate marketing

-   Learn one platform at a time
-   Save money on software
-   Keep things bare bone simple to start with
          Getting overwhelmed with platforms
-   Spreading yourself too thin across all of them
-   Never really master or pick up traction on any of them
-   Leads to getting burned out because of the time it takes & no results
          Spamming your link
-   Damage your reputation from the get go
-   People don’t care unless they at least know who you are
-   Lead to burn out as you will never get results
-   Upset a whole lot of people
          Not building assets
-   If something happens to your profile, what happens then?

-   Never really know when a platform will be shut down/could lose anything
-   Assets allow things to become more passive over time
