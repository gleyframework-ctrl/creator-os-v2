## MODULE 4: PFFA EMAIL SYSTEM

### 4.1 Sell → Nurture → Sell Framework

| Phase | Purpose | Timing |
|-------|---------|--------|
| SELL | Present offer | First |
| NURTURE | Build relationship | Days 0-4 |
| SELL | Present next offer | Days 5+ |

### 4.2 The 7 Sales Email Types

| Email | Purpose | Structure |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| 1. Announcement | "Doors open" | Hook + what's happening + why + urgency + CTA |
| 2. Story | Build trust | Relatable hook + story + shift + invite + CTA |
| 3. Testimonial | Social proof | Before + shift + testimonial + reflection + CTA |
| 4. Problem/Solution | Address pain | Problem + validation + shift + offer + CTA |
| 5. Objection Handling | Address hesitation | Call out objection + validate + reframe + CTA |
| 6. FAQ | Answer doubts | Intro + 3-5 Q&As + deadline + CTA |
| 7. Final Call | Last chance | Hook + recap + deadline + CTA |

### 4.3 PFFA Bootcamp Email Schedule

| Day | Intention | Subject |
|-----|-----------|---------|
| 0 | Welcome | Welcome to the Bootcamp |
| 1-4 | Nurture | FIX YOUR FUNNEL: Day X [TOPIC] |
| 5 | Invite | A special invite |
| 6 | Story | How I replaced launches with $30K months |
| 7 | Testimonial | She made $13K from $39 product |
| 8 | Case Study | Course launch breakdown |
| 9 | Objection | What if your course could sell without you? |
| 10 | Story | She didn't burn out to scale |
| 11 | Urgency | Closer than you think |
| 12 | FAQ | Questions answered + discount expiring |

### 4.4 Email Writing Principles

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| One goal per email | Clarity increases conversion |
| Short paragraphs | Easy to read on mobile |
| Personal voice | Builds connection |
| Clear CTA | Tell them exactly what to do |
| P.S. matters | Most-read part |

### 4.5 Subject Line Types

| Type | Example |
|------|---------|
| Curiosity | "She made $13K from a $39 product" |
| Urgency | "Discount expiring" |
| Value | "Day 1 ATTRACT" |
| Story | "How I replaced stressful launches" |
| Question | "What if your course could sell without you?" |
