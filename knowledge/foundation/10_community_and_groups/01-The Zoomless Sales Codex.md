ABOUT THIS LESSON
Let’s talk sales, the art of turning complete strangers coming into your group into high-paying clients all while
keeping things as simple & lean as possible.
Before we dive in, we first need clarity on a VERY real element to product sales online: The Customer Journey
A good way to look at it is like: Everything that you’re doing, day in and day out in your business is like a mini piece of
digital real estate. Whether it’s making you money right now, or not, you can start to see the fruits of your labor over
time.
And even if what you’re doing today isn’t cranking those commission emails to your inbox, it doesn’t mean that they
won't.
A couple of examples of this would be:
         There will be people you connected with quite a few times over time, Various social media channels,
        Mastermind groups, and some chats here & there, but eventually turned into a HT Sale
        People will come off referrals and had chats with people who’ve never bought anything from, but have referred
        dozens of sales or people to me, because of the convos that we’ve had
        There will be people who’re watching and lurking on your stuff, even if you never talk to them, and everything
        that you’re doing can inadvertently push them closer to the point of being ready to buy
FB Customer Journey (2 Days > 12 Months+)
Think one of the biggest hurdles many faces, is understanding that the customer journey can take different times for
each prospect that comes through your system, and whatever you’re doing.

You’ll have people that come in and buy almost instantly and people who come in and take months to be ready.
People are naturally taking longer to be at the point of buying, They’re doing more research, there is a lot more
scams & garbage out there so people are more skeptical.
By no means am I saying you’re going to need to keep to talking to people every day for 12 months, to get them to
the point of buying?
But everything that you’re doing, And consistently showing up in your biz (putting out some type of content will all
start to stack) & inadvertently move people towards your goal of sale.
        The more content people consume from you, the easier & easier sales will naturally become because they’re
        getting to build rapport with you, and with content this happens at scale.
