**The Cash Mullet**

Value On The Front - Sales On The Back

Now, this could be an awesome analogy for your business as a whole - but
we're going to specifically break this strategy in regards to Promos &
kicking off an offer/launch.

Funny Story - This is something both of us figured out in real-time, it
wasn't intentional at first - but as it continued to work over and over
again - we leaned further into it.

Over time we realized the real power of the strategy when kickin' off a
promo cycle or an offer.

**Free Training To Build Goodwill & Drop Tons Of Value + Extend
Invitation To Offer On The Back**

**We've done this with ALL OUR OFFER THIS YEAR:**

-   Group Juice

-   Juice Box

-   Tuan JV / Interview

-   MMM

-   PPT

-   Jill & Jeanette's Groups

-   The same thing we're going to be doing with Thami Bundle

You can run a free training VERY SIMILAR to the exact offer that you
have - preselling on the exact strategies that you're paid opportunity
is going to be solving.

Look at the live they just ran in SAA - Where Chris was diving deep into
copy & breaking down strategies behind it - then extended an invite to
join him on the masterclass

You'll get a real-time example of this if you watch what we're doing
with Group training with Thadd & Demi

Put Together Training you're going to run live - use this as an anchor
post throughout promo or even just to inject a couple of quick sales
when you'd like

-   PPT Training Was on the 4 pillars to build you first or next 100k
    > business (same things we dived deeper into over 8 weeks)

-   Thami training is on 5 strategies (all covered inside our training)

Also give you the scope to pull on resources or doc you already have to
put together the training you're running for free (less work on your
part)

Works well because naturally, you'll have a bit more volume attending
live because it's a "free thing"

The goal would be to actually give them a tangible thing on the free
training - so they could actually go implement something afterward
(makes working with you even more appealing / reinforces proof of
concept & that you know your shit)

**Power Of This**

-   Running the training in itself is PRESELLING your offer - especially
    > if you can embed wins/results into the strategy

-   It's a leveraged thing that you can continue to push traffic to -
    > even after the live for weeks/months - depending on how long
    > you're running a promo or however long you want to keep it inside
    > the group

-   The Sales on the back end are usually VERY LOW pressure - They've
    > spent X amount of time with you already & lit up about the fact
    > that you're dropping nuggets on them - Getting them into
    > Full-offer is a breeze

-   Can work really well to kick off the momentum of a full-promo cycle.
    > Snag a few sales right out of the gate & it makes all the other
    > components spicier

    -   Limited Availability/proof of concept because they'll see other
        > people tipping in or even interested to join

-   Step Further Away from DM - Resuming your regular programming
    > posting content & running promo / In dm people ask questions about
    > X - you can tag them on the training & have them circle back with
    > you after

**Pre-Value**

-   Give yourself 3 - 10 days to build up your curiosity about your
    > upcoming "free training"

-   Get Event Scheduled Inside group so you can invite everyone to
    > attend

-   Tap Content everywhere you can about the upcoming training

    -   Profile, Group, Stories, Content & Emails

        -   Tackle portions of the training you're going to be doing /
            > cycle content / outcome

-   Could lean into SHORTER "Purple Posts" as they'll usually get more
    > engagement/eyeballs and that's exactly what you want - To feed the
    > live

**Post Training**

If you had a few converts into offers on the back end of live - Can lean
heavily into this.

Follow Up With **EVERYONE** who joined you - even if they didn't say
"hey i want the offer"

**Feed The Training -**

-   Regular content strategy/rotation but mixing in a CTA to catch a
    > free training you just did on X to help the Accomplish X or
    > without X

-   Use this as "Bait" inside your group - so you can talk about it
    > externally & even inside the group

-   Embed takeaways from those who attended the training & results from
    > it

**Can Look at the live as an "anchor post" - That'll:**

-   Build goodwill

-   Handle Certain objections

-   Presell for you
