                                   Price

Let’s talk about price. This eight Deep Dive is completely dedicated to how we are influenced by
price. Again, let’s start with the definition of marketing: marketing is the process of communicating
the value – or ‘utility’ – of a product or service to customers, for the purpose of influencing buyer
behavior and/or precipitate behavioral change. So, actually we are selling value. But surprisingly,
there is almost no relationship between value and price. We have trouble explaining this finding.
Since we are ‘evidence everything’ and data driven, we should investigate this relationship!

This deep dive we will discuss how we place more emphasis on value than price. Besides that,
we will explain more about why so many people exit a website when it comes to paying and how
method- & timing of payment can prevent this behavior.
