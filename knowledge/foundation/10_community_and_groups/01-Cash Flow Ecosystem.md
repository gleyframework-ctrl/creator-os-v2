ABOUT THIS LESSON
The Core Focus of the organic FB System Should be group growth. Building out a tribe of people & a group of your
own.
Reasons This Is The MOST POWERFUL Part

      This is YOUR space, you’re the leader, and everyone in there is likely looking at you for advice. Your Rules.
      It adds a layer of exclusivity to your business, If you’re building out content on your profile or other platforms,

      and you have the training inside your groups it’s going to make people feel FOMO for not being inside.
      It gives you a different position for marketing, you can push your “free” group on the front end of everything

      you’re doing, while still getting exposure to your affiliate links, Brand & content, You can get market research &
      build your email address all at once.
      Eventually, It will grow by itself. If your group is set up properly, You will get organic in-bound leads daily.
      Interested in the topic your group is about.
      It will also give you an infinite amount of Inbound conversations, Most of your sales with FB will be done

      through Messenger & instead of scouring other people’s groups for leads or people who’d be interested, you’ll
      be able to add & dm people directly from your own group. Significantly increases the chances of a relationship
      enveloping you because they already know who you are.

Point ALL Of your Traffic Sources To Your FB GROUP.
       Tiktok
       IG
       Your Facebook Profile
       Pinterest
       Youtube Shorts
       Facebook Reels
(You can repurpose 1 piece of content on all of them)
Make the link/call to action for any platform you have content on to join your community. This will keep everything in
your business Linear, and allow you to nurture the members of your community & establish deep connections with
them.
Which ultimately, makes selling easier.
Visual Map Of Your Ecosystem:
    Group Juice Workflow - Structuring your Business to grow your group
Benefits of structuring your business in this fashion:

      It’s a simplistic
                      approach that keeps your business lean & linear, meaning at any point in the process you’re
      going to be able to identify what is & isn’t working so you can know where to put energy
      When done correctly, it can become highly leveraged, meaning you can build a flow that takes people from
      random group member to 4-fig+ paid clients seamlessly
      Overtime this structure can give you the ability to eliminate “selling from your profile”, adding friends from
      groups, or packing your calendar with calls because you have your own space where people can come in and
      indoctrinate themselves into your way of doing things

Pre-Client Flow

      Traffic Sources / Content Creation / Tiktok / IG / FB Profile
      Identification / Group Members Area - Outreach with the goal of gauging where someone is at

             Nurture Phase - Tagging newer (Green members on relevant content) Get them as active as possible in
             your community & start punching them in the face with value
             Pushing To Offer - People coming into your community who’re struggling and already problem/solution
             aware, moving into offers/sales convos

      Your Leveraged Content - Group Posts, Training, Offers, “Content Modes”
      The Follow-Up - Messaging & Sparking conversations with those who’re engaging on content, Commenting on
      your posts / Taking action on your CTA’s
      Making Offer & When Sale Happens Moving Into Buyers Flow
