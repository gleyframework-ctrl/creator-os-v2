ABOUT THIS LESSON
Post Content To Build Connections With People
Follow Up With the people who start engaging on your content (even if they’re just liking/hearting your posts)
Move into a conversation & see if they’d be a good fit for whatever you’re selling.
If they are, sell them something, if they aren’t dont.

To Recap On Your FULL FLOW: 1. Your Posting Content To Create Attention To Yourself With the end goal of getting
those folks into your group
2. Your creating in your community to dive deeper, presell on your offers, your process, your methodology 3. Your
connecting with folks in your worlds & chatting with them, starting a relationship
4. Your inviting the folks that WOULD BE A GOOD FIT / NEED YOUR THING to buy your offer
