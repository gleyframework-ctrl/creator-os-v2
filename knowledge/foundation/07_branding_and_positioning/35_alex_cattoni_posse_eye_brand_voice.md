## DAY 1: CONVICTION CODES

### 1.1 The 6 Questions

| # | Question | What to Answer |
|---|----------|----------------|
| 1 | What personal credo or belief is most important to you? | What do you think/worry/talk about? What do you wish people knew? |
| 2 | What incident/event defined this belief? | A specific story - where were you? |
| 3 | How does this belief play out in your business? | How did it inspire your business? |
| 4 | Where is this belief lacking in your industry? | What makes you angry? What myths exist? |
| 5 | How can this belief impact people's lives? | What is your audience craving? |
| 6 | How will this belief shape your future? | What impact do you want to make? |

### 1.2 Power Values Exercise

1. Brainstorm 10+ non-negotiables (core values)
2. Distill to **4 Power Values**

### 1.3 Conviction Codes Template

For each Power Value, write 3 "We believe..." statements:

| Level | Statement |
|-------|-----------|
| Company | How team upholds value |
| Customer | How you demonstrate in products/services |
| Community | How it shapes content/marketing |

**Example (Empathy):**
- Company: "We believe caring is cool. It's our superpower."
- Customer: "We believe everyone can change their life."
- Community: "We believe every voice deserves to be heard."

### 1.4 Example Power Values

| Brand | Power Values |
|-------|---------------|
| Copy Posse | Empathy, Integrity, Fun, Authenticity |
| Slack | Courtesy, Craftsmanship, Playfulness, Inclusivity |
| Prophet | Empathy, Growth, Sustainability, Commitment |

## DAY 2: THE MENTOR'S MIRROR

### 2.1 The 12 Brand Archetypes

| Archetype | Core Value | Desire | Fear |
|-----------|------------|--------|------|
| Innocent | Safety | Paradise | Being punished |
| Everyman | Belonging | Connection | Being left out |
| Hero | Mastery | Prove worth | Weakness |
| Caregiver | Service | Protect others | Selfishness |
| Explorer | Freedom | Authentic life | Trapped |
| Outlaw | Liberation | Disrupt | Powerlessness |
| Lover | Intimacy | Connect | Being alone |
| Creator | Innovation | Vision | Inauthenticity |
| Jester | Pleasure | Live in moment | Boredom |
| Sage | Knowledge | Truth | Being duped |
| Magician | Power | Dreams come true | Negative consequences |
| Ruler | Control | Order | Chaos |

### 2.2 Brand Profile Template

`[Brand] is a/an [Archetype]. We value [Core Value] above all else. We stand against [Problem 1], [Problem 2], [Problem 3] and are seeking to create [Change 1], [Change 2], [Change 3].`

### 2.3 Customer Profile Template

`Meet [Name], the [Archetype]. He/she values [Core Value]. He/she is experiencing [Challenge 1], [Challenge 2], [Challenge 3] and is looking for help to achieve [Benefit 1], [Benefit 2], [Benefit 3].`

### 2.4 Customer Archetype Elements

- Challenges they face (3 problems)
- Benefits they seek (3 outcomes)
- Give them a name

## DAY 3: MESSAGING HELIX

### 3.1 Mission Statement Formula

| Element | Question |
|---------|----------|
| Brand Point A | "We're fighting against..." |
| Brand Point B | "We envision a world where..." |
| Action Plan | "We [verb]..." - actual steps |

**Template:**
`We're on a mission to change [Point A] and achieve [Point B] by [action step 1], [action step 2]...`

### 3.2 Brand Promise Formula

| Element | Question |
|---------|----------|
| Audience | "We serve..." |
| Customer Point A | "They are looking to overcome..." |
| Customer Point B | "They dream of..." |
| Your Solution | "We create/offer/build..." |

**Template:**
`We create/offer/build [solution] to help [audience] overcome [challenge] and achieve [dream].`

### 3.3 Positioning Strategy

**Formula:**
`[Unique advantage in a few words] – [one-sentence explanation].`

**Examples:**

| Brand | Positioning |
|-------|-------------|
| Prophet | Creative & Sustainable Growth Strategy – We help you stay competitive through creative growth. |
| Slack | A Beautifully Simple Digital-First Ecosystem – We reduce noise for better workflow. |
| Copy Posse | The Raddest, Baddest Posse on The Internet – We're redefining modern marketing. |

## DAY 4: STORY EMPIRE

### 4.1 The Domino Effect (6 Dominos)

| Domino | Question |
|--------|----------|
| Empire | Your big scary vision |
| Today | Your brand now - who you serve |
| Momentum | When did you realize momentum? |
| Discovery | The "aha" moment |
| Echo | Defining event that led to discovery |
| Trigger | The moment that started everything |

### 4.2 3-Act Story Structure

| Act | Dominos | What to Write |
|-----|---------|----------------|
| The Challenge | Trigger + Echo | Status quo and core challenge |
| The Discovery | Discovery + Momentum | Discovery and early stages |
| The Empire | Today + Empire | Where you are and where going |

### 4.3 Story Template

**Act 1 - The Challenge:**
- Where were you?
- What happened?
- What was the struggle?

**Act 2 - The Discovery:**
- What was the turning point?
- What did you discover?
- How did momentum build?

**Act 3 - The Empire:**
- Where are you today?
- What's your vision?
- How can others join?

## DAY 5: BRAND ALCHEMY

### 5.1 Phraseology - Brand Glossary

**Questions:**
1. How would you informally explain your brand?
2. What ideas/concepts do you want associated with you?
3. Define at least 10 terms/phrases.

**Example (Copy Posse):**

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| The Posse | Our global crew |
| Rad | Heart-centered, empathetic, real |
| Possify | Make marketing fun and engaging |
| Empathy Empire | World where brands lead with heart |
| De-douchify | End manipulative sales tactics |
| Rally Cry | Unifying call to action |
| Benny | Our baddie bot |
| Juicy Bennies | Powerful benefits |
| #Win | Sharing successes |
| Modern Marketing | Values, branding, storytelling |

### 5.2 Tone of Voice

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| How do you want audience to FEEL? | List emotions |
| 4 adjectives describing tone | e.g., bold, playful, empathetic, direct |

### 5.3 Rally Cry Formula

Answer these questions:
- Why are you doing this? (Mission)
- Who do you serve? (Brand Promise)
- How do you create unique transformation? (Positioning)
- Include Call to Action

**Example (Copy Posse):**
> The Copy Posse is the raddest, baddest crew on a mission to de-douchify the Internet, redefine modern marketing and make caring cool again.
>
> Your message matters and your words can change the world.
>
> Through our programs and community, we've helped over 300,000 rad humans find freedom, have fun and sell more... without selling out.
>
> Join our empathy empire.

- Archetypes (Brand + Customer)
- 3-Part Brand Story
- Rally Cry
