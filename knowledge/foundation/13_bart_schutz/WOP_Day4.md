                       Needs & Motivation

This fourth Deep Dive is completely dedicated to understanding more about human needs and
motivation. When thinking about consumer psychology, one of the first questions that comes up
is: “how can we motivate consumers to buy our products or services?” In order to answer that
question, it is important to understand the psychology behind those needs and motivation. Once you
know and understand how they are formed, you can start to influence the formation of these needs
and motivations. And that’s where the fun starts. But let’s first dive into the theoretical basics.

order to do what’s good for us in the future”
