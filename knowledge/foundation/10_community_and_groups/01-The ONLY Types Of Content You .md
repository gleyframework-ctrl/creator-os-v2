ABOUT THIS LESSON
Let’s talk content, a piece that is arguably the hardest hurdle for most to jump over. Yet can be one of the most
powerful aspects of it all.
        The better job you can do with your content, the shorter & easier sales become
        Content has the ability to build deeper relationships with people at scale, meaning you can connect with
        multiple people at a singular time
        It will over time create more leverage inside of your business, the more content your audience people have

        been consuming from you, for longer periods of time, sales become a breeze.
But before we dive in:
Now, this is where we will differ from what most will tell you..
Model everyone else.. Copy trends.. Viral videos..
And I'm here to tell you - If you become a carbon copy of everyone else online - you will get stuck like the masses
online.
YOU - Inject yourself into your content.
      Your humor
      Your personality
      Lessons & experiences

Why?
Because no matter what you’re doing in this online world - whether you’re selling dildos, dump trucks, or digital
courses through the power of free content creation..
You’re selling yourself and building a personal brand - An audience of like-minded people embarking on the same
journey as you & want to do similar things.
And the one component that is required for anyone to buy anything - they need to know you.
The connection that you build through the content is what will make having $1,000 days regularly an easy thing..
The number of people you will repel - by being you is actually a good thing - you don’t want clients that you don’t vibe
with or connect with.
Why Are You Posting Content?
       To build a connection - without your prospects feeling like they know you, trust you & like you, they won’t buy
       from you
       To pre-sell - On your offers - Your beliefs - the strategies you’re leveraging - your brand as a whole
       To plant seeds - the more content you’re creating over prolonged periods of time - the shorter & shorter sales
       convos become because your content has built rapport with them before you’ve even made an offer

The Long-Form Content Structure -
      Eye-catching Headline - Here Is How I Had An 11k Day From A FB Group Of 43 People

      Lead into an ongoing problem (Mutual Frustration, Ex. Not Generating quality leads) Talk about how it was
      when you were dealing with the ongoing problem, How you felt, what was it that frustrated you, and what did
      that frustration cause in life and business, get specific here.

      Your Backstory (failures and downfalls in your journey)You can lean into the beginning of your journey, things
      that didn't pan out, failures, other challenges you faced, setbacks and real struggles etc
      What you found to work for you, leading into your offer (Ex. I started doing XYZ using ABC)….(more detail)
      What did you start seeing or realizing? What did you find that you were missing, and how did you come across
      it, be real.

      Results and Benefits of Discovering this method (All the cool shit that comes with your offer) What did you
      start experiencing? Growth in what areas, aha moments, mindset and perspective shifts, more sales more
      leads, engagement, group growth, followers, more confidence, more drive etc
      End it with your CTA (call to action) Ex. Drop a #me to get (whatever you are offering)Or to join your group etc

Go Pain & Solution Focused
      You’re selling a diet plan - you’re not creating content about how someone's life sucks because they’re
      overweight - your painting the picture as to how they would feel once they’ve lost weight
      You’re in the high-ticket affiliate marketing space - you’re not telling people how shitty their life is because they
      aren’t willing to invest in themselves - You’re revealing the actual benefit of investing into something & what
      it’s actually costing them not to (their time)
