**Monday - Vulnerable**

**Tell a Story in your post of your own that you can incorporate
business into. Be Intimate in what you're sharing to let people know who
you are, what kind of person you are. Go Live and encourage engagement,
ask questions that will be controversial to mindset, aha moments,
perspective shifts to spark comments and conversation**

**Tuesday - Announcement**

**Make an Announcement of an upcoming training you\'re hosting in your
group or via zoom. Share your wins, results, student results, things
you're working on etc.**

**Offer a Lead magnet or the fact you want to create one if the interest
is there. This should be a hand raising post to generate leads and
opportunity**

**Wednesday - Gift of the Soul**

**Give your Audience Something that will help them get a quick win or
Result. Something that you have learned that has helped you generate a
specific Result**

**( It doesn\'t have to be monetary )**

**Thursday - Engagement**

**Here you want to share a Fact or Joke, something to provoke Engagement
with your Audience. Get Creative with things you like as a hobby or
something you enjoy, Share your sense of Humor.**

**Friday - Lifestyle**

**Here you want to Really open up about who you are as a person and what
you really care about. Family, friends, kids, spouse. Things you like
today aside from business and work.**

**On all days make sure you have a very specific CTA. Optimize for
whatever it is you want to grow, your following, your group, or to
comment 'xyz' to get 'abc' Just have some fun for once and test things
for a bit.**
