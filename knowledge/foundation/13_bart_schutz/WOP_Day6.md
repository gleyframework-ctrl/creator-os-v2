                         DEEP DIVE 6
                   Self, Social & Situation

This sixth Deep Dive is completely dedicated to how we are influenced by situational factors. How
does our social relation to others in the situation affect our decisions? And what about other situational
factors like the weather, politics and technological innovations?

Our aim for today is to learn you:
1. Why behavioral and neuroscientists think ‘the self’ is an illusion that we need for interacting with
   others and the world.
2. To what extend ‘personality’ is influencing our behavior, and how we often incorrectly attribute the
   causes of our (and others’) behavior to personalities.
3. Why and how our online behavior is influenced by the fact that we are social beings.
4. How other external factors like competitors, the real-time context, and ‘macro influencers’ like
   the weather and technology are influencing our behavior.
5. How you can apply these theories and tactics to improve your online dialogue.

Enjoy this Deep Dive (and the social world surrounding you)!
