## 1. CORE TRUTH

Hooks are the first 2-6 seconds of a short-form video.

During this window, the viewer decides whether to opt in.

The hook matters EXPONENTIALLY more than the rest of the video.

If they churn at 4 seconds, the last 51 seconds are wasted.

---

## 2. CHOICE VS REFLEX

| Long-Form Content | Short-Form Video |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| Viewers choose to start based on headline | Videos are fed automatically |
| They already opted in | Forced to react with reflex |
| Lower bar for hook | Hook must be THAT much better |

Income Engine: Your TikTok viewers didn't choose you. The algorithm put you there. Your hook has 2-6 seconds to make them CHOOSE to stay.

---

## 3. THE 4 HOOK COMPONENTS

Every winning hook has 4 components working together.

| Component | What It Is | Example |
|-----------|------------|---------|
| Text Hook | What you write on screen | "STOP Burning Domains" |
| Visual Hook | What you show | Screenshot of error message |
| Spoken Hook | What you say | "If your emails keep landing in spam..." |
| Audio Hook | Music + SFX | Tension music, "ding" when solution appears |

### The Secret: MAX ALIGNMENT

The difference between 500 and 500,000 views is achieving max alignment across all 4 components.

If you say one thing but the visual shows something different = COMPREHENSION LOSS.

They literally don't understand what they're seeing.

---

## 4. TWO TYPES OF VALUE

| Type | What It Is | Example |
|------|------------|---------|
| Educational (Useful) | Tactical information solving pain | "Exact Postfix setting that stops burns" |
| Entertainment (Interesting) | Story creating emotional response | "I burned 17 domains before figuring this out" |

Both require the same psychological hook triggers.

---

## 5. ON-TARGET CURIOSITY LOOP

To hook a viewer:

1. ON-TARGET - Topic is relevant to them
2. CURIOSITY LOOP - Unanswered question creates circular thinking

If viewer is CERTAIN this video is about something they care about, AND the hook opens an unanswered question, they will stay.

---

## 6. ENGINEERING ON-TARGET (CLARITY)

How to ensure viewer knows what video is about:

| Tactic | What It Means | Example |
|--------|---------------|---------|
| Rapid context | Tell topic in first sentence | "If your emails keep landing in spam..." |
| Short simple words | Increase clarity | "Domains burn" not "Email deliverability degradation" |
| Distillation | Minimum words for maximum point | "Stop burning domains" (3 words) |
| Use "you/your" | Increases relevance | "Your domains keep burning because..." |
| Unmistakable clarity | If ambiguous, rewrite | "Here's why" -> "Here's why emails go to spam" |

---

## 7. ENGINEERING CURIOSITY LOOPS (CONTRAST)

Contrast = distance between reality and expectations.

The BIGGER the distance, on a topic they care about, the MORE hooked they will be.

| Topic | Common Belief | Contrarian Reality | Why Interesting |
|-------|---------------|---------------------|-----------------|
| Apples | All apples red | Purple apple exists | Never seen |
| CEOs | Wake at 4am | CEO wakes at noon, has $1B company | Challenges assumption |

### Two Ways to Create Contrast

| Type | How It Works | Example |
|------|--------------|---------|
| Stated Contrast | Compare two things directly | "Stop 401K. Open smart-yield account instead." |
| Implied Contrast | Introduce new thing different from baseline | "Use smart-yield account. Biggest hack rich people use." |

## 8. SPOKEN HOOK STRUCTURES

| Structure | How It Works | Income Engine Example |
|-----------|--------------|----------------------|
| Fortuneteller | Compare present to future because of something | "This Postfix setting will change how operators send email forever" |
| Experimentation | Frame pain point through live example | "I tested 5 SMTP providers. Here's the one that never burns." |
| Educational | Frame pain point through method | "If you want to stop burning domains, use the 3-2-1 method" |
| Secret Reveal | Reveal hidden truth | "99% ignore this VPS setting - costing them thousands" |
| Contrarian | State non-obvious belief | "Most operators waste money on expensive SMTP. Here's why." |
| Comparison | Compare multiple versions | "Top 5 VPS hosts... which one prevents domain burns?" |
| Question | Pose question they want answered | "Why do some send 200k/month while you burn domains weekly?" |
| Raw Shock | Instant scroll-stop | "This domain block cost me $5,000" |

## 9. VISUAL HOOK STRUCTURES

| Type | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Creator A-roll | Full screen you talking | Explaining infrastructure |
| Creator in B-roll | You doing something silently | Typing on server, configuring VPS |
| Subject B-roll | Someone else doing something | Client celebrating success |
| Non-subject B-roll | Something not a person | Server racks, code scrolling |
| Examples | Graphics showing other videos | Screenshot of campaign |
| Image Screenshots | Still images | Google Postmaster dashboard |
| Arrow/Highlights | Visual direction cues | Circle around "Domain Reputation: POOR" |

---

## 10. TEXT HOOK STRUCTURES

| Type | Examples |
|------|----------|
| Base captions | Repeat spoken words |
| "[X subject] does [Y thing]" | "This VPS prevents domain burns" |
| "Future of [X thing]" | "Future of email infrastructure" |
| "[X shocking thing]" | "17 domains burned in 6 months" |

### Text Hook Rules

- Fewest words possible
- Largest font possible
- Easy to read when overlaid
- Near "eye level" of subject
- Visible in feed preview

## 12. THE 10-MINUTE HOOK FRAMEWORK

### Step 0: Check Existing Visuals

Do you have B-roll or past clips? Build hook around that.

### Step 1: Write Spoken Hook

Ask:
1. What context do they need to decide to opt in?
2. What contrast between beliefs can I use?
3. Can I communicate both in one sentence?

### Step 2: Write Text Hook

- Elicit curiosity and emotional reaction
- Fewest words possible
- Largest font possible
- Use white/colored background behind text

### Step 3: Build Visual Hook

- High contrast, high color, high motion
- Switch visuals every 1-2 seconds
- Use arrows/circles to direct attention

### Step 4: Edit Hook

Check:
- Do all components feel ALIGNED?
- Is pacing fast enough to hold attention?

### Step 5: Final Check

| Check | Question |
|-------|----------|
| Silent watch | Without sound, clear on topic + curiosity? |
| Eyes closed | Listening only, clear on topic + curiosity? |
| Full watch | With sound, clear on topic + curiosity? |

## 15. HOOK RESEARCH METHOD

### Step 1: Build Creator List

Find 20-30 accounts in your niche:
- 50K+ followers
- 50+ posts

### Step 2: Find Them

- Start with one creator, check their "Following"
- Search niche terms, like posts - Explore fills in

### Step 3: Use Sortfeed

- Chrome extension (~$40)
- Pull creator's Reels
- Filter last 200 videos
- Sort by views

### Step 4: Analyze

Right-click, open in new tab, analyze hook.

Document what caught your eye in the 4 components.

### Step 5: Repeat

Run every 2-4 weeks to see what's working NOW.
