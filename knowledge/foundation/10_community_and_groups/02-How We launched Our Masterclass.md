**[How We launched Our Masterclass]{.underline}**

This all started with a message in messenger

I had been thinking that I needed to push forward and get out of my own
way. I clearly knew enough to teach people.

Jill always came to mind because we both blew up our FB groups around
the same time so we could base it on that.

I wrote to her in messenger and then deleted it and then typed and
deleted and then on December 8th I sent it.

It said why aren\'t we creating a mastermind around how we blew up our
groups. She said I'm IN!!

The next day I got a message from Nate & Flip saying Morning.. heard
you\'re about to form the next dream team.

We came together and figured out what we were both great at...

Jill is a tech wizard so she took that side of it and I am good with
sales as I come from a sales background. We are both great with content.

We also figured out what people need the most help with through our
months of talking to people. That is how we came up with what each day
of the Masterclass would be.

-   Tech Setup

-   How to Create and edit videos on TikTok

-   How to repurpose those videos everywhere

-   How to close sales in messenger

We wanted to offer support on the back end as that is still something
that isn\'t offered with many courses.

We also wanted to make it affordable.

About a week later we had a name..VIBE TRIBE and an rough draft of an
offer doc

The next week we teased it on a live we did together in both of our
groups and then continued that teasing in our content for that whole
week.

The week after we started promo for 3 weeks.

We broke down each day of our Masterclass in our content so everyone
knew exactly what was in the course and repeated that process everyday.

We continued doing lives in both of your groups once a week for those
three weeks where we actually gave training but also still talked about
Vibe Tribe.

We made posts of all the people who were jumping into Vibe Tribe and
posted them in our groups so people could see that everyday a few people
were jumping in causing FOMO.

We also asked the people that already jumped into Vibe Tribe, why they
jumped in and what was their tipping point.
