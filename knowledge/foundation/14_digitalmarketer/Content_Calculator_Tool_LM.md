HOW TO USE THE CONTENT CALCULATOR TOOL

Before we get into how to use the Content Calculator Tool, let’s take a look
at it so you can get familiar with it.




In the top left corner, you’re going to fill in the details about the piece of
content. This is the Content Description where you’ll fill in Content Title,
Type, and Estimated On-Page Conversion Rate.

Below that, you have your Goal section. This is where you want to write
what goals you have for that piece of content. For example, it might be to
create a certain amount of revenue, drive a set amount of leads, or bring
a certain number of page views. To the right of that, you’ll fill in how much
the product you’re hoping to sell from your content costs.

Next, you’re going to put together your Promotion Plan. For each channel
you’ll promote this piece of content on, you’re going to fill in the necessary

THE CONTENT CALCULATOR TOOL IN ACTION




For Recess.io, we created a mini magazine that we used as a lead magnet.
The Goal of the mini magazine was to generate $50,000 in MRR for an $80
product. You’ll see that filled in under the Content Description section.

We planned to promote it through email, social media, our newsletter,
paid advertising, and influencers/affiliates. For each of these promotional
channels, we put the necessary information needed to auto-fill in the
Estimated Results section in the top right corner.

You’ll see we filled in the number of emails we plan to send with the mini-
magazine and our click-through rate. The Content Calculator Tool filled in
the estimated clicks, conversions, and sales for us and then brought that
information right over to the Estimated Results section.

We did the same thing for our social media channels (Facebook, Twitter,
and Instagram), our newsletter, paid advertising, and influencers/affiliate
channels. Once we had everything filled in, we were able to look at our
