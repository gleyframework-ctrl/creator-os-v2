                   Attention & Perception

On this third Deep Dive, we will focus fully on our senses: how our brain processes sensory input
and creates sensory perceptions. When communicating online with the brains of consumers, it’s
obviously important to know how that consumer perceives your message. If you understand how
a consumer directs his attention, and how his senses and (primarily visual) cortex processes and
perceives your message, you can start to influence his attention and perception.

How relevant are each of our five senses online? How do we direct our attention? How much of
our sensory input is processed? Which parts of the brain process that sensory input, and in what
order? What really forms our perceptions (apart from sensory input)? How and when do we become
consciously aware of our perceptions? These are the ultimate questions with some mind-blowing
answers that we will attempt to answer during Deep Dive 3.

But first, test your own attention: Can you count how many passes the white team makes?
http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ahg6qcgoay4

Our aim for this third deep dive is to learn you:
1. What constitutes ‘awareness’?
2. How we humans ‘perceive’.
3. About the role of ‘attention’.
4. The ins and outs of Gestalt principles.
