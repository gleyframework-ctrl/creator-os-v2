**Quick Start Guide:**

This guide will help you get the most out of your account and have you
up and running in no time. We have tried to make this as simple and
easy-to-follow as possible.

The Steps to get started are as follows (with additional details and
walkthrough videos below):

1.  Connect Domain

2.  Complete the Custom Values

3.  Review and update the automations

4.  Review and update the funnel

**Step 1: Connect Domain**

When you login to your account, go to Settings → Domains.

If you're connecting the root domain
([[https://example.com]{.underline}](https://example.com)) you'll want
to use the A Record.

If you're connecting a subdomain
([[https://www.example.com]{.underline}](https://www.example.com) or
[[https://go.example.com]{.underline}](https://go.example.com)) you'll
want to use the CNAME Record.

**Step 3: Review the Automations**

The Workflows are organized for your convenience.

> Workflow 1.01 - 1.03 are all new leads to booked call automations.
>
> Workflow 2.01 - 2.03 all deal with scheduled calls and appointments
>
> Workflow 3.01 and 3.02 are miscellaneous automations - Follow-up Date
> Reminders and Contact Replied Alerts
>
> Workflow 4.01 - 4.02 deal with list management
>
> Workflow 5.01 - 5.04 are about payments and client onboarding
>
> Workflow 6.01 - 6.04 are for reviews and referrals
