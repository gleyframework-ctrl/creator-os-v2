FRAMEWORK: AWARENESS-MATCHED MESSAGING + DIMENSIONALIZED EMPATHY
WHEN TO USE: When writing the actual message/hook after choosing the segment (e5_m2_p1); when a member asks "what do I say to them?" or "why isn't my message landing?"

--- LESSON 5: AWARENESS LEVELS (PRECISE DEFINITIONS) ---
1. COMPLETELY UNAWARE: no awareness of the problem or the need for a solution.
2. PROBLEM AWARE: awareness only of the problem.
3. SOLUTION AWARE: awareness of the problem and the various solutions available.
4. PRODUCT AWARE: awareness of the problem and the type of solution they want.
5. MOST AWARE: awareness of what you offer and how it meets their needs — all about "THE DEAL."
LAW: Tailor the marketing message to MATCH the segment's awareness level. Most Aware = talk the deal (offer, terms, urgency). Completely Unaware = educate the problem first; never pitch the deal on first touch.
DECISION RULE FOR THE COACH: Ask which segment the traffic/message targets, then check the match. If it pitches price/deal to Problem-Aware or lower → mismatch → rewrite in sequence: Unaware → problem story; Problem Aware → solution landscape + your mechanism; Solution Aware → why your mechanism wins; Product Aware → proof + differentiation; Most Aware → the deal.
COMMON MISTAKES: Pitching the deal to cold traffic; educating Most Aware traffic (they just want the offer); one message for all levels.

--- LESSON 6: DIMENSIONALIZED 3D PROSPECT PROFILE (PROBING BATTERY) ---
WHEN TO USE: To fill the Empathy Map with concrete answers — dimensionalizing what the prospect is experiencing (problem side AND desired-solution side).
PROBES PER DIMENSION:
SEEING: others suffering? others succeeding? experts/family/friends? websites/social/TV/ads? want to see more of / less of?
HEARING: from experts? family/friends/social? about the problem? about solutions? about fears/desires? likelihood of success/failure? their future?
SAYING: to experts? to family/friends? about problem/solutions/fears/desires/future?
THINKING: about what they hear? about problem/solutions? success/failure? what they think is CORRECT? INCORRECT?
FEELING: about problem/solutions/fears/desires/future?
DOING: what are they doing NOW to address the problem? what are they NOT doing? correctly/incorrectly? working/not working? want more of / less of?
DECISION RULE FOR THE COACH: Use DOING answers to find the behavior your offer must REPLACE; use THINKING-incorrect answers as the beliefs to reframe; use SEEING/HEARING words as the prospect's own vocabulary for hooks.
COMMON MISTAKES: Generic answers ("they see ads") instead of dimensionalized specifics; skipping the desired-solution side; not mining the incorrect thoughts.

MODULE 2 COMPLETE DECISION FLOW FOR THE COACH:
1. Empathy Map (p1+p2) → prospect's own words, emotional arc, behavior to replace, beliefs to reframe.
2. P.A.P. (p1+p2) → ONE segment + awareness-matched message sequence + funnel length.
3. P.E.S. (p1) → promise saturation score → new promise / new mechanism / speed.
Then Module 3+ engineers the Big Idea & unique mechanism on top.
