CHUNK: BrandWidth — The Emotional Range of Your Brand
CATEGORY: builder
SOURCE: TTB — BrandWidth Framework
---
BrandWidth is the emotional range your brand covers. If your content only lives in one time zone, your brand feels one-dimensional. A profitable personal brand must span three distinct phases: Past, Present, and Future.
---
THE PAST (RELATABLE)
"Where I was." Your struggles, failures, humble beginnings, and hard-learned lessons. When you share the past, your Dream Follower says: "They are just like me. They understand my pain."
THE PRESENT (ADMIRABLE)
"Where I am now." Your current achievements, wins, lifestyle, and the fruits of your labor. When you share the present, your Dream Follower says: "They have the results I want. They know the way."
THE FUTURE (INSPIRATIONAL)
"Where I am going." Your big vision, massive goals, and ultimate destination. When you share the future, your Dream Follower says: "I want to be on that journey with them."
---
THE BRANDWIDTH TRAP
If you only show the Present (flexing wins), people admire you but don't relate — they think you are arrogant. If you only show the Past (struggling), people relate but won't buy — they think you are still figuring it out. You must balance all three to build deep, profitable trust.
---
DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION
Look at your last 10 pieces of content. Are you stuck in the Past, Present, or Future? Which time zone do you need to post about this week to balance your BrandWidth?
