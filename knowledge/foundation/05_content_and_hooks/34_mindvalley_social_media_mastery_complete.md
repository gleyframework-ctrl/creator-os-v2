## PART 1: STORYTELLING FOUNDATIONS (Nas Daily)

### 1.1 The 3 Golden Rules

| Rule | Meaning | Sales Application |
|------|---------|-------------------|
| Simple | Short sentences, simple words | Explain infrastructure plainly |
| Organized | Logical flow | Structure: problem → solution → transformation |

| Element | Purpose |
|---------|---------|

## PART 8: FOUNDER BRANDING (Vishen Lakhiani)

### 8.1 Founder vs Company

Founder accounts get 4x engagement.
Have two accounts - use collabs.

### 8.2 2-Hour-a-Week System

1. Record 10-20 min video (bullet points)
2. Upload to Dropbox, get transcript
3. Feed transcript to ChatGPT
4. Generate captions, identify snippets
5. Edit and post

### 8.3 ChatGPT Training (4 Prompts)

| Prompt | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| 1 | Act as copywriter for your brand |
| 2 | List copywriters to emulate |
| 3 | Show understanding of your business |
| 4 | Ask what more info needed |

### 8.4 Transcript Method

1. Record video
2. Get transcript
3. Paste to ChatGPT
4. Ask for captions
5. Ask for best snippet

### 8.5 Bullet-Point Recording

Write bullets on phone.
Place phone on knee.
Glance down, speak naturally.

### 8.6 Values-Driven Content

Don't state values - embody them.
Every post reflects core values.
