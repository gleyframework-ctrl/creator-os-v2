CHUNK: Ikigai for Business — Finding Your Core Mission
CATEGORY: builder
SOURCE: TTB — Ikigai Framework
---
Ikigai is a Japanese concept that translates to "a reason for being." In Time to Build, it is the tool used to find your core mission and the exact intersection of what you should build your business around.
It sits at the center of four overlapping circles:
1. WHAT YOU LOVE: Your passions, hobbies, and obsessions.
2. WHAT YOU ARE GOOD AT: Your hard skills, soft skills, and natural talents.
3. WHAT THE WORLD NEEDS: The problems people are desperate to solve, the inspiration they crave.
4. WHAT YOU CAN BE PAID FOR: The specific skills that have market value and can be packaged into a product.
---
HOW TO USE IT
Do not build a business in just one circle. If you only do what you love and are good at, but the world won't pay for it, you have an expensive hobby. If you only do what you can be paid for, you will burn out.
Tom Noske's Ikigai:
- Good at: Videography, writing, design, coaching.
- Loves: Conservation, sport, personal finance, helping others.
- World needs: Inspiration, content strategy, digital products.
- Paid for: Content strategy, coaching, offer development.
His Core Mission: "Helping creators build wealth through profitable personal branding."
---
DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION
Write down your four circles. Where do they overlap perfectly — and what core mission statement does that intersection give you?
