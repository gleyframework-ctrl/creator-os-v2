# **Part 1: Clarity On Who You Serve**

Most people get this wrong.

They think \"who I serve\" means demographics. Avatar worksheets.
\"35-year-old female entrepreneur who drinks oat milk lattes.\"

That\'s not clarity. That\'s a character sketch for a bad novel.

Here\'s the shift:

**Your avatar isn\'t a \"WHO.\" It\'s a PAIN.**

Not characteristics. Not demographics. A specific problem someone is
going through RIGHT NOW.

## **Why Pain \> Demographics**

When you target a demographic, you compete with everyone targeting that
demographic.

When you target a specific pain, you become the obvious solution for
anyone experiencing it.

Marketing is simply **mirroring their pain through your story until you
become the obvious solution.**

The more specific the pain, the louder the mirror.

## **Bad Targeting vs Good Targeting**

**Bad:**

-   \"I help people make money online\"

-   \"I help people lose weight\"

-   \"I help people build funnels\"

-   \"I show people how to use AI\"

These are categories, not positions. Everyone says the same thing.

**Good:**

-   \"I help online business owners with a \$500+ offer sell daily
    > without calls or lives\"

-   \"I help new moms shed the baby weight through 90-minute workouts
    > without tracking calories\"

-   \"I help business owners doing \$5K+/month install an automation
    > that converts cold leads in 7 days\"

-   \"I help married couples rebuild trust and communication after
    > infidelity\"

Specific pain. Specific situation. Specific outcome. Specific method.

## **The Targeting Formula**

**\"I help \[WHO - specific situation\] get \[VERY SPECIFIC RESULT\]
while removing \[THING THEY HATE\]\"**

The \"who\" isn\'t demographics. It\'s WHERE THEY ARE:

-   What stage of business/life

-   What they already have or don\'t have

-   What they\'ve tried

-   What\'s not working

The result isn\'t vague. It\'s finger-pointable:

-   Not \"grow your business\" → \"add \$10k/month\"

-   Not \"get healthy\" → \"lose 20 lbs in 90 days\"

-   Not \"better marketing\" → \"sell daily without calls\"

**Try your best to make this a TANGIBLE outcome**

The removal is something they actively hate:

-   Sales calls

-   Tracking calories

-   Daily lives

-   Cold outreach

## **The 80/20 Content Rule**

Once you nail this pain, your job is to hammer it.

**80% of your content** ties back to that ONE problem:

-   What you do

-   How you do it

-   What\'s part of your process

-   What problems get removed

-   Results & outcomes

-   Client wins

**20% of your content** is WHO YOU ARE:

-   Stories

-   Beliefs & values

-   Lifestyle

-   Failures & wins

Most people change what they do constantly. They never stay long enough
for people to know what they\'re about.

**The goal:** Pick a problem. Hammer it until people know exactly what
you do when they see you.

The longer you stay on that path, the easier things become.

# **Part 2: Messaging & Positioning**

You know the pain you solve.

Now we make sure your words land.

Because here\'s the thing:

You can have the perfect solution for the perfect person\... but if you
describe it in YOUR words instead of THEIR words, they won\'t recognize
it.

They\'ll scroll right past.

Great messaging isn\'t clever. It\'s accurate.

It sounds like the conversation already in their head.

## **Speaking Their Language**

Most experts use SOLUTION language:

\"I help you optimize your sales process.\" \"We create systems for
sustainable growth.\" \"My framework delivers transformational
results.\"

That\'s how YOU describe it.

THEY describe it like:

\"I\'m on calls all day and I\'m exhausted.\" \"I make money but it
doesn\'t feel like freedom.\" \"I\'ve tried everything and nothing
sticks.\"

**Your job: Say what they\'re thinking better than they can.**

### **Language Extraction**

Answer in THEIR words --- not jargon:

**How do they describe their problem to a friend?**
