**The Perfect Welcome Mail -- Indoctrination Series\`**

Ever heard the old saying... "You only get one shot at a first
impression!"?

That's exactly what the welcome mail is... it's your ONE and only shot
to make a strong first impression, connect with your new subscriber and
stand out in their mind (AND INBOX). So I think its safe to say that the
welcome mail is one of the most important emails in your arsenal!

The job of the welcome mail is to introduce yourself and your company to
your new subscriber, align yourself with them, set expectations for
exactly what they will receive now that they're "on your list", restate
the benefits of being a subscriber, get them to make micro-commitments,
and "open a loop" with them.

This email is ALL about engagement....
