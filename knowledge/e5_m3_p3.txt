FRAMEWORK: هندسة العرض والرسالة كـ"ناطحة سحاب" — الاستعارة، الصوت، والعمق النفسي
WHEN TO USE: When explaining Module 3 in Arabic; when a member builds a campaign and needs the correct build order; when their Unique Mechanism feels weak or copyable.

CORE METAPHOR (use in coaching): The marketing message is a skyscraper: hidden foundation (pre-seeding) → structural steel (Big Idea) → main engine (Unique Mechanism) → attractive facade (Offer) → final blueprint (Thesis). NEVER install the glass windows (the offer) before pouring the hidden foundation.

THE 5 CONSTRUCTION STAGES:
1. HIDDEN FOUNDATION (pre-seeding the backend sale): 5 strategies with zero direct selling: (a) nonchalant/offhand comments, (b) smart allusion to a product that solves the problem, (c) referencing valuable info coming later, (d) showcasing stunning client results, (e) useful-but-INCOMPLETE info that creates a curiosity gap.
2. STRUCTURAL SUPPORT (Big Idea): emotion first (brains process emotion faster than logic) + primary promise + decisive proof point + metaphors + Unique Mechanism + intellectually interesting element.
3. MAIN ENGINE (Unique Mechanism): the actual vehicle that delivers the promise on the ground. Without it the message is repeatable noise, ignored by audiences AND algorithms. Prevents the "Google Slap."
4. ATTRACTIVE FACADE (Offer): smart combination of offer types; every deliverable linked functional → dimensionalized → emotional benefit; financial engineering = contrast regular vs offer price + LOGICAL rationale for the discount (protects credibility) + price trivialization ("a cup of coffee per day") + risk-reversal stated positively to erase hesitation.
5. FINAL BLUEPRINT (Thesis): Desirable Result + Unique Mechanism = the thesis statement; the COMPASS that directs every word and every ad toward one strategic goal.

PSYCHOLOGY OF THE UNIQUE MECHANISM (additions):
- The mechanism gives HOPE: "maybe THIS time I'll experience the result."
- It creates certainty that past failures happened simply because THIS component was missing.
- 3 LEVELS: (1) actual bare component, (2) unspoken advantage competitors ignore, (3) TRANSUBSTANTIATED — wrapped in a unique secret that turns a plain tool into a revolutionary solution and re-frames the whole problem.

DECISION RULES FOR THE COACH:
1. Member rushing to write ads before the foundation? Stop them: run the 5 stages in order; pre-seeding comes first.
2. Member listing features as a bare list? Force the ladder: functional → dimensionalized → emotional ("what is delivered vs. its real impact").
3. Member discounting without a rationale? Credibility collapses — every discount needs a logical reason.
4. Mechanism at level 1-2? Push to level 3: name it, give it a secret, give it a story (transubstantiation).
5. Close every Module 3 session: does the thesis (result + mechanism) direct every ad? If an ad leaves the compass, delete it.
