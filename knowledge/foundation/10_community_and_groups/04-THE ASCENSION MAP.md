# **THE ASCENSION MAP**

**Companion Workbook: Lesson 4 --- Your Group Positioning**

## **What This Section Does**

You\'ve already defined what your group is FOR (the pain it solves, the
outcome it creates).

Now you need to design what happens AFTER.

This section maps the path from free member → engaged member → ready
buyer.

Entry positioning isn\'t about getting people in.

It\'s about making the way OUT obvious.

## **Part 1: The Problem Ladder**

Ascension happens when solving Problem A reveals Problem B.

Your free group solves Problem A. Your paid offer solves Problem B.

When this ladder is clear, selling feels like the logical next step ---
not a pitch.

**Problem A (What Your Free Group Solves):**

What\'s the specific problem people solve by being in your free group?

Not everything you could help with. The entry-level win.

**What \"Solved\" Looks Like for Problem A:**

When someone gets the win from your free group, what changes for them?

What do they now understand, believe, or have?

**Problem B (What Gets Revealed):**

Once Problem A is solved, what\'s the NEXT problem that naturally
appears?

This is usually:

-   \"Okay, I get it\... but how do I actually DO this?\"

-   \"This worked\... but now I\'m stuck on the next thing\"

-   \"I understand the concept\... but I need help implementing\"

What\'s YOUR Problem B?

**Why Problem B Can\'t Be Solved at the Free Level:**

What makes Problem B require more than what the free group provides?

☐ It requires personalized guidance or feedback

☐ It requires implementation support over time

☐ It requires accountability or access to you

☐ It requires a more complete system or framework

☐ It requires hands-on help, not just information

## **Part 2: Problem Ladder Examples By Niche**

Use these to check if your ladder flows naturally.

Notice: Problem B isn\'t random. It\'s the inevitable next step after
Problem A is solved.

**Business/Marketing:**

-   Problem A: \"I don\'t know what to post\" → Problem B: \"I need a
    > system to post consistently and convert\"

-   Problem A: \"I\'m not getting engagement\" → Problem B: \"I need to
    > turn engagement into sales\"

-   Problem A: \"I don\'t understand my offer\" → Problem B: \"I need to
    > sell it without calls\"

**Health/Fitness:**

-   Problem A: \"I don\'t know what to eat\" → Problem B: \"I need a
    > plan I can actually stick to\"

-   Problem A: \"I can\'t stay consistent\" → Problem B: \"I need
    > accountability and structure\"

-   Problem A: \"I\'m overwhelmed by information\" → Problem B: \"I need
    > someone to tell me what to do for MY situation\"

**Coaching/Consulting:**

-   Problem A: \"I don\'t know how to get clients\" → Problem B: \"I
    > need a repeatable system that doesn\'t require me chasing\"

-   Problem A: \"I\'m trading time for money\" → Problem B: \"I need
    > leverage and a scalable model\"

-   Problem A: \"I hate selling\" → Problem B: \"I need a way to sell
    > that doesn\'t feel gross\"

**Creative/Service:**

-   Problem A: \"I don\'t know how to price my work\" → Problem B: \"I
    > need to attract clients who pay what I\'m worth\"

-   Problem A: \"I\'m stuck doing everything myself\" → Problem B: \"I
    > need systems so I can scale\"

-   Problem A: \"I can\'t find consistent clients\" → Problem B: \"I
    > need a pipeline that fills itself\"

**Your Ladder Check:**

Does your Problem A → Problem B feel inevitable?

☐ Yes --- solving A clearly creates B

☐ Somewhat --- the connection is there but fuzzy

☐ No --- they feel disconnected

If fuzzy or disconnected, rework until B feels like the obvious next
obstacle after A is handled.

## **Part 3: The \"Leads to More\" Signal**

Most people either:

-   Hide that they sell things (creates surprise/trust issues later)

-   Shove offers in faces constantly (creates pressure/annoyance)

The middle path: **openly signal that more exists without pushing.**

**The Bridge Statement:**

This is language that acknowledges paid support exists without being
salesy.

## **Part 5: Ascension Signals**

When positioning is working, you\'ll see specific signals that people
are moving through the ladder.

**Green Light Signals (Ascension is Happening):**

☐ DMs include context (\"I saw your post about\...\" / \"I\'ve been
watching\...\")

☐ People reference specific wins or content when reaching out

☐ Questions shift from Problem A to Problem B

☐ \"What\'s the next step?\" / \"How do I work with you?\"

☐ Sales conversations feel like confirmation, not convincing

☐ Objections are low because they already understand the value

**Red Flag Signals (Positioning Needs Work):**

☐ Lots of lurkers, few conversations

☐ When you mention your offer, it feels random

☐ Every sales conversation requires full explanation from scratch

☐ High objections and pricing resistance

☐ People stay forever without moving forward

☐ You\'re chasing instead of being asked

**Your Current Reality:**

Which signals are you seeing most?

What does this tell you about your positioning?
