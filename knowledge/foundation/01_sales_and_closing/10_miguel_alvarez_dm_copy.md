## 1. CORE PHILOSOPHY

Micro-copy is your secret weapon in the attention economy.

Tiny messages packed with emotional impact can stop the scroll, spark curiosity, and guide conversations toward a sale - all without feeling "salesy."

**Core Principle:** Emotion drives action. Logic justifies it later.

## 3. THE S.I.P.E. FRAMEWORK

The secret to writing micro-hooks that get opened and read.

| Letter | Meaning | Description |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| S | Short | Keep it brief - one or two sentences max |
| I | Incomplete | Create an open loop; don't give away the whole story |
| P | Personal | Use "you," reference something specific about them |
| E | Emotional | Trigger an emotion (curiosity, desire, fear) |

### Example

"Hey [Name], I saw you're struggling with domain burns. I found a weird fix that might help. Want to see it?"

- Short: one sentence
- Incomplete: "weird fix" leaves them wanting more
- Personal: references their struggle
- Emotional: curiosity about the fix

Guide prospects through small, easy agreements instead of one big ask.

| Rung | Action | Example |

| 2. Accept Value | Agree problem is real | "Sounds like domain burns are killing your momentum, right?" |

## 9. POWER CONTENT FRAMEWORK

Turn pain points into DM-generating content.

### Elements

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Hook | First 3 seconds - stops the scroll |
| Value Bomb | 15-30 seconds of pure insight |
| Tease | Hint at more (open loop) |
| CTA | Direct them to DM you |

### Example

**Hook:** "If your domains keep burning, you're making this one mistake."

**Value Bomb:** Explain the mistake (shared IP reputation) and hint at fix (private VPS).

**CTA:** "Comment 'FIX' and I'll DM you the exact setup."

## 12. CONVERSATION MAPS

### Cold Outreach to Warm Lead

1. Opener: Curiosity Igniter or Personal Connection
2. Follow-Up: Unfinished Story or Micro-Question
3. Value Proposition: Brief intro + Value Dropper
4. Gauge Interest: Permission-Based Invitation
5. Micro-Close: No-Pressure Next Step

### Re-Engagement with Past Prospect

1. Opener: Gentle Re-Hook (Personal Connection)
2. Follow-Up: Relatable Struggle or Insight
3. Offer New Value: Update or new resource
4. Gauge Current Needs: Permission-Based Invitation
5. Micro-Close: No-Pressure Next Step

### Follow-Up After Event/Webinar

1. Opener: Thank you + contextual hook
2. Follow-Up: Offer additional value
3. Invite Questions: Curiosity Starter
4. Gauge Interest: Permission-Based Invitation
5. Micro-Close: Book a call or send info
