ABOUT THIS LESSON
Before you even begin, I would recommend starting to look at everything extremely long-term.
One of the biggest mistakes you can make when diving into this online business is thinking you're going to get
results extremely fast with very little work and ultimately one piece of content or a couple is going to change your
life.
 look at everything that you're doing or every piece of content that you're creating online as like a little mini piece of
Real Estate and the more you put out for the longest time that you're putting them out it really starts the compound
in add up.
 This is also why they say consistency is key, but getting into the right mindset before you even start is going to be
crucial especially when you're talking about a long-term brand and building a sustainable business.
