## 1. THE CORE PRINCIPLE

"People don't buy what you do, they buy why you do it."

Prospects buy from brands that share their values and beliefs. When your messaging consistently reflects your "why," you build trust and attract the right customers.

SALES APPLICATION: Every sales script, email, and content must communicate your "why" - not just your features.

---

## 2. THE 5 BRAND MESSAGING QUESTIONS

These five questions define your brand position and translate into sales messaging.

| Question | Purpose | Sales Translation |
|----------|---------|-------------------|
| WHAT? | Describe the look/feel of the brand | Visual and emotional vibe of sales materials |
| HOW? | Describe the tone/voice/attitude | Personality of sales scripts and emails |
| WHO? | Who do you champion? Who aren't you for? | Target avatar - and people you will NOT serve |
| WHY? | What are your beliefs and values? | Unique value proposition and emotional drivers |
| MESSAGING | Take value propositions and filter through tone/voice | Actual sales copy - words you use to sell |

---

## 3. FINDING YOUR WHITE SPACE (POSITIONING)

Undifferentiated brands compete on price. Differentiated brands create a unique position.

THE FRAMEWORK: Juxtapose two opposing tone/voice words to find a unique space.

EXAMPLES:
- 21st Century Books: Harper Collins
- Everyday Magic: Disney
- Consciously Mindless: NBC
- Insanely Great: Apple
- Curiously Strong: Altoids

INCOME ENGINE EXAMPLES:
- Technically Simple
- Quietly Powerful
- Unapologetically Reliable
- Fearlessly Practical

## 5. WHO DO YOU CHAMPION?

This is a powerful sales qualification and positioning tool.

### 5.1 Who Do You Champion?

EXAMPLE (Outdoor brand):
"For the modern day hippie, adrenaline junkie, rough handled, free spirits."

INCOME ENGINE:
"For the Arabic CPA operator who has burned domains, wasted money on SMTP, wasted hours on manual work, and now realizes infrastructure is the real bottleneck."

### 5.2 Who Are You NOT a Good Fit For?

This is polarizing - repels wrong clients, saves time, increases conversion.

EXAMPLE:
"We are NOT for beginners who want a quick fix. We are NOT for people who aren't willing to learn the infrastructure themselves."

## 7. THE ANTHEMIC SPOT FRAMEWORK

This is a powerful copywriting template for sales pages, videos, and emails.

### The Template:

Here's to the: [Who do you champion?]
The ones who: [Our beliefs and values]
You can't ignore them, because they: [Our Why]
While the world sees them this way:
We see them as:
Because the people who are crazy enough to think they can [Our Why] are the ones who do.

### Apple's "The Crazy Ones" Example:

"Here's to the crazy ones. The misfits. The rebels. The troublemakers. The round pegs in the square holes. The ones who see things differently. They're not fond of rules. And they have no respect for the status quo. You can quote them, disagree with them, glorify or vilify them. About the only thing you can't do is ignore them. Because they change things. They push the human race forward. While some may see them as the crazy ones, we see genius. The people who are crazy enough to think they can change the world are the ones who do."

### INCOME ENGINE ANTHEM:

"Here's to the operators. The ones who've burned domains. The ones who've wasted hours on manual work. The ones who know that rented infrastructure is a dead end. You can't ignore them, because they're the ones who will scale to 200k emails/month while others are still stuck. While the world sees them as non-technical, we see the next generation of CPA leaders. Because the people who are crazy enough to think they can own their infrastructure are the ones who do."

## 8. INTERNAL POSITIONING STATEMENT

A single sentence that acts as a compass for all sales messaging.

TEMPLATE:
[Brand Name] provides [what they do] to [customer] in a [voice] and [voice] environment while helping them feel [benefit] and giving them [benefit].

EXAMPLE (Trojan Storage):
"Trojan Storage provides storage solutions to local communities in a warm, professional and friendly environment while helping them feel secure and giving them peace of mind."

INCOME ENGINE:
"Income Engine provides private email infrastructure to Arabic CPA operators in a direct, educational, and empowering environment while helping them feel in control and giving them freedom from manual work."

---

## 9. THE ONLINESS STATEMENT

One sentence that defines what makes your brand unique and different.

TEMPLATE:
[Brand] is the only [what you do/category] that [radical differentiator] for [customer] in [location], who [describe a customer need] in a time when [supports or goes against a popular trend].

EXAMPLES:

Hooters:
"Hooters is the only chain of restaurants that hires overtly sexy waitresses for young men in the United States who want to indulge their libidos in an era of strict political correctness and feminism."

Tangoe:
"Tangoe is the only IT expense management company that provides complete oversight for Fortune 500 IT Directors and their bosses, with global operations, who need to maximize every dollar in an era of fast moving, disruptive tech companies."

INCOME ENGINE:
"Income Engine is the only infrastructure training program that combines done-with-you private VPS deployment with ongoing community support for Arabic CPA operators who have burned domains and wasted money on SMTP, in a time when most solutions are rented tools that fail."

## 10. ELEVATOR PITCH TEMPLATE

A short, pre-prepared speech that explains what you do.

TEMPLATE:
[Product] is a [general, relatable tool/service] that allows [2 adjectives + target user] to [feature/benefit].

EXAMPLES:

Dropcam:
"Dropcam is a cloud based video camera that allows concerned businesses and home owners to easily monitor their space without a complex set-up."

Tesla:
"Tesla Motors is an electric vehicle that allows environmentally + style conscious drivers to conveniently travel long distances while feeling good about reducing carbon emissions."

GoPro:
"GoPro is a waterproof action camera that allows athletes and adventure seekers to capture and document their exploits."

INCOME ENGINE:
"Income Engine is a private infrastructure training program that allows burned-out, technically frustrated CPA operators to automate their email lead flow and scale to 200k emails/month without burning domains."

## 11. HEADLINE WRITING FRAMEWORKS

### 11.1 The 4 Functions of Headlines

1. Get attention
2. Select the audience
3. Deliver a complete message
4. Draw the reader into reading the body copy

### 11.2 The 4 U's for Headlines

| U | Meaning | Example |
|---|---------|---------|
| Urgent | Act now, not later | "Last chance to lock in beta pricing" |
| Unique | Say something new | "The only infrastructure program that teaches you to own your stack" |
| Ultra-specific | Short tease | "How to stop burning domains in 48 hours" |
| Useful | Appeal to self-interest | "Save 10 hours a week with one simple change" |

### 11.3 Headline Mad Libs

Fill-in-the-blank headline templates:

- How I [improved something measurable] in just [timeframe]
- The Only Tool You'll Need to [accomplish something impressive] - Even if You've Never [done something related] before
- Here's What the Most Successful [group of people] Have in Common
- Find Out How [person] [accomplished measurable goal]. Hint: It Involves [topic]
- [Number] Quick and Easy Ways to [accomplish goal]
- Ready to [achieve something impressive]?
- The unfair advantage I used to [accomplish goal] and how you can too
- The [number] Biggest [topic] Blunders and How to Avoid Them

## 13. HOW TO USE IN SALES

### In Sales Calls
- Use WHO definition to qualify prospects
- Use WHY beliefs to build rapport and trust
- Use Onliness Statement to differentiate from competitors

### In Emails
- Use Headline Mad Libs for subject lines
- Use Elevator Pitch in first email
- Use Anthemic Spot framework for origin story email

### On Sales Pages
- Use Internal Positioning Statement as subheadline
- Use Beliefs → Messaging table for bullet points
- Use 4 U's to write the main headline
