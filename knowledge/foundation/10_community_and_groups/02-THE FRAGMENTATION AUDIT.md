## **Part 2: The Momentum Leak Test**

Now let\'s see where momentum is escaping.

Answer these honestly. No one is grading you. This is just clarity.

**Question 1: Where do buyers go after they purchase?**

When someone buys your offer, where do they end up?

☐ They stay in the same group as free members (visible)

☐ They move to a separate paid group (hidden)

☐ They get added to a course platform (isolated)

☐ They go to a private Slack/Voxer/Telegram (invisible)

☐ It depends on what they bought

☐ I\'m not sure

If you checked anything other than the first box, proof is leaving the
room.

**Question 2: Can free members SEE paid members winning?**

When a paying client gets a result, posts a win, or shares progress, who
sees it?

☐ Everyone in my main community sees it clearly

☐ Some people see it, but it\'s scattered

☐ Only other paid members see it

☐ Wins mostly happen in DMs or private channels

☐ I don\'t have a consistent place for wins

**The truth:** If free members can\'t watch paid members win, they have
to trust your word instead of their eyes. That\'s why selling feels
harder than it should.

**Question 3: Where do your best client questions get answered?**

When a paying client asks a great question, where does the answer live?

☐ Publicly in the main group (everyone learns)

☐ In a separate paid space (only paid members benefit)

☐ On private calls (the answer dies when the call ends)

☐ In DMs (only that one person benefits)

☐ Scattered across multiple places

**The truth:** Every great question answered privately is leverage
you\'ll never get back. Those questions warm up future buyers. When
they\'re hidden, you have to re-explain everything to every new person.

**Question 4: Where do wins and proof get posted?**

When you or a client has a win worth sharing, where does it go?

☐ One central place that everyone in my world can see

☐ Whatever group feels relevant at the moment

☐ Mostly on social media, not inside a community

☐ In a private paid space

☐ I don\'t have a consistent system for this

**The truth:** Scattered proof doesn\'t compound. Concentrated proof
builds belief automatically.

**Question 5: How many places could a new person end up?**

Someone discovers you today. They\'re interested. How many different
containers might they join or be invited to in the next 30 days?

☐ Just one (my main group)

☐ 2 different places

☐ 3-4 different places

☐ 5+ depending on what they buy or engage with

☐ I honestly don\'t know

**The truth:** Every additional destination dilutes clarity. Your
audience shouldn\'t need a map to navigate your world.

**Trust Cost:**

You\'re not failing because of effort.

You\'re failing because the structure keeps resetting.

## **Part 5: The One Group Decision**

Based on everything you just mapped, answer these:

**Which container has the most potential to become your ONE group?**

Consider: Where is the most momentum already? Where do you actually
enjoy showing up? Where would it make the most sense to consolidate
everything?

**What would need to happen to collapse everything into that one
space?**

Be specific. What would you need to migrate, shut down, communicate, or
rebuild?

**What are you afraid of losing if you consolidate?**

This is important. Name the fear. Is it losing members? Losing revenue
from a paid group? Feeling like you\'re abandoning people? Looking
inconsistent?

**The thing you\'re afraid of losing is usually the thing keeping you
fragmented.**

Most of the time, the fear is bigger than the reality. The people who
matter will follow. The ones who don\'t weren\'t going to buy anyway.
