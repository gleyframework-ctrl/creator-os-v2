ABOUT THIS LESSON
We all have the ability to use both sides of the coin when it comes to lead gen - which can change depending on
what you’re doing in your business & what's going on in your life.
The volume approach - Doing things in a way that will facilitate more and more leads / whether that’s things that will
directly produce mass leads now or over time & filling your pipeline
Low Volume Higher Quality - Having fewer leads - fewer conversations - more catered content - but the quality of the
people showing interest is higher - tipping them into a sale is usually shorter
One of the biggest shifts you can make - is being hyper-aware of where your prospect is on the spectrum - Are they
even problem aware - Solution Aware?
Let's talk leads -
Short Form - Lead Gen Content - (The color Posts)
These will get higher engagement - but usually, the lead's readiness to buy/take action will be lower
         “Thinking of creating a free training on X Who Wants In?”
         “If I created a (Free thing) To (Achieve Result) Without (Pain or hurdle) Who would use it?”
         “On a mission to help X achieve X in the next (time-frame) Who’s with me”
Different ways you can use short-form lead gen posts - To sell - know that a lot of the people who come off of short-
form posts will usually just be shopping for info / “Not qualified leads” so if you’re using them to move into a pitch -
you will have to pick up the slack in the dm
To fill your future pipeline - If you’re looking to generate convos - push people into your group - it can work well if
you’re detaching from the sale & are using them to build goodwill & build rapport with the audience
Example - using lead gen content to provide some value to deliver a lead magnet - but transition into a convo either
rapidly or down the line

Long-form 2-Step Lead gen content - Preferred - Punchy & longer
You will get less engagement on these - but the leads engaging and interacting on this type of content will be of
higher quality
Similar context to the short posts - but you’re able to beef them up & add more to them, so the people who’re coming
off the back end - know why they’re there.
Long-Form Lead Gen Framework -
        Hook Attention Grabber - Try to anchor your hook to the subject of the piece of content / a solution to a
        problem / or wow factor
        Ongoing Problem - Biz, Non biz - things that are going on in the market (if you can tie this to the solution your
        product solves the better)
        Backstory - Build Trust - Tie in results/experience
        I’ve done the work for you statement - Can work well if you’ve anchored some type of struggle and this
        opportunity is the same way you’ve overcame it
        Opportunity / Promise / They can do it without you
        CTA - With specific lead gen content - would go more aggressive with CTA - have them do something hyper-
        specific vs “If you have any questions, I’m only a message away”

Symptoms + Triggers = Leads
Content that speaks to:
        Frustrations

        Not even generating leads
        Not knowing what kind of content to create
        Convos are a graveyard
        Getting ghosted on the regular
        Prospects want all Free shit
        Attracting all broke folks

        Objections

        I just don’t see the reason in investing into another program - But you’re not getting results for months - Dive
        deep on this in content and drive it home
        I’ve already spent too much money - Relate and understand - Every month you don't fix this you lose x amount -
        The longer you drive a car with problems, the worse it gets.
        I can get this Info for free on youtube - Sure but do you know what to do with the info you have saved on every


        device collecting dust? Drive it home for them
        I already have a coach thats helping me     - Cool, but are they really helping you? What results have you gotten


        since hiring them? Pour salt in the cut

        Desires

        What do they want, why?
        What are they not getting, why?
        More Freedom to do as they please
        Pay off Debt, credit cards, house payment, more vacays, why?
        Tell their boss to fuck off, why?
        Kids
        Family
        More toys
        Confidence
        Impact others


        Symptoms

        They feel they are meant to do something else in life and not dwindle away at a job that doesn't appreciate or
        value them
        Lay awake at night dreading the next day driving to the office
        Business - Things just aren't going the way they expected and were promised
        No Clear plan to execute to hit their goals, be it personal or monetary
        Nobody takes them serious in the content


If you bring this to your content you'll continue to move people to becoming a buyer, hand raiser, hot lead etc and the
convos will have less diagnosis and turn into a sales convo much faster.
This isn't for one post at all, this could literally be weeks and weeks of content ideas that will generate leads.
Take one topic above and bring YOU into it while Speaking to THEM and letting them know you understand where
they are and you have the solution to get them OUT of it.
You CTAs should be super clear and Specific, not one line saying “ Drop a me to get info”
But more of “If you're sick to your back teeth with not getting (Insert want,desire) and you want to see what it looks
like to have me hold your hand to make this happen, give me a shout and we can have a no pressure chat.”
These will all cause inbound leads to raise their hands and reach out to chat.
You can also do more targeted outbound to get the ball rolling as well.
Scanning your feed on socials and seeing folks that may need a small tweak to get better results.
Messages can be smooth and easy.
Hey sally, just read your post on (insert topic) and I loved it! Left you a comment. Where did you learn that?
Compliments are great openers and instantly takes the guard from up to down real quick.
Regular chats can be diagnostic while still being Genuine as fuck.
Building the rapport this way is easier and less pressured.
As you do this you can lean into solutions you have or areas you can tie back to what theyre doing now that you can
help in.
Maybe a lead magnet, a free training in your group you did, live, google doc that presells while helping with a quick
win etc.
Being Proactive on outbound is just at effective as having inbound leads reach out to you.
All of your content should be handling the things listed above.
Take the objections for instance
If you content handle the most common objections each day while weaving in you, your previous struggles, how you

overcame them and what not, you'll relate to and attract more qualified leads that KNOW you've been there and can
help them with getting it fixed.
Then when the convo takes place they won't have many questions or objections, itll be more of what it looks like to
help, the plan and how they can get from A to B with your help.
