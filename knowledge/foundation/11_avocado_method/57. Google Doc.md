**[QUICK WIN STRATEGIES]{.underline}**

The below list is a collection of easy to implement strategies designed
to help you land clients quickly and "shake the tree" to find the low
hanging fruit.

You can pick and choose whichever ones most align with your business
model, style and stage you are in.

Choose what looks appealing and ignore the rest.

### **DIRECT REQUESTS**

1.  **[6.1.30 Posts :]{.underline}** Make a simple post saying that you
    > are looking for 5 people who want to get 1 result in the next 30
    > days. The 1 result should be related to your program and be
    > believable that they could achieve.\
    > "I'm looking for 5 busy health practitioners who want to date
    > their spouse again in the next 30 days." (Example 1, Example 2,) -
    > [[Complete Training from
    > 2020]{.underline}](https://www.skool.com/avocado-method-6165/classroom/47293abd?md=4f8574570c1346a092293859a85fa3da)

2.  **[9 word email:]{.underline}**

> Subject: Quick Question...
>
> Hey Alicja,

Are you still looking to lose 20 lbs and get in the best shape of your
life?

Dan

### **Ripening Leads (Nurture & Follow-ups)**

> The AVOCADO Method at its core is the idea that we can expedite the
> ripening process once they are in our "brown paper bag." And we still
> have to go check on them from time to time to see which ones are
> ready. Nurture and follow up is where ALL the money is made in
> business. These strategies are best used for prospects with whom
> you've already developed rapport and connection with.

9.  **[Value Prop- Follow up]{.underline}**

Schedule 89 minutes a week where you follow up with old leads.

> "Hey (first name), hope you're well! I just created a new training on
> x, and thought of you - did you want the link to check it out?
>
> ***The follow up 2-3 days later with:**\
> *
>
> "Hey (first name), Did you get a chance to see what I sent over?
>
> Curious to hear your thoughts.*\
> *

10. **[Follow up more directly with those who have booked
    > calls.]{.underline}**

Send direct DM's to all those you have had calls with and say:

"Hey (first name,) Hope you're well!

> We've been helping our clients achieve some pretty remarkable results
> inside of our program recently, and it made me think of you and your
> \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
>
> So I have a question for you: What would our program need to look like
> for it to be a no-brainer decision for us to work together

11. **[Follow up with no shows:]{.underline}**

For those who've you booked calls with and they did not show follow up

even more directly by sending them a DM like:

"Hey are you still interested in ..... (Result) "

### **PAST CLIENTS RE-ENROLMENT**

15. **[Past Client Follow Up]{.underline}**

> These clients who have already used your services, know, like and
> trust you and hopefully have a great relationship already built.
>
> No script for these.... Just genuine curiosity and invitation to
> follow up and see what has happened for them since the last time you
> worked together.
>
> Send either an email or messenger dm.
>
> Hey (first name),
>
> How have you been?
>
> I was just thinking about you and was wondering how you've been doing
> since we last chatted. Would love to hear how you are doing :)
>
> Cheers

16. The Price Increase Campaign

you raise your rates. Typically it means you are moving through one of
the key growth transitions:

-   Hourly to Program

-   Beta to Full Prices

-   General to Specialist

-   Specialist to Expert

a.  Set the 2 week Campaign Timer

b.  Create an epic offer post
