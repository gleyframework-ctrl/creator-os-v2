Opening up your laptop and logging into your ad account…

NOT EVERYONE IS A GOOGLE OR FACEBOOK AD

of the reins and lead your campaigns down a path of predictable success.

 1    STRATEGY 1:
      USE EXISTING LEADS TO YOUR ADVANTAGE
We all know ads are about getting more leads, but it’s way more than just
a numbers game. You don’t want to attract just any ol’ person—that is,
unless you want passive-aggressive sneers from your sales team.

The goal is to get qualified leads: the right person, at the right time.

One of the easiest ways to accomplish this is by using “Lookalike
Audiences” (also called “Similar Audiences” in Google Ads).

Basically, Facebook (or Google) uses a list of people you’ve already
determined to be “qualified” to find out what all those people have in
common. It then uses that info to help you target new people who fit that
criteria.

Here’s how you can create a Lookalike Audience inside Facebook
Ads Manager:

After setting your Campaign category, Facebook will ask you to choose an
audience. You’ll want to click “Create New” and choose “Lookalike Audience.”
Then it will ask you to choose a source—that is, they want to know where
they should look to get information (email, phone number, name, etc.)
you’ve collected from people.

You can go ahead and choose “Create New Source” and click “Custom
Audience.”




Next, you just choose a location. There are a ton of different options…

A great place to start would be “Lead Form” if you have one on Facebook.
If not, you could also use your website. To do this, you’ll have to create a
Facebook Pixel. These little silent detectives sit (invisibly) on a page of your
website and collect information about anyone who lands on that page.




You’ll want to pick a page that only the most qualified leads would land
on. That’s why confirmation pages for your core offer are a great idea. You
could also use confirmation pages for Lead Magnets, or other entry-point
offers you use.

And that's it, just finish creating the ad by setting your placement, budget,
and adding in your image, text, and URL. You can use lookalike audiences
for practically any type of campaign you’re running.

Once it’s set, Facebook will start showing your ads to people exactly like
the clients and leads you’ve already landed. It’s like cloning your best
leads, just without all the science ‘n stuff.

 2     STRATEGY 2:
       CONVERT LEADS RIGHT ON THE SPOT
Facebook knows that lead generation is an essential part of every business.
They also know their site is a cornucopia of customers just waiting to see your
offer and convert. That’s why they’ve created Lead Generation Campaigns.

Here’s how it works: if your prospect sees your ad and clicks on it, a form pops
up directly in the feed, and their information auto fills into the form. All they
have to do is fill out any questions you add to the form and click submit!

It’s super quick and easy for your prospect, which means they are more likely
to convert. I mean, who doesn’t love filling out quick forms? It’s like Buzzfeed
Quizzes, but actually helpful for you and your prospect.

Here’s how to set up a Lead Gen Campaign in Facebook:

When you choose an Objective, choose “Lead Generation.”

Then finish creating the ad, and your leads will be clicking and submitting

NEED SOME MORE TIPS? WE’VE GOT YOU.




1. TEST EVERYTHING!
If you haven’t heard, the jury is in: You should be testing your ads. Test
different creative. Test different audiences. Test different headlines.
Basically, just test everything.

You should also be rotating in new ads often. When you find a winner,
keep it running. When you find losers, kill them and try again.


2. TELL A STORY
Ever wonder why ads from Nike, Adidas, and Apple leave you feeling
dumbfounded, moved, a bit emotional, and in dire need of their
newest product?

It’s all about storytelling.

Now, I’m not saying you need to run out and hire some big wigs to come
film your next manifesto ad. I mean, you can, but it would probably be a
waste of money.

All you need to do is think of a central emotion, theme, or story your leads
will identify with, and make sure it is present in your ad.

Remember, sometimes the simplest stories/themes have the biggest impact.




                                                              Flood Your Funnel | 9
3. KEEP YOUR AD CREATIVE SIMPLE AND CONSISTENT
Chances are you’re not Michelangelo (if you are… well, I don’t believe you).
So when it comes to creating images for your ads, simple is usually better.

You also want to make sure the imagery you use on your ad matches the
imagery anywhere you send them. There’s nothing worse than landing on a
page and thinking “wait, why am I here?”

Consistent imagery helps keep leads engaged in the story your ad is
telling, and more likely to convert.




So take advantage of the leads you already
have, create engaging opportunities for your
prospects to convert, and keep your ads
clean, consistent and always evolving.

If you do that, you won’t feel dread the next
time you check your ads account—you’ll
feel delight!
