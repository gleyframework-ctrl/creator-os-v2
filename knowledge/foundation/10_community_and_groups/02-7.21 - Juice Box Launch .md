How we got pulled in \$20,000 in 21 days to create an evergreen asset
that we can sell over and over again..

(Likely for years - as we kept most of the stuff inside of it evergreen
/ semi-future proof)

And how you can replicate it with or without an actual product of your
own.

**You can do this with:**

-   Affiliate product - with various different price points \$1-\$1,000+

-   An offer of your own (can even package things you've already created
    > into it.

-   A mixture of the two

**[Idea -]{.mark}**

[One of the key things to pre-selling an offer - is validation.]{.mark}

[Make sure there is a demand for it - either directly inside of your own
audience or it is a nagging problem happening inside the market as a
whole.]{.mark}

[How do identify problems? Ask]{.mark}

-   [Inside communities of your own]{.mark}

-   [To your clients]{.mark}

-   [In your content]{.mark}

-   [Other spaces]{.mark}

[Don't have a product of your own - look at the main offers you're
promoting & see what common burning problems & questions are being asked
inside.]{.mark}

**[This will be the fuel for -]{.mark}**

-   [The offer]{.mark}

-   [The coming content that you're going to be creating]{.mark}

-   [The actual training you're rolling out\
    > ]{.mark}

    -   [You know it's in demand - It's relevant & you will have the
        > option to bolt it onto X product and make it even better if
        > that\'s the route you're planning on going]{.mark}

[We also knew that there was a demand for a product from us for complete
beginners because we'd never done it - one of the prerequisites to join

ticket offer.]{.mark}

[Because - The intention wasn't to produce cash flow on the

-   [The objective was to build a pool of buyers that we could

-   [Make one of our core offers Group Juice a complete package -
    > because it would now encompass advanced strategies and beginner

-   [To build an asset (was a big thing in the front of our minds from

    > wanted to keep it clean for the end goal)]{.mark}

[We compiled all the market research we had and dove head first into our
resource catalog and built out the flow and structure of the

-   [Pulling on resources we already had - Docs & training we'd already
    > run - Just spin them into something new & fresh and relevant to

[Don't sleep on the power of your own assets that you likely already
have - changing the positioning and how they're presented can make the

[You already have a doc break down 2 or 3 strategies - pull pieces of

**[Marketing -]{.mark}**

[Know when you're leaning into a super heavy promo - People are going to
get sick of you.. It will be a weird feeling because most of the content
is going to be with the objective of getting eyeballs on the
offer.]{.mark}

[If you're coming off a recent launch - offer mode - Do 3-7days of just
pure value-based content.]{.mark}

**[Angles -]{.mark}**

-   [Look at your offer as a whole and see what pain points & what
    > solutions you're selling is actually providing. THIS will be the
    > fuel for your content.\
    > ]{.mark}

-   [Your offer covers 5 different things - You now have 5 different
    > cycles of content you can rotate through - Don't just stick to ONE
    > pain point for the entire promo. (3 or 4 days talking about the
    > same thing) This will allow you to talk to a wider number of
    > people inside of your audience\
    > ]{.mark}

[**Dynamics** -]{.mark}

    > it with a live video) Content to Live - Live to offer\
    > ]{.mark}
