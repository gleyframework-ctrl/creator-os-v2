ABOUT THIS LESSON
Understanding that it’s a snowball effect, everything you do in this online space will compound on top of itself.
Making sales & getting new clients as the ball is rolling is 100X easier than it is getting your first few.
         Throw Together An INSANE OFFER - when you’re at ground zero maybe you have a couple of sales or none
         This one thing can really kickstart everything and put things in motion.
You could be horrible at copywriting, your content might sub-bar, but if you have products and bonuses that are so
enticing the rest of the shit really doesn’t matter.
If you’re promoting a product that pays out $1000   ,   spend $500 of that on bonuses. Get them a Chromebook,
AirPods, Books, something that NO ONE is doing.
Yes, it’ll eat your profit margins, but the compound effect is where everything will set in motion.
You’ll have other people posting and talking about what they got from you, then word of mouth sets in, FOMO, people
are going to start feeling like “how can I get that”
          Coaching And Consulting - Go heavy on your first few sales, some of my first few high ticket sales, I was
         doing 1 on 1 call EVERY week.
Help them do whatever they’re doing, Setting up, clarity calls, the options are limitless, build up testimonials, and as
you’re able to help them start having success (doesn’t have to be huge) Can be something small like Nate helped me
get more comments on my post. Helped me do this…
          Leverage Results - Share Everything! Results DO NOT have to be monetary. Real tangible shit.
○ I just invested in x
○ I learned x
○ I overcame this challenge
○ Something I learned
○ A book I read
○ I helped a student do x….
○ Had a strategy session with x…
        Student Success - Testimonials
○ Can be screenshots of convos
○ Breakthrough they’ve had
○ Support you’ve given them
○ Things you’ve helped them with
○ Pictures of them with bonuses you’ve sent them
        Assets -
You likely already have a TON of material, resources, lead magnets, bonuses, things already at your disposal, the
beauty of the internet economy is you can use your takeaways & lessons from what you’ve learned and create your
own.
Using them as a part of your offer on top of what you’re promoting increases the perceived value of what you’re
selling without doing extra work.
         Document Everything And Share It -
People get inspired by seeing others achieving what they want to, even if it’s micro achievements. Put all of it into
your content.
The goal is to make them feel connected to you before you ever even get into a 1 on 1 convo with them because it
will make selling something to them extremely easy.
Spend More Time Where It’s Important
The 2 things that should always be a Priority:
1- Perfecting your content. Testing different things and mixing in lead gen content - Short form posts that will pull
your leads out of your friend’s list/inside of your group
        Getting Better At Creating Short-Form Video Content
        Getting Better At Creating Long-Form Content That Sells FOR YOU
2 - Messenger, by far the most important part of the biz. Accompanied by…. the follow-up, messaging, and
presenting offers to your leads. (aim for at least 5 a day)
Always prioritize the people who’ve shown interest first, and move to cold leads last.
