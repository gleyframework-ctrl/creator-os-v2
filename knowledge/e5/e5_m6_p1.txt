SOURCE: E5 C.A.M.P. Masterclass — Module 6 (Lesson 19 & Evaluation Flow Chart)
FRAMEWORK: MVF TESTING, METRICS & DIAGNOSTIC FLOWCHART
WHEN TO USE: When a member launches their Minimum Viable Funnel (MVF) and needs to know if it's a success, a failure, or what specific part to fix. When they ask "How much should I pay for clicks?" or "My funnel isn't converting, what do I tweak?"

--- TOOL 1: THE MVF TRACKING TRIAD ---
LAW: You cannot improve what you do not measure. Every MVF must be tracked across 3 dimensions:
1. MVF ECONOMICS: Cost Per Click (CPC), Earnings Per Click (EPC), Return On Investment (ROI).
2. MVF STAGE PERFORMANCE: Opt-In Rate (Traffic Captivation), Sales Conversion Rate (Sales Vehicle), Order Form Completion Rate (Checkout).
3. MVF CONSUMPTION: Which of the 4 message elements are they consuming/dropping off at? (Big Idea, Lead, Marketing Argument, Offer).

--- TOOL 2: THE INITIAL MVF TEST PROTOCOL ---
RULES FOR THE FIRST TEST:
- Send ONE source of traffic to the MVF (do not mix traffic sources).
- Test with a random segment of your House List OR Cold traffic.
- Minimum 100 prospects must hit the MVF to get statistically relevant baseline data.

--- TOOL 3: THE CPCmos FORMULA (Cost Per Click Margin of Safety) ---
WHEN TO USE: To determine the absolute maximum you can afford to pay for a single click on paid ads (Facebook, YouTube, etc.) while remaining profitable.
FORMULA:
- Lower Bound: (Product Price x 1.5) x 0.004
- Upper Bound: (Product Price x 3.0) x 0.005
EXAMPLE: For a $97 front-end product:
- Lower Bound: ($97 x 1.5) x 0.004 = $0.58 per click.
- Upper Bound: ($97 x 3.0) x 0.005 = $1.45 per click.
DECISION RULE: If your actual CPC on Facebook is higher than your CPCmos Upper Bound ($1.45), your funnel economics are broken. You must either increase the product price, increase the Average Order Value (AOV) with upsells, or improve the conversion rate.

--- TOOL 4: THE MVF EVALUATION FLOWCHART (Diagnostic) ---
WHEN TO USE: When the MVF test finishes and the numbers are bad. Use this to isolate the "leak".
SCENARIO A: HOUSE LIST TEST
- If Opt-In Rate (Lead) is > 2% S.C. (Statistically Congruent) -> The Big Idea & Lead are working. Move to Cold Traffic.
- If Opt-In Rate is < 2% -> The Big Idea or Lead is failing. RE-ENGINEER the Big Idea/Lead before spending money on cold traffic.
SCENARIO B: COLD TRAFFIC TEST
- Examine Economics: Is ROI > 60%?
- If YES -> Funnel is profitable. Scale traffic and enhance funnel performance (add upsells/backend).
- If NO (< 60% ROI) -> Look at Stage Performance. Where is the drop-off? 
  - Low Opt-in? Fix the Traffic Captivation Page (Headline/Lead).
  - Low Sales Conversion? Fix the Marketing Argument or Offer (Thesis, 1-2-3 Chunks, Risk Reversal).
  - Low Order Form Completion? Fix the Checkout (Remove friction, add guarantee seal).

ARABIC TERMINOLOGY:
MVF Testing = اختبار القمع الأدنى القابل للتطبيق | CPCmos (Margin of Safety) = هامش أمان تكلفة النقرة | House List = القائمة البريدية الداخلية / الجمهور الدافئ | Cold Traffic = الزيارات الباردة | Opt-In Rate = معدل التسجيل | Sales Conversion Rate = معدل التحويل إلى مبيعات | Stage Performance = أداء مراحل القمع | Re-Engineer = إعادة هندسة.
