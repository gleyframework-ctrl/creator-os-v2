# **THE 7-DAY ONE GROUP INSTALL PLAN**

Install once. Let it compound.

## **WHAT THIS WEEK IS ACTUALLY ABOUT**

This is not a \"content challenge.\"

It is not about posting more or being perfect.

This is about cleaning up your environment, installing the right
structure, and creating your first momentum loop.

By the end of Day 7:

Your group will make sense

Ascension will be clear

DMs will feel natural

Selling will feel lighter

Do not skip days. Do not overbuild. Do not add extra steps.

## **DAY 1 --- THE ONE GROUP DECISION**

**Objective:** Eliminate fragmentation.

**Actions:**

Decide which community is your primary ecosystem.

Archive, pause, or de-prioritize all other groups.

Make the internal decision: this is the room that matters.

**Outcome:**

Your attention consolidates. Your audience stops splitting. Momentum can
actually build.

## **DAY 2 --- MINIMUM VIABLE COMMUNITY (MVC)**

**Objective:** Install the smallest structure that can convert.

**Actions:**

Clean the group container.

Install a clear name and a simple description anchored to ONE outcome.

Remove extra tabs, old resources, and confusing sections.

**Outcome:**

New members understand why they\'re there. Overwhelm drops. You stop
managing chaos.

## **DAY 3 --- ENTRY POSITIONING**

**Objective:** Make the outcome obvious at the door.

**Actions:**

Write or rewrite your Welcome Post.

Anchor it to the specific problem you fix, the quick win they\'ll get,
and the fact that deeper support exists.

**Outcome:**

Buyers self-identify. Non-buyers self-filter. Selling stops feeling
awkward.

## **DAY 4 --- PIN & KILL CLEANUP**

**Objective:** Protect clarity and momentum.

**Actions:**

Pin only 3 things:

1.  Welcome + expectations

2.  Proof/Momentum

3.  Start here/Next step

Kill everything else---old posts, resource dumps, and multiple CTAs.

**Outcome:**

Progress is visible. Direction is clear. The group stops resetting
itself.

## **DAY 5 --- QUICK PAIN REMOVAL POST**

**Objective:** Create your first trust-building win.

**Actions:**

Post ONE piece of content that removes friction or reframes the real
problem.

Do not teach everything. Do not sell hard.

Focus on a belief shift or a mistake to stop making.

**Outcome:**

Members feel relief. Trust is earned. This one post does more than 10
random \"value\" posts ever will.

## **DAY 6 --- ACCLIMATION DMs + SIGNAL AWARENESS**

**Objective:** Build connection without pressure.

**Actions:**

Send acclimation DMs to new members.

Watch for thoughtful comments or \"what\'s next?\" language.

**Outcome:**

Familiarity increases. Resistance drops. You start spotting buyer intent
naturally.

No selling yet. Just awareness.

## **DAY 7 --- ASCENSION CLARITY + NEXT STEP**

**Objective:** Make the path forward obvious.

**Actions:**

Add or reference a \"Next Step\" post or bridge explanation.

Respond to live signals with calm, permission-based conversations.

**Outcome:**

Ascension feels natural. DMs feel earned. You are not pushing. You are
guiding.

## **WHAT YOU SHOULD NOT DO THIS WEEK**

Do NOT build a course

Do NOT add more content or chase engagement

Do NOT run lives or overexplain your offer

Structure first. Momentum second. Scale later.

## **WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE AFTER DAY 7**

Your group feels lighter.

Members know why they\'re there.

Conversations feel easier.

You\'re no longer guessing what to do next.

This is the foundation---everything that comes next compounds.

## **FINAL REMINDER**

You don\'t win by doing more.

You win by installing the right structure once and letting it work for
you every day after.
