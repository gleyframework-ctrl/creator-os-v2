Your first EVER Tiktok videos will get more views naturally as TikTok figures out who your content supposed to be for
if you can nail your first handful of videos with a good hook, valuable middle & clear cta to follow you. Hitting 1k

Naturally, shorter content as a whole will get more views and more “reach” so it’s not a bad idea to mix in a bit of
shorty / trendy-ish content that’s relevant to your biz in the early stages to soar you past that 1k mark.
The three most important factors are:

      Your watch time
      Your % completion rate
      Whether or not TikTok thinks they have an audience for your content.

For videos <15 seconds you need 100% watch time, for videos 16-30 seconds long you need 75%+ watch time and
for videos 31 seconds or longer you need 50%+ watch time
If you achieve those metrics and your post didn’t get above average views refer

to steps #2 and #3 For completion rate, you should aim for 50%+
For whether or not TikTok has an audience for your content this is going to be based on the keywords you use both
with text on the screen and the video. Please make sure you’re including keywords that have been used in other
viral videos for your niche/demographic. completely through.

The VAGEL Content Rotation to follow that’s responsible for 1,000’s of followers & $1,000’s of dollars made (the
same one flip used after he lost his first tiktok account)
Monday - Vulnerable Tell a Story in your post of your own that you can incorporate business into. Be Intimate in what
you’re sharing to let people know who you are, what kind of person you are. Go Live and encourage engagement, ask
questions that will be controversial to mindset, aha moments, perspective shifts to spark comments and
conversation

Tuesday - Announcement Make an Announcement of an upcoming training you're hosting in your group or via zoom.
Share your wins, results, student results, things you’re working on etc. Offer a Lead magnet or the fact you want to
create one if the interest is there. This should be a hand-raising post to generate leads and opportunity
Wednesday - Gift of the Soul Give your Audience Something that will help them get a quick win or Result. Something
that you have learned that has helped you generate a specific Result ( It doesn't have to be monetary )
Thursday - Engagement Here you want to share a Fact or Joke, something to provoke Engagement with your
Audience. Get Creative with things you like as a hobby or something you enjoy, Share your sense of Humor.
Friday - Lifestyle Here you want to Really open up about who you are as a person and what you really care about.
Family, friends, kids, spouse. Things you like today aside from business and work.

On all days make sure you have a very specific CTA. Optimize for whatever it is you want to grow, your following, your
group, or to comment ‘xyz’ to get ‘abc’ Just have some fun for once and test things for a bit.
This Content strategy is to show your audience that you are a real person that has learned how to do what most
people don't even know about while building up that know, Like and Trust factor. This will allow you to move
conversations to a sales convo much faster while generating leads each week.
