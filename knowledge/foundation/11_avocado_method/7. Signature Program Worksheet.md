**Guarantees:**

The number one fear people will have when it comes to working with you
is "What if this doesn't work?"

Most of our competitors assume zero risk, and put all the risk on their
client. By having a guarantee, you're positioned as more trustworthy.

Guarantees are about calculated risks. By providing a guarantee you are
positioning yourself for more sales. So while you might get more
refunds, you'll be net positive due to more sales.

**Two main types of guarantees:**

1.  Unconditional guarantees ("For whatever reason whatsoever")

    a.  *This could be forever, or for a certain duration - 30 day, 90
        > day, lifetime etc.\
        > *

2.  Conditional guarantees ("If you don't \_\_\_\_, in \_\_\_\_\_... We
    > will \_\_\_\_\_\_\_")

    a.  *"If you don't (HIT GOAL), in (TIMEFRAME) We will (GIVE YOUR
        > MONEY BACK)"*

    b.  *"If you don't (HIT GOAL), in (TIMEFRAME) We will (WORK WITH YOU
        > FOR FREE TILL YOU DO)"*

    c.  *"If you don't (HIT GOAL), in (TIMEFRAME) We will (CANCEL YOUR
        > CONTRACT)"*

For coaching businesses I love conditional guarantees. The stipulations
& conditions need to be reasonable for both you and the client. They
shouldn't have to jump through 100 hoops to be eligible, and you also
shouldn't overpromise on the goal or timeframe.
