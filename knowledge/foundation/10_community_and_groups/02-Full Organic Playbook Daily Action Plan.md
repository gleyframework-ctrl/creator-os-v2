**Groups**

-   3 to 5 Group posts per week

-   5 comments on other people's group posts per day

-   Send friend requests to anyone who likes or comments on your posts
    > or comments. (add at least 10 friends per day)

-   **DO NOT** post the same post in multiple groups on the same day. It
    > looks spammy and can get you blocked by Facebook.

**Personal Profile**

-   10 to 15 personal profile posts in week 1 to 'prime' your profile.

-   1 to 2 personal profile posts per day after week 1.

-   Every 3rd-4th personal profile posts a CTA with your schedule link.

-   Be sure to check that your Facebook profile settings are set to
    > [public]{.underline}.

**Messenger**

-   Initiate a conversation with at least **ten people per day** that
    > have accepted your friend request. Have a REAL conversation. It's
    > not about just getting them on the phone.

-   Give yourself 30-60 seconds to look at the individual's profile to
    > find *one point of rapport* to initiate the conversation (i.e.
    > travel pics, their cover photo, they work out, etc).

-   Do not message them until they have accepted your friend request,
    > otherwise your message will be pushed to a filtered folder and
    > they may not see it.

Purpose

-   If you're doing posts solely to get Strategy Sessions, then you're
    > doing it for the wrong reason and consequently will prolong your
    > results → Post organic because it's the right thing to do; because
    > you want to help set people free.

Formula

-   **Quality + Quantity + Consistency**

-   Quality = transformational leadership posts

-   Quantity = example: 20 unique posts in 1 week vs. 1 month

-   Consistency = on-going, not just "once and done" \>\>\> you are
    > planting seeds that may bear fruit in 1 week, 1 month, 3 months+

Transformation vs. Information

-   Posts should be transformational (giving them a new perspective),
    > rather than informational (giving them new tips)

-   Education doesn't help people feel the pain that will move them to
    > take the action they need to take

Future Pacing

-   Posts should inspire people to have a different result than what
    > they are experiencing right now

-   Use your organic posts to paint a picture on your profile of what is
    > possible for your prospects

Power of Story

-   Sharing stories (from your own life, clients, etc.) uncovers things
    > about your prospect that they couldn't uncover on their own,
    > creating those perspective shifts

-   Story creates greater trust and connection with you

What to Post

-   Your avatar is a holistic person, so you can speak to the lifecycle
    > of your avatar (not just one part of them)

People, Not Robots

-   Remember when you post you are starting relationships with real
    > people, so talk to and treat them as you would in-person.

Polarizing

-   Write posts that polarize people, so you are getting your ideal
    > avatar raising their hand (engaging) and turning away everyone
    > else

-   Use your copy/messaging to speak to the specific struggles of the
    > people who you want to serve (their pains/desires are different
    > from people at an earlier or later stage)
