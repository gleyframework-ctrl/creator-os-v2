**Are You Still?/Have You Yet?**

The purpose of this email is to "call out" a subscriber who indicated
they were interested in achieving something (write a book, start an
ecommerce store, get 6-pack abs, take a second honeymoon, etc.) and ask
them if they've taken that action... **MOST of the time the answer is
going to be no.**

Now that you've reminded them of their desire to do X, you need to
provide them with a path to achieving that goal.

This email can be "stacked" with almost any other series but works best:

-   Immediately BEFORE a *"Need Help?"* email...

-   Before OR After a *"Join Me In Congratulating*" email...

-   After a *"Gain, Logic, Fear"* series

**SUBJECT:** Have you **\[TOPIC/ACTION THEY WERE SUPPOSED TO TAKE\]**
yet?

ACHIEVE\]**?

TAKE\]**, then you\'re **probably \[RESULT OF ACTION\]** as we speak.

How good does that feel to say?

**\[ACTION/RESULT DESIRED\]**... and this should help.

**\[LINK TO VIDEO/TRAINING/ETC.\]**
