YOUR MILLION DOLLAR MESSAGE

MDM = SA\*(C+M+T)-P

***In 24 words or less***

Clearly more than 24 words

SA= Single Avatar

C= Currency

M= Metric

T= Timeline

P = Pain

**[Examples]{.underline}**

I help new entrepreneurial men develop authentic confidence in 90 days
so they can explode their business without all the doubt, fear and
anxiety.

I help entrepreneurial men develop unshakeable confidence in 90 days so
they get results fast without doubt, fear and anxiety....

I help entrepreneurial men (SA) develop unshakeable confidence (C ) so
they make sales and create more freedom without fear.

**I help entrepreneurial men (SA) develop unshakeable confidence in 90
days (C ) so they can build a business they love without overwhelm and
anxiety.**

**I help entrepreneurial men (SA) develop unshakeable confidence in 90
days so they can be a more effective leader without overwhelm and
fear.**

**Keep going... Write 20 of these until you find one that totally
clicks.**
