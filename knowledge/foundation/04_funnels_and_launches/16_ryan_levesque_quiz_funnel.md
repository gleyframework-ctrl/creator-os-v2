## 1. CORE PSYCHOLOGICAL PRINCIPLES

### 1.1 Self-Discovery Drives Engagement

People love learning about themselves. A quiz taps into innate curiosity.

By providing a personalized result, you create an emotional connection and make the lead feel "seen and understood."

### 1.2 Micro-Commitments

Each question is a small "yes" that builds momentum.

By the time they reach the lead capture page, they are psychologically invested and more likely to give their email.

### 1.3 Band-Aid vs. Cure

| Concept | Definition |
|---------|------------|
| Band-Aid | The free quiz result - quick, valuable insight |
| Cure | Your paid offer - the deeper solution |

The quiz delivers the Band-Aid. The art is smoothly pivoting to the Cure without over-delivering on the freebie.

## 2. THE HOOK - FINDING YOUR QUIZ TOPIC

### 2.1 Three Quiz Frameworks

| Framework | Description | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| TYPE | Segments people into categories | "What's Your Leadership Style?" |
| KILLER | Identifies a weakness or blocker | "What's Your #1 Wealth Blocker?" |
| SCORE | Rates the user on a scale | "What's Your Resilience Score?" |

### 2.2 Hook Thought-Starters

Ask yourself:

- What is a topic your customers are already talking about?
- What topic offers the chance for self-discovery?
- What generates a strong emotional reaction (fear, desire)?
- What is trending in the media?
- What is an evergreen problem that never goes away?

### 2.3 Testing Your Hook (4-Way Split Test)

Before building your full quiz, TEST your hook with paid traffic.

**Process:**

1. Create 4 different Facebook/Instagram ads
2. Keep image and body copy IDENTICAL - only change the headline (hook)
3. Each ad links to a unique SMIQ (Single Most Important Question) landing page
4. Run ads until you have at least 30 link clicks on the LOWEST-performing ad
5. Use statistical significance calculator to determine the winner

**Income Engine Hooks to Test:**

- Hook 1: "What's Your Domain Burn Risk Level?" (Score)
- Hook 2: "Which Email Infrastructure Blocker Is Killing Your Campaigns?" (Killer)
- Hook 3: "What's Your Email Operator Personality?" (Type)
- Hook 4: "Are You Making These 3 Fatal Deliverability Mistakes?" (Killer)

## 3. THE BUCKETS - SEGMENTING YOUR AUDIENCE

Segment quiz-takers into 3-5 buckets for personalized outcomes and follow-up.

**Why 3-5?**
- Fewer than 3 = not enough personalization
- More than 5 = unnecessary complexity

### Income Engine Buckets (for CPA operators)

| Bucket | Name | Description |
|--------|------|-------------|
| 1 | The Burner | Domains burn frequently, no automation, high manual work |
| 2 | The Stalled | Some automation but deliverability issues, stuck at 50k emails/month |
| 3 | The Scalper | Good infrastructure but no ownership, paying high SMTP fees |
| 4 | The Architect | Ready to build private infrastructure, needs guidance |
| 5 | The Beginner | Just starting, not ready for high-ticket, needs entry-level education |

## 5. THE RESULTS REPORT (PDF)

Send a PDF report to every quiz-taker (delayed 15-30 minutes).

### 5.1 Report Structure (8 Sections)

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| 1. Cover | "Your Unique Results Report" with quiz hook |
| 2. Cover Letter | Congratulate them, introduce yourself, tease results |
| 3. Your Result | Clearly state the result (can use icons) |
| 4. What That Means | Explain the result |
| 5. Common Mistakes | List mistakes |
| 6. What Happens If Not Addressed | Agitate |
| 7. What You Can Do | Set buying criteria |
| 8. Next Steps (CTA) | Pivot to your offer with link |

### 5.2 Key Best Practices

- Use the SAME COPY as your outcome video script
- Keep it visually consistent with your brand
- Include page numbers and copyright notice
- **Do NOT send as attachment** - host PDF and provide link
- Use a short, memorable URL (e.g., `incomeengine.com/burner`)

## 6. EMAIL FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCES

Three levels: Easy, Medium, Hard.

### 6.1 Easy: One Email

**Subject:** Your Quiz Results Are Here

**Content:**
- Deliver the result
- Include visual of the result
- Link to PDF report
- P.S. - Soft-sell your Cure

### 6.2 Medium: Delivery Email + Nurture Sequence

**Step 1:** Same as Easy

**Step 2:** Ongoing nurture sequence (same for all buckets)

**Nurture Email Formula:**

1. Tell a Story - relatable, engaging
2. Teach a Lesson - valuable content
3. Soft Call to Action - invite to download, attend webinar, etc.

**Sending Frequency:**

| Period | Frequency |
|--------|-----------|
| Day 0 | Email 1 (delivery) |
| Day 1-14 | Every day |
| Day 15-30 | Every other day |
| Day 30+ | At least once a week |

### 6.3 Hard: Customized Nurture per Bucket

**Step 1:** Same delivery email

**Step 2:** Nurture sequence where SOME emails are customized for each bucket

Customize emails closer to the sale (case studies, social proof, specific problems).

### 6.4 Profitable Email Formula

| Rule | Action |
|------|--------|
| Shorter Emails | 55% more clicks. Cut fluff. |
| No Images | Higher deliverability (except delivery email) |
| Max 2 Links | Too many links trigger spam filters |
| 80/20 Rule | Can break rules in 20% of emails |

### 6.5 Hit-Reply Email (Day 0 Bonus)

Send 1 hour after delivery email to boost engagement and deliverability:

```
Subject: Did you get my email?

Hey,

A little earlier today I sent you an email with your quiz results. I just wanted to make sure it made it to you.

Can you do me a quick favour? Hit reply to this email and let me know you got it.

Thanks,
[Your Name]
```

## 8. TRAFFIC STRATEGIES

### 8.1 YouTube (Dan Rose)

- Use "Take Quiz" instead of "Learn More" - higher CTR
- Skip stopper: First 3-5 seconds must grab attention
- Ad script: Skip stopper → believable promise → early CTA → details → final CTA
- Remarketing: Separate campaigns for each bucket
- Channel targeting: Start with 10 relevant channels, expand using "Where Your Ads Ran" report

### 8.2 Instagram (Rudy Mawer)

- Instagram is visual: 90% effort on creatives
- Story ads: Selfie-style videos with text
- Pattern interrupts: Controversial statements, confessions, "What if" questions
- User-generated content: Blast email list for video testimonials
- Compliance: Avoid calling out person, focus on positive outcomes
- Retargeting: Separate ads for each bucket

### 8.3 Google Ads (Kristen Corr)

- Acquisition: Use all ad units (video, display, responsive)
- Retargeting: Customer Match (upload emails of each bucket)
- Pixel segmentation: Set up events for each question/outcome
- Lookalike audiences: Create from bucket audiences (need 100+ people)
- Attribution: Use Campaign Manager for cross-platform impact

### 8.4 LinkedIn (Josh Turner)

- Organic: Post status offering quiz, ask people to comment for link
- LinkedIn Groups: Find niche groups, post quiz or sponsor group owner
- Sponsored InMail + Retargeting: Install Insight Tag, retarget visitors
- Pixel data: Audience insights show who's visiting (job titles, seniority)

### 8.5 Facebook (Curt Maly)

**The BELT Method:**

| Stage | Purpose | Content |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Belief | Build rapport | Longer, educational videos (top of funnel) |
| Engage | Build trust | Content, testimonials, stories (middle) |
| Lead | Drive conversion | Conversion campaigns to engaged people |
| Transact | Close the sale | Retarget people who clicked but didn't buy |

- Video view ads: Run 10-20 videos at $1/day each to build warm audience
- Hot 28: Retarget anyone who engaged for 28 days
- Don't chase clicks: Focus on impressions and video views

## 9. BENCHMARKS & OPTIMIZATION

### 9.1 Quiz Funnel Benchmarks

| Stage | Target |
|-------|--------|
| Pre-quiz welcome page (Start Quiz clicks) | 50% |
| Per-question completion | 97% |
| Lead capture page (email opt-in) | 50% |
| Post-quiz outcome page (click to offer) | 50% |
| Offer conversion (within 30 days) | 5% average |

### 9.2 Optimization Strategies

1. **Test screams, not whispers** - Make radical changes
2. **Test from the front** - Start with welcome page before going deeper
3. **Addition by subtraction** - Remove non-essential elements

### 9.3 Pixel Segmentation for Retargeting

1. Install Facebook/Google pixel on quiz pages
2. Tag each question and answer with unique event
3. Create custom audiences based on events
4. Build lookalike audiences from custom audiences
