ABOUT THIS LESSON
Huge misconception…(primarily in the high ticket space)
Partnering with a company is an offer. (its more to it than that)
You need to add bonuses, resources, and support to whatever you’re promoting to make it unique to you.
Increase the perceived value of the product you’re selling.
This whole space is about selling yourself, building connections through your content, and monetizing those
connections with your offers.
What is going to make you stand out from everyone else promoting the same thing or the owner of that product?
If you’re selling something that pays you $500+ per sale, you can deliver massive bonuses on top of the product and

still be very profitable as a business owner.
