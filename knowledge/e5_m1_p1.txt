SOURCE: E5 C.A.M.P. Masterclass — Module 1 (Prospect Analysis), Lesson 1
FRAMEWORK: PROSPECT ANALYSIS — "Crawl Inside Their Head"
WHEN TO USE: When a member defines their market, writes an offer/message/ad, or when ads/content don't convert. ALWAYS before crafting the Big Idea or unique mechanism.

CORE LAWS:
1. Wants are more compelling than needs. Channel existing desire — never try to create it.
2. Customers want outcomes (results), not products.
3. Prospects buy on feeling (greed, fear, anger, envy, embarrassment, belonging, guilt, inadequacy, escape, lust), then justify with logic.
4. The prospect is NOT a blank slate: they already hold beliefs about the problem, the marketplace, your product type, and competitors. Map beliefs before persuading.

STEPS:
1. DEMOGRAPHIC PROFILE: age, gender, ethnicity, job/income, location, marital status, education, political affiliations.
2. BEHAVIOR PROFILE: prior purchases, experts followed, movies/TV, periodicals, books.
3. W.E.B. ANALYSIS:
   - WANTS: what do they deeply want or believe they need?
   - EMOTIONS: how do they feel now? point of angst? how do they want to feel?
   - BELIEFS: what do they believe about (a) the fundamental problem, (b) the marketplace, (c) your product type, (d) other sellers?
4. IDEAL CLIENT EXERCISE: imagine your greatest success-story client. List their EXACT results. Then list the secondary transformations those results created (secondary benefits).
5. NOW & AFTER GRID: write NOW vs AFTER on 4 axes: HAVE, AVG. EXPERIENCE, STATUS, FEEL.
6. SECRET DESIRE DIG: take the surface want and ask "so they can FEEL what?" Map:
   - earn more / launch business / win with stocks → feel successful
   - healthier / live longer / more energy → feel less mortal
   - learn / master skill / fix broken → feel independent
   - save / nest egg / passive income → feel secure
   - lose weight / look younger / get stronger → feel attractive
   - make friends / dates / better sex → feel wanted
   - fame / praise / rewards → feel respected
   - sleep better / no pain / no stress → feel comfortable
   - indulge / treat self / have the best → feel "worth it"
   Deeper drivers: elite club, admiration, appeal to opposite gender, esteem/ego/pride, one-upmanship, make a statement, self-image, self-confidence, revenge.
7. SCHWAB CHECKLIST — people want to GAIN (health, time, money, popularity, appearance, comfort, leisure, pride, advancement, enjoyment, confidence, prestige); BE (good parent, sociable, up-to-date, creative, influential, efficient, first, authority); DO (express personality, resist domination, satisfy curiosity, emulate admirable, appreciate beauty, collect, win affection, improve); SAVE (time, money, work, discomfort, worry, doubt, risk, embarrassment).

DECISION RULE FOR THE COACH: When a member names their niche, never answer generic. Walk them through Steps 1-6 one question at a time, then output their completed Now & After Grid and the ONE deepest secret desire to build the message on.

EXAMPLE: Fitness niche. Surface want: "lose weight." Emotions now: embarrassed, inadequate; wants: attractive, confident. Belief: "diets fail for me." Secret desire: feel wanted / feel worth it. NOW: have=tired body, status=invisible, feel=ashamed. AFTER: have=strong body, status=admired, feel=attractive. Message built on "feel wanted," NOT "buy my program."

COMMON MISTAKES:
1. Selling the product instead of the outcome/feeling.
2. Targeting "everyone" — no specific profile.
3. Trying to create desire instead of channeling existing desire.
4. Arguing with prospect beliefs instead of mapping and honoring them.
5. Stopping at surface wants; never digging to the secret desire.
