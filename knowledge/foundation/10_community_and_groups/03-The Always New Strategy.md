ABOUT THIS LESSON
This is my "Always New" strategy that will allow you to regularly pull in quick cash flow into your business simply by
changing the way that you're selling your products.

The real power of this strategy is by leveraging buying triggers like: time sensitivity, limited spots or a combination of
the two regularly to light a fire under your potential buyers without having to roll out "something new" all of the time.
