# **[The Predictable Profits Framework]{.underline}**

Now, this is everything we've personally all year with over 10 different
offers & joint ventures this year and how we've ended up doing the SAME
strategy as a whole - every single time. (Cash Mullet - Some type of
live anchor post with an invitation on the back end)

1.  Taking An Idea & Turning It Into An Income

2.  Packaging It Into An Offer & An Asset That Will Capture Our Energy

3.  How We PRE-SELL Every Offer We Launch So We Can Get Paid To Create

### [Things YOU NEED Prior]{.underline} 

-   An audience - Even if it's small - you can still do this

-   Some goodwill built-up (If you've been selling hard for the next
    > couple of weeks you don't want to roll into this right away)

#### [Why This Works:]{.underline} 

-   New

-   Exclusivity

-   Scarcity

-   Time / Limited Spots

-   Personal - Custom

-   Bonuses / Extra Value

### [Goal/Outcome]{.underline}

-   Get paid to create an asset - That will allow you to bolt it onto
    > any relevant affiliate product & increase the perceived value &
    > make you stand out from other affiliates.

-   Prioritize YOU, building your own pool of buyers - whom you can
    > start building long-standing relationships with, because they'll
    > like to buy additional products from you

-   Kickstart a snowball of momentum by stacking monetary wins,
    > testimonials & create more leverage for you to go out there and
    > have more angles in your content

### [Picking The Product / Pricing]{.underline} 

-   10 spots upon purchase of \$100 product

-   5 Spots Upon purchase of \$1,000 product

Chose something just a bit above what you feel you can accomplish - if
you've yet to snag a few high ticket sales, don't be afraid to do this
on the back end of a lower ticket affiliate program.

You have some results under your belt go bigger in price but for less
people

### [Look for AMMO/ideas/things to run your campaign:]{.underline} 

-   Your own results & strategy / unique process

-   Client results or community results of the said process

-   Any & All proof of concept you can leverage (IE - a 4 week intensive
    > how I grew my Facebook group by X)

-   Things you're seeing the market that you have the ability to solve

-   Look at anything and everything that you already have that you could
    > spin off / leverage into an asset & change the delivery (docs -
    > Resources - Work Smarter not harder)

### [Putting together the timeline:]{.underline} 

Depending on what season you're business is in - or if you're coming off
a super hard promo - you'll need to build that goodwill back up..

But giving yourself enough time to run the campaign

Would aim anywhere between 10-30 days for a full-on push can be longer
if you go very heavy into a [[cash mullet
strategy]{.underline}](https://docs.google.com/document/u/2/d/1xLkz2T8AiEzOBr08vxkjZ9GhTB7mJ5XLJLhFGQ-771k/edit)
(Lots of value of front - sales on back)

**Note:** Once you start hitting the content hard about promo - Keep the
Date vague/open on the back - Give you audience time to acclimate/give
yourself the ability to get a temp check & figure out what type of
content will resonate best.\
\
Can lean into X number of spots - but don't start openly hammering "this
is gone in 5 days - unless you're getting some eyeballs/hand raisers"

Can also go vague on spots until you have a good gauge on the interest
of your audience so you can change it as you go if needed (Power in
publicly selling your offer completely out because it'll start your next
campaign off with fomo)

**\*Do not beat yourself if you don't sell out your first campaign
though\* It happens - we had a hard time filling up all the spots for GJ
launch**

### [Decide On Delivery]{.underline} 

Now if the purpose of rolling out your micro launch is to create an
asset - That you can either sell as a standalone product or add as a
bonus on top of affiliate product would usually look at delivering
inside a Private Buyer Group on a live or prerecorded

Either way - PRESELL spots - AKA Sell spots before you start doing the
trainings

### [Put Together The Offer]{.underline} 

-   Who it's for

-   What specific problem it's going to solve & what outcome they're
    > going to accomplish

-   Proof of concept - Your own + Any that you can leverage

-   Relevant bonuses/support

-   Reasons for them to act now (Limited availability - X time - Cost
    > Savings)

-   Any way that you can derisk the investment (I will work with you
    > until...X now & X after - can still do this with affiliate
    > products)

#### [Important Things Went Putting Together Your Offer]{.underline}

-   Go **YOU** focused.. In this world you're virtually selling
    > yourself - Highlight your own things - Bonuses - Resources and
    > things that you're providing on top of X program. **\
    > **

-   **Example -** For those who're promoting Group Juice or an offer
    > with a Doc of their own - Leverage their doc but mix in a quick
    > bridge type doc highlighting your support, Bonuses & results &
    > then interlink to their doc

-   Less Is More - Add hyper-relevant things to your offer - Things that
    > will enhance the product you're selling & what they're going to do
    > for the person who purchases.

-   Look at things you have already - If you have training you've done,
    > Videos, Lives, or Resources, Look at things you've already created
    > that you can add to X program to increase the perceived value of
    > your offer

**Aim for 4-8 training that YOU are going to do/run inside of your own
private buyer\'s group - Try to make them as linear or complementary to
main product as you can..**

**Examples:**

-   Jill's unique framework for blowing up her group by 2,000+ people in
    > 28 days

-   Kates process for closing sales almost every other day all of Oct

-   Mark's content framework that has had a handful of people sending
    > him messages asking him "Where do i buy"

-   Rachels process growing her TikTok & going live

You can literally spin anything that you're doing in your business daily
that will help someone two steps behind you..

### [Offer Doc Flow]{.underline} 

-   **Opening -** Who It's for - Digging into certain pain points they
    > are actively struggling within their business.\
    > \
    > Painting a picture about where they're currently sitting at in
    > there biz & the struggles of being there.

-   **Results Snapshot -** Tie in your own results / Client results -
    > the closer you can tie these to the product you're selling or the
    > strategies taught inside of what you're selling the better\
    > \
    > If you don't have your own leverage community results & results of
    > people inside of the program (creators and students)

-   **Highlight benefits of the program -** Not the features of your
    > product but how it will solve the problem & thing you dug into at
    > the beginning of the doc\
    > \
    > Speak about the "feeling" or place they'll be once they've
    > implemented the product.

> If you can highlight the benefits of the program while "eliminating" a
> certain thing it can be very powerful.\
> \
> How we did with GJ & the without cold dm's strat

-   **Transition into Core features of your product -** break down the
    > different features of the product & what those will bring to your
    > client\'s biz.\
    > \
    > IE - On Day one you'll learn how to build out your business so you
    > can effectively grow & monetize, giving you the ability to make
    > money online through free social media content vs On day one we'll
    > show you how to build an online business.

-   **Transition into Bonuses -\
    > **

-   **Briefly dig into pain point & transition into solution/product
    > with a credibility statement (can be a few sentences)\
    > **

-   **Availability - Spots / Days Left to get in or combination of the
    > two\
    > **

-   **Pricing\
    > **

-   **Wrap up - Either prompt to hit the check out inside doc or follow
    > back up**

### [Cash Mullet Kick-Off]{.underline} 

Free Training To Build Goodwill & Drop Tons Of Value + Extend Invitation
To Offer On The Back

(Can be very similar to offer itself / a 1000ft overview of the process
in general)

Works well because naturally, you'll have a bit more volume attending
live because it's a "free thing"

The goal would be to actually give them a tangible thing on the free
training - so they could actually go implement something afterward
(makes working with you even more appealing / reinforces proof of
concept & that you know your shit)

-   Going Live (Emotional Buyers Jump In) If you can snag a couple sales
    > off the live it'll really set the tone for the whole promo /
    > enhance everything else) (1-3 Days)

-   What they get

-   How to get it

-   How much it is

-   Bonuses

-   \# of spots

-   Timing etc (Can leave this vague/open if you're running the free
    > training early in the promo / you don't wanna shoot yourself in
    > the foot saying this closes down in 72 hours if you don't get any
    > traction in that timeframe)

### [Curiosity / Hype Phase]{.underline} 

Hammer content pumping curiosity and what is to come with your free
training (Go into the free training with the goal of actually giving
them takeaways & over-delivering on the free info)

Feed the upcoming live event for 2-7 days - depending on audience size -
would lean into shorter, punchier content to build up as much
hype/attention as possible / so you can get as much attention to the
thing you're doing

(Good rule of thumb - kick off any promo with a shorter content style -
get hand raisers / interest / stack momentum from start of promo)

Aim for 45-90 minutes for the free training (anything much longer seems
to be hard to maintain retention)

Temp check throughout live - prompt for engagement / bait people for
questions "Drop me a 1 if you'd would be interested in eliminating cold
dm's from your business?"\
\
(can do this with different portions of the offer to build interest in
the offer itself)

Can tease about the offer throughout training - "If you stay until the
end, I have something special for a select few"

Extend an invitation to check out the offer doc / have a chat / briefly
break down an offer on the live depending on how long you ran

-   Hype building/teasers (3-7 Days)

-   Results that your new thing has achieved

-   Testimonials & credibility building

-   Why it is different from what people have seen before

-   Who it is for & who it isn't for

-   When it is going live & how to get it

-   Any relevant scarcity (time or quantity)

### [Pre-Training DM's]{.underline}

Hit up everyone you've chatted with previously about an offer, who's
shown interest in what you're doing that's been semi responsive at one
point - Let them know that you have an upcoming training covering X &
lob them an invite.

"Hey Name, I know it's been a while since we chatted but I've got this
super cool training planned for X date that's going to break down
exactly how I was able to -insert outcome- if that's something you wanna
check out.. "Let me know - Join me here - link to group"

### [Power Of This]{.underline}

-   Running the training in itself is PRESELLING your offer - especially
    > if you can embed wins/results into the strategy

-   It's a leveraged thing that you can continue to push traffic to -
    > even after the live for weeks/months - depending on how long
    > you're running a promo or however long you want to keep it inside
    > the group

-   The Sales on the back end are usually VERY LOW pressure - They've
    > spent X amount of time with you already & lit up about the fact
    > that you're dropping nuggets on them - Getting them into
    > Full-offer is a breeze

-   Can work really well to kick off the momentum of a full-promo cycle.
    > Snag a few sales right out of the gate & it makes all the other
    > components spicier

    -   Limited Availability/proof of concept because they'll see other
        > people tipping in or even interested to join

-   Step Further Away from DM - Resuming your regular programming
    > posting content & running promo / In dm people ask questions about
    > X - you can tag them on the training & have them circle back with
    > you after

## **[The Pivot]{.underline}** 

Now, you don't necessarily have to make a massive shift in the content
as a whole - Primarily just the CTA & where you're directing people.

Would usually say the back half of the campaign calendar is when you'll
start leveraging CTA's about the specific offer. (can still tease about
the offer prior in content - would make the bulk of CTA towards the free
training)

-   Post about ANY & EVERY single spot filling up

-   At this point - hammer the deadline (If you're getting traction) -
    > limited availability

### [Injecting A Double Deadline]{.underline} 

Adding something mid-promo to further fuel FOMO & spice up the campaign
even more (as the beginning and the end will usually have the most
attention with a tad lull in the middle)

-   Adding a special bonus mid promo (we did this with the AMA & 3C's
    > bundle)

-   An extra layer of support / 1 on 1

-   Change a pricing structure

IE - "Next 2 People Who Snag Their Spot In The Offer I Extended At The
Free Training Get...."

-   Be ok with giving it to everyone who jumped in prior too

### [Follow-Up Reversal (Something from JC - he used when he was promoting Group Juice)]{.underline} 

As you're skating along in the promo & following up with people about
the offer - flip it on it's head..

Make it about them & the outcome that your offer is going to solve.

If they've already raised their hand on something admitting that they
needed help with that thing.. Then go stale in convo - you know on a
deeper level there is a desire there..

Pivoting the convo to something that is important to them - will
increase the response rate & give you scope to redirect into the offer..

"Hey Name, How big is your Facebook group currently?"

### [Give Them The Exit]{.underline} 

Let them feel like it is 1000% ok to say no (something I've been leaning
very heavily into lately - super detached / low pressure & it usually
draws them back into the convo)

But also do it from a stand point that it's alright for them to tell you
know - The faster they let you know it isn't for them - you can move
onto the next..

Can also be something as simple as adding "If this is something you'd
like to do **OR NOT**" statement
