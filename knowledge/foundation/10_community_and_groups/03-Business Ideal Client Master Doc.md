The Interview Prompt:


   Act as a senior business strategist, market researcher, and ideal client profiler.

   Your job is to deeply understand my business, offers, and ideal clients so an AI writer
   can speak with authority, clarity, and precision.

   This interview will be used to train an AI to:


        Write content that attracts the right people

        Repel the wrong people

        Speak directly to real buying motivations


   Interview rules:


        Ask one question at a time

        Wait for my response before continuing

        Ask follow-up questions to clarify vague answers

        Push for specificity when I generalize

        Challenge assumptions if something sounds generic


   Your objective:

   By the end of this interview, you should know my business well enough to:


        Clearly articulate who my ideal client is and is not

        Understand why they buy

        Know how they describe their problems in their own words


   Topics you must cover during the interview:

   1. Personal Background and Credibility


        Who I am and what I am known for

        Why people trust me

        Results, experience, or proof that gives me authority


   2. Business Overview


        Business name and what it actually does

        What makes it different from alternatives

        Mission, vision, and values

        What problem the business exists to solve


   3. Ideal Client Deep Dive


        Who my best clients are in real life

        What stage of life or business they are in

        What they have already tried and why it failed

        What keeps them stuck

        What outcome they want but struggle to articulate

        What they are afraid of if nothing changes

        What they believe is the problem versus what actually is

        Who this business is not for


   4. Offer and Transformation


        Products and services I sell

        How each offer helps the client

        The before and after transformation

        What success looks like for the client


   5. Brand and Positioning


        How I position myself in the market

        My unique selling proposition

        My story and why it resonates

        Competitors or alternatives and how I differ


   6. Marketing and Growth


        Revenue model and pricing logic

        How people currently find me

        Content platforms and messaging strategy

        What has worked and what has not

        Metrics I care about most


   7. Operations and Customer Experience


        Business structure and team

        Customer journey from discovery to success

        Support and delivery style


   8. Innovation, Risks, and Constraints


        Industry trends impacting my business

        Risks or challenges I face

        How I adapt or plan to adapt


   9. Values, Culture, and Impact


        Ethical boundaries

        Community involvement

        Internal culture and standards


   10. Language and Buyer Psychology


        Words my clients use to describe their problems

        Objections they raise before buying

        Triggers that increase trust

        Red flags that indicate a poor fit


   Begin the interview now by asking the first question.


The Report Prompt:


   Based on everything you learned from the interview, create a comprehensive,
   professional Business & Ideal Client Profile.

   This report will be used to train an AI writer, so clarity and specificity are critical.

   Use all available details and organize the report with clear headings, bullet points,
   and concise paragraphs.

   Include the following sections:

   1. Executive Summary


        What the business does

        Who it serves

        Why it exists


   2. Mission, Vision, and Unique Selling Proposition


        Core mission

        Long-term vision

        Clear USP and differentiation


   3. Ideal Client Profile (Deep Level)


        Who the ideal client is

        Stage of life or business

        Core pain points and frustrations

        Internal beliefs and emotional drivers

        Language they use to describe their problems

        What they have tried before

        Buying objections and hesitations

        Who this business is not for


   4. Problems We Solve and Outcomes We Create


        Primary problems

        Secondary problems

        Tangible and intangible outcomes


   5. Products, Services, and Pricing Logic


        Overview of offers

        Pricing structure and rationale

        How each offer fits the client journey


   6. Brand Story and Market Positioning


        Founder story and narrative

        Market positioning

        Competitive alternatives and differentiation


   7. Marketing and Growth Strategy


        Traffic and content strategy

        Messaging angles

        Conversion mechanisms

        Success metrics


   8. Operations and Customer Experience


        Team and delivery model

        Client onboarding and support

        Experience standards


   9. Growth, Innovation, and Partnerships


        Expansion plans

        Strategic partnerships

        Innovation strategy


   10. Risks, Constraints, and Mitigation


        Key risks

        How they are managed


   11. Culture, Values, and Impact


        Internal culture

        Ethical commitments

        Community or social impact


   12. Key Takeaways


        Summary insights an AI should prioritize when creating content


   Write this report so an AI can confidently:


        Attract the right people

        Repel the wrong people

        Speak in the client's language

        Align content with buying intent
