1.  **Feedback Request**

A few pointers:

1)  Make sure you are focusing on asking feedback from your ideal
    > clients, not just friends or family who want to do you a favor.

2)  Don't wait for people to say yes when you post... actively seek them
    > out and ask them for their opinion and feedback.

3)  Commit to doing whatever it takes to show your offer to 20 people
    > and ask for their feedback. Be relentless on getting feedback and
    > discerning about which feedback to take into consideration.
