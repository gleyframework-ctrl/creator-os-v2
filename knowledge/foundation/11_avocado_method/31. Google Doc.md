**[THE FUNNEL]{.underline}**

One of the big challenges with workshops is getting people to show up.

If you have 100 Registrations you are lucky to get 50% to show up live
and then from those who are live it is common to be able to book about
5-10% of those who attend onto a discovery call... which is 2-5 Booked
appointments.

But what about all the people who don't show up live? What about those
50 people?

So we decided to try something different and essentially put a booking
page right after the workshop registration page and we found that
10%-15% of registrants actually booked a call right then and there...
capturing both those who would attend live and those who would not
resulting in 10-15 booked appointments BEFORE the workshop even
happened.

And thus... the workshop that doesn't matter was born.

The funnel is super simple... It is 2 pages and a couple of emails.
