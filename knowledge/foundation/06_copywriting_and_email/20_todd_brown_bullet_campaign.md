## 1. CORE PHILOSOPHY

"More offers, more frequently" - without burning out your house file.

A Bullet Campaign is a highly formulaic, mostly automated email promotion that runs over a short period (typically 3-5 days) and can be repeated endlessly with different products.

---

## 2. THE 3 CORE PIECES

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Marketing/Sales Vehicle | Landing page, sales page, or video that presents the offer |
| 2-Step Email Sequence | First: generate interest (clicks). Second: convert interest into sales |
| Dynamic Deadline | Creates urgency with personalized countdown timer |

---

## 3. INTEREST (CLICK) SEQUENCE

### 3.1 Purpose

Identify who is interested by getting them to "raise their hand" (click a link).

Those who click get tagged as "interested" and moved to Offer Sequence.

### 3.2 Structure

| Element | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Duration | 3 days |
| Frequency | 2 emails per day (8:30 AM and 8:30 PM) |
| Style | Teaser / lift-note style - short, curiosity-driven |
| Goal | Generate clicks to Marketing/Sales Vehicle |
| Tagging | Clickers tagged as "interested" |

### 3.3 Interest Email Formula

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| Hook | Start with relatable problem, observation, or intriguing statement |
| Story | Briefly introduce expert, discovery, or new challenge |
| Tease | Hint at valuable information without giving away |
| CTA | Link to Marketing/Sales Vehicle |

## 4. OFFER (CONVERSION) SEQUENCE

### 4.1 Purpose

Turn interested prospects (clickers) into buyers.

### 4.2 Structure

| Day | Frequency | Focus |
|-----|-----------|-------|
| Day 1 | 2 emails (8:30 AM, 8:30 PM) | Offer details, benefits |
| Day 2 | 2 emails (8:30 AM, 8:30 PM) | Social proof, bonuses |
| Day 3 | 4 emails (8:30 AM, 1:30 PM, 6:30 PM, 11 PM) | Deadline urgency |

### 4.3 Offer Email Formula

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| Recap | Remind them of what they saw in interest phase |
| Problem | Re-emphasize the problem the offer solves |
| Solution | Present the offer - what's included, bonuses |
| Proof | Testimonials, case studies, results |
| Urgency | On final day, include countdown timers |

## 5. DYNAMIC DEADLINE

### 5.1 Purpose

Create artificial urgency that feels real and personal.

Each prospect gets their own countdown timer starting when they first visit the page.

### 5.2 How It Works

1. Prospect clicks interest email and lands on your page
2. Dynamic deadline tool drops a cookie and starts timer
3. Timer counts down (e.g., 3 days)
4. When timer expires, offer changes (price increases, bonuses disappear)

### 5.3 Tools

| Tool | Description |
|------|-------------|
| DeadlineFunnel.com | Integrates with pages and emails; triggers different offers on expiration |
| Countdown Hero | Simpler countdown timers for pages and emails |

### 5.4 Income Engine Application

- Use 3-day dynamic deadline with "beta pricing"
- When they first visit: "Your beta pricing expires in 2 days 14 hours"
- After deadline: Price reverts to $997 or page redirects to "doors closed"

## 7. ICE SCORING FOR PRIORITIZATION

Use ICE to rank product ideas.

### 7.1 The Criteria

| Criteria | Description | Scale |
|----------|-------------|-------|
| Impact | How much will this move the needle? | 1-10 |
| Confidence | How certain are you of the impact? | 1-10 |
| Ease | How easy/quick to create and launch? | 1-10 |

**Score = Impact × Confidence × Ease**

### 7.2 Example

| Idea | Impact | Confidence | Ease | Score |
|------|--------|------------|------|-------|
| VPS Setup mini-course | 8 | 9 | 7 | 504 |
| Postfix setting video | 7 | 8 | 9 | 504 |
| Domain audit checklist | 6 | 8 | 9 | 432 |

Pick highest score for first campaign.

## 8. IMPLEMENTATION GUIDE

### 8.1 Pre-Launch Planning

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| How many gap weeks in your calendar? | |
| When will you launch first campaign? | |

### 8.2 Step-by-Step

1. Map promotional calendar - identify gap weeks
2. Brainstorm 5-10 product ideas
3. ICE score and pick winner
4. Write Interest (Click) Sequence - 6 teaser emails over 3 days
5. Create Marketing/Sales Vehicle - page or video
6. Set up dynamic deadline tool
7. Write Offer (Conversion) Sequence - 8 emails over 3 days (2/2/4)
8. Segment list - exclude prior buyers, tag clickers
9. Launch and monitor
