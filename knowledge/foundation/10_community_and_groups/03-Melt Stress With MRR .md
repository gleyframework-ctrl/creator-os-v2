**Melting Stress With MRR**

The popularity of Monthly Recurring has taken off like wildfire the last
few months - partly because it was a big piece of what was taught at a
massive marketing event this year.

And partly because folks who primarily run are acquisition business are
getting burnt out at starting every month at zero

Now we've been bullish on recurring income streams for a few years &
have actively been prioritizing ways to build it every way we can.

So let\'s riff on **Melting Stress With MRR**

Giving you a snapshot of a training we ran for our core clients we ran
in August - like the ones we run every week

We\'ll break it up into 3 different buckets & things to make the most of
them

1.  Affiliate payment plans - post-purchase relationships are going to
    > be MASSIVE in this area - Naturally affiliate payment plans have
    > less retention - because there is usually not as deep of a
    > connection from buyer to product creator - Combat this with going
    > heavy on YOUR own support - ex - payment plan runs x month they
    > get x months of x from you personally

2.  Low Ticket Continuity/membership - need to be value packed with a
    > couple different nuances

    a.  It becomes something they actually need to operate their
        > business (think software/funnels - folks can\'t leave without
        > a huge hassle)

    b.  Elemental of \"game\" - something to keep folks
        > interacting/engulfed in the mentorship (think dripped
        > trainings, new fresh strat, prize or incentives)

3.  High ticket continuity - (Core) - usually need some type of
    > relationship or result prior to making this viable for someone to
    > join - tiny number of folks = big recurring revenue

You can do this in a couple of different ways:

1.  On the back end of an affiliate program - buy product X & gets X
    > amount of time in your back-end coaching offer/buyers group for
    > support with the goal of getting them to pay a monthly retainer
    > after time is up

2.  As a Standalone Offer - X amount of time in coaching offer or until
    > X is accomplished

**Continuity Structures / Components**

1.  **Higher Ticket - \$500-\$2,000 per month**

-   Support Chats - Group Messenger Chat - Slack Threads

-   Private Community For Personalized Feedback - Offer - Content &
    > conversation review

-   1 To 1 Support - Weekly - Monthly - Different Stages (kick off -
    > Middle - End)

-   Group Coaching Calls - Zoom (more personalized) + structured
    > training (will build a catalog of assets you have to pull on)

2.  **Lower Ticket - \$\$50 - 500 Per month**

-   Support Group - For Questions - Review / your overall support

-   Q & A structure - Weekly Lives where you dive into any questions
    > people are having

For a lower ticket - Less 1 to 1 / not as much tied to "your time"
because if you're pumping more volume into it you wanna make sure that
it's leveraged / doesn't require "more work" on your end.

EX - It doesn't matter who buys group juice - all the support & weekly
calls is something we're already doing.

VS letting people into our coaching - it's our time directly tied to
helping them individually

1.  **Feeding The Offer - High Ticket**

Gaining Clarity on the ascension process - where the main "feeder" for
your continuity program is going to come from

Example of "juice's" - Cash Curriculums \> Group Juice \> GJ Core
Accelerator

Pushing people through layers before they have access to your "coaching"
will ensure:

They're good candidates to work with you long-term / have what you're
looking for as a "ideal client"

Become even more "sold" on you & as a brand owner - Moving people into
back-end coaching who've already gotten X result with you based on
something you've already given them is Very Low Pressure

-   That you'll be able to help them get results with what you're
    > teaching relatively quickly - Making Having the stick with you for
    > a LONG time more likely / having some sort of "Requirements for
    > your coaching"

-   X Number of followers - X Number Of Group Members - X Amount Earned
    > Online

-   (will ensure you're filling your high-touch coaching program with
    > "quality people" that are who you'd actually like to be working
    > with / can help get results faster)

This model for many of you inside of here could look like:

Affiliate Sale On X \> Your Own Buyers Pool \> Ascension Either Post
Payment Plan Or Next Thing They Want To Solve

2.  **Feeding The Offer - Lower Ticket**

Now, you can be less strict with this if most of the stuff you're doing
inside of it is leveraged / won't require you to link your 1 on 1 time
to it

An effective way to do this:

-   Stack Your Medium Ticket Buyers into your "Support system"
    > continuity program on a trial basis

-   Sell Standalone spots for those who're not looking to get into
    > another High ticket program

This is what we\'re essentially going to be doing with skool - low
ticket monthly recurring subscription model - which will basically just
become a breeding ground for our strategy & concepts

Which will then have the opportunity to ascend to our other offers.

Filtering folks with a low ticket commitment & getting paid to make
offers / pre-sell as we\'re be diving behind the scenes of why we\'re
doing things

1.  **The Overlooked Power Of This**

Beyond the "Pay" the asset creation.

When you end up doing this over prolonged periods of time - you'll start
building a bigger pool of assets that you can sell..

You've been running calls on Topics for X number of weeks you can now:

-   Sell each training as an individual training that'll help someone
    > solve specific problems

-   Package it into a complete program (would advise doing it
    > intentionally if planning this)

-   Have a portfolio of assets that you can pull on / that will allow
    > you to step away from your business even further

-   Instead of having to individually answer questions over and over
    > again - you'll build a plethora of things that will allow you to
    > direct your client\'s need it
