## **Build Sheet #1: The Single Job**

Your community must have ONE primary job. Not support. Not value. Not
vibes.

**The Job:** Help people move away from one specific pain by watching
you run a process in real time.

If you can\'t answer what pain this group removes in one sentence,
everything else falls apart.

**The Pain Statement:**

Complete this sentence:

\"People join this group because they\'re struggling with
\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.\"

Be specific. Not \"growing their business.\" That\'s too vague.

Examples:

-   \"\...getting clients without sales calls\"

-   \"\...staying consistent with content\"

-   \"\...converting their group members into buyers\"

-   \"\...building an audience that actually engages\"

**The Process Statement:**

Complete this sentence:

\"Inside this group, they watch me
\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.\"

What are you documenting? What are you doing in real time that they get
to witness?

Examples:

-   \"\...build and sell offers using community instead of funnels\"

-   \"\...grow my audience using short-form video\"

-   \"\...run my business in 90 minutes a day\"

-   \"\...turn conversations into clients without pressure\"

**The Single Job Statement:**

Now combine them:

\"This group helps people who are struggling with
\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
by watching me
\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.\"

Write your complete statement here:

**The Gut Check:**

Read your statement out loud.

-   Does it name ONE specific pain? (Not three. Not \"and also.\")

-   Does it describe something you\'re actively DOING, not just
    > teaching?

-   Would someone know within 10 seconds if this group is for them?

If you answered \"no\" to any of these, simplify until it passes.

## **Build Sheet #2: The Welcome Post**

Every new member must instantly understand:

1.  Why this group exists

2.  What happens inside

3.  That you sell things

No surprises. No trust debt. This is alignment.

**Welcome Post Template:**

Use this as your starting framework. Customize the brackets.

**\[GREETING + WHO YOU ARE\]**

Hey, I\'m \[Name\].

Welcome to \[Group Name\].

If you\'re here, it\'s probably because you want to \[outcome they
want\] without \[obstacle they hate\].

You\'re in the right place.

**\[WHAT THIS GROUP IS\]**

This is where I document \[what you\'re doing/building/testing\] in real
time.

No fluff. No filler. Just what\'s working, what\'s not, and what I\'m
learning along the way.

You\'ll see \[type of content they can expect: wins, breakdowns,
behind-the-scenes, lessons, etc.\].

**\[HOW TO GET THE MOST OUT OF THIS GROUP\]**

1.  **Start here:** \[Point them to a specific pinned post or resource\]

2.  **Engage:** Don\'t lurk. Ask questions. Share where you\'re at. The
    > people who participate get the most out of this.

3.  **Watch the process:** Pay attention to what\'s happening inside the
    > group. The wins, the conversations, the progress. That\'s the real
    > value.

**\[THE HONESTY STATEMENT\]**

One thing you should know: I sell things.

When you\'re ready to go deeper, there are paid ways to work with me.
I\'ll mention them openly. No hidden pitches. No bait-and-switch.

If it\'s a fit, you\'ll know. If it\'s not, no pressure.

**\[CALL TO ACTION\]**

Drop a comment below and tell me:

-   Where are you at right now with \[topic\]?

-   What\'s the ONE thing you\'re trying to figure out?

Let\'s go.

## **Build Sheet #4: Journal-Style Content**

Your group content is not a feed. It\'s a living record of what you\'re
doing, testing, and learning.

Format doesn\'t matter. Frequency doesn\'t need to be perfect. Polish is
optional.

People buy because your journey feels real and current.

**Journal Content Categories:**

Check the types of content you\'ll post in your group:

☐ **What I\'m working on** --- Current projects, focus areas, things in
progress

☐ **What I\'m learning** --- Lessons, realizations, things that clicked

☐ **What\'s working** --- Wins, results, things worth repeating

☐ **What\'s not working** --- Honest breakdowns of failures, pivots,
changes

☐ **Behind the scenes** --- The unsexy stuff that actually moves the
needle

☐ **Questions I\'m asking myself** --- Thought process, decisions being
made

☐ **Client/student updates** --- Progress happening inside your world

**The Journal Mindset Shift:**

Old way: \"What content should I create to provide value?\"

New way: \"What am I doing right now that\'s worth documenting?\"

You\'re not creating content FOR them. You\'re documenting your process
and letting them watch.

**Journal Prompts to Use When You\'re Stuck:**

-   \"This week I\'m focused on\...\"

-   \"Something I realized recently\...\"

-   \"A mistake I made that you can avoid\...\"

-   \"Here\'s what happened when I\...\"

-   \"I used to think\... now I think\...\"

-   \"The question I\'m trying to answer right now is\...\"

-   \"Something I\'m testing\...\"

-   \"A conversation I had that changed how I see\...\"

**Your First Journal Post:**

Write a rough draft of something you could post today. Don\'t overthink
it. Just document something real.

**Content to STOP Creating:**

MVC dies when you add content that doesn\'t serve clarity, proof, or
conversation.

What type of content have you been posting that you should STOP?

☐ Vague \"value\" posts that don\'t connect to anything

☐ Content made just to \"post something\"

☐ Overly polished content that takes hours to create

☐ Multiple offers or CTAs in the same post

☐ Content that confuses rather than clarifies

What specifically will you stop posting?

## **Build Sheet #5: Conversation Triggers**

Your community\'s job is to surface questions and create hand-raisers.

You\'re not looking for engagement metrics. You\'re looking for buying
signals.

**The Trigger Types:**

These are the signals that someone is moving from watching to
considering:

**Direct Hand-Raises:**

-   \"How do I work with you?\"

-   \"What\'s the next step?\"

-   \"Do you have something for \[specific problem\]?\"

**Belief-Based Engagement:**

-   Consistent commenting on your posts

-   Referencing things you\'ve said before

-   Answering questions for other members

-   Sharing their own progress publicly

**Problem Statements:**

-   \"I\'m stuck on\...\"

-   \"I\'ve been struggling with\...\"

-   \"I can\'t figure out\...\"

**Future-Focused Questions:**

-   \"What would you recommend for someone who\...\"

-   \"How did you handle \[specific situation\]\...\"

-   \"What\'s the best way to\...\"

**Conversation Starters to Use:**

These are questions you can ask in posts that naturally surface
hand-raisers:

-   \"What\'s the ONE thing you\'re trying to figure out right now?\"

-   \"Where are you stuck?\"

-   \"What would change for you if you solved \[specific problem\]?\"

-   \"What have you tried that hasn\'t worked?\"

-   \"What\'s your biggest question about \[topic\]?\"

**Your Trigger Post:**

Write a post designed to surface questions and start conversations:

**The Bridge to DM:**

When someone engages with trigger language, you have context to reach
out.

The formula:

1.  Notice the signal (they commented, asked a question, showed repeated
    > engagement)

2.  Acknowledge what they shared

3.  Ask a follow-up question or offer to help

Example: \"Hey \[Name\], saw your comment about \[thing\]. That\'s a
common spot to get stuck. Mind if I ask what you\'ve tried so far?\"

That\'s it. No pitch. Just conversation with context.
