**Lesson Summary**

Great DMs don't feel like sales pitches --- they feel like real
conversations. In Week 3, you'll learn how to guide a chat from first
reply to final decision using a simple conversational flow. We'll walk
you through the Conversational Selling Map, show you how to escalate
desire using micro-stories, and teach you how to invite people into your
offers without pressure or awkwardness.\
\
You'll also get 5 golden rules for micro-conversations that sell,
including the art of asking better questions, using permission-based
CTAs, and matching the pace of the platform. If you want to sell through
DMs without ever sounding "salesy," start here.
