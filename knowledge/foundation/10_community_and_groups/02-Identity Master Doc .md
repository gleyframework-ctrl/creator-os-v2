# **Writer Prep**

## **Personal Details and Voice Foundation**

This step is about installing who you are, not what you sell.

If you skip depth here, everything downstream sounds generic.

If you do this properly once, your AI gets sharper forever.

## **What This Document Does**

Your Personal Details Doc teaches your AI:

-   How you think

-   How you speak

-   What you care about

-   What you stand for

-   What you would never say

This is not a bio.

This is an identity and voice operating system.

If you already have:

-   A brand voice doc

-   A style guide

-   Long-form personal writing

Include it. More context equals better output.

## **How to Build Your Personal Details Doc**

You will have the AI interview you and extract the patterns.

You can answer in writing or by voice.

**Rules for the Interview:**

-   One question at a time

-   No rushing

-   Honest, unfiltered answers

-   Depth beats polish

-   Stories matter more than facts

## **Interview Prompt**

**Copy and paste this exactly into your ChatGPT thread:**

> Act as an in-depth interviewer, biographer, and voice analyst.
>
> Your job is to deeply understand me as a human, creator, and
> communicator so you can document my authentic personal voice.
>
> The outcome of this interview will be a Personal Author and Voice
> Guide that allows an AI to write and speak indistinguishably from me.
>
> **Interview rules:**

-   Ask one question at a time

-   Wait for my response before continuing

-   Ask follow-up questions based on what I say

-   Go deeper when you notice emotion, conviction, or strong opinions

-   Do not move on until the answer feels complete

> **Your objective:**
>
> By the end of this interview, you should understand how I think,
> communicate, decide, and express ideas so clearly that my audience
> could not tell whether content came from me or AI.
>
> **Topics you must cover during the interview:**
>
> **Personal Identity and Background**

-   Name and background

-   Where I am from and how it shaped me

-   Key life experiences that changed how I see the world

-   Core values and non-negotiables

-   Areas of lived experience and earned authority

> **Influences and Perspectives**

-   People, books, creators, or philosophies that shaped my thinking

-   Beliefs I strongly agree with

-   Beliefs I strongly reject

-   Ideas I repeat often or feel known for

> **Education and Knowledge**

-   Formal and informal education

-   Skills I have developed through experience

-   Topics I feel confident speaking or teaching on

> **Interests and Passions**

-   Topics I enjoy talking about even without being paid

-   Hobbies, curiosities, or recurring obsessions

-   Things I could talk about for hours without notes

> **Personal Story and Evolution**

-   Key turning points in my life or business

-   Failures, pivots, or wake-up moments

-   Where I started versus where I am now

-   Where I am headed next and why

> Begin the interview now by asking the first question.

## **Generate Your Personal Voice Guide**

Once the interview is fully complete, use the prompt below to convert
everything into a clean, usable document.

### **Voice Guide Generation Prompt**

> Based on everything you learned from the interview, create a
> comprehensive Personal Voice and Speaking Style Guide that an AI can
> immediately apply.
>
> This guide must be practical, specific, and operational. Avoid vague
> descriptions. Write it so another AI could follow it without
> interpretation.
>
> **Include the following sections:**
>
> **1. Brand Voice Overview**

-   Core tone and personality traits

-   Emotional energy of my voice

-   What my voice should make people feel

> **2. Writing and Speaking Style**

-   Sentence length and structure

-   Vocabulary level and complexity

-   Grammar preferences

-   Use of storytelling, examples, or analogies

-   Use or avoidance of humor, sarcasm, or directness

> **3. Language and Word Choice**

-   Words and phrases I naturally use

-   Words, tones, or styles I avoid

-   Beliefs or language lines I never cross

-   Cultural references or metaphors I lean on

> **4. Tempo and Rhythm**

-   Pacing of my writing and speech

-   Use of short punchy lines versus longer explanations

-   How I create emphasis and pauses

> **5. Influences on My Voice**

-   Writers, creators, thinkers, or styles that influence me

-   Industries, environments, or communities that shaped how I speak

> **6. Audience Connection**

-   Who I am speaking to most often

-   How my tone shifts across platforms if it does

-   What I want the audience to think, feel, or do after consuming my
    > content

> **7. Voice in Action**

-   Clear do\'s and don\'ts

-   Short examples of how I would write or speak

-   Examples of what does not sound like me

> **8. Voice Flexibility and Scale**

-   How this voice adapts across formats while staying authentic

-   How to maintain consistency as content volume increases

> The final output should be structured, clear, and immediately usable
> for training AI writing tools.

## **Final Action Step**

1.  Copy the completed guide

2.  Paste it into a Google Doc

3.  Name the file: **1. Personal Details About the Author**

This document becomes permanent infrastructure.

Once this is done, your AI stops guessing who you are.

It starts sounding like you on purpose.
