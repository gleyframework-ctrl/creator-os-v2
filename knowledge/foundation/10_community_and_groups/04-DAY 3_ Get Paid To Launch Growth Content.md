# **Part 1: Launch & Promotional Strategies**

## **The Reality Check**

Creating a mini-course or workshop sounds cute.

But it\'s a full-blown sprint.

-   You\'re going to make more content than usual

-   You\'re going to be in conversations every day

-   You\'re going to feel like you\'re \"saying the same thing\" a
    > hundred different ways

**And that\'s the point.**

Most people wing this stuff and wonder why nobody buys.

If you want to make money from your offer, you need a plan.

This is that plan.

## **Before You Start Promoting**

**A few notes:**

-   Don\'t start deadline-heavy if you don\'t have a waitlist or lead
    > flow yet. Give yourself at least 21 days from when you start
    > getting traction --- not from your first post.

-   If you\'ve been offer-heavy lately (BUY, BUY, BUY), spend some time
    > giving value, giving things away, or focusing purely on getting
    > folks to your free group.

## **The 3-Phase Launch Flow**

### **PHASE 1: PRE-PROMOTION (Angle Finding Mode)**

**Goal:** Get attention. Build interest. Tease what\'s coming.

**Timeline:** Start 21+ days before your workshop/offer goes live.

Don\'t shout your offer from the rooftop yet.

Your job is to:

-   Show up more consistently

-   Talk about your story, your method, your \"why\"

-   Tease what\'s coming without giving it all away

**Use this time to:**

-   Build curiosity

-   Get people into your group

-   Identify hot angles that trigger engagement

**The 4 Content Buckets for Pre-Promo:**

1.  **Story** --- Share your journey (struggles, pivots, \"why\")

2.  **Value** --- Tips that helped YOU (what you wish you\'d known)

3.  **Results** --- Wins you\'ve had or helped others get

4.  **Behind the Scenes** --- What you\'re building and what\'s coming

### **PHASE 2: PROMO PART 1 (Days 1-10)**

**Goal:** Build momentum. Fill your group.

This is the warm-up round.

You\'re not selling the offer yet.

You\'re getting as many of the RIGHT people into your group as possible
while seeding the concept of your workshop.

**What Your Content Should Do:**

1.  Show the problem your workshop solves (make it hit)

2.  Introduce your process (tease what\'s inside)

3.  Prove you\'ve done it (or helped others do it)

4.  Invite people into your group with clear CTAs

**What to Post:**

-   \"Here\'s the exact 3-step plan I used to go from \[problem\] →
    > \[result\]\"

-   \"Most people mess this up\... here\'s how to avoid it\"

-   \"If you\'re stuck doing X, this is what I\'d do instead\...\"

-   \"I was sick of seeing \[problem\] so I\'m helping \[X people\] for
    > free\"

**2-Step Lead Gen Prompts:**

-   \"Who here has thought about \[thing related to your workshop\]?\"

-   \"I\'m dropping a training on \[benefit\] inside my group this week.
    > Who wants in?\"

-   \"Inside my group, I just dropped a guide on \[mini-win\]\"

-   \"Dropping part 1 of \[training name\] tomorrow in the group. You
    > in?\"

**Your CTA (everywhere):**

#1) \"Come hang in the group. I\'m breaking this all down in
real-time.\"

#2) " On (X Date) I'm hosting a free live workshop helping you (Remove
specific Pain). If you want a free seat comment (relevent word)"

#3) If you're sick and tired of (Insert a pain), I'm thinking about
hosting a free live event that will help you (insert result or outcome)
without (insert thing they don't like doing), would you be interested?\
\
Let me know by dropping a comment with (word)

**They cannot get access to what you\'re creating unless they join your
group.**

Make your group feel like THE PLACE to be.

Create FOMO without needing to say \"limited spots.\"

You\'re turning your group into a **pop-up hub** for this promo window.

### **PHASE 2.5: THE DROP (Deliver the Workshop)**

**Goal:** Deliver a real win while building belief and previewing your
offer.

Drop your workshop in 3-6 short lessons over 3-6 days.

Each lesson should:

-   Be short and tactical (5-60 mins max)

-   Solve 1 real problem

-   Build toward your paid offer

**Example Breakdown:**

-   Part 1: Choosing a high-impact problem

-   Part 2: Outlining a mini course that\'s fast to create

-   Part 3: Creating content that builds trust and sells

-   Part 4: Getting people into your group and starting convos

-   Part 5: What to do next if they want to monetize it

-   Part 6: Invitation to chat about your offer

**Delivery Options:**

-   Go live in the group

-   Post videos and tag members

-   Use polls, \"drop \[keyword\]\" prompts to boost engagement

**Every training should include this (at the end or during):**

-   \"If you want help with this part, let\'s talk. I\'m working with a
    > few folks to launch theirs this month.\"

-   \"If you wanna chat about XYZ, comment \[word\] and I\'ll message
    > you\"

**DO NOT HIDE THE FACT YOU HAVE AN OFFER.**

This keeps the vibe helpful, not pitchy, while setting up the next
phase.

### **PHASE 3: THE PUSH (FOMO, CTAs, and Conversions)**

**Goal:** Convert interest into action.

Now you turn the heat up.

**Your job:**

-   Make clear invites to your offer

-   Reinforce the value of your system

-   Answer objections before they ask

**What to Post:**

-   \"What I wish I had when I started (and what I\'m giving people
    > now)\"

-   \"How this one doc helped me make \$X without XYZ\"

-   \"3 reasons most people fail with \[thing\]\"

-   \"I\'m taking X people who want to \[outcome\] together over the
    > next \[timeframe\]\"

**Objection Handlers:**

-   **\"Don\'t have time?\"** --- Share how you did it while working
    > full-time / raising kids

-   **\"Not sure it\'ll work for me?\"** --- Share wins from others with
    > zero experience

-   **\"Money tight?\"** --- Break down the price. Hammer the cost of
    > NOT taking action

## **Example 21-Day Promotional Cycle**

### **Days 1-7: Dopamine Heavy / Attention Building**

-   2-Step lead gen content related to what you\'ll reveal

-   Your unique model --- what you do, how you do it

-   Promise #1 --- outcome they\'ll accomplish

-   Free training push --- what you\'ll reveal, where it\'ll help them
    > get to

-   Result post --- reinforce you know your shit

-   24-hour call out --- training is coming

**Anchor Post:** Live training, pre-recorded, or written breakdown.
Either offer-centric (what\'s inside, the game plan) OR value-driven
(the model you\'ll help them deploy).

### **Days 8-15: Content Loops + Training Refeed**

-   Promise #2 --- additional outcome you\'re helping with

-   Your unique process --- what you\'re doing, invite to learn or do it
    > together

-   Proof of concept --- what you\'ve accomplished or someone else has

-   30k ft overview of the plan/model

-   Why this is different from other things they\'ve tried

-   Objection handler (No time? Here\'s how I did X with a busy life)

-   Lead gen: \"Who missed the free training on XYZ?\"

### **Days 16-18: The Promo Pivot (More Aggressive)**

-   Promise #3 --- unique thing you\'re helping with, how you de-risk it

-   Objection handler: Financial --- make investment look smaller
    > (\"Instant access to the model that generated \[result\] for \$X
    > per day\")

-   Simplification --- what will they actually be doing?

-   Lead gen: Question related to outcome

-   Offer post: Looking for X folks that wanna do X together

-   Audience call out: Who this specifically helps

-   Why you wish you had this when starting

### **Last 72 Hours: Aggressive Push**

-   72 hours: \"72 hours left to have me help you do XYZ\"

-   48 hours: \"What\'s going down in 48 hours\"

-   36 hours: \"The end goal I\'m helping folks accomplish\"

-   24 hours: Color block + beef up comments

-   12 hours: \"Before X goes away\"

-   6 hours: \"Last chance to save \$X on X\"

-   Final call: \"60 minutes until this is gone\"

### **Close Down (Important for Future Launches)**

Publicly announce:

-   It\'s gone

-   Price increased

-   Spots filled

-   They can no longer grab it

**This builds real scarcity for next time.** People know you\'re not
faking it.

# **Part 2: Content To Community Framework**

## **Your Limitless Lead Magnet**

Your free community can become your lead magnet on steroids.

But in the beginning, it doesn\'t grow by itself.

If you want to grow your group, you need to fuel growth via content and
short-form videos.

## **Pick ONE Main Platform**

**If your group is on Facebook, your main platform should probably be
your FB profile.**

-   Where your focus is

-   Where you\'re optimizing content

-   Where you\'re getting reps and improving

**Example:** We repurpose the same videos across multiple platforms. But
unless you\'re coming into our groups --- we\'re not worried about the
other platforms.

**Our focal points for years:**

1.  Facebook profile

2.  TikTok

**Choose your singular focal point** for creating attention and driving
folks to your group.

**This could be:**

-   Facebook Profile/Reels → Facebook Group (amazing synergy)

-   TikTok → Facebook Group

-   Facebook Profile/Reels → Skool Group

-   TikTok → Skool Group

-   YouTube Shorts → Discord Group

-   Instagram → Facebook Group

Where you create content doesn\'t matter. What matters is where you
COMMIT to building consistently.

## **Strategically Driving Folks to Your Group**

**1. Create an Inside vs Outside Effect**

The more you talk publicly about what\'s happening INSIDE your group,
the more curiosity you create.

**2. Layer Value Behind Group Entry**

Sharing a live? Giving away a resource? Make them join your group to get
it.

-   **How most do this:** Content → Lead Magnet → Group (where they\'re
    > not active anyway)

-   **How WE do this:** Content → Group → Resource/Lead Magnet

**3. Your Group First**

Post or create something inside your community first. Then turn around
and talk about it publicly.

**4. Future Value**

\"I really want to do this thing for my audience\... but I\'m only
releasing it once my community hits X members.\"

**5. Playing the Angles**

Everything you share in your group can be positioned as \"free value\"
to drive people in.

## **The Flow**

****Your Social Media → Your Community → Your Sales Process

Your objective for public content: Drive people into your group where
they can consume, go deeper, and have you help them fix a problem
you\'ve overcome.

# **Part 4: The 4 Buckets of Content**

## **Speaking to Different Awareness Levels**

**Brand New to You (Not Problem Aware)**

-   Tell parts of your story and journey

-   Struggles you went through

-   What you did to make it over the hump

-   How you were thinking in the beginning and why

**Slightly Know You (Semi Problem Aware)**

-   Cover different topics that are relatable

-   Agitate the problems by describing them

-   What emotions do they trigger?

-   Talk about solutions based on your experience

-   Why would they want to fix this problem?

-   If they get it fixed, what changes?

**Know You (Fully Problem Aware)**

-   Start talking more about what you do

-   The plan you follow (or tried and failed)

-   Throw in free things that helped you

-   Aim to get convos going from engagement

**Fully Indoctrinated (Problem & Solution Aware)**

-   They know you and what you\'re about

-   Drive all the pains home

-   Then show them the green grass (your side)

-   What works for you and why

-   What have you removed that made a huge difference?

-   How are things different now that you have the solution?

## **The Content Buckets**

**1. Brand Growth**

-   Methodology

-   Philosophy

-   Beliefs

-   Personality

-   Results / client results

**2. Lead Generation**

-   External: TikTok, short-form, YouTube, profile posts

-   Internal: Inside community, reviving older convos, buyer pools

**3. Direct Offers**

-   Anchor posts (written or live) that lay out what\'s inside, who
    > it\'s for, where it helps them go

-   Use supplementary content to feed that anchor post

-   Go micro: break your offer into individual training pieces = weeks
    > of content

**4. Structure / BTS / Documentation**

-   How you create content

-   Where you create content

-   What your business looks like

-   What you have to do day-to-day

-   How you sell products

-   Real-time examples of sales you\'ve closed

**5. Value / Resources**

-   Give more

-   Resources

-   Tactics

-   Strategies you\'re implementing

-   Things that worked for you

-   Things that didn\'t work and what changed

## **The Anti-Plan**

Heavy on who the plan is NOT for.

**What this helps you do:**

-   Build a divide in your audience

-   Get people accustomed to what is and isn\'t in your offers

-   Highlight negatives/caveats of your thing (comes across as genuine)

-   Who your products aren\'t for

-   What they\'d need BEFORE working with you

**Example:** \"Ignore this if you\'re not making X, not doing X, don\'t
have X.\"

# **Part 5: Anti-Viral Short Form Videos**

## **Why Short Form Works**

Every platform has its own version of TikTok. Leverage it.

**But this isn\'t your average TikTok training.**

We\'re not telling you to copy viral videos and hop on trends.

We\'re all about **Anti-Viral Content** = Quality over massive
quantities.

While trends can work for followers, that doesn\'t mean more sales.

**Anti-Viral:** Creating content that isn\'t catered to just getting
views --- but getting your message across to the RIGHT people.

When you do go viral, it\'s for the right things. The leads you pull in
are people you\'d actually want to work with.

**Example:** GJ student Jill Anne had a single video grow her group by
2,000+ members --- and it was TARGETED content.

Our audiences are smaller than most on short-form platforms. But even
with minimal views, it comes out to about \$1 per follower per month
because the content is targeted. (less now because flip blew up on
tiktok)

## **Video Styles**

**Short Text Videos** Big bulky text the viewer reads. Can work in a
pinch but shouldn\'t be your whole strategy.

**Talking Videos** You dropping knowledge, sharing views, experiences,
lessons. Fewer views but AMAZING for connection.

**Trends** Good for quick initial growth. But don\'t make them your core
focus. Build YOUR brand, not a carbon copy.

**Stitches & Duets** Piggyback off viral videos. Spin them into your
own.

**Responses** Respond to people engaging with your content. Gets
exposure on both videos.

**Series Videos (Extremely Powerful)** Find a style/angle that works?
Double down. Turn it into a series you repeat.

## **The Short Form Template**

**🎬 Hook (0-3 seconds): NO DEAD SPACE**

Start with a pattern interrupt. Bold statement, question, or
controversial take.

**Examples:**

-   \"If you\'re still doing \[common mistake\], you\'re wasting your
    > time.\"

-   \"Nobody talks about this, but it\'s the real reason you\'re
    > stuck.\"

-   \"I tried \[popular strategy\] for 30 days\... here\'s what actually
    > happened.\"

**🎬 Value (3-15 seconds)**

Break down the problem or mistake. Tease the solution.

\"Most people think they need \[wrong approach\], but that\'s why
they\'re struggling with \[pain\]. Instead, here\'s what actually
works\...\"

**🎬 Solution (15-30 seconds)**

Give the actionable tip, strategy, or process. Keep it simple. Make it
relatable with a personal example.

\"Instead of \[ineffective strategy\], do this instead: \[step 1, step
2, step 3\]. This is exactly how I \[achieved result\] without
\[frustration\].\"

**🎬 CTA (30-60 seconds)**

Get them to comment, DM, or drive to your community.

**Examples:**

-   \"Try this and let me know how it goes.\"

-   \"If you want the full breakdown, I shared it in my group --- drop
    > \'me\' for access.\"

-   \"Save this video so you don\'t forget it.\"

-   \"Follow me for daily tips around \[goal\].\"

**Superman CTA:** \"If you want more videos like this, hit follow after
you comment your favorite part, and don\'t forget to save this video.
Comment \'Me\' if you want my free \[thing\] so you can \[result\].\"

## **Bonus Tweaks for Higher Reach**

-   Jump cuts / transitions (makes creation easier too)

-   Text on screen reinforcing key takeaways

-   Facial expressions, movement, ENERGY --- look like you want to be
    > there

## **The Biggest Thing With Short Form**

**TEST TEST TEST**

Different styles, lengths, variations.

Content that performs well for me might not for you.

**You have to find YOUR content.**

# **Part 6: Written Post Formats**

## **Long-Form Content Structure**

**Why Are You Posting Content?**

1.  **To build connection** --- without trust, they won\'t buy

2.  **To pre-sell** --- on your offers, beliefs, strategies

3.  **To plant seeds** --- the more content over time, the shorter sales
    > convos become

## **The Long-Form Framework**

1.  **Eye-catching Headline** \"Here\'s How I Made \$3k With a FB Post\"

2.  **Lead into Ongoing Problem** Talk about how it was when you dealt
    > with this. How you felt. What frustrated you. Get specific.

3.  **Your Backstory** Beginning of journey, things that didn\'t work,
    > failures, challenges, setbacks.

4.  **What Worked for You** What did you start seeing? What were you
    > missing? How did you find it?

5.  **Results and Benefits** What did you start experiencing? Growth,
    > mindset shifts, confidence, sales.

6.  **CTA** Specific call to action. Not just \"drop a #me\" --- make it
    > clear what they get.

**Better CTA:** \"If you want to see exactly how you can start
generating leads every day with less work and close more sales, drop a
#me below and I\'ll get you plugged in.\"

## **Short & Punchy Posts (Color Blocks)**

These get higher engagement but leads are usually colder.

**Templates:**

1️⃣ \"Thinking about putting together a free training on \[topic\]. Who
wants in?\"

2️⃣ \"If I gave you a step-by-step breakdown of how to \[result\] without
\[struggle\], would you use it?\"

3️⃣ \"I\'m pulling back the curtain on \[challenge you overcame\] which
helped me \[result\]. Want access?\"

4️⃣ \"I\'ve got something coming that will make \[challenge\] way easier.
Who wants early access?\"

5️⃣ \"No sales calls. No crazy funnels. Just a simple process that helped
me \[result\]. Want the details?\"

6️⃣ \"On a mission to help \[X people\] \[outcome\] without \[struggle\].
Are you in?\"

7️⃣ \"Been testing a new way to \[result\]. Should I break it down?\"

8️⃣ \"If I put together a \[cheat sheet/checklist/training\] to help you
\[goal\] faster, who wants it?\"

9️⃣ \"I just documented the exact process that took me from \[struggle\]
to \[result\]. Want the playbook?\"

🔟 \"Thinking about giving away my \[X-step strategy\] for
\[challenge\]. Should I drop it?\"

## **Comment Stacking**

You can go super short (ethical clickbait) then add context in the
comments.

**Example:**

-   Post: Short hook

-   First comment: Deeper explanation + CTA

This fuels engagement AND gives more context.
