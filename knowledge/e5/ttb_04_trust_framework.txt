CHUNK: The TRUST Framework — 5 Letters of a Profitable Personal Brand
CATEGORY: builder
SOURCE: TTB — TRUST Framework + TRUST Audit
---
"A profitable personal brand is your ability to build trust at scale." — Tom Noske. TRUST measures that ability in five components:
T — TARGETED AUTHORITY: You are the expert on one specific mission and niche. They listen because of your mission.
R — RELATABLE HUMANITY: Vulnerability, personal stories, emotional connection. They buy because of who you are.
U — UNSHAKABLE STORY: Your origin story, the iceberg under the surface. They connect because of your story.
S — STATUS-DRIVEN ADMIRATION: Achievements, wins, the fruits of your work. They admire because of your results.
T — TIMELESS SERVICE: Giving without expecting anything back, value for free. They trust because of your generosity.
---
HOW THE LETTERS WORK TOGETHER
Authority opens ears. Humanity opens wallets. Story opens hearts. Admiration raises your status. Service seals long-term trust. A brand that shows only status (S) looks arrogant. A brand that shows only struggle (R) is liked but never bought from. You need all five in balance.
---
THE TRUST AUDIT (monthly ritual)
Score yourself 1-5 on each letter. Find your single weakest letter. Build a 30-day plan that attacks ONLY that letter. Re-measure next month. Mamba's law: "If it can be measured, it can be managed. If it can be managed, it can be improved."
---
EXAMPLE: A coach with great client results (high S) but zero personal stories (low R) gets admired in the comments but no sales. The fix is not more results — it is more humanity.
---
DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION
Score yourself 1-5 on T, R, U, S, T right now. Which single letter is weakest — and what is one piece of content this week that would strengthen exactly that letter?
