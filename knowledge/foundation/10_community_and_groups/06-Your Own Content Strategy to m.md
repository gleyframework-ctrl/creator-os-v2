ABOUT THIS LESSON
Picking your non-negotiables based on what’s going on in your life & biz, the thing to note is that you can change/
pivot energy depending on the actual outcome you’re looking for / micro goal but as a whole, it comes down to a few
things
        Showing up for your audience / future audience publicly - Making it easy for people to:
              Know who you are, as an individual, a business owner
              Who do you help, and what types of problems do you help people solve
              What you don’t do / what kinds of things do you not enjoy/stand behind
              Understand what is inside your offers, where it is going to take them, what has it done for you, where it
              is going to help them go
Figure out Your OWN cadence that will allow you to show up more & consistently for LONGER periods of time (now
this doesn't mean you have to go hard every single day 24/7)
But figuring out a flow that you can maintain consistently for longer because the more you’re showing up &
consistently showing up for bigger pockets of time, the easier & easier sales will start to become

Whether this looks like:
       Going live a few times a week with shorter written content to fuel those lives
       Doing fewer lives & more written content
The ultimate goal is to show up in your content in some form or some fashion if you have the bandwidth - would aim
for 1 post a day in your community & 1 post on your Facebook profile.
The piece of content on your profile can be a snapshot of what you put inside your group while directing them to that
or prompting for engagement to get hand-raisers so you can move them over.
Example Of My Current Group Content Rotation:
         Monday - Live Training / Value
         Wednes - Result, My own or client
         Thursday - Lead gen/hand raiser to convo or group asset already created
         Friday - Long form content - educational, lifestyle, what’s going on in biz
Profile primarily is now reels mixed in with written content fueling the group growth & lifestyle content unless we’re
active in a promotion
I'll usually post a Group piece of content in the morning, then write a short lead gen style post on my profile getting

interested around what I put in my group + post a TikTok about the piece of content I just wrote & why someone
would need it, then repurpose it onto reels & story.
Now, we're not telling you that you need to go out there and start writing 2 pieces of content a day, but instead
implore you to look at your current situation and commit to a rotation.
Because your content is an accumulative game, one, or even a dozen pieces aren't going to change your life, the
power of it lies in doing more of it for longer periods of time.
What that could look like is:
       Monday - a long-form piece of content inside your community + short color post on profile talking about what
       you put over in your group
       Tuesday - Lifestyle content - picture - something about what you're doing that day
       Wed - Hand raiser content - profile or group - to generate conversations / create opportunities for group
       members (Need to be outcome driven to pop)
       Thursday - long-form piece of content - a story - belief shifting
       Friday - Value post / give away - lead magnet - live video - resource
       Saturday Weekly Group Welcome Post

Use your group content to speak to yourself 30, 60, 90 days ago & continue to evolve as you grow (how you can
always be the 'expert' and hel someone through what they're struggling with because you've been through it)
Use your profile to seed your community & your higher value assets in your world.
