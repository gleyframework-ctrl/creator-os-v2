# **Video 2: Setup and Prep**

## **How You Build the Master File That Powers All Future Content**

Before we create content, we need to create context.

The goal of this system is not to teach you prompts.

It is to leave you with a master file that explains your business so
clearly that any AI tool can create on brand content for you in seconds.

By the end of this process, you will have one clean document that
captures:

-   Your business

-   Your ideal client

-   Your language and tone

-   Your point of view

-   Your standards

-   Your boundaries

This becomes the input for everything else.

## **The Outcome We\'re Building Toward**

When this is done correctly, you will be able to:

-   Upload one file into any AI tool

-   Ask for content

-   And get output that already sounds like you

No re explaining. No fixing tone. No rewriting everything.

The AI is no longer guessing who you are or who you serve.

## **One Chat Rule**

You will do this entire process in one single chat.

One thread. One conversation. One growing context window.

Why this matters:

-   Every answer you give sharpens the next question.

-   Every document improves the next output.

-   The AI begins to understand patterns instead of instructions.

Starting new chats resets that intelligence.

## **One Document Rule**

Everything we build will live in one Google document.

This document becomes your master file.

Not a prompt library. Not a swipe file. A clean, structured explanation
of your business and voice.

Think of it like this:

This document teaches AI how to think like you.

And turning that into structured context.
