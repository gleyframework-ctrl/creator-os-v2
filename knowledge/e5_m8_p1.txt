SOURCE: E5 C.A.M.P. Masterclass — Module 8, Part 1 (Lessons 25-29: Scaling, Acquisition Aggression, Tracking & Metrics)
FRAMEWORK: CUSTOMER INVESTING & THE SCALING ARCHITECTURE
WHEN TO USE: When a member's funnel is profitable and they want to scale, when they are afraid to increase ad spend, when they want to spy on competitors, or when they need to understand the deep economics of customer acquisition.

--- TOOL 1: THE 3 LEVELS OF ACQUISITION AGGRESSION (Lesson 25) ---
LAW: Most entrepreneurs don't scale fast enough because they aren't aggressive enough. You must view marketing through the lens of "Customer Investing" (buying a customer asset).
LEVEL 1: "MOM & POP"
- Mindset: Needs to make a profit on every single front-end transaction.
- MAAC (Maximum Allowable Acquisition Cost): Limited by Profit Margin.
- Result: Slow growth, easily outspent by competitors.
LEVEL 2: BREAKEVEN
- Mindset: Front-end marketing just recoups ad spend. Profit is made on the backend.
- MAAC: Limited by Front-End Average Order Value (AOV).
- Result: Faster growth, acquires customers for "free."
LEVEL 3: CUSTOMER INVESTOR (The Big Leagues)
- Mindset: "Going Negative." Willing to lose money on the front-end to acquire a customer because they know the Lifetime Value (LTV) and have cash reserves.
- MAAC: Determined by LTV + Cash Reserves + Breakeven Speed.
- Result: Market domination. You can outbid everyone because you are buying an asset, not just a sale.

--- TOOL 2: THE B.A.C.O. SCALING PROTOCOL (Lesson 28) ---
WHEN TO USE: When a campaign is profitable and hitting MAAC, and the member asks "How do I scale this without breaking it?"
SCALE IN THIS EXACT ORDER:
1. BUDGET: With an existing channel, audience, and offer, double the budget every 3 days. Monitor ROI until it maxes out.
2. AUDIENCES: Use competitive analysis to find new targets, interests, and lookalikes within the existing channel. Go beyond the obvious.
3. CHANNELS: Take the winning audience and offer to new platforms (e.g., from Facebook to YouTube, Native, or Display).
4. OFFERS: ONLY after maxing Budget, Audiences, and Channels, introduce a completely new offer/funnel following the B.A.C.O. protocol.
COMMON MISTAKE: Jumping to Step 4 (creating a new offer) when the current offer hasn't maxed out its Budget and Audiences yet.

--- TOOL 3: THE 3 LEVELS OF METRICS & BENCHMARKS (Lessons 27 & 29) ---
LAW: What doesn't get measured can't be managed. Track each campaign by each traffic source by each target.
LEVEL 1: CAMPAIGN ACTIVITY: Total Visitors, Total Leads, Total Customers.
LEVEL 2: CAMPAIGN PERFORMANCE (The Benchmarks):
- Opt-in Conversion Rate: > 20%
- Sales Conversion Rate: > 1% (or 1.5% for cold traffic)
- Order Form Completion Rate: > 65%
- Bump Offer Conversion Rate: > 20%
- Upsell Conversion Rate: > 15%
LEVEL 3: CAMPAIGN PRODUCTION: Cost Per Visitor, Revenue Per Visitor, Average Transaction Value.
THE 12 KEY DIRECT RESPONSE METRICS: Must track Average Lead Value and Average Customer Value at Day 14, 30, 45, 60, and 90 to accurately calculate LTV.

--- TOOL 4: COMPETITIVE INTELLIGENCE ARSENAL (Lesson 26) ---
WHEN TO USE: To spy on competitors, find new audiences, and reverse-engineer their funnels.
- Domain/Tech: Whois.net (age), BuiltWith.com (tech stack), WhatWPTheme.
- History: Wayback Machine (archive.org) to see their old offers and pivots.
- Traffic/Demographics: SimilarWeb.com, Alexa.
- Ad Spying: SpyFu.com (Google Ads), AdBeat.com (Display/Banner), Moat.com, Facebook Darkpost Engine.
- Funnel Pages: Google search `site:domain.com` to find hidden upsell and thank you pages.

DECISION RULES FOR THE COACH:
1. Member is terrified to increase Facebook ad budget despite a winning campaign? Diagnose: Stuck at Level 1 (Mom & Pop). Force them to calculate their 90-day LTV and upgrade to Level 3 (Customer Investor). Tell them to "Go Negative" on the front end.
2. Member wants to build a brand new funnel because the current one "isn't scaling"? Stop them. Enforce the B.A.C.O. protocol. Ask: "Have you doubled the budget every 3 days? Have you exhausted all audiences on this channel?"
3. Member's Order Form completion is <65%? Diagnose severe checkout friction (refer back to Module 5 MVF rules).
