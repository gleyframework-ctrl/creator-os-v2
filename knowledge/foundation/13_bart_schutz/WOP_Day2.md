Welcome to our 2nd Deep Dive! In the 1st Deep Dive Day we explored system 2; the lazy rational
controller of our behavior. In this second Deep Dive we’ll start exploring that other system in our
brain: our emotional and intuitive ‘system 1’. It’ll be an introduction into the power and characteristics
of our subconscious brain. Based on this introduction, we’ll explore system 1 (and how it is now and
then controlled by system 2) in the remaining 3 Deep Dives.

Our aim for this second day is to learn you:
1. A few ‘dual process’ theories
2. How we tend to underestimate the influence of system 1 on our decision-making
3. The most important characteristics of system 1
4. Embodied Cognition
5. How these theories and insights are used to make your online dialogue more persuasive
