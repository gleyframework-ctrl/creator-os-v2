SOURCE: E5 C.A.M.P. Masterclass — Module 7 (Upsell Sequence Worksheet)
FRAMEWORK: THE PERFECT TRANSACTION MAXIMIZATION SEQUENCE (The 4-Step Upsell Stack)
WHEN TO USE: When a member is building their checkout flow and needs to know exactly what offers to present, in what order, and at what price points to maximize Average Order Value (AOV) without causing buyer's remorse.

THE 4-STEP MASTER UPSELL STACK:
1. REVERSE ADD-ON (The "Try Before You Buy" Continuity Offer)
   - Price Architecture: 60-80% of the initial front-end price.
   - Mechanic: "Pay nothing for 14 days" (Free Trial into continuity/subscription).
   - Purpose: Extremely low barrier to entry immediately after the initial purchase dopamine hit.
2. SOFT-OFFER ADD-ON (The Speed/Ease Upgrade)
   - Price Architecture: 2x to 3x of the initial front-end price.
   - Purpose: Offers a way to get results faster or with less effort (e.g., "Done-for-you" templates, advanced coaching, implementation shortcuts).
3. MID-TIER ADD-ON (The High-Value Masterclass/System)
   - Price Architecture: Example: 10x of the initial front-end price.
   - Purpose: Captures the "Hyper-Buyer" (the top 1-5% of buyers) who wants the complete ecosystem, mastermind, or advanced implementation system.
4. THANK YOU PAGE GIFT OFFER (The Continuity Anchor)
   - Price Architecture: Continuity with Free Trial.
   - Purpose: Captures the buyer into a long-term community or software subscription after the one-time transaction sequence is complete.

CORE OFFER ANGLES (What to sell in the stack):
- Increase speed of results
- Make using the product more enjoyable
- Make getting results easier
- Automate the work required
- Reduce risk of error
- Equip them for the next step on their journey

DECISION RULE FOR THE COACH:
If a member only has one product and no checkout upsells, they are leaving 70% of their revenue on the table and making customer acquisition too expensive. Force them to build at least a 2-step stack (e.g., Front End + Reverse Add-On). Ask: "What is the logical next step your customer needs immediately after solving their first problem?"
