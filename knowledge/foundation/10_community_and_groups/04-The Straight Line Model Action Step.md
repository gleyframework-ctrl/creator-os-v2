This session isn't about adding anything new.

It's about seeing your business clearly & the moving parts for what they
are

Most businesses feel chaotic because they were built in pieces.

Offers here. Content there. Community, doing its own thing.

Nothing clearly feeds the next step.

The Straight Line Model exists to answer one question:

Is this creating leverage, or is it just work?

🔴 Lesson #3 Action

Before moving on, do this 👇

Grab a piece of paper, whiteboard, or notes app, and mind-map your
business as it exists right now.

Boxes. Arrows. Messy is fine.

Then take a picture and post it in the comments.

PS: For a simple visual, almost every online business is bucketed into 3
parts

1\) Attention - Where are you getting it, how are you getting it

2\) Nurture - How are you creating depth in your audience? What do you
have for them?

3\) Conversions - How are you \"selling\" - What\'s the process you use
to convert sales
