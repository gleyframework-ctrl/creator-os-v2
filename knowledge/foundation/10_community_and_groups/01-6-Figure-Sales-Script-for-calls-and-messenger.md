The 5 Rules
       Record the call every time.

       Make sure you have a notebook and a pen.

       Silence all of your devices and notifications.

       Completely detach from the yes and the sale. If you need the sale you will not get it.

       Be sure to dial the minute the clock hits the time agreed on


More on the Rules
We know that a doctor's job is to diagnose the problem. Prescribing a medication without
understanding what the problem really is would be malpractice.

The price of your offer must be absolutely anchored To the cost of missed opportunity.Don't
even bring up the price until you fully understand their problem As well as the cost of not fixing
their problem. ThenYou can tie your price to the price of them not doing nothing.

Folks should be coming to you because they don't know how to do something by
themselves.Something that you know exactly how to do. This puts the ball in your court. The
cost of them doing business with you is way lower than the cost of them doing it alone. They
don't want to waste their time Effort and money If you can shortcut the entire process.

Objection: I dont have the money

Response: Isn't that why you jumped on this call? Because you don't have the money that you

Dig into this right here. It's not you being asshole, But it's you simply highlighting the
