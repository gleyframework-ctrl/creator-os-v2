You just learned that only 3 types of content belong:

1.  Orientation (here\'s what you\'re in)

3.  Bridge (here\'s what comes next)

Orientation and Bridge are straightforward. You built those in previous

They\'re not the same thing.

This section helps you:

-   Find your actual Quick Win (the specific insight that earns trust)

-   Test whether it\'s a real quick win or just content

-   Identify and kill the noise in your current group

## **Part 1: Value vs. Quick Win**

Most people think they\'re delivering quick wins when they\'re actually
just posting content.

Here\'s the difference:

**\"Value\" Content:**

-   Helpful information

-   Something they could find elsewhere

-   Makes them think \"that\'s interesting\"

-   They save it and never come back

-   Doesn\'t change how they FEEL about their problem

**Quick Win Content:**

-   Creates immediate relief

-   Changes how they see the problem

-   Makes them think \"damn, that actually helped\"

-   They remember who gave it to them

-   Builds trust because something SHIFTED

**The Test:**

After someone consumes your content, do they feel:

*\"That was helpful\"* → That\'s value. Not enough.

*\"That changed something for me\"* → That\'s a quick win. That\'s what
builds trust.

**The Core Difference:**

Value = Information transfer

Quick Win = State change

You\'re not trying to teach them. You\'re trying to create relief.

## **Part 2: Finding Your Quick Win**

Your Quick Win is the ONE insight, shift, or fix that creates the
fastest relief for someone in your world.

It\'s not your whole framework. It\'s not everything you know.

It\'s the thing that makes them think: *\"Oh\... THAT\'S why this
hasn\'t been working.\"*

**Question 1: What do you find yourself saying to almost everyone?**

The thing you repeat because everyone needs to hear it. The correction
you make constantly.

**Question 2: What misconception do people have when they first enter
your world?**

What do they believe that\'s actually keeping them stuck?

**Question 3: What\'s the smallest shift that creates the biggest
relief?**

Not a full system. Not a 10-step process. One thing that immediately
changes how they feel about the problem.

**Question 4: When someone thanks you, what specifically helped?**

Think about DMs, comments, testimonials. What do people actually
reference when they say you helped them?

**Question 5: What do people try that doesn\'t work --- that you can
quickly correct?**

The common mistake that keeps them spinning. The thing they\'re doing
wrong that you can fix in one conversation.

## **Part 4: The Quick Win Test**

Not every insight is a quick win. Test yours.

**Speed Test:**

Can someone \"get\" this in under 5 minutes?

☐ Yes --- it clicks fast

☐ No --- it requires too much explanation

If no: Simplify. Quick wins are fast by definition.

**Relief Test:**

Does this create an immediate feeling of relief (not just
understanding)?

☐ Yes --- they feel lighter, clearer, or less stuck

☐ No --- they understand but nothing shifts emotionally

If no: You\'re teaching, not relieving. Find the emotional shift.

**Memory Test:**

Will they remember who gave them this insight?

☐ Yes --- it\'s distinctive enough to stick

☐ No --- it\'s generic enough that anyone could have said it

If no: Make it more specific to your perspective.

**Action Test:**

Can they do something different TODAY because of this?

☐ Yes --- there\'s a clear next move

☐ No --- it\'s just a new way of thinking

If no: Add the \"so what\" --- what do they DO with this insight?

**Results:**

If you checked YES to all four: You have a real Quick Win.

If you have any NO answers: Revise until it passes all four tests.

## **Part 6: The Content Kill List**

Your group probably has content that doesn\'t belong.

Anything that isn\'t Orientation, Quick Win, or Bridge is noise.

Time to identify and kill it.

**Noise Indicators:**

Check any that exist in your group:

☐ Content you made just to \"post something\"

☐ Long tutorials that overwhelm more than help

☐ Multiple pieces on the same topic (redundancy)

☐ \"Value\" that doesn\'t connect to a specific outcome

☐ Old content that doesn\'t match your current direction

☐ Resources people save but never use

☐ A full \"course\" dumped in the classroom

☐ Content that makes people ask \"where do I start?\"

**The Kill Question:**

For each piece of content in your group, ask:

*\"Does this help someone get a win faster or move closer to the next
step?\"*

If YES → Keep

If NO → Kill or archive

If UNSURE → Ask \"what will someone DO with this?\" If you can\'t answer
clearly, kill it.

## **Part 8: The Ongoing Filter**

Use this before adding ANY content to your group going forward.

**Before You Post, Ask:**

1.  What type of content is this? ☐ Orientation ☐ Quick Win ☐ Bridge ☐
    > Proof ☐ Journal

2.  Does this help someone get a win or move to the next step? ☐ Yes ☐
    > No

3.  Will this create relief or just information? ☐ Relief ☐ Information

**If you can\'t clearly answer these: Don\'t post.**

**The Mantra:**

Simple beats impressive.

Linear beats layered.

Paths convert. Libraries don\'t.
