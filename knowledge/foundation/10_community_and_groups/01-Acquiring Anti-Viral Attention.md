ABOUT THIS LESSON
There is no denying it - Short Form Video Content is hot - Every platform has rolled out its own version of TikTok
basically - leverage it while you can!
Now, this isn’t going to be your average TikTok / short-form video training - where we’re going to tell you to copy viral
videos & hop on trends.
While yes - these can work great for “Followers” that doesn’t mean more sales or a direct correlation to the amount
you make.
As the name implies - Anti Viral - the act of creating content that isn’t catered to just getting more views but instead
getting your message across.
That way when you do go viral (because yes it can still happen) It’s for the right things & the leads & audience that
you’re pulling in is people you’d actually want to be working with
An example of this would be a GJ student Jill Anne - Who had a single video grow her group by over 2,000 members -
and it was a TARGETED Piece of content.
If you look at both of our Audiences combined they’re smaller than most (especially on short-form platforms, but

even when we’re averaging minimal views - it would come out to about $1.00 Per follower each month because the
content is targeted)
