Most people don\'t fail because they don\'t post enough.

They fail because they build a container without a job. Add features
before flow. Confuse activity with momentum.

This lesson is about installing a system that sells through your group..

Your goal is not to build a \"great group.\" Your goal is to build a
working environment.

Minimum Viable means:

Just enough structure to create clarity. Just enough visibility for
proof. Just enough friction to filter buyers. Just enough consistency to
start conversations.

Anything else comes later.

If you overbuild here, you stall. If you keep this lean, momentum starts
immediately.

The lesson breaks down the 6 non-negotiables every profitable community
needs before anything scales.
