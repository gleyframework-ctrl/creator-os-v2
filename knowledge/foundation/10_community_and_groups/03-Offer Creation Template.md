### [Offer Doc Flow]{.underline} 

If you can cover these 14 building blocks your offer doc/sales letter
will bring more profits into your business

1.  **The Subheadline** - This targets the very person that needs your
    > offer and grabs their attention

2.  **Headline** - This is the tone setter for the rest of your copy,
    > its only job is to get the reader to keep reading

3.  **Stacking the deck -** This should reinforce your idea from 1 & 2
    > and intrigue the reader even further

4.  **Leadoff** - This will break down the bulk of the criteria of who
    > this offer is for and whats really in it for them (outcome,
    > results or desire)

5.  **The Body** - Carries the hard hitting weight of the entire
    > offer/letter which will carry on to the elements below

6.  **Subheadline reinforcement** - Small and short breakdowns
    > separating other elements or components of you offer (spicy and
    > specific copy)

7.  **Readers Rapport** - This section should demonstrate that you know
    > the reader by describing their pain and problem and the
    > frustration that keeps it company

8.  **Bullet points** - These should be short and punchy statements,
    > preferably things you know they want. Examples:: type of results,
    > freedoms, clarity, productivity, certainty or anything that is
    > directly desired and can be accomplished with your offer

9.  **Credibility** - Briefly explain why/how this will work for them or
    > why they should listen to you. This can also be client results or
    > your own results

10. **Testimonials** - Perfect time to anchor a specific 'proof of
    > concept' via a real client or customer result that validates your
    > solution (offer) does what you say it does

11. **Value & Justification** - This section should highlight the true
    > value of the offer while easily dwarfing the initial investment

12. **Reverse the Risk -** Aim to remove the biggest obstacle/objection
    > to making the sale, which usually money or fear

13. **Bonuses** - This is where you mention your unexpected gift that
    > enhances the shit out of your offer overall. (get creative here)

14. **Explicit and Specific CTA** - where you are basically asking the
    > sale and tell them what you would like them to do, do not leave it
    > up to them to guess their next step. Make it easy for them the
    > make a decision
