ABOUT THIS LESSON
Leverage, the key element that will take you from consistently doing a couple of thousand dollars a month to 20k,
30k, 100k+ now naturally most look at leverage as “hiring out”
while there can be a time and a place for that, most aren’t at that stage, so instead we’ll arm you with the concept of
virtually cloning yourself.
Now as most of you know we’re HUGE on the relationship piece, not only prior to sale, but post purchase as well
because this will ultimately lead to deeper relationships, longer clients & ascension on the tail end of whatever your
current offers are.
What should be included in your community to create leverage? To be typical.. Everything.
The more that you’re able to cover inside of your content inside of your group or on the world wide web, it will give
you the ability to build relationships with dozens, hundreds or even thousands of people all at once.
Reinforcing what we talk about prior, when you nail the content dynamic, you ultimately remove ANY need to jump on
a call with someone before you ever even have a conversations with them about buying your product.
Tactics behind what you should cover:
        Who you are, where you’re at now
        What you’re currently doing & your goals
        Strategies you’ve learned & things you’ve accomplished
        Portions of offers you have to sell
        Client wins, the community wins, your own wins
        Failures & things you’ve not had the best of luck at
        Morals, beliefs, ethics, things you stand for and don’t stand for in your industry
        Burning problems or questions you see being asked over and over again that you can answer
Best deliver format: some form of video content in your group, because it can basically become a mini micro
masterclass where people can come in & get up to speed with an offer that you have.
ANSWER all of the questions that someone would have to feel confident in making a buying decision & let those live
there inside of your community in your guides section where that’s the FIRST thing you’re trying to get them into.
