ABOUT THIS LESSON
Reels has opened up an insane new dynamic to growing your presence on Facebook that wasn’t available before.
You can now leverage these short-form videos, to grow your FB profile, Your Group & just about everything else
you’re doing with your organic FB system.
It also gives you the ability to reach a completely different audience, for most of us we’re playing in the same space,

Group, and are active in very similar pools. Reels will reach a completely different demographic.
There is a beautiful synergy with leveraging Facebook reels because you’re able to have the attention of short-form
video, the culture of a community & intimacy of messaging all in one place.
How To Post Them:
Easiest to post them from mobile - have seen features how you can post them from desktop as well too


Ifyou’re already creating TikToks, this is a great way to get another place of exposure to your brand and your
content. If you’re already created a bunch of Tiktok Videos - I would Recommend Going Through ALL OF THEM.
Downloading them & Uploading All Of Them to FB reels.
(when the feature launched I uploaded 150+ videos the first day)
Downloading Tiktok Videos -
You’re going to want to remove the watermark you can use Snaptik
For Android, they do have an app - https://play.google.com/store/apps/detailsid=com.video.downloader.snapx
But if you’re using iPhone, I’ve heard that you have to download from their website -
https://snaptik.app/en
Even If you have videos that don’t perform well on Tiktok - They Can Still Perform extremely well on FB.
We’ve had videos that got minimal views or interaction on tiktok that got millions of views & hundreds of community
members by using them.

What To Expect
It’s not as instantaneous as TIktok, you usually won't start going viral right away. The videos seem to get picked up
overtime & kind of snowball (especially if you can get people commenting on them)
Highly Recommend promoting for comments
You will likely get a TON of trolls, especially in the MMO space (have had a massive influx of people who engage &
troll your content, But that also helps it get more reach & has allowed me to connect with people who’re not as
closed-minded)

Keep in mind
Utilizing reels to grow your profile reach, Exposure to your content & your group growth can work really well, but the
audience is usually going to be cold / very cold.
You will nurture them more in comparison to people you’re adding from groups & other places like that because
some of them won’t even be aware of Affiliate marketing or what you’re doing.
Push them into your Group & tag them on relevant content/training get them into your ecosystem.
Would Recommend creating barebones training inside your group /The fundamentals to affiliate marketing so you
have dedicated training to tag your cold audience on / the new people that are coming in that aren’t a problem or
solution aware

Your Triple Thread CTA (This WILL EXPLODE YOUR COMMUNITY)
As you’re posting your reels come back through into the comment section & add an additional cta inside of the
comments about something that is inside of your group & what they’ll get out of joining your community
2 Different options for comment CTA:


      What their going to get from joining the community (Grab a free training breaking down how i turned a 60
      second tiktok video into a $1,000 day here - *insert group link*)
      Similar CTA but instead of using your group link you can tag your group with @group name
