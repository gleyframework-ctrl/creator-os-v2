ABOUT THIS LESSON

Building Your Foundation
The Foundations: We really only need a few things at the end of the day

      Attention - Simple and effective way to get eyeballs on you
      Hub/Ecosystem - The safe place to get more from you
      Demonstration - Your mechanisms that showcase your super powers
      Conversions - The most efficient way for your to get customers & clients

Attention is its own little beast nowadays because the attention span is so short.

You don't have to use 12 different platforms for the attention, it can be just one that you hammer consistently
We just need the right attention and a simple way to get them deeper into our world
You can shake your ass or lick your lips, but we prefer valuable & punchy info nuggets for our short form content &
written content lol
Get creative and have some fun as if you are trying to find as many humans like you
       Facebook - profile posts, stories, reels, FB group
       Instagram - carousels, canva photos, infographics, IG stories, IG reels
       Youtube - Long form videos or YT shorts or both
       Tiktok - 5 second to 10 minute videos & TT stories
       Lots of options other than these
Just pick 1 or maybe 2 and go hard on them to get attention and eyeballs that are pointed towards your H&E

The hub and ecosystem can be many different things these days as evolution takes place, but ya
need some kind of community for your most loyal fans (Both Free & Paid)
       FB Group
       Slack
       Whatsapp
       Skool Community
       GHL
       FB messenger chat
Just a place where you can communicate and share more knowledge and value
Here is where they can binge on your trainings and content til they get their fill and are ready to come to chat with
you for one the actual offers
If done correctly your free trainings can presell your paid stuff and position you as the go to source for more info on

the offers etc
Here is where you should have some bomb ass trainings that folks can consume and get some results before they
ever buy anything from you - Over deliver here and you will have more qualified folks reaching out asking about your
paid offers

The Demonstration piece can also be simple

If you dont have a video breaking down all the pieces of how you operate, you definitely need one
We have the 60 minute video (CCS) that lives in a few places - Can loop back to or send to folks at anytime
In it we/you should cover:
        You and who you are as a person
        Your results, awards, accomplishments (both non-monetary and monetary)
        Your strategy and plan of how you have gotten the above results as well as results of those you’ve helped
        Any moving parts in your offer that folks will be and have used to get specific results around the problem(s)
        you solve
        Feel free to show the inside of the offer aka pull the curtain back and give a detailed sneak peek
        What we really wanna do here is walk them through what it looks like to work with you and how the info will
        help them - result based + the plan that leads to it aka what they will actually be doing, work involved etc
        Sprinkling in any symptoms or pain points you know that they have and you can help solve or eliminate
        Include a story of your own or a story of student/client success (Started at XYZ and ended at ABC with your
        mechanism)
        In closing, be sure to make it clear of how they can get more info on the offer or where they can go to chat a
        bit more to you
        Just a super solid and specific CTA
Once this is recorded it can live in your group, YT channel, or your email sequence. Anytime someone wants to learn
more about your offer and what it looks like then you can send them this video to get up to speed as it presells on all
of you and your offer
This is good for them and you because it does the heavy lifting for you, frees ya up, and educates and informs them
to a point of being ready or needing just a few questions answered

Now the last part that everyone loves - The conversion or making the sale or getting paid,
whatever ya wanna call it
I shouldnt have to say this but I will, funnels work, zoom calls work, VSLs work, PDFs & G-Docs work….they all work
You just need to have a way to get paid and onboard customers and clients
We love the PDFs & G-Doc way because we dont have to get on calls and repeat ourselves answering or covering the
same things over and over - if calls are your jam and fuck yea, roll with it.
After your potentional customer/client has went through the attention phase, the hub & ecosystem phase and then
the Demonstration phase then they are completely up to speed with who you are and what you do and how you do it.
Those that have convos with you after being through this are easier to talk to and are way higher on the buying
spectrum and do NOT need convincing or hard selling
This is where the messages that say “send me the link” or “how do I pay you” come from
Stripe is pretty easy to set up payment links and invoices and so is paypal, zelle or even venmo
You can embed them on your funnels, PDFs, and G-Docs
You can also send the links directly in emails or messages both FB and Text
Close the sale and the real work begins for you and the person you sold to
Now we help them get results and get things in place to repeat the wins over and over annnnnd thennnnnnnnnn you
use all of this good shit to start back at the Attention phase - sharing all thats happening in the business - this fuels
allllllll of your content
This is the cycle of simplicity
