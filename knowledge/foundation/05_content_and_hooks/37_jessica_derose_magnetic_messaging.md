## DAY 1: PSYCHOLOGY BEHIND MAGNETIC WORDS

### 1.1 Character Traits to Magnify

List the character traits you want in your ideal dream client.

**Examples:**
- Ambitious but overwhelmed
- Values-driven but profit-focused
- Creative but lacks systems
- Committed but needs guidance
- Action-taker but needs direction
- Open-minded but skeptical

**Your Task:** List 5-10 character traits.

### 1.2 Core Desires vs Surface Wants

| Surface Want | Core Desire |
|--------------|-------------|
| More clients | Security and confidence |
| More money | Freedom and proof of ability |
| Better funnel | Trust that business runs without them |
| More time | Control over their life |
| More leads | Validation that their offer works |
| Less stress | Peace of mind |

**Your Task:** Write your dream client's actual core desires.

### 1.3 The Peel the Onion Framework

| Layer | Question | Example |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Knows | What do they KNOW? | "I need more leads" |
| Doesn't Know | What don't they know? | "Their lead magnet is the problem" |
| Doesn't Know They Don't Know | Blind spots | "Their messaging attracts wrong people" |

## DAY 2: 3 TYPES OF CONTENT THAT MOVE PEOPLE

### 2.1 Educational Content

**Purpose:** Showcase expertise, build trust, position as authority.

**Examples:**
- "Here's how to audit your funnel in 10 minutes"
- "The 3 biggest mistakes in email sequences"
- "Why your offers aren't converting"

### 2.2 Storytelling Content

**Purpose:** Create emotional connection, relatability.

**3 Types:**

| Type | Purpose | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| Your Story | Why you started, struggles, breakthroughs | "I was $50k in debt before I figured this out" |
| Client Story | Social proof through transformation | "Sarah came with X, here's what happened" |
| Observation Story | Pattern you've noticed | "Successful founders all do THIS one thing" |

### 2.3 Activation Content

**Purpose:** Get them to take action.

**Examples:**
- "Comment 'FUNNEL' and I'll send you my audit checklist"
- "DM me 'MESSAGE' if you want help"
- "Click link in bio to book a call"

### 2.4 Content Mix

| Content Type | Purpose | Frequency |
|--------------|---------|-----------|
| Educational | Build authority | 40% |
| Storytelling | Build connection | 40% |
| Activation | Drive action | 20% |

## DAY 3: THE MAGNETIC MESSAGING FRAMEWORK

### 3.1 The Domino Belief

**Formula:** Vehicle + Superlative = Transformation

| Component | What It Is | Example |
|-----------|------------|---------|
| Vehicle | Your method/framework | "My 5-step funnel framework" |
| Superlative | Bold claim | "The fastest way to" |
| Transformation | Outcome | "6-figure launches on autopilot" |

**Template:**
`My [framework] is the [superlative] way to [transformation].`

### 3.2 Limiting Beliefs List

List beliefs holding your dream client back.

**Examples:**
- "I need a bigger audience to make sales"
- "Funnels are too complicated for me"
- "I'm not technical enough"
- "I need to figure it out myself first"
- "I can't afford to invest right now"
- "I'm not ready for a coach"

### 3.3 The 8-Step Framework

| Step | Question | Your Answer |
|------|----------|-------------|
| 1 | What is your current offer? | |
| 2 | Who is the ONE dream client? | |
| 3 | What's your A→B framework? | |
| 4 | What problems occur at each step? | |
| 5 | What are they currently doing? | |
| 6 | What do they actually need? | |
| 7 | What results have YOU gotten? | |
| 8 | What results have clients gotten? | |

### 3.4 Framework Problems Mapping

For each piece of your framework, list all problems.

**Template:**

| Framework Piece | Problems (in their words) |
|-----------------|---------------------------|
| Step 1 | "No one's engaging," "Content isn't converting" |
| Step 2 | "No one opens emails," "Don't know what to write" |
| Step 3 | "Sales page isn't working," "People ghost me" |

## DAY 4: CONTENT THAT SELLS OFFERS

### 4.1 The 4 Types of Conversion Content

| Type | Purpose | Hook Example |
|------|---------|--------------|
| Problem-Aware | Speak to known pain | "Struggling to get leads?" |
| Solution-Aware | Position your solution | "Here's why my clients book calls in 24 hours" |
| Objection Handling | Address fears | "What if you're not 'ready'?" |
| Social Proof | Show results | "How Sarah booked 5 calls in one week" |

### 4.2 Hook-Caption Formula

For each framework piece, create hooks that address problems.

**Template:**

| Framework Piece | Problem | Hook |
|-----------------|---------|------|
| Attract | No leads | "Posting daily but no one's booking? Here's why." |
| Nurture | No engagement | "The real reason your emails aren't opened" |
| Convert | No sales | "Your sales page isn't the problem (it's this)" |

### 4.3 Caption Structure

| Part | Content |
|------|---------|
| Hook | Stop the scroll |
| Problem | Agitate the pain |
| Solution | Your framework piece |
| Proof | Your/client results |
| CTA | What to do next |
