**[AVOCADO CONTENT STRATEGY]{.underline}**

**[GOAL:]{.underline}** To develop a rhythm with your content that
touches, moves and inspires your audience to want to be better and
ultimately work with you. We do this by using the 7 different types of
content and developing your own content cadence.

**[Where do I post and how much?:]{.underline}**

**Post Daily...** On Facebook Profile / Instagram

Understand that the main goal of posting on your profiles is to build
your audience and influence so that you can book more calls, make more
offers and ultimately grow your business.

**Reminder:** Find your style and delivery method...

Do you prefer video or written? Pre-recorded or live? Instagram or
Facebook?

Wherever you post just ensure that you are consistent... this is the
magic sauce. The more content you create the better your content will
be.

And while there are 7 different types of content the 4 different types
that go especially well on your personal facebook profiles and instagram
page are highlighted below:

**[The 7 Types of Content are:]{.underline}**

**Authority:** To position yourself as the expert and share your wisdom

**Validation**: To prove that you can help people get results

**Opener:** To give away value and start conversations / book
appointments

**Challenge:** To shift beliefs and be a thought leader

**Authentic:** To build trust and connection

**DM Me:** To give away value and start conversations / book
appointments

**Outsource:** To share other peoples work and associate with other
experts

Authority + Challenge + Authenticity **= Influence**

DM Me **= Call To Action**

**The Ask Me Anything Post:**

This is another Authority post framework that helps you take a question
that you have either found in your market research, or that a client has
asked or that you just know would be helpful to your community.

How to use this framework:\
\
1) Ask the question

2\) Share where the question came from

3\) Reveal the mistake being made

4\) Explain the consequences of making this mistake

5\) Offer your solution or a better way

6\) share the results or "consequences" of fixing this mistake

If you want to share these posts in other peoples groups do not put a
Call to Action.

![](media/image9.png){width="6.5in" height="4.888888888888889in"}

**[VALIDATION POSTS]{.underline}**

Validation posts are some of the most powerful posts that you can share
for building trust and influence. This is why developing your offer and
continually improving it to serve your clients and help them get better
and better results is so key.

These are the best content marketing pieces you can have. In addition to
sharing video testimonials or written testimonials, screen shares of
clients wins etc you can use this framework to share a story.

1)  Write a hook

2)  Seque into the story

3)  Reveal his main problem

4)  Share your "magic trick", method or exercise

5)  Share the result

6)  Invite readers to engage with a CTA

community is the most ripe and ready to begin engaging with you and

you.

When you do a good job with all of your other content these posts work
really well.

**[CHALLENGE / CHARACTER POSTS]{.underline}**

[The Challenge post and the Character post are 2 totally different
posts.]{.mark}

[Character posts are primarily sharing about your life, sharing your
character and the core values that you hold with high regard. What are
the aspects of your character or personality that you want to share with
the world?]{.mark}

[A Challenge post on the other side... is taking commonly held beliefs
and helping people have a paradigm shift. This can be beliefs that you
previously held and have shifted and had a major impact on you... OR...
it could be beliefs you know that your ideal clients have and writing a
post in a particular way to reverse that thought or break that belief.\
\
I will provide 2 frameworks for shifting beliefs that you can use and
when done correctly you will discover that these are often your most
engaged with posts.]{.mark}

**[First ...]{.mark}**

**[The Challenge Post:]{.mark}**

1)  [Research Commonly held beliefs]{.mark}

2)  [Call them out with a polarizing title]{.mark}

3)  [Show compassion for them]{.mark}

4)  [Begin to discredit the belief by speaking about how it came to
    > be]{.mark}

5)  [Help them change the belief by introducing a new way]{.mark}

6)  [Show them the benefits of changing their beliefs]{.mark}

7)  [Optional CTA at the end.]{.mark}

[**The Manifesto:** Let's get religious...]{.mark}

The idea behind the manifesto post is to think about all the limiting
beliefs that your avatar has and then developing your own 10
commandments...

So the exercise is as follows:\
\
1) Think about top 10 struggles or problems your avatars run up against

2\) Then link those 10 struggles to the beliefs that they must hold
about it

3\) Then come up with 10 opposite beliefs and use them as a community
mantra

4\) Have some fun with it and write it out like the 10 commandments

-   Thou Shalt..

-   Thou Shalt not...

![](media/image25.png){width="6.5in" height="4.861111111111111in"}

Continuing on with the previous exercise you can then create the 10
principles that if your people adopted you know it would help them
identifier with a higher version of themselves and ultimately help them
to achieve new results.

Design your manifesto by developing your 10 principles.

Once you have these thought out, the application of how you can use them
are only limited by your own creativity.

    > Unique Mechanism + Benefit) This post is typically limited by the

mentors who are likely more known that you are. Association is powerful.

story, add your own unique take on it and share why it was so useful for
