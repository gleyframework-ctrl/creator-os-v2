ABOUT THIS LESSON
The art of keeping your pipeline full without being in a hard promo with a deadline or limited availability. In other
words - the anti-2022 strategy Flate has been using.
Main Content Buckets To Leverage -
       The plan & Processes -
              Things you’re doing in your business, Goals, Strategies, Things you’ve eliminated, what started working
              for you & how.
       Results -
              Any & all proof of concept - Client, Yours, Monetary Non-Monetary
       Free Asset Leverage -
              Funnel leads & people into things that you already have that are heavily focused on the value on front/
              selling on the back (builds goodwill and sells in unison)
       Lifestyle -
              Non-Biz Highlight who you are, what you’re doing, personality traits, and things you enjoy (Note you
              want to weave you & your personality into ALL YOUR content, not just this style)
              Hobbies and fun shit that you do with the family or your friends

Exercise For You To Do (To Pool content ideas - Adding because the biggest thing is usually “I don’t know what to
post”)
Take a doc & brain dump.
      Things you’ve done
      Where were you 6 months ago - 12 months ago
      What have you accomplished
      How have you accomplished it
      What things have you dont that worked
      Things that haven’t worked
      What's happening inside communities or programs you’re a part of
      Conversations you're having
      Comments you’re getting or things you’re seeing in the market / other people's comments
      Virtually Anywhere & everywhere that you can (you don’t have to explicitly use “So and so said X” could be
      more of the overarching topic)

Try to let your business fuel your content as much as you can (A big one - If you make it a point to keep your eyes
peeled - You’ll have infinite content ideas)
Leaning more into your own strengths when it comes to content delivery
        Better & more comfortable doing lives - Run quick punchy lives in place of some long-form written posts
        Or vice versa prefer written - prioritize that as the bulk of content strat with lives & video mixed in
Don’t eliminate or not do the other - but you do have the ability to make the main focal point of your content rotation
in a different format - Use them to feed one another
        Video feeds Written
        Written feeds video
A couple of lines I picked up from JC - We want our content to specifically paint the picture that we know what the
fuck we’re talking about but leave them feeling like your results as a whole are amazing & that you’re the person
living a life they want to be.
Very similar as far as content styles - it’s just not as much hammering deadlines - Aggressively posting about Offer X
or when it’s going to be closing.
Cycling through CTA’s Like:
      If  you’d like to have a chat about deploying this strategy inside your own business pop me a message here /
        comment X below & I’ll send over a message
        For a complete breakdown on X Thing (how they did, how you did, result you accomplished, How they
        accomplished) drop me a #x below & i’ll send over a free training on (Insert thing / with or without)
        Going with a less aggressive CTA - prompting for a chat / convo / checking out the “Thing” - but less of an “i
        have an offer to send you” (doesn’t mean you won’t move into that when you’re in the DM)
        No CTA At All (yes you read that right) - Will generally get more engagement with these & will still tap sales &
        build goodwill overtime
Your main goal is simple to Make it easy for folks to see you, find you, binge on you, and come into your world
(Facebook group) where they can get more of you
Having one layer thats working well whether its Tiktok to Group or FB profile to Group, whatever it may be. Then add
another layer (one at a time)
Building your ecosystem bigger and bigger as your Personal Brand Follows suit
Your Results, Your Environment, Your Network and your Content is what builds your Reputation
It also builds the most important thing in your business

Relationships
You want people to see your content and think “I like this MFer”
“I never thought of it that way” “I can do that” “oh that makes sense”

“They know exactly what Im dealing with” “I know they can help me”
“Thats cool as fuck” “I want that” “I want to work with them”
You’ll do that by nailing the things in this doc
What symptoms are they dealing with right now?
Get specific.
Ex. You want to make more sales but something is missing
Vs
“You sit on your couch with netflix playing in the background racking your brain on the missing piece in your business
but cant quite put your finger on it but you know that once you do you’re going to be smashing at least 4 Ht sales
every single month so you can pay off that credit card bill so Gamish Patel stops calling you every friday fucking up
your vibe.
We place that missing piece to the profit puzzle in its place so you can book that vacation and tell Mr. Patel to fuck
off.
Hows that sound?”
Thats the difference in bland copy and spicy specificity
Potential Cons:
       Won’t be much scarcity - or need for your prospects to jump in now (can always add layers to create the
       scarcity) - But will usually mean there won’t be massive highs & lows of people jumping in left and right
       Sales could be a tad harder to regularly pull in if you’re not nailing the content aspect of it as there isn’t
       external factors that will cause them to want to buy NOW
Benefits Of leaning into an “evergreen selling content strategy” / Less public offers or promos
       Consistency & constant goodwill are being built (won’t be as volatile as a full-on promo where it’s a bunch of
       sales & a bit of a lull afterward)
       In a sense - you’re delaying the ask publicly - which in turn creates a bigger & bigger demand for when you
       actually do put together a promo/public offer. (The longer you delay - the bigger you can ask)
       (How you’ll see JC shoot the lights out of any offer he puts out there publicly & everyone flocking to it like flies
       - because  it’s not “regular” content rotation - most are low-pressure extended invites to chat)
       Less stressful (not as much going on as there is in a full-on launch/promo) Might only be relevant if you’re
       actually launching your own thing
Following and tapping in these things into your content will act as small daily deposits so you can make large
withdrawals later
Get in there and chat to folks to nudge them along the customer journey of the 3 buckets
