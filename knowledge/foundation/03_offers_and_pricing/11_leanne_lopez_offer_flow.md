## 6. LOW-TICKET TASTER MENU

Your low-ticket offer should be a taster menu for higher offers.

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Go deep, not wide | Focus on one specific micro-win |
| Display your genius | Show unique approach |
| Provide micro-wins | Small, quick results that build confidence |
| Thread to higher offers | Naturally lead to next step |

How to Create:
- Take a piece of your bigger offer and pull it out
- Ask: What would high-ticket clients need to know about your genius to make jumping in a no-brainer?

## 7. MILLION DOLLAR HOOK FRAMEWORKS

### 7.1 Problem Solver Hooks

Framework 1:
"You don't have a [problem they think], you have a [real problem]... and it's the reason you're not [desired outcome]."

Income Engine Example:
"You don't have an SMTP provider problem, you have an ownership problem. And it's the reason you're burning domains."

Framework 2:
"The reason you get stuck at [where they're stuck]? Isn't because of [what they think]. It's because [what they're doing now] isn't the same as [what they need to do]."

Income Engine Example:
"The reason you keep burning domains? Isn't because you haven't found the right SMTP. It's because renting infrastructure will always fail."

Framework 3:
"Spending hours on [something they do] to only [small result] sucks. It sucks more when just changing [what you teach] could have [desired result]."

Income Engine Example:
"Spending 3 hours manually extracting emails to only get 500 leads sucks. It sucks more when one Python script could have 5,000 leads flowing while you sleep."

### 7.2 Authority Hooks

Framework 1:
"[Trend statement]. It's the same reason [trend 1]. It's the same reason [trend 2]. PIVOT: Here's what is working..."

Income Engine Example:
"Email deliverability is getting harder in 2025. It's the same reason shared SMTP is failing. PIVOT: Here's what is working - private infrastructure."

Framework 2:
"You're [repelling something they want]. It's the same reason you're [evidence]. PIVOT: The good news? These small tweaks will have you [desired result] faster than you can say [short thing]."

Income Engine Example:
"You're repelling high-ticket operators in your content. It's the same reason you're getting DMs from beginners who can't afford. PIVOT: These tweaks will have premium operators in your DMs faster than you can say '$497'."

Framework 3:
"There is a difference between [what they're doing] and [what creates result]. In fact, [what they're doing] can often [create opposite]. PIVOT: There is only [what you teach] that creates [desired result]."

Income Engine Example:
"There is a difference between content that gets likes and content that gets clients. PIVOT: There is only one type of content that builds authority AND attracts high-ticket clients."

Framework 4:
"[Share specific things client is doing that keep them stuck] even when you know [where they're playing small]. [Your genius] will eliminate [what they don't want], [create what they want] and [create another result they desire]."

Income Engine Example:
"Chasing cheaper SMTP and quick fixes attracts the wrong kind of operators - the ones who will churn at the first sign of trouble. Private infrastructure will eliminate deliverability worries, take the cap off your scaling and attract serious operators who want to own their systems."

### 7.3 Curiosity Hooks

Framework 1:
"[Experts] everywhere will hate me for this, but..."

Income Engine Example:
"SMTP providers everywhere will hate me for this, but..."

Framework 2:
"Your last 12 [experts] wouldn't tell you this, so I will..."

Framework 3:
"Dear [Client Identifier]... You have been lied to..."

Framework 4:
"I've got a secret to tell you..."

Framework 5 (High Value Experience):
"[Share a high value result you've created] (with something they think can stop them). Here's how..."

Income Engine Example:
"I went from burning 17 domains to sending 200k+ emails/month in 30 days (without being a tech genius). Here's how..."

Framework 6 (The Call Out):
"A list of things that didn't help me create [result]. PIVOT: Here's what did..."

Income Engine Example:
"A list of things that didn't help me stop burning domains. PIVOT: Here's what did..."

Framework 7 (High Value Result):
"POV: you're [high value experience] and you're still creating [result]."

Income Engine Example:
"POV: you're sleeping 8 hours a night and you're still waking up to 5,000 fresh leads."

Framework 8 (I Am The Authority):
"Exactly what I would do if I wanted to [create result] in [timeframe]."

Income Engine Example:
"Exactly what I would do if I wanted to fix my deliverability in 30 days."

Framework 9 (Micro-Specific Client Result):
"The 3 [micro-specific things you teach] that took my client from [where they were stuck] to consistent [result] within [timeframe]."

Income Engine Example:
"The 3 Postfix settings that took my client from burning domains weekly to consistent 200k emails/month within 30 days."

Framework 10 (Question Focused):
"Want [FREE] access to my secret [framework] that creates [result] in under [timeframe]?"

Income Engine Example:
"Want FREE access to my private VPS setup checklist that gets you automated in under 30 mins?"

### 7.4 Storytelling Hooks

Framework 1 - Dramatic Memory:
Share a dramatic memory like the opening of a movie.

Income Engine Example:
"I was staring at my 17th domain block alert, ready to quit CPA forever."

Framework 2 - Event/Moment of Contrast:
Share a specific date showing then vs now.

Income Engine Example:
"January 2025: I burned my 3rd domain that month. Today: 5 VPSs running 200k+ emails each."

Framework 3 - Vulnerable Share:
"POV: [sharp contrast of then vs now]"

Income Engine Example:
"POV: looking at my old SMTP bills knowing I wasted $5,000 on rented infrastructure."

Framework 4 - The I Have Never:
"I have never shared this before..." or "I can't believe I am sharing this..."

## 8. COMPOUND CONTENT FRAMEWORK

### 8.1 What is Compound Content?

Compound Content creates comments for days, weeks, and months to come. Like compound interest, results compound over time: post less, get more.

### 8.2 Top-of-Caption CTAs

Place first CTA at TOP of caption in CAPITALS.

Template 1:
BUT FIRST COMMENT [TRIGGER WORD] TO GET [ACCESS] TO MY [OFFER] TO [CREATE RESULT]

Template 2:
You're ready to [create result]? Comment [TRIGGER WORD] for the exclusive link.

Template 3:
[DESIRED RESULT]?
COMMENT: [TRIGGER WORD]
And I will send you my [training] on how you can start [doing something specific] completely FREE today

Income Engine Example:
COMMENT FIX TO GET INSTANT ACCESS TO MY VPS CHECKLIST TO STOP BURNING DOMAINS

## 9. 7-FIGURE NURTURE EMAIL SEQUENCE

### Email 1: Welcome
Subject: Welcome to [offer name]

Content:
- Thank them for joining
- Remind them of their desire
- List 3 things they'll learn
- Date/time/location details
- P.S. CTA to share/tag/join group

### Email 2: Your Story and Authority
Subject: Why I'm your new favorite [mentor/coach]

Content:
- Share a powerful story about you and your work
- Position yourself as an authority
- How it threads to what you're sharing
- Reminder to join

### Email 3: Addressing the Real Problem
Subject: Change this to [desired result]

Content:
- The reason your [desire] isn't happening isn't because you're not a genius
- It's not that [what they think is wrong]. It's because [tell them what it actually is]
- Share specific insight into what you teach
- Thread back to previous offer
- Share exclusive training video
- CTA with urgency (bonus expiring)

### Email 4: Social Proof + Urgency
Subject: How to [desired result]

Content:
- There is a reason my clients get insane results quickly
- If you: [list 2-3 symptoms of stuck state]
- I can almost guarantee it's your [what it is]
- Client results: [insert social proof]
- If you're getting some [result] and ready for [insane result], [offer] is for you
- You have just 24 hours left to claim your spot
- CTA

### Email 5: The 3 Types Framework
Subject: The 3 types of [what you teach] that make [desired result]

Content:
- List client results
- What do all these results have in common?
- They happened as a result of simple [what part of your work made it happen]
- The frameworks I teach use proven strategies that instantly skyrocket results
- 3 types of [what they need] to [desire]:
  1. [Pillar 1]
  2. [Pillar 2]
  3. [Pillar 3]
- If your [what you teach] doesn't [what it should be doing], something needs to change
- Speak to a core pillar in depth
- CTA

### Email 6: Client Story + Final Call
Subject: Did you see this?!

Content:
- If you have [what they have] and you're [getting an ok result], you are as little as [timeframe] away from [doubling your income]
- When my client [name] came to me in [timeframe], she was [where she was stuck]
- She told me exactly what she wanted. She had been [what she had been doing] and it wasn't working
- I told her what to do (using my [method] in [offer]), and within just [timeframe], she completely [achieved the result]
- Write out their achievement
- Expand on their story
- Summarize why she was able to achieve this
- Mirror your dream clients' pain points and desires to the client result you share
- Insert video testimonial or written testimonial
- It's not just [client name]. Clients come to me daily desiring [what they desire]
- You can try and figure out [what they want] on your own... or join [offer] and get access to the exact [method/strategies/tools]
- This is your final call to join [offer] and get access to [bonus]
- If you're ready to [desire] - then [offer] is for you
- CTA

Use in conversation with dream clients.

| Question | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| What's the #1 problem you struggle with when it comes to [topic]? | Surface problem |
| If I had a magic wand and could solve those problems, what would that look like in detail? | Deep desire |

## 11. OFFERS THAT SELL WORKBOOK

### 11.1 Three Components of a Transformational Offer

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| ACTIVATION | Creating success-based beliefs for your work |
| TEACHING | The "how" - steps, lessons, modules |
| ANCHORS | Tools to anchor in the work (audios, workbooks, templates) |

### 11.2 Activation Questions

For each offer, answer:
- What would my ideal client need to BELIEVE is possible to get the best results?
- What do they currently believe (limiting beliefs) that could keep them from getting results?
- What do YOU believe to be true about what's possible for them - and why?
- What would activate your ideal client to tap into what's possible?
- What does your ideal client need to KNOW about the work?

### 11.3 Teaching Questions

- If I were to reverse-engineer the exact steps to get them from stuck to desired result, what would those steps be?
- If I were to break this into lessons and modules, what would that look like?
- What would be the best format to teach this topic?
- How do I teach most potently?

### 11.4 Anchor Questions

- What tools would anchor in each of the steps?
- How will I create these to ensure they are the most user-friendly for my dream client?
