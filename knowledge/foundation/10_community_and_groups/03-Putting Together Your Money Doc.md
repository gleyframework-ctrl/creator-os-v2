Put Together The Offer

      Who it’s for
      What specific problem it’s going to solve & what outcome they’re going to accomplish
      Proof of concept - Your own + Any that you can leverage
      Relevant bonuses/support
      Reasons for them to act now (Limited availability - X time - Cost Savings)
      Any way that you can derisk the investment (I will work with you until…X now & X after - can still do this with
      affiliate products)
Important Things Went Putting Together Your Offer



      Go YOU focused.. In this world you’re virtually selling yourself - Highlight your own things - Bonuses -
      Resources and things that you’re providing on top of X program.

      Example - For those who’re promoting Group Juice or an offer with a Doc of their own - Leverage their doc but
      mix in a quick bridge type doc highlighting your support, Bonuses & results & then interlink to their doc



      Less Is More - Add hyper-relevant things to your offer - Things that will enhance the product you’re selling &
      what they’re going to do for the person who purchases.

      Look at things you have already - If you have training you’ve done, Videos, Lives, or Resources, Look at things
      you’ve already created that you can add to X program to increase the perceived value of your offer

Aim for 4-8 training that YOU are going to do/run inside of your own private buyer's group - Try to make them as
linear or complementary to main product as you can..

Examples:
      Jill’s unique framework for blowing up her group by 2,000+ people in 28 days
      Kates process for closing sales almost every other day all of Oct
      Mark’s content framework that has had a handful of people sending him messages asking him “Where do i
      buy”
      Rachels process growing her TikTok & going live
You can literally spin anything that you’re doing in your business daily that will help someone two steps behind you..

Offer Creation Exercise: (Be Specific! It Will Make Your Life Easier When You Put Together Doc)



Offer Doc Flow

      Opening - Who It’s for - Digging into certain pain points they are actively struggling within their
      business. Painting a picture about where they’re currently sitting at in there biz & the struggles of being there.
      Results Snapshot - Tie in your own results / Client results - the closer you can tie these to the product you’re
      selling or the strategies taught inside of what you’re selling the better
      If you don’t have your own leverage community results & results of people inside of the program (creators and

      students)
      Highlight benefits of the program - Not the features of your product but how it will solve the problem & thing
      you dug into at the beginning of the doc Speak about the “feeling” or place they’ll be once they’ve implemented
      the product.if you can highlight the benefits of the program while “eliminating” a certain thing it can be very
      powerful. How we did with GJ & the without cold dm’s strat
      Transition into Core features of your product - break down the different features of the product & what those
      will bring to your client's biz.
      IE - On Day one you’ll learn how to build out your business so you can effectively grow & monetize, giving you
      the ability to make money online through free social media content vs On day one we’ll show you how to build
      an online business.
      Transition into Bonuses -
      Briefly dig into pain point & transition into solution/product with a credibility statement (can be a few
      sentences)
      Availability - Spots / Days Left to get in or combination of the two
      Pricing
      Wrap up - Either prompt to hit the check out inside doc or follow back up
