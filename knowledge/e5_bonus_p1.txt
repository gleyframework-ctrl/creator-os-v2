SOURCE: E5 C.A.M.P. Bonus Materials (Agora Financial Copywriting, MFA Traffic, Presuasion)
FRAMEWORK: 8-FIGURE EXECUTION PLAYBOOKS (Copyboarding, FB Warmup, Presuasion)
WHEN TO USE: When a member is staring at a blank page, when their Facebook ads are getting banned or CPL is too high, when they need to edit a VSL, or when they want to scale a winning campaign without breaking it.

--- PLAYBOOK 1: AGORA FINANCIAL COPYWRITING PROTOCOL (Todd Brown) ---
LAW: Million-dollar campaigns are not written; they are assembled from research and outlines.
STEP 1: RESEARCH (The Foundation): Deep insight into Prospect (W.E.B.), Competitors, and Product before writing a single word.
STEP 2: THE BIG IDEA: Must be emotionally compelling and intellectually interesting. 
STEP 3: CHOOSE THE LEAD TYPE (Match to Awareness Pyramid):
1. Offer Lead (Most Aware - Direct)
2. Big Promise Lead (Product Aware)
3. Problem-Solution Lead (Solution Aware)
4. Prediction Lead (Problem Aware)
5. Secret System Lead (Problem Aware)
6. Story Lead (Unaware - Indirect)
7. Invitation Lead
STEP 4: COPYBOARDING (The 5-Step Outline):
1. Start with the Big Idea.
2. List all possible objections the prospect has.
3. Organize objections into a logical story arc.
4. Turn those objections into benefit-driven subheads.
5. Fill in the copy between the subheads. (This allows new copywriters to write $10M campaigns in 7 days).
STEP 5: EDITING (The C.U.B.A. Test): Read out loud and mark:
- C: Confusing (tweak)
- U: Unbelievable (add proof)
- B: Boring (juice up)
- A: Awkward (cut)
STEP 6: THE OFFER: Keep the product transparent; dimensionalize the BENEFITS. Overcome 6 perceived risks: loss of face, loss of time, inconvenience, hassle, unsatisfactory results, disappointment.

--- PLAYBOOK 2: MFA FACEBOOK TRAFFIC & SCALING ---
LAW: Facebook hates direct response squeeze pages. Compliance and Relevance are the keys to scale.
COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST:
- NO squeeze pages. Send traffic to content/vanilla pages.
- Complete footer (Phone, Address, TOS, Disclaimer).
- NO direct claims ("You will make $10k"). Use "I did" or "Bob did".
THE WARMUP SOLUTION (Fixing High CPL / Low Relevance):
- If relevance is 2-4 and CPC is high, do NOT just change the ad.
- Create a Dark Post. Run a Page Post Engagement (PPE) ad to it.
- Spend $100 to generate 25-100 likes/comments.
- Once social proof is built, switch to Conversion ads. Relevance jumps to 8-9, and CPL drops.
SCALING PROTOCOL:
- Increase winning ad budgets 25% every 3 days.
- Duplicate EXACT proven winning ad to new audiences.
- NEVER test more than 1 variable at a time.
SEQUENTIAL RETARGETING: Treat the user on a journey. Don't just blast "Buy Now". Retarget based on behavior (e.g., "Get IN the webinar" -> "Get ON the webinar" -> "Buy").
AD CREATIVES: Congruence is King. The Ad Image = The Headline. Use EBM (Education-Based Marketing) content as ads to build value and bypass ad blindness.

--- PLAYBOOK 3: PRESUASION & PSYCHOLOGICAL TRIGGERS ---
- ZEIGARNIK EFFECT: Use open loops, cliffhangers, and nested loops to force the brain to seek closure (keeps them reading/watching).
- UNITY & KINSHIP: Create a strong perception of "togetherness" and shared identity. Use "we" and "our".
- METAPHORS: The most powerful influence factor. Connect the unknown to the known.
- FOCUS ON ABSENCE: Highlight the presence of your product's positive features absent of any comparison to competitors.
- RECIPROCITY: Make giveaways meaningful, unexpected, and customized.

--- FUNNEL TEMPLATE ARCHITECTURE ---
Standard E5 MVF (Minimum Viable Funnel), Free+Shipping (Tripwire), Evergreen Webinar, Live Event, 12-Week Launch, Software Demo.

DECISION RULES FOR THE COACH:
1. Member staring at a blank sales page? Force them to use COPYBOARDING. "List your prospect's top 5 objections, turn them into subheads, and fill in the blanks."
2. Member's FB ads are getting banned or CPL is skyrocketing? Check COMPLIANCE (kill the squeeze page, add a footer) and run the WARMUP SOLUTION (PPE ads for social proof).
3. Member's VSL feels flat? Check the LEAD TYPE against the Awareness Pyramid. Edit the script using the C.U.B.A. framework.
4. Member wants to scale? Enforce the 25% every 3 days rule and Sequential Retargeting.
