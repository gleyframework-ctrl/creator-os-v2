**The Belief Shifter Post:**

1.  Topic: Pick something you want to talk about

2.  Niche: Call out your avatar and the mistake that they make about
    > this topic

3.  Explain why it is a mistake (Remember to be speaking to internal
    > beliefs)

4.  Talk about the consequences

5.  Offer a new way with a New belief

6.  What can they expect the results to be

7.  Segue into a CTA

8.  Call To Action
