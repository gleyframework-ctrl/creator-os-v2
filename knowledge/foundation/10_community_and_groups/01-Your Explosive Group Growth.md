ABOUT THIS LESSON
Your Explosive Group Growth & Audience Building Model
One of the best aspects of leveraging short-form videos Like Tiktok is that it works extremely well at getting results
& building an audience quickly.
You can also leverage one piece of content on multiple social media platforms, Establishing omnipresence for your
brand without having to do any additional work
You can create 1 piece of content & Put it onto 5 different platforms.
       Tiktok
       Instagram Reels
       Facebook Reels (Insane For Organic FB Marketers)
       Pinterest Stories
       Youtube Shorts
Now one thing you need to know about creating short-form content is with those quick wins & viral traffic, you do
need to nurture these leads more than other platforms or strategies.
The goal of these videos is to fuel your Group, so you can build rapport quickly and identify people who would be
potential buyers.
Short Form Video Content > Group Hub > Membership Question / Qualifiers > Messenger
The main goal is to leverage the virality of the short-form traffic sources to set your Organic FB system on fire. For a
long time, the only way to grow your profile & group would be to get active in other people’s communities, add friends
& manually build up your audience.
Using This Strategy To Grow your Group Will Allow You To:
       Be Different from the hundreds of others by leveraging Organic FB, poaching the same groups
       Have your leads & prospects coming to you and your community, changing the entire dynamic of the
       conversations, because you’re the leader and the authority.
       Grow your group extremely quickly in comparison to traditional routes… Resulting in virtually an infinite
       number of convos & sales opportunities
       Be different from the masses on short-form video platforms just slanging links & product sales
       Build security in your business over long periods of time because you’re building a culture of your own vs
       feeding someone elses ecosystem
The Main Goal
The Core Focus Of All Of it is to keep everything linear… Wherever you’re creating content, the face of whatever you’re
promoting is your Facebook group.
That way as you get people into your ecosystem, you can identify where they’re at, reach out and see if they’d be a
good fit to either work with you right away or push them into a nurturing sequence.
There is no denying it - Short Form Video Content is hot - Every platform has rolled out its own version of TikTok
basically - leverage it while you can!
Now, this isn’t going to be your average TikTok / short-form video training - where we’re going to tell you to copy viral
videos & hop on trends.
We’re all about that “Anti-Viral Content” = Quality Over Massive Quantities Of People
While yes - these can work great for “Followers” that doesn’t mean more sales or a direct correlation to the amount
you make.
As the name implies - Anti Viral - is the act of creating content that isn’t catered to just getting more views but
instead getting your message across.
That way when you do go viral (because yes it can still happen) It’s for the right things & the leads & audience that
you’re pulling in is people you’d actually want to be working with
An example of this would be a GJ student Jill Anne - Who had a single video grow her group by over 2,000 members -
and it was a TARGETED Piece of content.
If you look at both of our Audiences combined they’re smaller than most (especially on short-form platforms, but

even when we’re averaging minimal views - it would come out to about $1.00 Per follower each month because the
content is targeted)
