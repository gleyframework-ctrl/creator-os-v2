FRAMEWORK DEFINITIONS - CORRECT ANSWERS
NEPQ Framework (Jeremy Miner)
Neuro-Emotional Persuasion Questioning
5 questioning types: Opening, Problem, Solution, Objection, Closing
Source: Jeremy Miner NEPQ training
SIN Framework (Todd Brown)
S = Superior (better than competition)
I = Irresistible (impossible to say no)
N = No-brainer (obvious choice)
Source: Todd Brown - The SIN Protocol
LEAP Framework (Laura Meyer)
L = Limit (single objective)
E = Establish (create plan)
A = Act (execute)
P = Progress (review & adjust)
Source: Laura Meyer - Fractional Freedom
AIM Sequence (Alex Cattoni / Jessica Tutton)
A = Acknowledge (validate their situation)
I = Include (share story, build belonging)
M = Mobilize (invite to next step)
Source: Alex Cattoni - Own The Inbox / Jessica Tutton - PFFA
Conviction Codes (Alex Cattoni - Posse Eye)

Company level
Customer level
Community level
Source: Alex Cattoni - Posse Eye Brand Voice
