# **Writing Style Analysis**

## **The Difference Between Generic AI and Content That Converts**

This is the most important step in the entire system.

If this section is weak, everything sounds like AI.

If this section is done correctly, nobody can tell.

There are no shortcuts here.

## **Non-Negotiable Rules**

-   DO NOT use AI-generated content

-   Use only:

    -   Your own writing

    -   Your own speaking (transcribed)

-   This content must already exist in the real world

-   If you would not post it again today, do not include it

If you break these rules, the output will be diluted.

## **What Content to Use**

Include content that represents you at your best.

**Required:**

-   Best performing posts

-   Posts that generated sales

-   Story or identity content

-   Long-form writing you are proud of

**Minimum:** 5 pieces

**Recommended:** 10 to 20 pieces

More context equals higher accuracy.

## **How to Prepare Your Input**

-   Paste all selected content into one single block

-   Do not edit for polish

-   Do not \"clean it up\"

-   Preserve:

    -   Line breaks

    -   Spacing

    -   Formatting

    -   Imperfections

Those imperfections are signals.

## **Style Analysis Prompt**

Copy and paste this exactly into your ChatGPT thread:

> You are an expert in linguistics, natural language processing, and
> author style replication.
>
> Your task is to analyze the writing below and produce a detailed,
> operational style guide that allows an AI to generate brand-new
> content while preserving this author\'s exact voice.
>
> This is not a summary. This is a replication blueprint.
>
> **Analyze and document the following:**
>
> **1. Voice and Tone**

-   Overall tone and personality

-   Level of formality

-   Authority versus relatability balance

> **2. Emotional Texture**

-   Dominant emotional moods

-   Emotional range and intensity

-   How emotion is implied versus stated

> **3. Sentence Structure**

-   Typical sentence length

-   Use of short versus long sentences

-   Patterns in sentence openings

> **4. Transitions**

-   How ideas move from one to the next

-   Common transition patterns or phrases

-   Use of breaks, pauses, or resets

> **5. Rhythm and Pacing**

-   Overall pacing

-   Use of punchy lines versus explanation

-   Where emphasis is created

> **6. Signature Patterns**

-   Repeated phrases or constructions

-   Unique punctuation or formatting habits

-   Structural patterns that repeat

> **7. Word Choice**

-   Vocabulary complexity

-   Commonly repeated words or phrases

-   Preference for simple versus technical language

> **8. Domain Language**

-   Industry or niche-specific terminology

-   Accessibility of language to non-experts

> **9. Meaning and Connotation**

-   Emotional or cultural weight behind word choices

-   How meaning is layered or implied

> **10. Formality and Audience Fit**

-   How formal or informal the language is

-   How this aligns with the intended audience

> **Hard Requirements:**

-   Ensure heterogeneous paragraph lengths

-   Ensure heterogeneous sentence lengths

-   Default to short, clear sentences

> **Output Requirements:**

-   Write this as a style guide another AI could follow without guessing

-   Use concrete rules, patterns, and examples

-   Avoid vague descriptions
