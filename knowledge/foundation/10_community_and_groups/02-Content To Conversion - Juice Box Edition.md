EB and flow With Content Modes -

ITS OK to **not** make an offer in a post. Not every post needs to
hammer a CTA or even a pitch.

Depending on what mode you're in - dial back a promo & do just value.

Or vice versa if you've been doing Value, Value, Value roll into a full
on promo for a week.

A good way to look at your audience is like a bucket of water - and
everytime you run an offer it depletes the bucket & you fill it back up
with value.

Framework: **All based on you and your situation/offer**

1.  Hype building / teasers (3-7 Days)

    a.  Results your new thing has achieved

    b.  Testimonials & credibility building

    c.  Why it is different from what people have seen before

    d.  Who it is for & who it isn't for

    e.  When it is going live & how to get it

    f.  Any relevant scarcity (time or quantity)

2.  Going Live (Emotional Buyers Jump In) (1-3 Days)

    a.  What they get

    b.  How to get it

    c.  How much it is

    d.  Bonuses

    e.  \# of spots

    f.  Timing etc etc

3.  Slicing & Dicing (Logical Buyers Pushed Off The Fence (3-14 Days)

    a.  Objections

    b.  False Beliefs

    c.  Stories

    d.  Results

    e.  Why It Will Work For Them
