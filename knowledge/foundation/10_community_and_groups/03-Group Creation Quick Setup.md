Optimizing Your Group Settings:
      Who Can Join: Profiles
      Who Can Approve Member Requests: Only Admins & Mod’s
      Who’s Pre-Approved: Nobody
      Anon Posting: Off
      Who Can Post: Anyone In the Group
      Require Post Approval: Yes (So you can screen everyone's content prior to posting)
      Potential Spam: Off (Will cause problems if you have it on)
      Sort Posts: New Posts
      Sort Comments: All Comments
      Default Tab: Discussion
