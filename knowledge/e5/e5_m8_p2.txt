SOURCE: E5 C.A.M.P. Masterclass — Module 8, Part 2 (Lesson 30: Expanding Through Your Backend)
FRAMEWORK: BACKEND EXPANSION & THE RFM SCORE (The Profit Engine)
WHEN TO USE: When a member's front-end works but they make no real profit, when they neglect existing customers, or when they need a monthly KPI to measure the health of their business.

--- CORE LAW: THE BACKEND IS THE REAL BUSINESS ---
LAW: "This is the real business! This is where the profit is made." The front-end's job is to acquire customer assets (at breakeven or even going negative). The BACKEND's job is to generate the profit. If a member says "my funnel works but I'm not making money," they are looking at the wrong half of the business.

--- RULE 1: BE PROLIFIC WITH NEW OFFERS ---
LAW: Your customer segment is the one you should be MOST prolific with when it comes to making new offers. Existing customers already trust you. Continuously engineer new offers, ascension products, and continuity programs for them — not one offer per year.

--- RULE 2: THE RFM SCORE (Monthly Backend Vital Sign) ---
WHEN TO USE: To measure and improve the health of the backend every single month.
THE 3 COMPONENTS:
- RECENCY: % of customers who participated in a transaction in the last 30 days.
- FREQUENCY: % of customers who have purchased 3 or more times.
- MONETARY VALUE: % of customers who have spent $1,000 or more.
CALCULATION: Add the 3 scores, then divide by 300.
EXAMPLE (Growth Trajectory):
- January: 40/12/8 = 20%
- February: 45/13/10 = 23%
- March: 50/15/11 = 25%
- April: 55/16/14 = 28%
LAW: Track this monthly. A rising RFM means the backend engine is compounding. A flat or falling RFM means the business is stagnating — make new offers immediately.

DECISION RULES FOR THE COACH:
1. Member says "my funnel breaks even but I make no profit"? Diagnose: They only built the front-end. Force them to build the backend: ascension ladder, continuity, new offers to existing customers.
2. Member asks "who should I sell to first?" Answer: Your existing customers. Be the most prolific offer-maker to your own list.
3. Member needs a KPI? Have them calculate their monthly RFM score and track the trend. If it's below 20% or declining, the backend needs new offers NOW.
