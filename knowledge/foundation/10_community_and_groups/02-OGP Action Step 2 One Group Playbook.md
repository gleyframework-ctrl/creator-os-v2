Model]{.underline}](https://www.skool.com/group-juice/classroom/af734968?md=f4ba49db8e8d4712bf1c5a67ebbf9ecb)

Every time someone buys from you, one of two things happens:

Momentum stays visible. Or momentum leaves the room.

Most business models are built around option 2.

You build a free community, move buyers somewhere else, and repeat. On
paper it looks clean. In reality, it quietly breaks your business.

When buyers move out of the main space, proof becomes private. Wins stop
being seen. Trust takes longer to build.

Your time becomes the bottleneck. Not because demand is gone. Because
the structure resets itself after every sale.

We shut down Facebook groups with 8,000+ members and Skool communities
with 1,300+ members.

Not because they failed. Because they fractured attention, culture, and
proof.

We collapsed everything into one group. Free members. Paid members.
Buyers at different stages.

Together.

That\'s when momentum stopped resetting.
