In the previous two Deep Dive Days, you learned how to influence consumers by tapping into their
needs and motivation by influencing the way consumers pay attention to, and thus, view your site.
Now it is time to dive deeper into the human brain. In this Deep Dive Day, we will explore how our
memory functions and how we learn.

My aim for today is to show you that:
1. Humans are bad at remembering things.
2. The “biggest problem” with our memory is “retrieval” (recalling events).
3. Our memory of events is usually different from how they actually happened.
4. We can aid our memory by applying theories on learning.
