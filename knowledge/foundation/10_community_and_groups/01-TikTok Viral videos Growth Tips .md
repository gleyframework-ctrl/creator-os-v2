Now, Pay attention to How I structured these videos. The Headlines are
important. Your first 1-3 seconds are Vital. Make sure you grab their
attention.

Transitions from frame to frame are a good way to keep them on their
toes. Utilize the info that you already know.

Tell parts of your story, Lifestyle, Business, childhood, adult life,
kids, wife, Your failures, struggles, your wins, accomplishments.

Also pay attention to other Creators and what\'s trending. Hop on the
trend but incorporate your niche into it.

You should have some kind of CTA (call to action) in every video.
Whether its to Follow you, comment, share, check out the link in your
bio, smash the heart button.

Entertainment is why most folks are on the APP.

Education is a close second

And Motivational content usually does well.

Don't overthink this stuff

Get Creative here. Feel free to Model the Videos that are listed above.

Use the sounds and test different effects, length of the videos are a
factor as well. The shorter the better, even though I got over 200k
views on a 3 minute video lol.

You\'ll see that I'm not a fan of the fake side hustle videos and
that\'s one of my angles for my Content.

The Framework of those videos are good and they perform well, so I
recommend using the framework but use it for things that you know about
and have personally done.

Post at least 3 times a day at different times a day. I say Morning,
noonish and then after 7pm so you can check your analytics after a week
or so to see when your followers are most active.

Then you can be sure to adjust your upload schedule to compliment when
your audience is the most active

If you can post more I definitely would and that will cause you to grow
much faster.

Handle objections in your content and be sure to take them down a path
that makes them comfortable.

Give them a reason to want to go and check out the link in your bio or
to ask for more information or simply to follow you to investigate your
personal brand

Your Content should be true and honest as well as valuable to your
viewers

For those of you that are under 1,000 followers, focus on giving value
and telling your story about who you are and what you\'re doing.

Go LIVE whenever you can, especially after you Post a video, and even
more so if one of your videos is doing well. I've asked a few followers
to let me know if hey see my content on the FYP because if it is, You
will have more viewers on your live and it will push you video that\'s
doing well

A deep Dive into what your Product or service can do for them.

-   Tips to help them

-   Mistakes you made

-   Challenges you had

-   Aha moments you experienced

-   Anything that will help your viewers

What transformation does it provide

What Problem does it actually solve

Think about your ideal customer and what they are looking to hear and
learn

Most importantly is that you make your content about them, your ideal
customer.

People should watch your videos and think " Yep, that was me " or " He's
speaking directly to me.

With everything I have sent, there should be plenty for you to
Implement.

Test as many different angles as you can so you can figure out what your
audience wants to see.

Whatever your motive is make sure you give a specific call to action

-   Follow me for more vids like this

-   Comment your thoughts

-   Share this to someone that needs it

-   Save this video for later

-   Or all of the above.

All depending on what your goal is for your account and what niche youre
in should determine your content and CTAs

In a nutshell you need these:

Solid Hook/Headline

Value/Point of your video relevant to your hook/headline

Specific CTA of what you want your viewers to do

And Be unapologetically you!!
