The Pivot
Now, you don’t necessarily have to make a massive shift in the content as a whole - Primarily just the CTA & where
you’re directing people.
Would usually say the back half of the campaign calendar is when you’ll start leveraging CTA’s about the specific
offer. (can still tease about the offer prior in content - would make the bulk of CTA towards the free training)
        Post about ANY & EVERY single spot filling up
        At this point - hammer the deadline (If you’re getting traction) - limited availability
Injecting A Double Deadline
Adding something mid-promo to further fuel FOMO & spice up the campaign even more (as the beginning and the
end will usually have the most attention with a tad lull in the middle)
        Adding a special bonus mid promo (we did this with the AMA & 3C’s bundle)
        An extra layer of support / 1 on 1
        Change a pricing structure
IE - “Next 2 People Who Snag Their Spot In The Offer I Extended At The Free Training Get….”
        Be ok with giving it to everyone who jumped in prior too

Follow-Up Reversal (Something from JC - he used when he was promoting Group Juice)

As you’re skating along in the promo & following up with people about the offer - flip it on it’s head..
Make it about them & the outcome that your offer is going to solve.
If they’ve already raised their hand on something admitting that they needed help with that thing.. Then go stale in

convo - you know on a deeper level there is a desire there..
Pivoting the convo to something that is important to them - will increase the response rate & give you scope to
redirect into the offer..
“Hey Name, How big is your Facebook group currently?”
Give Them The Exit
Let them feel like it is 1000% ok to say no (something I’ve been leaning very heavily into lately - super detached / low
pressure & it usually draws them back into the convo)
But also do it from a stand point that it’s alright for them to tell you know - The faster they let you know it isn’t for
them - you can move onto the next..
  Can also be something as simple as adding “If this is something you’d like to do OR NOT ” statement
Deadlines
You’re going to feel like a spam artist here..
Don’t be shy & don’t slow down..
        Close Down (Last 72 Hours)
        72 hours to go
        48 hours to go
        24 hours to go
        12 hours to go
        6 hours to go
        1 hour to go
        10 minutes to go
Hit every single point touch point that you can - letting them know that “thing is going away in X time” (will usually
pin offer doc in comments on the final countdown)
        Profile posts
        Group Posts
        Stories
        Inside Messages
        Emails
BE SURE TO PUBLICLY ANNOUNCE THAT YOUR OFFER SHUT DOWN - Will help with future promotions / when you
mention anything related to scarcity / deadlines
