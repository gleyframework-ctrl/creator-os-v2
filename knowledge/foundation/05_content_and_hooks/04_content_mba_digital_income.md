## 2. 7 AUDIENCE RESEARCH QUESTIONS

Use these to uncover emotional drivers for sales copy.

### Question 1: What Keeps Them Awake at Night?
Example: "Will my domain get blocked tomorrow? Am I wasting money on SMTP?"

### Question 2: What Are They Afraid Of?
Example: "Losing months of campaign momentum because of blacklisting."

### Question 3: What Are They Angry About?
Example: "Frustrated with SMTP providers who oversell, angry at themselves for not learning infrastructure sooner."

### Question 4: What Content Do They Follow?
Example: CPA forums, Telegram groups, YouTube tutorials on email marketing.

### Question 5: What Trends Are Occurring?
Example: "More competition, stricter email provider policies, need for automation."

### Question 6: What Do They Secretly Desire Most?
Example: "To wake up to 5,000 leads without lifting a finger. To be the top operator in their network."

### Question 7: What Makes Them Laugh?
Example: "Domain burn" jokes, insider slang like "warm-up", "inbox placement".

## 10. VIRAL CONTENT PRINCIPLES

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| STRONG HOOK | Open with bold claim, question, or unexpected visual |
| VALUABLE INFO | Provide actionable tips viewers want to save |
| SHAREABILITY | Make content people want to share with peers |
| COMMENT GENERATION | Ask polarizing question or challenge assumptions |
| EDITING AND PACING | Use quick cuts, zooms, dynamic visuals |
| PAYOFF | End with satisfying revelation or emotional uplift |
| VISUAL APPEAL | Invest in lighting, composition, color |
| VIRAL AUDIO | Use trending music for discoverability |
| RELATABILITY | Show human moments, flaws, humor |
| STORYTELLING | Use narrative structure (conflict, resolution) |
