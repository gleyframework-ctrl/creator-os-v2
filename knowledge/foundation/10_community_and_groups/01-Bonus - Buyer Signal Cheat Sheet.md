BONUS: Buyer Signal Cheat Sheet
How to know when someone is ready to buy without guessing or being salesy.




THE CORE TRUTH
People rarely buy the first time they engage.

They buy after belief forms, trust compounds, and certainty increases.

Your job is not to rush that process. Your job is to recognize when it is already happening.

Buyer signals show up in four categories. Category #2 is the sleeper.




CATEGORY 1: HIGH-INTENT SIGNALS (ACT
IMMEDIATELY)
These are requests, not curiosity. When these appear, the "Green Light" is on.

Direct Hand Raises: "Can you tell me more about X?" or "How does this work?"

CTA Compliance: Commenting the specific word you asked for or DMing after a piece of
content.

Outcome-Focused Questions: "What should I do next?" or "How do people go deeper?"

The Rule: Be direct. Reduce small talk. Respect their momentum.




CATEGORY 2: BELIEF-BASED ENGAGEMENT (THE
SLEEPER)
This is where most creators hesitate—and shouldn't. This is alignment, not just "engagement."

Consistent Presence: They comment on most posts, show up to lives, and reference things
you've said in the past. They are mentally "choosing" you already.

Advocacy: They start answering questions for other members or tagging people in your
content. They are already acting like a customer.

The Rule: They trust how you think. A DM here is simply a continuation of a mental process
they've already started.




CATEGORY 3: MEDIUM-INTENT SIGNALS (STACK
THESE)
One alone may not be enough. Two or more usually indicates readiness.

Topic Circling: Repeated engagement on the same specific problem.

Language Mirroring: They start using your phrases, frames, or models.

Frustration Statements: "I'm tired of this not working" or "This is exactly where I'm stuck."




CATEGORY 4: LOW-INTENT SIGNALS (DO NOT DM YET)
These do not indicate readiness. Pushing here creates friction.

Likes Only: Acknowledgment, not intent.

One-Off Comments: No pattern established yet.

New Joins: Curiosity, not readiness. Let them orient first.




THE "READY-TO-DM" FILTER
Before you reach out, run this mental check:

"Do they believe in my process, or are they still evaluating?"

Belief is there? → DM

Belief is forming? → Wait/Observe

Belief is absent? → Do not DM

SIGNAL TIMING & WHAT TO SAY
The best time to DM is after a micro-win, after repeated exposure, or once belief shows up
publicly.

You don't pitch—you acknowledge the alignment.

Clean Openers for Belief-Based Signals:

"Hey, I've noticed you've been pretty active around here. Mind if I ask what you're working
toward right now?"

"You've been engaging on a lot of this lately. Happy to help if you want to go deeper."

"Sounds like you're aligned with how we approach this."




FINAL FRAME
People don't buy because they are impressed.

They buy because they trust the path and trust the guide.

Belief-based engagement is the clearest indicator of that trust.

When belief, pain, and clarity collide, selling becomes the obvious next step.
