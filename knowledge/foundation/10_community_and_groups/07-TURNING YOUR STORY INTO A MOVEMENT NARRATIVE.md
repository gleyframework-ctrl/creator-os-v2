# **TURNING YOUR STORY INTO A MOVEMENT** 

*Your past becomes the invitation*

## **Why Your Story Matters More Than Credentials**

People don\'t follow credentials.

They follow paths.

They look at you and ask one question:

\"Did they start where I am? And did they get where I want to go?\"

If the answer is yes, they trust you.

Not because you have certifications. Not because you have a fancy title.

Because you\'ve walked the road.

## **The Problem With \"Expert Positioning\"**

When you lead with credentials, you create distance.

\"I\'ve been doing this for 15 years.\"

\"I have 3 degrees in this field.\"

\"I\'ve worked with Fortune 500 companies.\"

Cool. But also\... cold.

It doesn\'t make people feel anything.

It positions you as above them. Which is the opposite of belonging.

Movements aren\'t built by experts on pedestals.

They\'re built by guides who\'ve been through it.

## **The Simple Narrative Arc**

Every movement leader follows the same arc:

I was here → I figured this out → Come with me

That\'s it.

Not complicated. Not fancy.

Just honest.

## **Part 1: I Was Here**

Where did you start?

What was broken? What was hard? What did you struggle with?

This is where you connect.

The more specific, the better.

Not: \"I struggled in my business.\"

But: \"I was posting content every day, getting likes, and making zero
dollars.\"

Paint the picture. Make them see themselves.

## **Part 2: I Figured This Out**

What changed?

What did you discover? What shift happened?

This is where you establish credibility - not through titles, but
through transformation.

Not: \"I learned the right strategies.\"

But: \"I realized I was chasing tactics instead of building something
people actually believed in.\"

The insight matters more than the method.

## **Part 3: Come With Me**

Where are you going now?

And how can they come with you?

This is the invitation.

Not: \"Buy my course.\"

But: \"I\'m building something for people who are tired of the old way.
Want in?\"

You\'re not selling. You\'re inviting.

## **Why Stories Create Belief Faster Than Proof**

Proof is logical.

\"Here\'s the data. Here\'s the case study. Here\'s the result.\"

Logical doesn\'t create movements.

Stories are emotional.

\"Here\'s what happened. Here\'s how it felt. Here\'s what changed.\"

Emotional creates movements.

People don\'t need to be convinced. They need to feel like you
understand them.

Stories do that. Data doesn\'t.

## **The Key Line**

People follow paths, not experts.

They don\'t need you to be the smartest.

They need you to have been where they are.

Your story isn\'t a liability. It\'s your greatest asset.

## **Key Takeaways**

Your story matters more than credentials. People follow paths, not
titles.

Expert positioning creates distance. Guide positioning creates
belonging.

The simple arc: I was here → I figured this out → Come with me. That\'s
all you need.

Stories create belief faster than proof. Emotion beats logic for
building movements.

Your past is the invitation. The struggle is what makes you relatable.

## **Your Narrative Check**

Can you clearly say:

Here\'s where I was (the struggle).

Here\'s what I figured out (the shift).

Here\'s where I\'m going (the invitation).

If not, that\'s your homework. Write it out.

This becomes the foundation of everything you share.
