Any time we enter a new market, launch a new product or test a new funnel, our
process is the same.
First, we need to get LEADS…

Step 1: Brainstorm at least 30 “Problem Statements”

Problem Statements, as the name suggests, are statements that articulate specific
issues and challenges our customers and clients face in their own words) and run a
$100 test to see which messages will ATTRACT and ENGAGE our ideal prospects.

We use tools like the Ideal Client Profiler and Core Message Canvas (with a little help
from AI tools like ChatGPT) to do the research…




…and we run very basic (some might even call them “ugly”) Impression Ad Campaigns
in Meta to determine which of these “Problem Statements” resonate the most with our
target audience based on which ads get the most clicks and engagements.




                      Examples of “ugly” Problem Statement image ads.
Step 2: Create a “Splinter Lead Magnet”

…that solves the specific issue/challenge we uncovered in our “Problem Statement”
research and testing.

We call it a “Splinter” Lead Magnet because, typically, the resource can be “splintered
off” of the existing core product or service we want to sell, meaning you don’t need to
create something new from scratch.

This not only saves you time and money, it also ensures there’s a clear “through-line”
from the free resource they just accessed to the core product or service you eventually
want them to buy.

Some examples of Splinter Lead Magnets include:

   ●​ Tools and Templates
   ●​ Swipe Files
   ●​ Worksheets/Handouts
   ●​ One-Off Tip/Recommendation
   ●​ Assessments
   ●​ Product samples

…you get the idea.

The goal of a splinter lead magnet is to be “useful but incomplete.” It’s useful
because it fully addresses the specific issue/challenge, but it’s incomplete because it
doesn’t resolve the larger issue/challenge your core offer addresses.

Step 3: Build a landing page and thank you page for the lead magnet.

We begin the build process by running a “100 Headline Test” to determine the lead
magnet title and landing page headline that is likely to perform the best, and then we
build a simple landing page based on the winner of this headline test.

      SIDE NOTE: We have tested literally thousands of landing page templates, and
      the only variables that seem to matter are the 1) title/promise of the lead magnet
      and 2) headline on the landing page, so this is where we focus 90%+ of our
      testing efforts.

Once the lead magnet and landing page are built, we can convert the “ugly” Problem
Statement ad campaigns into nicer-looking, brand-friendly ad campaigns utilizing the
copy and images from both the Problem Statement Tests as well as the “$100 Headline
Tests.” Between the tested and proven ad campaigns and the tested and proven landing
pages (both of which cost us less than $200 to validate), we can know with confidence
that quality leads will begin flowing into our database.

Step 4: Run a “Quick-Cash Handraiser Campaign” to test the lead
quality and generate an immediate ROI.

It’s a fact: Only 3 - 6% of the leads in your database will be ready to buy at any given
moment. The problem is, we don’t know who these 3 - 6% are, and that’s where the
“handraiser campaign” comes in.

As the name suggests, the goal of the “handraiser campaign” is to get the leads who
are ready, willing, and able to take action now to “raise their hand” and confirm they are
interested in learning more. Not only is this a great way to confirm that the leads you’re
getting are targeted and qualified, but it’s also a great way to generate an immediate
ROI from your lead generation efforts.

These handraiser campaigns all follow the same formula…

       I’m looking for X [ideal clients] who want [known desired end result] in the
       next XX days, without/with [something they DON’T want/something they
       DO want].

       Just reply to this email with “Tell me more” and I’ll send you the details.

For example…

       I’m looking for 10 stay-at-home moms who want to lose 10 - 15 pounds in
       the next 6 weeks only doing exercises you can do with your kids or while
       they’re napping (so no need to go to a gym or change your schedule).

       If you’re interested, just reply to this email with “Tell me more” and I’ll send
       you the details.

There are many variations of the “Quick-Cash Handraiser Campaign,” but I think you
get the idea:

   ●​ Make a direct promise…
   ●​ To a specific person, and…
   ●​ Give a clear call to action.

The 3 - 6% of your leads who are ready now will reply, and when they do you can send
them a link to your sales page, have them book a meeting, or do whatever you would
typically do to sell your core offer.

Eventually, we’ll incorporate this “Quick-Cash Handraiser Campaign” throughout our
“First 30” indoctrination series (see below), but in these early stages, we prefer to send
these handraiser emails manually any time we need to create a spike in sales.

By executing the steps above (and specifically the “Problem Statement Ads” and “$100
Headline Test”), we’re typically able to increase traffic and leads by 50 - 100%.

Step 6: Create gated and ungated Pillar Content…

…that educates your audience on what you do and bonds them to your brand.

Let me ask you a question…

What’s the one piece of content you wished all your leads and visitors could see before

It could be a:

       SIDE NOTE: The report you’re reading right now is an example of Pillar

Once we determine what our Pillar Content should be, we create an ungated version
(i.e. a YouTube video where the viewer does NOT need to opt-in to access) and a gated
version (i.e. a premium version such as a webinar, assessment, or special report that
users must register for to access), both of which should point back to one another as
well as to your core product/service.

Said another way, the ungated version of your pillar content should encourage people to
opt-in for the gated version, and the gated version should encourage people to
consume the ungated version if they haven’t yet.

Again, the primary goal is CONSUMPTION of this content, because we know this
content will lead to more sales.

And speaking of more sales, both the versions of your Pillar Content (gated and
ungated) should direct viewers to your core offer and present it as the “next logical step”

   1.​ Raise a bunch of capital so you can afford to lose money on every sale and

   2.​ Increase your average customer value so you can afford to invest more

Think about it. If you can DOUBLE the value of each and every customer, you can

margin.

It’s just math!

Here’s how we do it…

Step 7: Add upsells, cross-sells, slack adjusters and other “Margin
Maximizers” with the goal of DOUBLING your average customer value
(ACV).

There are literally hundreds of tactics you can deploy to increase your average
customer value, but the simplest (and most consistently effective) is to create an upsell
that is 10X more expensive than your current average order but only appeals to the
10% of your customers/clients who are most engaged and enthusiastic.

Do the math…

If you can get 10% of your customers to spend 10X more than the average, your total
average customer value will DOUBLE.

That’s why we call these kinds of offers: “10X for 10% Margin Maximizers.”

These 10X for 10% upsell offers tend to fall into one or more of the following categories:
   1.​ SPEED: Get them the promised result FASTER. ​
       (ex. faster delivery, VIP access, more powerful features, pre-built bundles,
       done-for-them services, etc.)

   2.​ AUTOMATION: Get them the promised result with LESS WORK. ​
       (ex. setup/assembly, done-for-them services, software, etc.)

   3.​ ACCESS: Get them the promised result while enjoying a HIGHER STATUS
       experience.​
       (ex. mastermind/coaching, 24/7 support, reply time guarantee, “Backstage”
       access, better seating, early access, etc.)

The best upsell offers will combine one or more of these categories, and when executed
properly, it’s common for us to be able to increase average customer values by 50 -
100% by implementing just one or two “10X for 10” upsells.

                          What Is a Growth Flywheel?
A Growth Flywheel is a set of three (3) integrated marketing assets that systemize the
customer acquisition process so growth becomes predictable, repeatable, and scalable.

These three assets are:

   1.​ Growth Engine - a visual flowchart of the customer acquisition process

   2.​ Growth Scorecard - a metrics dashboard that clarifies KPIs must be improved to

   3.​ Growth Plan - a strategic plan that outlines the projects and key initiatives that
       must be completed over the next 90 days to optimize critical KPIs and unblock
       bottlenecks in the Growth Engine
