**Email #1 -- Send Immediately [(TO NEW SUBSCRIBERS ONLY)]{.mark}**

Ever heard the old saying... "You only get one shot at a first
impression!"?

That's exactly what the welcome mail is... it's your ONE and only shot
to make a strong first impression, connect with your new subscriber, and
stand out in their mind (AND INBOX). The job of the welcome mail is
to...

-   Introduce yourself and your company to your new subscriber

-   Align yourself with them

-   Set expectations for exactly what they will receive now that they're
    "on your list"

-   Restate the benefits of being a subscriber

-   Get them to make micro-commitments

-   And "open a loop" that captures and holds their attention

In short, this email is ALL about engagement....

**Email #2 -- Send 1 day after the welcome email**

The following email is designed to welcome new subscribers and expose
them to your best content. This series is especially ideal for brands
that have invested heavily in content marketing and have evergreen
articles, videos, and even podcasts they want to showcase.

**NOTE:** Since this series is designed to be stacked with a "Welcome
Mail," it's best if the welcome mail that precedes this series can
include a P.S. (or P.P.S.) that alludes to the fact that they're getting
some of your best content, otherwise the recommended subject lines
become incongruent and will need to be changed.

**SUBJECT:** I'm keeping my promise...

**SUBJECT:** Here's your free gift (as promised)
