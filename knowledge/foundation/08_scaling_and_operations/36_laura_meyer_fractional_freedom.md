## PHASE 1: CONSULTING OFFER JUMPSTART

### 1.1 Consulting Foundations

| Element | Question |
|---------|----------|
| Niche | Who exactly do you serve? |
| Outcome | What single, measurable result do you deliver? |
| Methodology | What proven process do you use? |

### 1.2 Rules for Success

| Rule | Principle |
|------|-----------|
| 1 | Focus on revenue-generating activities only |
| 2 | Never work for free – always have clear scope |
| 3 | Charge for outcomes, not hours |
| 4 | Document everything – create systems from day one |
| 5 | Always under-promise and over-deliver |

### 1.3 The Magic of a Single Objective

Your consulting offer should focus on **one clear objective**.

**Examples:**
- "Double your email list in 90 days"
- "Launch your first evergreen funnel"
- "Stop burning domains and scale to 500k emails/month"

### 1.4 Consulting Brand & Messaging

- Position as **"thought partner"** – not just service provider
- Use Posse Eye framework for brand voice
- Create one-page **Consulting Offer Overview**:
  - Single objective
  - Methodology (3-5 steps)
  - Who it's for
  - Deliverables
  - Investment range

### 1.5 Content Strategy for High-Value Clients

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Topic pillars | Problems ideal clients face |
| Content types | Case studies, "how we think about X", behind-the-scenes |
| Lead magnet | Video training or PDF solving specific problem |

## PHASE 2: ANALYZE ASSETS & FIND HIDDEN MONEY

### 2.1 The Power of Existing Assets

| Asset Type | Examples |
|------------|----------|
| Owned | Email list, traffic sources, customer data |
| Content | Blog posts, videos, lead magnets |
| Relationships | Past customers, partnerships, affiliates |
| Infrastructure | Funnels, automations, systems |

**Look for underutilized assets:**
- Email list rarely emailed
- Webinar that could be evergreen
- Lead magnet that could be optimized
- Past customers not re-engaged

### 2.2 Repeatable Data Request System

**Data Request Checklist:**

| Category | Data Points |
|----------|-------------|
| Revenue | Monthly revenue, average order value, customer LTV |
| Traffic | Visitors, sources, conversion rates |
| Email | List size, open rates, click rates, revenue per email |
| Offers | Products, pricing, conversion rates |
| Customers | Demographics, purchase history, churn rate |

**Process:**
1. Send spreadsheet template
2. 30-minute "data deep dive" call
3. Identify top 3 opportunities

### 2.3 Choosing the Best Growth Path

**Diagnose the bottleneck:**

| If Problem Is... | Focus On... |
|------------------|-------------|
| Traffic | Lead generation, ads, content |
| Conversion | Funnel optimization, copy, offer |
| Follow-up | Email sequences, retargeting |
| Retention | Customer experience, upsells |

**Prioritize:** Fastest win with least effort

### 2.4 Present 3 Options

| Option | Timeline | Investment |
|--------|----------|------------|
| Quick win | 2-4 weeks | Low |
| Medium-term growth | 1-3 months | Medium |
| Long-term transformation | 3-12 months | High |

### 2.5 Revenue Drivers vs Vanity Metrics

| Revenue Drivers | Vanity Metrics |
|-----------------|----------------|
| Revenue per subscriber | Open rates |
| Conversion rate | Follower count |
| Average order value | Likes |
| Customer lifetime value | Comments |

## PHASE 5: CLIENT ACQUISITION SYSTEM

### 5.1 Lead Generation Method

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Create "Problem-Aware" Lead Magnet |
| 2 | Drive traffic (organic, network, LinkedIn) |
| 3 | Nurture with 3-email sequence |
| 4 | Invite to Application Call |

### 5.2 Application Funnel

**Instead of "Book a Call" – use application first.**

**Qualifying Questions:**
- What's your biggest challenge?
- What have you tried?
- What's your budget range?
- Are you ready to invest in a solution?
- What would success look like?

**Only qualified leads → your calendar.**

### 5.3 On-Demand Video Training (Lead Magnet)

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Length | 20-30 minutes |
| Structure | Problem → Solution → Case study → CTA |
| CTA | "If you want personalized help, apply here" |

### 5.4 Templates to Create

| Template | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Lead magnet outline | Attract qualified leads |
| Email nurture sequence | Build trust |
| Application form | Qualify leads |
| Discovery call agenda | Run productive calls |
| Proposal template | Present offer |
