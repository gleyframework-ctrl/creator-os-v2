Pre-Work Steps:

1) You Will Need A ChatGPT Plus Account, at least for the initial setup ($20 p/m)

2) Do the entire Process start to finish in a SINGLE chat thread

3) When the reports have been generated, upload them into a single doc/folder that you can
download from once finished

4) You Will NEED at minimum 5x pieces of longer form content that you have personally
created (video or written) - that AI didn't touch yet


Extra Tips & Tricks:

    Use the microphone feature inside GPT to finish the interview process faster instead of
    typing

    Be as in-depth as possible with your answers & interview process - the better the input
    that you give, the better output you get
