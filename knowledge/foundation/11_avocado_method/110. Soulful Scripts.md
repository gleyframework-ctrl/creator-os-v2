**[INSIGHT & ENROLMENT CALL]{.underline}**

### **S.O.U.L.F.U.L. Sales Steps**

1.  **[Stillness:]{.underline}** Before launching into any sales
    > activities whether it is on the phone or in chat it's important to
    > be in the right frame of mind and bring the right energy. Take 90
    > Seconds to breathe, get present and commit to being of service,
    > listening deeply and creating win-win scenarios.

2.  **[Overview:]{.underline}** Build Rapport, Connection, Take the lead
    > and tell the prospect what they can expect on this call.

3.  **[Understanding:]{.underline}** Ask the questions to understand
    > what it is they want to achieve, where they are at now and what
    > obstacles they see might be in the way.

> **(End of insight call)**

---\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\--

4.  **[Listen for Impact:]{.underline}** Ask the deeper questions, what,
    > how, why, when, to understand the emotional reasons for them and
    > gauge their level of commitment.

5.  **[Frame:]{.underline}** Pivot the conversation by recreating them
    > and ensuring that they feel understood. Reveal to them your
    > diagnosis of the problems and offer them suggestions on what their
    > next steps should be.

6.  **[Underwrite:]{.underline}** This is where a bit of a power dynamic
    > shift happens as you begin asking them if they believe they
    > "**qualify"** to work with you. If they are a good fit client,
    > you'd like to work with them and you can actually help them then
    > you can proceed to frame how your offer is different and set
    > yourself up to ask permission to share.

7.  **[Love it or Leave it:]{.underline}** Ask them if they would like
    > to hear about your program or offer and how it is you help
    > clients. Speak to the 3 big pillars, don't get lost in the details
    > and share the elements of your program that would mean the most
    > for them and be the most helpful and impactful. Remember to check
    > in and ask " does that make sense?" or do you have any questions
    > about that.

> **7a.** Once you are finished sharing your offer - Recap the problems
> that you solve and ask if they have any questions. They will probably
> ask how much does it cost. Before you reveal the price you can say ok
> awesome happy to share what the investment is ... but let me ask you
> first "assuming that the logistics and investment made sense... is
> this something that you feel is a fit and could see yourself moving
> forward with?"
>
> **7b.** If they say yes proceed to share the price by framing it
> against everything they get. Ok cool so just to recap what you get
> is.... And the investment is.....
>
> **Then be quiet.**

8.  **Handle objections**
