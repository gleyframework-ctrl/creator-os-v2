ABOUT THIS LESSON
One of the biggest benefits of structuring your business in a linear fashion is the ability to have a crystal clear map of
what you need to do and where.
It also makes pre-selling inside your content & messaging 100x easier.

The more specific you can become inside your copy & the problems you’re solving - the more seamless the sales
process becomes.
The Core Focus of the organic FB System Should be group growth. Building out the tribe of people & a group of your
own.

MOST POWERFUL Aspect
      This is YOUR space, you’re the leader, everyone in there is likely looking at you for advice. Your Rules.
      It adds a layer of exclusivity to your business, If you’re building out content on your profile or other

      platforms, and you have the training inside your groups it’s going to make people feel FOMO for not being
      inside.
      It gives you a different position for marketing, you can push your “free” group on the front end of everything

      you’re doing, while still getting exposure to your affiliate links, Brand & content, You can get market research &
      build your email list all at once.
      Eventually, It will grow by itself, If your group is set up properly, You will get organic in-bound leads daily.
      Interested in the topic you’re group is about.
      It will also give you an infinite amount of Inbound conversations, Most of your sales with FB will be done

      through messenger & instead of scouring other people’s groups for leads or people who’d be interested, you’ll
      be able to add & dm people directly from your own group. Significantly increases the chances of a relationship
      enveloping because they already know who you are.

Maximizing Your Content Based On Your Goals & What's Going On In Your Day-To-Day Life!
Content is by far one of the part most “struggle with” and it’s a hard topic to teach because everyone's content is &
should be different.
And instead of feeling discouraged because it’s not the same as the masses.. Lean into it. Be different. Find your
voice. Style. And way of writing.
It is ONE of the most important components to growing your business. Especially the longer you do it & the more

consistent you’re posting because it all has leverage.
Content Types:
        Straight offer post - listing out product features / different outcomes/transformations / proof of concept
        Value Posts - can be anything from a Quote, breakthrough, lesson / educational post. Sharing a takeaway /
        causing a belief shift.
        Results - Monetary, non-monetary, student wins (also monetary & not) Community results - Connected with
        someone who can talk about them (wouldn’t post their screenshots, but you can leverage them) IE - I’m a part
        of a masterclass & its students are achieving XYZ
        Lives / Training (Would usually only recommend doing them inside your group) so you have an angle to
        promote your group / Something to “give away” upon entry. Can also help in the future because you can start
        tagging on training you’ve done vs explaining everything inside messenger
        Lead Gen / Offer Posts highlighting pain points and what solutions your products can solve. Longer posts will
        usually have less engagement - but the quality of those leads are higher (they will know why they’re there,
        commenting because they know your making an offer) Shorter Posts (color blocks) - Engagement will be
        higher on these - but a lot of the people who engage are usually looking for free info / a lead magnet a video.
        More info.. You can use these to move into sales convos / your offer the conversion process will just be more
        intensive on your part. These also work really well to get more people into your group/tag people in your group
        on a training. EX - Who hasn’t checked out my training on how to go from x to x without x.
