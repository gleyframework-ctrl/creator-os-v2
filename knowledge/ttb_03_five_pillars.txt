CHUNK: The 5 Pillars & the 90-Day Roadmap
CATEGORY: builder
SOURCE: TTB System Overview — 5 Pillars + 90-Day Plan
---
Time to Build is five interconnected pillars executed across a 90-day roadmap. The order is the strategy — skipping pillars is exactly how people stay stuck.
PILLAR 1 — BUILD THE OFFER (Days 1-30): Turn your skill into a product people pay for (course, coaching, membership, cohort). Define exactly who it is for — and who it is NOT for. Choose your delivery model. Price with confidence, not guesswork. Goal: an offer ready to sell.
PILLAR 2 — BUILD THE AUDIENCE (Days 30-60): Create content that attracts potential buyers, not just viewers. Build an email list you own instead of rented followers. Convert followers into waiting buyers. Goal: an audience that wants your offer BEFORE you launch.
PILLAR 3 — GENERATE PROFIT (Days 60-90): Run a simple launch — one outcome, one audience. Make real sales. Collect feedback. Improve the offer. Goal: your first $2,000+ and your investment earned back.
PILLAR 4 — TRUST: The engine under all three pillars. A profitable personal brand is the ability to build trust at scale.
PILLAR 5 — REPEAT & SCALE: Continuous improvement, regular launches, sustainable growth toward $10,000+/month.
---
WHY THE ORDER MATTERS
Skip Pillar 1 and create content first = the Content-First trap (followers who never buy). Skip Pillar 2 and run ads to strangers = the Business-First trap (sales that die when ads stop). Offer first, audience second, profit third.
---
EXAMPLE (results from doing the work — examples, never promises): Ani made $10,000 in 8 weeks with only 2,200 followers. Stephen made $101,000 in his first launch with 4,500 followers. Both followed the pillar order instead of chasing audience size first.
