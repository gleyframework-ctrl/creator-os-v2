## 1. THE 63-STEP PROFIT OPTIMIZER

A systematic checklist for improving profitability across six categories.

### 1.1 Time-Based Profit Improvements

| Action | Target Saving |
|--------|---------------|
| Get 70% of data needed vs 100% (stop analysis paralysis) | 30% time reduction |
| Cut 50% of accounting resources - replace with profit-focused people | 50% of current cost |
| Trade forecasts for rough budgets | 100% of forecasting time |
| Set meetings for minimum time needed, not default 1 hour | 50% of meeting time |
| Call 5-minute meetings to make decisions, not discuss possibilities | Varies |
| Eliminate all written/computerized reports; make people justify adding them back | 60-90% of reporting time |

### 1.2 Labor-Based Profit Improvements

| Action | Target Saving |
|--------|---------------|
| Average is 3 managers for every 1 needed - eliminate 2/3 | 67% of manager costs |
| Hiring freeze on non-profit-making staff without CEO approval | 100% of new non-revenue staff |
| No new outside contractors/consultants without CEO approval | 100% of new consultant fees |
| Reduce outside contractors by 50% | 50% of total consultant fees |
| No new temps without CEO approval | 100% of new temp fees |
| R&D priority: 1) lower cost, 2) customize, 3) improve existing, 4) new products | Significant savings |
| Pay profit-contributing employees 30% over market, others 10% | Better performance |
| Benefits: provide only what employees value; cut 100% of non-valued | 100% of non-valued costs |
| No automatic bonuses - irregular, merit-based only; give titles over bonuses | Better culture |
| Periodic headcount reductions - target 25% of bottom performers | 25% of payroll |

### 1.3 General Profit Improvements

| Action | Target Saving |
|--------|---------------|
| E2P: Turn expenses into profit centers | 100% of expense becomes profit |
| Best Month Ever: deconstruct and replicate | Profit = best month x 12 |
| Increase prices - test 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18% increases | 100% of increase = profit |
| Extend value ladder - add ultra-high-price products | 10X pricing vs lowest |
| Use "As-Applied" pricing - charge more based on packaging | 2X to 10X current value |
| Decrease voluntary churn - 13-step plan | 50% of churn cost |
| Decrease involuntary churn - 5-step plan | 80% of churn cost |
| Reduce office space by 50% | 50% of rent/mortgage |
| Move from urban to suburban location | 25% savings |
| Reduce new computer purchases 100% | 100% of new computer cost |
| Cut office supplies expense by 40% | 40% of current cost |
| Cut digital subscriptions 50% + review quarterly | 50% of current cost |
| Cut SaaS subscriptions 50% + review quarterly | 50% of current cost |
| Cut trade association memberships 50% | 50% of current cost |
| Cut business travel expenses 50% | 50% of travel cost |
| Cut air travel by 25% - coach vs first class | 40% of airfare |
| Cut lodging by 40% | 40% of lodging |
| Bias to cut every cost - if you don't know where revenue lost, cut it | Varies |
| Pass credit card fees to customers via surcharge | 100% of merchant fees |
| Switch customers from credit card to ACH | 90% of merchant fees |
| Renegotiate merchant fees | 20% of current fees |

### 1.4 Supply + COGS Profit Improvements

| Action | Target Saving |
|--------|---------------|
| Cut express messenger services unless justified | 100% of current cost |
| Price freezes: send CEO letter saying no increases for 12-18 months | 75% of supplier increases |
| Tell supplier any price increase triggers competitive bid | Leverage |
| Request 15% immediate reduction in purchased products | 15% x 80% of spend |
| Request 30% immediate reduction in purchased services | 30% x 80% of spend |
| Put top 50 expenses to competitive bid once per year | 20% of each expense |
| Find who competitors buy from and quote them | 20% of all operating expenses |
| Cancel all equipment maintenance contracts - self-insure | 100% of maintenance contracts |
| Cancel 75% of supplier subscription contracts | 75% of subscription costs |
| Downgrade phone systems to only what's needed | 25% of phone costs |
| Review checks 2x month for 30 minutes to see ALL expenses | 10% of operating expenses |

### 1.5 CapEx-Based Profit Improvements

| Action | Target Saving |
|--------|---------------|
| Freeze all computer purchases - determine actual usage | 100% of new computer costs |
| Make people ask boss for furniture and fixtures | 100% of new furniture |
| Make people ask boss for any new equipment | 100% of new equipment |
| Quantify productivity gains vs cap-ex investment | 50% of cap-ex |
| Eliminate wasted product features | 25% of product cost |

### 1.6 Cash-Based Profit Improvements

| Action | Target Saving |
|--------|---------------|
| Extend accounts payable to 30, 45, 60, 90, 120, 180 days | 50% of AP deferred 3 months |
| Deplete inventory to JIT or minimum days needed | 20% of inventory cost |
| Add process to verify inventory before ordering more | 100% of duplicate orders |
| Reduce inventory on hand by 15 days | 50% of inventory cost |
| Negotiate 100% merchant reserve releases | 100% of reserves |
| Pre-capture COGS when customer places order | 100% of COGS financed by customer |

## 5. EXPENSES TO PROFITS (E2P)

### 5.1 Core Concept

Your biggest expenses can become your biggest profit centers.

### 5.2 Process

1. Identify biggest expenses
2. Ask: "How can we turn this cost center into a revenue center?"
3. Package and sell what you're already doing

### 5.3 Examples

**Content Team Example:**
- Cost: $50,000/year
- Creates content that drives traffic
- Package as course/templates/service
- Generate: $300,000 revenue with $91,000 margin

- Now: $45B profit center
- Earns 10.7X the profit margin on 86.8% less revenue than retail

## 7. DEAL-STACKING TECHNIQUES

### 7.1 Financing Methods

| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Seller financing | 80% of deal |
| Earnout | 10% based on performance |
| Integrator equity | Partner runs business |
| Sponsor sales | Sell sponsorships to cover cost |
| Debt assumption | Take over existing debt |
| Deferred down payment | Pay later |
| Revenue-based financing | Pay from future revenue |

### 7.2 Deal Stack Worksheet

Calculate:

- Purchase price
- Less seller financing
- Less earnout
- Less integrator investments
- Less sponsorship sales
- Less investor funding
- = Cash needed at closing (often $0)

## 9. BEST MONTH EVER EXERCISE

### 9.1 Process

1. Identify your highest-profit month ever
2. Deconstruct every element:
   - What offers were you selling?
   - What traffic sources were working?
   - What was your pricing?
   - What was your messaging?
   - What was your close rate?
3. Replicate across all months

### 9.2 Income Engine Application

Even if your best month was small:
- What worked?
- What was different?
- How can you repeat it?
