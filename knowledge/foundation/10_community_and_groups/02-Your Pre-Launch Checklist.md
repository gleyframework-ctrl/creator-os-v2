Pre-Launch Checklist
Now, this is everything we’ve personally all year with over 10 different offers & joint ventures this year and how we’ve
ended up doing the SAME strategy as a whole - every single time. (Cash Mullet - Some type of live anchor post with
an invitation on the back end)
       Taking An Idea & Turning It Into An Income
       Packaging It Into An Offer & An Asset That Will Capture Our Energy
       How We PRE-SELL Every Offer We Launch So We Can Get Paid To Create

Things YOU NEED Prior

      An audience - Even if it’s small - you can still do this
      Some goodwill built-up (If you’ve been selling hard for the next couple of weeks you don’t want to roll into this
      right away)

Why This Works:

      New
      Exclusivity
      Scarcity
      Time / Limited Spots
      Personal - Custom
      Bonuses / Extra Value

Goal/Outcome

      Get paid to create an asset - That will allow you to bolt it onto any relevant affiliate product & increase the
      perceived value & make you stand out from other affiliates.
      Prioritize YOU, building your own pool of buyers - whom you can start building long-standing relationships
      with, because they’ll like to buy additional products from you
      Kickstart a snowball of momentum by stacking monetary wins, testimonials & create more leverage for you to
      go out there and have more angles in your content

Picking The Product / Pricing

       10 spots upon purchase of $100 product
       5 Spots Upon purchase of $1,000 product
Chose something just a bit above what you feel you can accomplish - if you’ve yet to snag a few high ticket sales,
don’t be afraid to do this on the back end of a lower ticket affiliate program.
You have some results under your belt go bigger in price but for less people

Look for AMMO/ideas/things to run your campaign:

      Your own results & strategy / unique process
      Client results or community results of the said process
      Any & All proof of concept you can leverage (IE - a 4 week intensive how I grew my Facebook group by X)
      Things you’re seeing the market that you have the ability to solve
      Look at anything and everything that you already have that you could spin off / leverage into an asset &
      change the delivery (docs - Resources - Work Smarter not harder)

Putting together the timeline:

Depending on what season you’re business is in - or if you’re coming off a super hard promo - you’ll need to build
that goodwill back up..
But giving yourself enough time to run the campaign
Would aim anywhere between 10-30 days for a full-on push can be longer if you go very heavy into a cash mullet
strategy (Lots of value of front - sales on back)
Note: Once you start hitting the content hard about promo - Keep the Date vague/open on the back - Give you
audience time to acclimate/give yourself the ability to get a temp check & figure out what type of content will
resonate best.
Can lean into X number of spots - but don’t start openly hammering “this is gone in 5 days - unless you’re getting
some eyeballs/hand raisers”
Can also go vague on spots until you have a good gauge on the interest of your audience so you can change it as
you go if needed (Power in publicly selling your offer completely out because it’ll start your next campaign off with
fomo)
*Do not beat yourself if you don’t sell out your first campaign though* It happens - we had a hard time filling up all
the spots for GJ launch

Decide On Delivery

Now if the purpose of rolling out your micro launch is to create an asset - That you can either sell as a standalone
product or add as a bonus on top of an affiliate product would usually look at delivering inside a Private Buyer Group
on a live or prerecorded
Either way - PRESELL spots - AKA Sell spots before you start doing the training
