Lesson 8: [[The 7-Step Install
Plan]{.underline}](https://www.skool.com/group-juice/classroom/af734968?md=90e295d6688842ed8a6d9be36404ab30)

This is not a content challenge.

It\'s not about posting more or being perfect.

This is about cleaning up your group. Putting the right stuff in place.
And getting momentum moving.

By the end:

Your group makes sense. Sales feel easy. DMs feel natural. You stop
guessing what to do.

Don\'t skip steps. Don\'t overbuild. Don\'t add extra stuff.

Here\'s the install:

Step 1 --- Pick your One Group. Stop splitting attention.

Step 2 --- Clean the space. One outcome. Simple description. Remove the
clutter.

Step 3 --- Fix your Welcome Post. Make it clear why they\'re here and
what comes next.

Step 4 --- Pin only 3 things mentioned in the lesson

Step 5 --- Post one quick win. Remove friction. Build trust.

Step 6 --- DM new members. Watch for signals. No selling yet.

Step 7 --- Make the next step obvious. Guide people forward.

You don\'t win by doing more. You win by putting the right stuff in
place once and letting it work.
