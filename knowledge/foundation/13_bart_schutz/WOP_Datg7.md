This seventh Deep Dive is completely dedicated to how we are influenced by product, placement
and promotion. In day 6 we distinguished 2 types of external influencers. First, there is a huge bunch
of ‘external factors’ that out of our span-of-control. As a marketer, you can take these factors into
account, but you cannot influence them. These factors were the main subject of day 6 “Social &
Environment” (from context, to the social, cultural, macro and competitive environment).

Luckily for you, there are also external factors that you as a marketer can influence. This 7th day
will be all about external influencers that you are in control of (and probably familiar with). Since your
customers’ decision to buy, use and promote your products depends – at least science says so –
partly on your marketing efforts. So today we go back to the good old’ 3- of the 4P’s of Marketing
(product, placement & promotion. Price will be discussed in day 8). This is a huge field in research
going on, we see a growth of applying neurosciences and behavioral sciences. The insights we
already had are now supported by neuroscientific evidence, because we are able to measure brain
activity. This is giving us lots of insights in what drives our behavior.

=> So, welcome on more familiar ground! But… your customer is still a brain. So of course, we’ll
deal with the Marketing dimensions from a more psychological point of view; Therefore, we prefer to
refer to day 7 as “Teaching Brain Science to Marketing”
