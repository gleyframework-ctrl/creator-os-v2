ABOUT THIS LESSON
The seriously slept on concept of you simply using your content to share portions of your life & treat it as your digital
journal. Why?

      You simply shedding light on where you’re at now & what you’re doing as you’re doing it will start to take
      people along on this journey with you, they’re naturally going to feel a part of and become inspired just by you
      doing things they’ve wanted to.

      It’s a low-pressure  way to approach your content, you don’t need to be some expert with 100k’s in the bank
      already or pulling in huge numbers, you’re simply, YOU building your thing & taking people along the way with
      you, which also mean those who are a couple of steps behind you will naturally see you as the expert.

      Itdoes a kick-ass job at building deeper relationships with people because there is a layer of intimacy in your
      content (not all business) - which is a REQUIRED ingredient to sell products to anyone, a level of trust/
      relationship established.

EVERYTHING CAN BE CONTENT.

A big disconnect is the fact that you can’t “talk about content outside your niche” because it’s not relevant to the
product you’re selling.

But we’re in the games of helping people build personal brands, and as the name implies, personality & YOU need to
be in it.

You never know what you post that’s going to strike up an interest from someone & they’ll follow you / come into
your world and end up buying from you.

Ex: someone sees you posting about your favorite foods then a few days later they see you’re creating content about
how you’re building a business online.

You just sparked a business conversation indirectly off of a lifestyle piece of content.
