## 1. THE S.I.N. OFFER FRAMEWORK

### 1.1 What is an Offer?

An offer is EVERYTHING your prospect gets (good and bad) and everything they must give to get it.

| Good | Bad |
|------|-----|
| Product features & benefits | Risk (financial, performance, social) |
| Premiums & bonuses | Work, effort, time |
| Guarantees | Fear, discomfort |
| Payment terms | |

**Key Insight:** Offer determines 40% of campaign success.

Audience = 40%, Copy/Model/Automation = 20%

---

### 1.2 The S.I.N. Acronym

| Letter | Meaning | Description |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| S | Superior | Objectively better than any competitor's offer |
| I | Irresistible | Value feels lopsided in prospect's favor |
| N | No-Brainer | Perceived risk eliminated. They'd be crazy to say no. |

**Goal:** Prospect thinks "Why wouldn't I do this? I'd be crazy not to."

## 11. THE GOLDEN RATIO - CPA vs AOV

**Golden Ratio = CPA = AOV**

When Cost Per Acquisition equals Average Order Value, you acquire customers for FREE. Scale infinitely.

### 11.1 Calculating CPA

CPA = Total ad spend ÷ Number of new customers

**Example:**

$1,000 ad spend ÷ 10 customers = $100 CPA

### 11.2 Calculating AOV

AOV = Total revenue from front-end campaign ÷ Number of customers

**Example:**

$1,500 revenue ÷ 10 customers = $150 AOV

### 11.3 Two Levers to Hit the Ratio

| Lever | How | Difficulty |
|-------|-----|------------|
| Decrease CPA | Increase sales conversion rate | Harder (deals with prospects) |
| Increase AOV | Add bumps, upsells, raise prices | Easier (deals with buyers) |

**Focus on AOV.** It's more within your control.

## 12. MAXIMUM CHECKOUTS - REDUCING ABANDONMENT

### 12.1 What is Order Form Abandonment?

When prospect clicks "add to cart" but doesn't complete checkout.

**Average abandonment rate: 60-70%** (higher on mobile)

### 12.2 How to Calculate

Abandonment Rate = 1 - (Orders Completed ÷ Checkouts Initiated)

**Example:**

300 checkouts initiated, 100 completed

100 ÷ 300 = 0.33

1 - 0.33 = 0.67 → **67% abandonment rate**

### 12.3 The Impact of Reducing Abandonment

| Metric | Before | After (3% reduction) |
|--------|--------|---------------------|
| Checkouts initiated | 1,000 | 1,000 |
| Abandonment rate | 70% | 67% |
| Completed orders | 300 | 330 |
| AOV | $50 | $50 |
| Revenue | $15,000 | $16,500 |

**A 3% reduction = 10% more customers and revenue**

### 12.4 5 Reasons People Abandon (and Fixes)

| Reason | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Security concerns | Add SSL, trust badges, security icons, testimonials near payment |
| Order form length | Keep only essential fields (Name, Email, Payment) |
| Unexpected expenses | Show full price upfront; no surprise fees |
| Additional offers (bumps) | Monitor impact; test different bump or remove |
| Price not clear before clicking | Show price on button or bring to price summary |

## 13. AOV BOOSTERS - 7 STRATEGIES

### 13.1 Strategy 1: Increase Your Prices

Test increments: $79 → $99 → $129 → $199

**Key Insight:** Goal is contribution to AOV, not conversion rate.

**Formula:**

Contribution to AOV = Price × Conversion Rate

**Example:**

- Upsell at $79 converts 6% = $4.74 contribution
- Upsell at $199 converts 3% = $5.97 contribution

The $199 wins despite lower conversion.

---

### 13.2 Strategy 2: Increase Quantity of Offers

Add more upsells, downsells, and bumps.

**Guideline:** Once customer says no to two offers, end the sequence.

---

### 13.3 Strategy 3: Impulse Bump Offers

Checkbox offer on order form. Should:
- Be small percentage of core price
- Require little copy to explain
- Be congruent with main offer
- Feel like impulse purchase

**Performance Target:** 30-40% take rate. 50%+ is gold.

---

### 13.4 Strategy 4: Order Form Upgrades (Multiple Tiers)

Present higher-priced options only on order form.

**Example:**

- Basic: $47 (marketed)
- Advanced: $79 (includes extra bonuses)
- Expert: $129 (includes even more)

Use "Decoy Effect" - middle option makes higher option look like a steal.

---

### 13.5 Strategy 5: Upsells (Post-Purchase)

One-time offers after initial purchase. Should be:
- Congruent (same transformation, deeper)
- High perceived value
- Often priced higher than core offer

---

### 13.6 Strategy 6: Downsells

If they reject upsell, offer stripped-down version at lower price.

---

### 13.7 Strategy 7: Thank You Page Offers

After purchase, offer another relevant product. Pure profit (no ad cost).
