# **CONTENT FRAMEWORK MASTER COLLECTION**

**7 Plug-and-Play Templates for Your 90-Minute Daily System**

## **How To Use These Frameworks**

These frameworks are designed to plug directly into your custom AI
writer. Each one gives you a repeatable structure for creating content
that builds trust, shows authority, and drives action.

**The Simple Process:**

1.  Pick the framework that matches what you want to say

2.  Copy the prompt starter into your AI writer

3.  Paste the full framework template

4.  Let it generate, then tweak to sound like you

**Quick Reference:**

-   **The Connection Post** - Share transformation stories that connect
    > and convert

-   **The Authority Post** - Drop lessons and strategies backed by
    > experience

-   **The Results Post** - Show results without hype, position as the
    > expert

-   **The Value Post** - Teach something practical, shift a perspective

-   **The Polarizing Post** - Call out industry BS, polarize with
    > purpose

-   **The Thought Leadership Post** - Share observations and takeaways
    > from your lens

-   **The Lead Gen Post** - Drive inbound DMs with your free resources

## **1. THE CONNECTION POST**

***Use when:** You want to share your journey, client wins, or hard
lessons learned. This creates emotional, story-driven content that
connects and converts.*

**Prompt Starter:** Write me a social media post for \[platform\]
sharing \[topic/pain/outcome\] following this framework:

**Step 1: The Hook (Pain or Gain)**

Lead with a hard-hitting line that speaks to a painful struggle or
desirable result.

*Examples:*

-   \"I almost quit trying to grow my business 3 separate times\...\"

-   \"This one shift helped me go from 200 views to \$2,000 in sales.\"

**Step 2: Amplify the Emotion**

Make the pain/gain real. Describe the emotion, confusion, or
frustration.

*Examples:*

-   \"I\'d wake up at 5am to create content\... and still feel
    > invisible.\"

-   \"It wasn\'t even about the money at that point - I just wanted
    > something to work.\"

**Step 3: The Story (Before State)**

Tell the story that led up to the problem or win. Use 1-2 short
paragraphs. Keep it simple. Be human.

*Example:*

-   \"I was trying everything - Reels, hashtags, cold DMs\... nothing
    > stuck. I felt like I was always starting over.\"

**Step 4: The Transformation (After State)**

Where are you now? Describe what changed, what\'s different, how
life/business feels post-shift.

*Example:*

-   \"Now I wake up to messages from people who are already sold - no
    > calls, no begging, just a simple process that works.\"

**Step 5: The Shift (Aha Moment)**

What changed your perspective? What realization does your audience need
to experience too?

*Examples:*

-   \"I didn\'t need more strategies\... I needed one system that fit my
    > life.\"

-   \"Turns out, my content wasn\'t the problem - my positioning was.\"

**Step 6: The Action (CTA)**

What should they do next? Make it natural and easy.

*Examples:*

-   \"DM me \'READY\' and I\'ll send you the free training.\"

-   \"Comment \'SYSTEM\' and I\'ll send over the framework.\"

-   \"Grab the link in my bio and start today.\"

## **2. THE AUTHORITY POST**

***Use when:** You want to drop lessons, strategies, or takeaways from
interviews, personal research, or lived experience. Perfect for
carousels, newsletters, or \"X tips that actually work\" reels.*

**Prompt Starter:** Write me a social media post for \[platform\]
sharing \[topic/strategy\] following this framework:

**Step 1: Choose Your Headline Style (Pick ONE)**

**Option A - Studied Success:** \"I studied \[name/group\] and how they
\[achieved goal\]. Here are their top \[#\] strategies anyone can use.\"

**Option B - Interview-Based:** \"I interviewed \[name/group\] this
week. Here\'s what they told me about \[topic\].\"

**Option C - Pattern Recognition:** \"After studying \[#\] \[type of
person\], here are the \[#\] lessons that\'ll help you \[achieve
goal\].\"

**Step 2: Stack Your Insights (3-5 Total)**

Each point should teach something practical, reference what others
said/did, and end with a quick action step.

**Template for Each Insight:**

**\[Insight Title\]**

-   **Why it matters:** \[Explain the importance\]

-   **What they do:** \[Describe the action\]

-   **Quick action:** \[One thing they can do today\]

**Step 3: Close With Passion + CTA**

\"I\'m obsessed with studying what works from \[type of people\]. Follow
\[@handle\] for more breakdowns like this - and share this with someone
who\'s hungry to grow.\"

## **3. THE RESULTS POST**

*Use when: You want to showcase how you get real results without relying
on overhyped or outdated tactics. Positions you as the go-to expert
while building trust.*

**Prompt Starter:** Write me a social media post for \[platform\]
sharing \[topic/pain/outcome\] following this framework:

**Step 1: Lead With The Win (No Fluff)**

Start with what you\'ve achieved - and what you DIDN\'T have to do to
get there.

**Template:** \"I \[achieved result\] without \[overhyped thing people
think they need\]. Here are \[#\] strategic moves that made it
possible:\"

*Examples:*

-   \"I scaled to consistent \$10k+ months without a big audience or
    > running ads.\"

-   \"I grew a community of 1,200 buyers without going viral or posting
    > 24/7.\"

-   \"I helped 87 clients grow to \$5k+ months without needing sales
    > calls.\"

**Step 2: Break Down The Moves**

Each move should be clear, rooted in real experience, and contrast what
most people do wrong.

**Template for Each Move:**

**\[Move Title\]**

-   **Why most people get this wrong:** \[Common mistake\]

-   **What to do instead:** \[Better approach\]

-   **Quick action step:** \[Something they can do today\]

**Step 3: Close With Confidence**

\"That\'s exactly how I \[repeat result\] - no fluff, no BS, just
focused execution. Want more like this? Follow me \[@handle\] and I\'ll
show you how to do it too.\"

## **4. THE VALUE POST**

*Use when: You want to teach something practical, shift a perspective,
or share your top tools/tips without sounding preachy or robotic. Great
for carousel-style posts and value content.*

**Prompt Starter:** Write me a social media post for \[platform\]
sharing \[topic/goal/strategy\] following this framework:

**Step 1: Choose Your Headline Style (Pick ONE)**

**Option A - Outcome + Simplicity:** \"You can \[do outcome\] this week
by doing these \[#\] things.\" Start with a quick credibility line:
\"After spending 3 years chasing shiny objects\...\"

**Option B - Quick Tips + Proof:** \"\[#\] simple tips to \[desired
outcome\] that actually work.\" Add: \"This works - I\'ve tested it
across 300+ client communities.\"

**Option C - System Breakdown:** \"How I \[get result\] for my clients
(without \[painful thing\]).\"

**Step 2: Break Down Your Insights**

**Template for Each Insight:**

**\[Action-Oriented Title\]**

-   To \[achieve X\], stop \[common mistake\].

-   You need to \[action that actually works\].

-   Once you nail this, \[result becomes easier\].

**Step 3: Close With A Simple Plan**

\"Most importantly, block off \[timeframe\] each \[day/week\] to focus
on \[relevant action\]. Put it in your calendar. Stick to it. And watch
your \[benefit\] start to transform your \[big picture outcome\]. Follow
me \[@handle\] for more insights that actually move the needle.\"

## **5. THE POLARIZING POST**

***Use when:** You want to stand out, stir the pot, or call out the
\"industry norm\" that\'s keeping your audience stuck. Best when you
genuinely believe something different and are ready to challenge the
herd.*

**Prompt Starter:** Write me a bold, opinionated post using the
Polarizing Post framework about \[topic\]. Challenge a popular belief,
give 2-3 better strategies, and wrap with a clear CTA.

**Step 1: Pick Your Controversial Hook**

**Option A - Myth Buster + Proof:** \"\[Topic\]: does it \[help\] or
\[hurt\] your \[goal\]? Let\'s break it down based on \[what you\'ve
learned/tested\].\"

**Option B - Call Out the BS:** \"Anyone telling you to \[common
advice\] is \[lying/trying to sell you\]. Here\'s what actually works.\"

**Option C - Data + Drag:** \"\[Insert data/statistic\] And yet people
still tell you to \[bad strategy\] like it\'s 2017.\"

**Step 2: Build The Content**

Each point should challenge something your audience has believed - and
offer a better alternative.

**Template for Each Point:**

**\[Strategy/Opinion Title\]**

-   **Contrarian Take:** Most people say to \[conventional advice\]. I
    > say that\'s exactly why they\'re stuck.

-   **What to Do Instead:** \[Give a new, clear direction\]

-   **Why It Works:** \[Logic, proof, or your results\]

-   **Quick Win:** \[Action step for today\]

**Step 3: Close With Authority**

\"Thanks for reading. \[Topic\] is something I live and breathe. Follow
me \[@handle\] if you\'re tired of the fluff and want the real strategy
to \[outcome they care about\].\"

## **6. THE THOUGHT LEADERSHIP POST**

*Use when: You want to turn your observations, expertise, or data-backed
takes into value-packed content. Perfect for carousels, threads, or
hook + comment chaining on Facebook.*

**Prompt Starter:** Write me a social media post for \[platform\]
sharing \[topic/goal/strategy\] following this framework:

**Step 1: Choose A Headline Style (Pick ONE)**

-   **Transformation Promise:** \"Become \[outcome\] in \[timeframe\]\"

-   **Pattern Recognition:** \"I\'ve noticed the best \[people\] do
    > \[#\] things to \[outcome\]\"

-   **Industry Prediction:** \"\[Industry\] is changing. Here are my
    > \[#\] predictions\"

-   **Punchy Plan:** \"The plan to \[outcome\]: \[point 1\], \[point
    > 2\], \[point 3\]\"

-   **Unexpected Insight:** \"Finding out \[something surprising\] made
    > me rethink how I \[do thing\]\"

-   **Personal Setup:** \"Before I \[achievement\], I was \[struggle\].
    > These \[#\] things changed my life.\"

-   **Research Blueprint:** \"After \[studying/interviewing\] \[#\] of
    > \[type\], here\'s the blueprint to \[outcome\]\"

-   **Authority Play:** \"Intro to \[topic\]. From someone who \[quick
    > credibility line\].\"

-   **Outcome Made Simple:** \"\[Outcome\] made simple. \[Avatar\] hit
    > \[result\] when they do these \[#\] things.\"

**Step 2: Break It Down (3-5 Perspectives)**

**Template for Each Point:**

-   **What to do:** \[The action\]

-   **Why it matters:** \[The importance\]

-   **Quick action:** \[One thing they can do this week\]

**Step 3: Wrap Up With Clarity + Encouragement**

\"You can \[achieve goal\] by focusing on the right small moves. Keep
showing up, even when it\'s messy - that\'s how momentum builds. Follow
me \[@handle\] if you want to \[what you help them do\] in
\[timeframe\].\"

## **7. THE LEAD GEN POST**

*Use when: You want to give away your training, system, or free resource
and drive inbound DMs. Choose a CTA keyword that aligns with the result
(e.g., \"START,\" \"CLIENTS,\" \"RESULTS\").*

**Prompt Starters:**

-   For longer posts: \"Write me a post for \[platform\] on
    > \[topic/pain/outcome\] using the following template.\"

-   For 2-step/color block posts: \"Write me a very short, 2-step style,
    > lead generation post on \[topic/pain/outcome\] using the following
    > template.\"

**Template 1: The Quick Hit Value**

I just dropped a free training called: \[Name of Training\]. It\'s a
behind-the-scenes look at how I \[quick result\]. Wanna check it out? DM
me \"\[KEYWORD\]\" and I\'ll shoot it over.

**Template 2: The Frustration to Freedom**

Tired of seeing \[avatar\]s struggle to \[main goal\]? After helping \[#
of clients\] do exactly that, I decided to give away the process that
actually works. Want access for free? DM me \"\[KEYWORD\]\" and I\'ll
send it your way.

**Template 3: The System Tease**

Still guessing your way through \[problem\]? You don\'t need another
tactic. You need a system. I\'m giving away my \[Name of System\]
today - the one that helped me \[win/result\]. Want access for free? DM
