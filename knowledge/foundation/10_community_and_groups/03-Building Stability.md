ABOUT THIS LESSON
A Common Theme that we’ve seen time after time in the online space, Blow shit out of the water, growing to 6 or
multi 6 figures very rapidly & then eventually vanishing.

      Because what they were doing stopped “trending”
      They didn’t build a solid foundation that could stand the test of time & hold the weight of their digital empire
      They didnt know why it was working so well - No Process

We’ve also watched this unfold from high-level,7, 8 & 9-figure marketers. Amassing a massively successful business,
just to end up exiting to blaze a new path or pursue something that brings them more fulfillment beyond the dollars
hitting the bank by the truckload.
So what we have found to be quite important:
        Having a clear goal to become laser focused on
        Knowing what levers to pull to ensure healthy growth across the board
        Creating healthy leverage to maintain a fun work/life balance
        Anchoring to the type of brand that you’re wanting to build, do you see yourself managing a team, Do you want
        smaller biz with low overhead (this will likely evolve over time either way, but having a picture of what type of
        business you’d want to operate)
In order to do this you must have a clear picture of your Process while being able to identify your own bottle necks
that are hindering your growth
Flate Flow




#1 Thing you should know is ‘What Problem(s) you solve and for Who’
      What is the problem
      Who has the problem
      Why do they have it
      What is the solution
      How is it deployed
      Outcome after its in place
Look at where you want/need more leverage
      What do you enjoy doing (leverage not needed)
      What do you not enjoy (leverage needed)
      Seek ways to minimize, outsource or eliminate what you dont like
We have seen a few areas repeat themselves over and over as business killers aka bottle necks
      Ability to turn leadflow on or off at will or maintain steady flow
      Lead magnets
      Content strategy
      Group Assets
      Ecosystem as a whole
      Opportunities to present offer
      Messenger conversations
      Lead Magnets with embedded offer
      Group Assets (Trainings that presell a particular offer)
      2 Step lead gen strategy (long and short term)
      Deliverables
      Communicating these in a desirable way
      Content that explains the benefits
      Being familiar with actual Deliverables within offer
      Support structure
      Not sure where they fall as the affiliate
      No Duration of support after sale
      Different layers of support systems Group / 1 on 1 - Building deeper relationships with buyers pool &
      increasing overall LTV throughout different stages of biz
      Ascension Game Plan
      Having a medium to make more PER PERSON & a way to compound your results with your clients vs having to
      start over every single month wondering where you’re going to get fresh blood into your “high ticket”
      Doesn’t have to be a slimy upsell either, the bigger weight that you’re putting onto support structures &
      relationships it becomes NATURAL for your peeps to want to work closer with you, especially if you’re helping
      them accomplish their goals (they’ll want proximity)
      Ultimate goal would be to have a buyers pool where you can potentially get paid to build relationships and
      have a leveraged medium to identify the people you’d enjoy working closer with.
