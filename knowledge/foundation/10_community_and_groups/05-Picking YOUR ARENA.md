ABOUT THIS LESSON
To Make This Work...
You HAVE to pick a general industry (niche) you want to be in
Because the goal is to become an authority inside of the space you're playing in.. So you can help those who're
looking to get started with the same thing..
The 3 Main Buckets ALL products could be
categorized in: Health.. Wealth.. Relationship
Prioritize A Passion..
Trading working at a 9 to 5 you hate.. To build an online biz that revolves around something you don't enjoy..
Is just trading one evil for another.
You have the scope to build a business that revolves around something you're passionate about.. This also makes
showing
up day in & day out a whole lot easier.. Because you like what you're doing..

Now the 3 main approaches you could tackle with this:

      Something, more expensive, needs less sales per month to hit your income goals.
      Something with MRR baked in (Monthly recurring revenue - Stacks and compounds over time)
      Value ladder (Front end thing + Back end thing)

The main reasons for this, are profit margins are higher, time of delivery is less, and can get them into motion faster
