What This Lesson Covers:

    Why entry positioning designs the path OUT of the group, not just into it

    How clear outcomes create natural ascension without pressure

    The 4 components of entry positioning (problem-based entry frame, visible process,
    clear "this leads to more" signal, natural problem ladder)

    Why selling starts the moment someone enters, not when you pitch

Results:

    Welcome Post written and pinned that anchors to one specific problem

    Buyers self-identify and non-buyers self-filter automatically

    The "next problem" becomes obvious after solving Problem A in the group

    Selling stops feeling awkward because ascension is built into the structure
