ABOUT THIS LESSON
Now, the most challenging part about breaking down conversations / leveraging a script/framework is the fact that
they aren’t linear & not every single one of them is going to be the same
Things that make the most significant difference in how easy or the length of the conversations you’re having with
people.
        How much content they’ve consumed from you
        How deep they feel like they know about who you are and what you do
        The belief that you’re the one that has the ability to help them fix what they’re struggling with
        To an extent - an internal belief in themself that they actually have what it takes / confidence or enough pain in
        where they’re currently at
If you want to make more sales in the DMs here are the 4 main things that will make a huge difference for you


       Building trust and rapport via the relationship
       Finding out what their FOR REAL problem is that they have (could be multiple)
       Getting to a point where you can softly share the solution ya have that you think would be best for them based
       on #2
       If it's a good fit and they qualify you are just helping them make the best decision for themselves and leading

       them to the sale


The two main things that will put you miles ahead in convos:

       The ability to read the convo & become aware of the prospects responsiveness

                If someone is extremely short with you, there is a problem/something isn’t clear to them / internal issue
                - they’re skeptical, not sold on you, the relationship isn’t there




                Understanding Their Lead Temp

                      The interaction that you’re having with someone who just jumped into your group yesterday who’s
                      never seen any of your content is going to be drastically different from one you’re having with
                      someone who’s been binging your content for weeks or months

Being able to read these 2 separately quickly will give you the ability to figure out where you need to direct the convo
/ how you need to act quickly.

       If   that’s something you wanna check out, let me know.
       Cheers ☕ Hope You're Having A Wonderful Day!




       A Voice note/message because they’re going to have to open the message and listen to it (will usually get a

        Stroke their ego, People are posting things because they want feedback, Compliment them, even if you’re

        hey, loved that piece of content you put out, looks like you’re crushing it with XYZ… People will actually open
        up fairly easily to this approach.

My Go-To Hand Raiser Flow / Off Offer Posts / Longer Content (if relationship/rapport isn’t established through
content this WILL NOT WORK AS WELL)

       Hey! Saw you were looking to check out some more information on ‘insert thing / offer’
       Awesome �� So, I'm opening up a couple of spots To(Solve a pain point) For (Exclusive number of people) To
       achieve XYZ Is that something you'd be interested in doing?
       Sweet, So XYZ is for those who’re looking to ‘insert the desired outcome’. It’s primarily for ‘insert ideal
       candidate / who it’s not for the statement’ Does that sound like where you’re at?

                Ifthey say “no” here either move on or try to sell them something more suitable. (push them into more
                value/something relevant you have in group) If they say “yes” straight up we can roll on

       Perfect, I have all the details handy for you on a quick ‘insert doc / video’ happy for me to send it your way?
       Sweet I’ve got that here ready to go now. Do you have a few moments to read/watch it now & hit me back to
       let me know if this is something you’d like to move forward with or NOT?
       �� Great here it is ‘insert link’
       After you’ve checked it out drop me back a message back & let me know if you’re in or out.


The last part leaves the convo open-ended - gives you a touch point to follow back up
Post Offer Sent
Assuming they watch it or & hit you back, one of 3 things will happen.

       They go “yep I’m in!”
       They go “Not for me thanks”
       Or they’ll have questions.

They’re in:
They could alternatively buy directly after
“Great. That’s awesome. Are you happy for me to send over my link for you to complete the investment & we can get
you started now?”
“No Thanks, Not For Me”
If they watch your video or read your doc & it isn’t for them.

You can either try find out why & handle the objections using standard objection handling training or you can just let
it go.

“Questions”
If they have a few questions, that’s great - field them directly & in a clear, confident & succinct manner.

Note: this is why it is critical you go through whatever program you’re promoting yourself & understand it well.
Good answers here can close a deal quickly. Shitty uncertain ones can kill it just as fast.
If they ask more than 3 or 4 & it starts getting into their life story I’ll leave it in your hands how far you want to take it.

Your time is valuable, don’t become everyone else’s therapist or this business becomes very painful very quickly.
Circle back to the sale & see if you can get it done or move on to the next lead, there’s plenty more fish in the sea.
The Follow-Up:
This would usually go by a rule of thumb of 24-48, 48-72 hours, with a more aggressive follow-up if you’re in a mid-
promo / coming down to the wire with X offer.
        Hey just checking in with you & wanted to see if you had any thoughts on the ‘insert thing you sent’
        Hey, just wanted to pop you a quick reminder that ‘insert deadline / limited availability’ to grab it, if you have
        any questions give me a shout.
        �� Just bumping this to the top of your inbox
        How’s it coming along with ‘insert pain-point they’ve mentioned they struggle with or what the offer solves’
