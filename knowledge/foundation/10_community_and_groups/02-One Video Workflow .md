# **The One-Video Workflow: Execution SOP**

**Objective:** Turn 1 long-form video into 20+ pieces of weekly content.

**Rule:** Follow the process in order.

## **STEP 0: Decide the Format (Before You Touch Anything)**

**Choose strictly ONE for this session.** Do not mix formats
mid-session.

⬜ **Live Video** (High energy, minimal editing required later)

⬜ **Pre-recorded Long-form** (Best for batching without live pressure)

**Guidance:**

-   **Talking Head:** Easiest if you are editing manually later.

-   **Screen Share:** Fine if you are using AI clipping tools.

## **STEP 1: Build the Question List (Fuel for the Video)**

Create a simple document titled: **\"Weekly Video Questions\"**

**A. Populate using Primary Sources (Use First)**

⬜ Client onboarding answers

⬜ Client questions (Direct DMs/Emails)

⬜ Community posts & comments

⬜ Live call questions

**B. Populate using Secondary Sources (Only If Needed)**

⬜ AnswerThePublic.com

⬜ AnswerSocrates.com

⬜ AI web search for *"most asked questions in \[your niche\]"*

**C. Apply The Filtering Rule** *Only keep questions that fit these
criteria:*

-   Common questions / FAQs

-   Repeated sales objections

-   "Do I need..."

-   "Why doesn't..."

-   "What's the best way to..."

-   How to..

**✅ Output Check:** 10--25 clear questions. One question per line. **No
scripting.**

## **STEP 2: Prepare the Recording Setup**

**Before hitting record:**

⬜ Open the Question Document.

⬜ Position it where you can easily reference it while looking at the
camera.

⬜ **STOP:** Do NOT write answers.

⬜ **STOP:** Do NOT outline the talk.

**Why?** This ensures your answers are authentic and conversational.
Conversational tones perform better on short-form video than scripted
tones.

## **STEP 3: Record the Long Video**

**Target:** 30--90 minutes total raw footage.

**The Recording Rules:**

1.  **Ask the question out loud** (This creates the \"Hook\").

2.  **Immediately answer it** (This is the \"Value\").

3.  **Move to the next question** (No dead air).

**The Strategy:** By reading the question out loud, you create a natural
cut point for the editor/AI. Each question becomes a standalone clip
automatically.

## **STEP 4: Choose ONE Clipping Method**

**Pick one path for this week.** Do not switch halfway through.

**Option A: AI Auto-Clipping (Fastest)**

⬜ Upload full video to tool (e.g., OpusClip, Munch).

⬜ Let the tool auto-clip and caption.

⬜ Review and **delete** weak clips.

⬜ Export the winners.

*Tradeoff: Fastest, but less granular control.*

**Option B: Text-Based AI Editor (Control)**

⬜ Upload video to tool (e.g., Descript).

⬜ Use the transcript to highlight strong answers.

⬜ Turn highlights into standalone compositions.

⬜ Caption and export.

*Tradeoff: Takes slightly longer, but clip selection is intentional.*

**Option C: Manual Editor (Free/Legacy)**

⬜ Upload video file and transcript.

⬜ Manually cut at the \"Question asked\" and \"Answer finished\"
points.

⬜ Export clips.

*Tradeoff: Time-intensive, but total control.*

## **STEP 5: Content Output Check**

**Verify your assets before closing the project.**

**From this one session, you should have:**

⬜ 20--40 short-form clips (Reels/TikToks/Shorts)

⬜ Each clip answers **ONE** question

⬜ Each clip stands alone (no context needed)

## **STEP 6: Publish for the Week**

**Execution:**

⬜ Post 1--2 clips per day minimum.

⬜ Pull captions directly from the question you asked.

⬜ **Do not** overwrite captions or overthink hashtags.

⬜ **Do not** reshoot anything.

## **STEP 7: Weekly Reset (Non-Optional)**

**At the end of the week:**

⬜ Collect new questions from comments on this week\'s videos.

⬜ Add them to the \"Weekly Video Questions\" doc.

⬜ Repeat Step 0.

## **⚠️ FINAL ENFORCEMENT RULE**

**If content creation feels hard, you broke the system.**

-   If you are stuck, you skipped **Step 1** (Sourcing).

-   If editing is hard, you skipped **Step 3** (Reading questions out
    > loud).

-   If you are tired, try to be creative instead of procedural.

**This is not a content strategy. It is a production line. Run it like
one.**
