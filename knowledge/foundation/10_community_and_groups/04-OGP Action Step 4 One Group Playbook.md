This isn\'t about getting people into your group.

It\'s about designing the path out.

Most people think selling starts when they pitch.

Nope. Selling starts the second someone walks in.

If your positioning is clear, sales happen on their own.

Here\'s where most people mess up:

They position their group as \"free value\" or \"a place to hang out.\"

No direction. No momentum. Paid offers feel random. Selling feels weird.

Not because the offer sucks. Because the group has no clear outcome.

Here\'s what changes everything:

When your group points at one specific outcome, people join with intent.
Progress shows up. The next problem becomes obvious.

Your paid offer just solves that next problem.

That\'s it.

No convincing. No pressure. Just the next step.

The lesson breaks down the 4 pieces that make this work.
