## 1. THE 3-STAGE CONVERSATION MODEL

### Stage 1: COLD

**Goal:** Build genuine connection

**Mindset:** You are a human, not a salesperson

**Actions:**
- Use low-pressure, personal openers
- Find common ground (profile, content)
- Keep conversation going with simple questions
- Transition to Warm by asking about their situation

---

### Stage 2: WARM

**Goal:** Understand where they are; uncover if you can help

**Key Insight:** Many prospects are not aware they have a problem. You may need to help them see it.

**Actions:**
- Use market assumptions to probe for pain
- Ask about current situation, goals, challenges
- Identify a problem that matches your solution
- Dig deeper to understand the obstacle

**Outcome:** You know their desired outcome and what's stopping them

---

### Stage 3: HOT

**Goal:** Get them on a call

**Actions:**
- Propose a call using low-pressure invitation
- Overcome resistance (objections)
- Send calendar link and confirm
- Follow up to ensure they book and show up

---

## 2. THE 3-LEVEL PAIN FRAMEWORK

| Level | What It Is | Example (CPA Operator) |
|-------|------------|------------------------|
| Surface Problem/Goal | The problem they state right now | "I want to scale my CPA business" |
| Level 2 Problem | Related problem that needs solving | "I need better deliverability because domains burn" |
| Level 3 Problem | Personal interest related to pains | "If I can't fix this, I'll lose money and feel like a failure" |

**The Rule:** You only need Level 2 pain to book a call. Level 3 is powerful but not always necessary.

**How to Uncover Level 2:**
- Ask: "What do you mean by that?"
- Ask: "What's causing that?"
- Ask: "What have you tried?"
- Use market assumptions

---

## 3. MARKET ASSUMPTIONS

Instead of waiting for prospects to reveal pain, lead the conversation with assumptions based on common industry problems.

### How to Create Market Assumptions

- List most common struggles in your niche
- Identify a common "overhyped" concept
- Think of a problem you or clients faced
- Consider something your market won't admit
- Think of a common "down the line" solution

### Income Engine Examples

| Assumption | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| "Most operators I talk to are struggling with burned domains. Is that something you're dealing with?" | Probe for deliverability pain |
| "You seem consistent with content. Usually that means you're scaling income – is that the case?" | Probe for growth goals |
| "I noticed you've been posting about CPA. Are you getting the results you want with deliverability?" | Direct problem probe |

## 9. OBJECTION HANDLING

### "Is this Jason or a setter?"

"Hey bro, I have team members that help me keep things moving, but I am here on FB always overseeing everything and taking care of my inbox every time I can."

---

### "I am not gonna jump right in on the call, I don't want to waste your time."

"There is no need to make a decision on the spot. It is more of a conversation to see if we can help in the first place. Just because you don't buy doesn't mean you're wasting my time."

---

### "What's the purpose of the call?"

"At first we will dive into your situation to see if we can help. If we can, then we will lay down a customized plan on how the process would look for your specific situation, needs, and goals. Makes sense?"

---

### "I want to do the call with you, not a setter."

"I totally understand where you're coming from. I recently had to make a structural change as I was stacked with 1-on-1 client meetings and it wasn't sustainable. My team is amazing and they have been with me since the beginning. What I do say is: if my team can't help for some reason, they will schedule you in with myself. Does that work for you?"

---

### "I want to start in 3 months."

*Send voice note:*

"Hey [Name], of course no pressure at all. I was thinking – if you want to start in 3 months, we can still have a call this week just to go over exactly what I do and if I can help in the first place. Then at least you know what you're working towards. Again, no pressure, just want to make sure you're moving in the right direction as fast as possible."

---

### "I'm working with another coach."

"That's awesome! Investing in yourself is so important to your success! How do you like their program so far?"

*Proceed to ask about results, whether they've gotten what they expected. If not, propose a call to help them move forward.*

---

### "What's different about you compared to all other coaches?"

"I mean, the biggest thing always really is for me to build a relationship, as that's more valuable than anything. I noticed there are lots of 'big' programs out there where people end up like a 'number' instead of genuine support. So instead of giving you methods or information, I genuinely want to make sure you're going to move the needle by helping you execute. Execution = Results. So not just being your coach, but also making you a valued friend that I will give guaranteed help until you have those results you signed up for."

---

### "What's your pricing?"

"Our prices range from $1,650 to $9,500 depending on what you need help with most. We also have payment plan options to make it easier for your cashflow."

*Be direct – this qualifies them early.*

---

### "Can you send me your website first?"

"I don't have a traditional website at the moment as I'm using Facebook mainly for my business. What kind of information would you like to see?"

*Or:*

"I'm happy to talk about it here, but it depends on where you currently are to see what will help you the most. Regardless of whether you become a client, I'd love to see if I can give you some direction on [problem] on a quick call so you can move forward!"

---

### "I have to wait until I have money to invest."

"No worries! Appreciate the transparency. When do you think you'll have finances available to invest back into yourself and your business?"
