# **When & How to DM Community Members**

Turning signals into sales without being awkward, pushy, or random.

## **WHAT THIS LESSON IS REALLY ABOUT**

DMs are not a sales tactic. DMs are a response.

If you ever feel weird DMing someone, it\'s usually because there was no
signal, the timing was off, or the conversation had no context.

This lesson fixes that.

You will learn:

When it actually makes sense to DM someone

How to open the convo cleanly

How to guide it toward a sale without pressure

## **THE BIG RULE (BURN THIS IN)**

You do not DM for attention. You DM in response to intent.

When you DM from intent, people welcome the message and selling feels
like help.

When you DM without intent, it feels spammy, resistance shows up, and
you hesitate to follow through.

## **THE 3 GREEN-LIGHT MOMENTS (NON-NEGOTIABLE)**

If none of these are present, do not DM.

### **1️⃣ They Raise Their Hand (Clear Intent)**

This is the cleanest scenario.

They comment asking for info, reply to a CTA, or DM you first.

**The Move:** Be direct. Reduce small talk. Respect their
momentum---they already want clarity.

### **2️⃣ They Display Buyer Behavior**

This is the most overlooked one.

Buyer behavior looks like repeated engagement on the same topic,
thoughtful comments (not emojis), or referencing your specific
frameworks.

**The Move:** Recognize that they are already mentally processing a
decision. Your DM is simply a continuation of that process.

### **3️⃣ They Hit a Wall You Can Fix**

This is where selling becomes service.

They post about frustration or being stuck between options.

**The Move:** DMing here is not pitching. It\'s acknowledging their
reality and offering a bridge.

## **WHEN YOU SHOULD NOT DM**

Do NOT DM because:

You want to make sales today

Someone just liked a post or joined the group

You feel behind on revenue

That \"need\" energy leaks into the message and kills trust instantly.

## **HOW TO OPEN THE DM (WITHOUT THE WEIRDNESS)**

Your opening message should always do two things:

Anchor context and ask permission.

**Clean Openers (Use These):**

\"Hey \[Name\], saw your comment on \[Topic\]. Appreciate you sharing
that.\"

\"Hey \[Name\], you mentioned \[Problem\] in the group earlier. Mind if
I ask you something quick?\"

Never start with links, pitches, or assumptions.

## **PERMISSION IS THE PRESSURE RELEASE VALVE**

Before you guide anything, ask:

\"Before I dive in, mind if I ask you a couple quick questions to see if
I can actually help?\"

If they say Yes, proceed.

If they say No, respect it and exit.

Pressure dies the moment permission is given.

## **HOW TO GUIDE THE CONVO**

You are not interrogating. You are orienting.

1.  Clarify what they want to fix

2.  Reflect it back to them

3.  Frame what solving it usually requires

4.  See if they\'re open to that

The offer should never be a surprise.

By the time you mention it, they should already be thinking it.

## **OFFERING THE NEXT STEP**

\"Based on what you shared, we built \[Offer\] specifically to help with
\[Outcome\]. Want me to send you a quick breakdown so you can see if
it\'s a fit?\"

Wait for the \"Yes.\"

**Then get a micro-commitment:**

\"Do you have a few minutes now to check it out so we can decide if it
makes sense or not?\"

## **IF THEY DON\'T RESPOND (THE FOLLOW-UP RULE)**

Follow-up is not chasing. It\'s closing the loop.

Use this rhythm:

**24--48 hours:** \"Hey \[Name\], did you get a chance to look at
that?\"

**48--72 hours:** \"Totally cool if now\'s not the time, just wanted to
check in.\"

**72+ hours:** Pivot to value or exit cleanly. No guilt. No endless
nudging.

## **IMPORTANT REMINDER**

If DMing feels heavy, go upstream.

Fix your Entry Positioning and your Visible Proof.

When those are right, DMs feel obvious, selling feels calm, and revenue
stops feeling forced.
