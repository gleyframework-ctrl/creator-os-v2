BONUS: The One Group Audit Checklist
Find what's killing momentum and fix it in under 15 minutes.




HOW TO USE THIS CHECKLIST
This is a diagnostic, not a judgment.

Read each line and answer Yes or No honestly.

Every "No" points to friction. Every friction point slows ascension.

The Rule: Do not fix everything at once. Fix the earliest broken layer first based on the Priority
Rule at the bottom of this page.




SECTION 1: STRUCTURE
The environment everything depends on.

   ●​ [ ] Do you currently have one primary community that is the center of your business?
   ●​ [ ] If you have multiple groups, do you have a clear plan to consolidate them into one
      shared ecosystem?
   ●​ [ ] Is it clear to your audience which group is the "main room" and which ones are
      legacy?
   ●​ [ ] Have you communicated that this is a new phase/operating system?
   ●​ [ ] Are you actively inviting members from other groups into this one with context?

Note: You are not abandoning old groups. You are merging momentum. If your attention is split,
your audience's attention is too.




SECTION 2: ENTRY POSITIONING
The front door experience.

   ●​ [ ] Can a new member understand what this group does for them in under 60 seconds?

   ●​   [ ] Is the outcome of the group obvious without scrolling?
   ●​   [ ] Does the positioning speak to a specific problem, not "general value"?
   ●​   [ ] Is it clear that this group is for progress, not just discussion?
   ●​   [ ] Is it openly acknowledged that deeper, paid support exists?




SECTION 3: PINS & VISIBILITY
What people see first and most often.

   ●​   [ ] Do you have three or fewer pinned posts?
   ●​   [ ] Is there one clear Welcome + Expectations post pinned?
   ●​   [ ] Is there one post pinned that shows Proof, Wins, or Momentum?
   ●​   [ ] Is there one post pinned that explains Where to Start?
   ●​   [ ] Have you removed old pins that no longer reflect your direction?




SECTION 4: CONTENT FUNCTION
What your posts are actually doing.

   ●​   [ ] Does your content help people get a quick win or a clarity shift?
   ●​   [ ] Are you documenting what you're doing, rather than trying to teach everything?
   ●​   [ ] Does your content make progress visible (for you or your members)?
   ●​   [ ] Are you avoiding content dumps or long, heavy curriculums?
   ●​   [ ] Can someone point to a post and say, "That helped me think differently"?




SECTION 5: ASCENSION CLARITY
Does the next step feel obvious?

   ●​   [ ] Is there a clear problem that the free community helps solve?
   ●​   [ ] Is there a clear next problem that cannot be solved at the free level?
   ●​   [ ] Does your paid offer logically solve that next problem?
   ●​   [ ] Is the paid path framed as a continuation, not pressure?
   ●​   [ ] Do people naturally ask "what's next"?

SECTION 6: DMs & CONNECTION
How conversations actually start.

   ●​   [ ] Are you sending acclimation DMs to welcome new members without pitching?
   ●​   [ ] Are you waiting for buyer signals before moving into ascension conversations?
   ●​   [ ] Can you identify the difference between acclimation and ascension DMs?
   ●​   [ ] Do your DMs feel contextual and calm, not forced?
   ●​   [ ] Are you comfortable letting someone not buy without trying to convince them?




SECTION 7: BUYER SIGNAL VISIBILITY
Can you see readiness forming?

   ●​   [ ] Are members engaging consistently across posts, not just once?
   ●​   [ ] Do people mirror your language or reference your specific ideas?
   ●​   [ ] Are frustration or "stuck" posts showing up?
   ●​   [ ] Do people advocate for you or answer questions for others?
   ●​   [ ] Do you trust yourself to recognize readiness without a script?




THE PRIORITY RULE
If multiple sections have "No" answers, fix them in this specific order. Never skip ahead:

   1.​ Structure
   2.​ Entry Positioning
   3.​ Pins & Visibility
   4.​ Quick Win Content
   5.​ Ascension Clarity
   6.​ DMs
   7.​ Signals




FINAL REMINDER
You don't need more content, more platforms, or more complexity.

You need one room, one outcome, and one clear path forward.
