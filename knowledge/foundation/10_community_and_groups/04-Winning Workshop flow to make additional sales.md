ABOUT THIS LESSON
Sales on autopilot, the dream right? While we’re not big on the “passive income business” where sale flood into your
inbox daily without working.
We have nailed down a process that converts brand-new members into paying clients with ease & some do in fact
end up buying directly from the training themselves.

We ran a free workshop in our community on Feb 6th to do a couple of things for us:
       Create more leverage for our communities / a streamlined process for brand new folks to come in & have
       something that’ll help them make a decision as to whether they’d want to move forward with us or not
       Chop relationship process down on a 1 to 1 level - Giving them consumables that are “flate focused” - while
       intertwining each post & piece together with the next one
       Have a public “Suite” selection of what we have & how people could get involved & giving the option to choose
       based on what they saw
               We did do a brief offer breakdown including price, but only for 2 of our offers - Jbox - Group Juic
       Facilitate more volume / gives us the ability to push more folks into the community without it being directly
       tied to operation / our energy
       All most common questions are now answered + in-depth training to presell on everything we are about so by
       the time we do talk to them more less work is involved in every spectrum of biz
               Outside of the group it now becomes a free workshop that’s leveraged
               Group Automation and initial focus can pump them into pieces of training
               Convos can be sparked off the engagement/people interacting on the training
So what happens when someone comes into our communities:
Group entry > Welcome Post > Training 1 > Training 2 > Training 3 > Training 4 > training 5 > Offer
We also have our email automation set up to funnel people back to the training on autopilot over the span of 10 days,
meaning brand new folks are coming into the world & you’re punching them in the face with as much value as you
can until you make an offer.
As a group juicer, you’re more than welcome to leverage this EXACT training in your own group so you can grow your
community if you’d like - training on white labeling the workshop here
Not planning on promoting our products, is completely fine too, we’ll riff on how you would emulate this in your own
group with your own training.
The 3 main things that someone will want to know are: Has this worked for you, has it worked for someone else &
can it work for them?
So when you’re looking at doing this you need to reverse engineer it backwards.
