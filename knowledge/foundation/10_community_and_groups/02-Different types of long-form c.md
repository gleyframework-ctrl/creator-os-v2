Longer posts will usually have less engagement - but the quality of those leads are higher (they will know why they’re
there, commenting because they know your making an offer)

Shorter Posts (color blocks) - Engagement will be higher on these - but a lot of the people who engage are usually
looking for free info / a lead magnet a video. More info.. You can use these to move into sales convos / your offer
the conversion process will just be more intensive on your part.

These also work really well to get more people into your group/tag people in your group on a training. EX - Who
hasn’t checked out my training on how to go from x to x without x.
Knowing What “Mode” You're In
Understand what mode you're actually in, and what I mean by this is are you running a promo are you providing value
are you coming off of the heavy promo? Are you extremely busy and your day-to-day life like in your job or at your
work?
The type of content with change based on your goals or what you have going on.
        Coming off a hard promotional period - do value & lifestyle posts for about a week. Rebuild the Goodwill with
        your audience.
        In promo Mode? Hammer 2 Step posts, offer posts & lead gen content (tieing different pain points to your
        product)
        Busy? Not feeling the motivation.. Look at repurposing older posts you have.. Changing them up a bit &
        throwing them out there. Or even just taking a few days off on posting.
Creating Layers In your content
One thing I started doing in September 2021 was changing the style of content i was posting based on where I was
posting it.
Profile - Highlight reel, Lifestyle, results, Belief shifting content & 2 Step lead gen posts (usually to push into my
group), And that's basically what I hammer on my profile. Rarely make offers on my profile usual objective is to get
people into my community.
Group - Where the magic happens - the educational posts, training, “the secret sauce”, this is where you’ll provide the
actual VALUE. Causes a great dynamic because everyone outside your group feels like they’re being left out.
And you know everyone inside your community is LOOKING to get started with what you’re teaching.
Vs quite a few of the people on your profile.. Are farther back.. Don’t really know what it is you’re doing..


Specificity
One of the biggest shifts you can make in your content is becoming HYPER-specific.
Specific sells. And tying that specificity to REAL WORLD THINGS.
       Most can’t picture what an “online business” looks Like
       What a $1,000 commission feels like
       What does more leads & traffic means?
Use your content to become very specific on pain points/solutions and relate them to real-world things.
An example - Instead of creating content all about growing and monetizing a group "More leads and more sales. "
Make the focus of your copy things like -
       Writing your own paychecks from Group requests..
       Turning your Group into a Walmart parking lot on a black Friday sale
       Getting paid to spend time with family..
       Having a line of people with their credit cards out ready to buy your products
CTA
Always have some type of CTA whether you’re in value mode or promo mode.
Types of call to action -
       Pop me a DM with the word “Example” (Offer)
       Drop a #Me below if you to check out the doc. (Offer)
       Comment if you wanna check out the training/lead magnet. (lead gen hand raise / group pitch or tag on
       training)
       Lemme know your thoughts below! (value post)
Prompt for some type of engagement based on your goals or the intention behind why you’re producing that
content.
Whether it's for a sale, for a new group member, to promote a training you have inside your group or pure value
