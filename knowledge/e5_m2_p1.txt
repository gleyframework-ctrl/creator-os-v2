SOURCE: E5 C.A.M.P. Masterclass — Module 2, Part 1 (Worksheets 00 + Lesson 4)
FRAMEWORK: MESSAGE POSITIONING STACK — Empathy Map → Awareness Pyramid → Promise Exposure Spectrum
WHEN TO USE: After Module 1 research, before writing the Big Idea / unique mechanism / hook. When a member asks "who do I target?", "is my market saturated?", "why isn't my message converting?"

--- TOOL 1: THREE-DIMENSIONAL PROSPECT PROFILE (EMPATHY MAP) ---
WHEN TO USE: To write messages that mirror the prospect's world; to find the exact words for the hook.
STEPS: Fill 6 dimensions TWICE — once for the CURRENT problem/situation, once for the DESIRED solution:
1. SEEING — what they see around them (environment, competitors, ads)
2. HEARING — what they hear from friends, experts, media
3. SAYING — what they say out loud
4. THINKING — what they think privately
5. FEELING — what they feel
6. DOING — what they actually do
DECISION RULE FOR THE COACH: Use the prospect's own SEEING/HEARING/SAYING words as raw hook material; use the gap between problem-column FEELING and solution-column FEELING as the emotional arc of the message.
COMMON MISTAKES: Filling the map with what YOU see/think/say instead of the prospect; filling only the problem side, skipping the solution side.

--- TOOL 2: PROSPECT AWARENESS PYRAMID (P.A.P.) ---
FRAMEWORK: 5 awareness segments, top→bottom: MOST AWARE → PRODUCT AWARE → SOLUTION AWARE → PROBLEM AWARE → COMPLETELY UNAWARE.
LAW: The LOWER on the pyramid, the BIGGER the audience & opportunity — but the LONGER the funnel must be and the HARDER the conversion.
STEPS: (1) Circle the ONE segment the message targets. (2) Set funnel length: most aware = short direct funnel; unaware = long education funnel.
DECISION RULE FOR THE COACH: Never mix segments in one message. If a member says "my ads don't convert," check: are they speaking most-aware language ("buy now") to problem-aware traffic (needs education)? Fix the match first.
COMMON MISTAKES: Targeting "everyone"; short funnel for unaware traffic; expecting unaware prospects to buy on first touch.

--- TOOL 3: PROMISE EXPOSURE SPECTRUM (P.E.S.) — Lesson 4 ---
FRAMEWORK: How exposed the market is to your promise, 5 stages:
1. Your promise has NOT been made yet (virgin).
2. Promise made already but NOT expanded by competitors.
3. Promise expanded but NOT presented with your mechanism.
4. Your promise + mechanism presented but NOT expanded.
5. Your promise + mechanism presented AND expanded (saturated/commodity).
3-STEP ANALYSIS:
Step 1: Determine the PRIMARY PROMISE of your marketing funnel.
Step 2: Review your Competitor Analysis worksheets (their promises).
Step 3: Determine what stage your prospects are at on the spectrum.
LAW: DIFFERENTIATION in marketing is key. Higher stage (4-5) = weaker position.
DECISION RULE FOR THE COACH: If the member's promise sits at stage 4-5, do NOT tweak ads — move the position: make a new promise (back to 1-2) OR attach a genuinely new unique mechanism (back to 3). If stage 1-2, move fast and expand before competitors do.
COMMON MISTAKES: Fighting at stage 5 with price or louder ads instead of a new mechanism; copying a competitor's promise (stuck at their stage); scoring before writing the primary promise down.

MODULE 2 PART 1 DECISION FLOW FOR THE COACH:
1. Empathy Map → extract the prospect's own words + emotional arc.
2. P.A.P. → pick ONE awareness segment + set funnel length.
3. P.E.S. → score promise saturation → decide: new promise / new mechanism / speed.
Then (next lessons) engineer the Big Idea & unique mechanism on top.
