ABOUT THIS LESSON
Everything you’re doing can be used for content (written or short-form video)
If you're producing good FB posts or group posts we can take those and create multiple short-form contents for your

platforms.
If you’re already moving & creating content on FB / long-form posts, you can turn those into Tiktoks / short-form

video.
Breaking them into series - ultimately turning one content framework into multiple (usually the shorter the better
when it comes to Titkok for engagement/number of views)
        Let folks into your life (different from what we are raised to think)
        Share intimate things about what youre doing in life and biz
        Let folks see that you are a real human being
Break it up into multiple segments to have even more content (test different series subjects with different hooks/
openings of the video)
This could change how much they’re getting exposure / how well they perform.
(Tiktok Especially, is a platform where if you have a content style that works, you can model that same type of
content over & over again & it will usually perform)
You can also reverse the roles with this strategy & turn short-form videos into your content, your videos into longer-
form posts on FB, in/outside your group.
As you build up a catalog of content inside your group, Lives, Interviews, training, posts. These can all become lead
magnets to get people into your community & further fuel for your content ideas.
What You Can Start Regularly Talking About Inside Your Content:


1.) You can never go wrong with Challenges
      Show them the real frustrations they are dealing with to showcase you know where they are
      Layer in some specificity & explain in detail their current challenges by using a story to paint the picture for
      them, kinda like a mini-movie script
      Present them their own struggles and prove that you know them way better than they do
      What are the present problems & frustrations that are keeping them where they are now, the no results ride

2.) Folks love a Success Map/Blueprint/Game Plan/Profit Path
      How is it you are going to help them accomplish their short and long term goals?
      What are you doing and how are you doing it?
      What have you done and How have you done it?
      Give them a birds eye view of your steps of achieving X without Y or Z thing theyre doing now or have already
      tried
      Walk them through the steps and what it looks like as a paid customer/client
      What will they get from you/product in order to get to the green grass

3.) Most important thing you can sell people on is YOU
      Who the frick fack fuck are you? Your flaws, your Strengths & even the stinky shit
      What do you do for fun, how’d ya grow up, stuff ya didnt enjoy
      Your voice and pen are your needle and thread, sew in how you see the world as a whole
      What are your Perspectives, Beliefs, Thoughts on Life and Biz
      Do you have a dark sense of humor like Nate? Show that shit lol
      What makes you tick in a good way?
      What makes you fly off the handle?
      What are you out here breathing for

4.) Lastly, talk about the very thing that scares most folks, Money/Investment
      What have you invested in yourself and knowledge, why? Go deep if ya want
      You can do this comfortably by talking about your own risks and investments
      Have you invested $1,000s, 10s of $1,000s?
      Investment is always money either, time is an investment, Learning is an investment
      If you mix in the first 3 buckets well and nail #4 and ya still generate leads and convos then you will smash

      more sales because folks know exactly what you’re about and still want some of ya
      Folks should know you have something to sell quickly after getting into your world

By simply talking about what youre doing and whats happening in your business you will naturally become an
interesting person to other folks, intrigue and curiosity are powerful when used correctly
All the content you create around these 4 buckets will, by default, presell folks on you more and more over time and
soon enough you will start to have more and more inbound folks hit you up to chat about YOU and what you have to
OFFER, of hit ya with a “I wanna sign up with you now”
Every single Bucket above is Pre Selling on YOU / All that you stand for and Offer at any given time no matter if its
video or written, short or long.
