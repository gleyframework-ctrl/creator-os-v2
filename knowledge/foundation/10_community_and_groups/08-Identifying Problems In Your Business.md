4 - closing - with a stacked doc & offer
Pivoting Your Energy Based On WHAT IS ACTUALLY HAPPENING IN YOUR BUSINESS
Problem - Content Not Performing (can be tricky base content off of conversations/sales coming in NOT
engagement - ex quite a few of my group posts lately have had 0 engagement)
Solution - Changing the style of content, work on your copy, test different lengths (prevalent for written or video)
Problem - Not Hitting Income Goals / Not Making Sales
Solution - Are you having enough convos / generating enough convos (through your content inbounds or outbounds
from people who’re engaging on content) are you presenting enough offers - would recommend putting out an offer /
multiple daily
Problem - Not Having “Enough Convos” inbox is stagnant -
Solution - Content, changing up your content to facilitate more inbound conversations - Outbounds - these don’t have
to be hyper-aggressive “buy my shit” it can be a simple - hey thanks for engaging in my group - detach from turning
an outbound into a sale right now - it will fill your future pipeline
Problem - Convos Flowing - Offers Being Seen But Not Converting
Solution - Analyze the pre-qualifying process - are you sending offers too soon / before they’ve sat in their pain a bit /
jumping the gun on shooting over your offer Is the offer compelling enough - look at enhancing the copy inside your
offers
Take this and apply it daily in some way and we will guarantee that your business changes and becomes better than
it has ever been
