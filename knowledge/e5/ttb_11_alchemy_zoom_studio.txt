Zoom here is not a call tool — it is a closed-loop, live-to-tape broadcast suite. The MP4 Zoom exports is the final flawless deliverable: zero editing, ready to publish.
GOLDEN RULE OF CAPTURE — isolate the window: share WINDOW ONLY, never the entire screen. Isolating guarantees studio-grade output immune to desktop clutter, menus, notifications.
SECRET WEAPON — internal audio routing: check 'Share Sound' so Zoom captures digital audio directly from the source file (e.g., a Telegram voice note played on the computer). Playing audio over speakers and recording via microphone sounds muffled and amateur; Share Sound gives perfect fidelity, zero room noise.
THE ZERO-EDIT CHOREOGRAPHY (pause/resume cycle):
1) THE DOWNBEAT: start Zoom recording + play the original audio file for Slide 1.
2) THE CATCH: audio ends → instantly click Pause Recording.
3) THE BREATH: (not recorded) change the slide.
4) THE RELEASE: click Resume Recording.
5) THE NEXT DOWNBEAT: play audio for Slide 2. Repeat.
Result: a single uninterrupted MP4 that jumps seamlessly slide-to-slide with ZERO trimming.
MASTER TEMPLATE GOLDEN RULES: 1. Never start from scratch. 2. Always duplicate the Master Template. 3. Change only the text title per new module. Result: massive time savings, unshakeable consistency, instant authority.
