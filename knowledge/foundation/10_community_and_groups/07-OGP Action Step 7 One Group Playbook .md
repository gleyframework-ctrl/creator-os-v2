DMs are not a sales tactic.

DMs are a response.

If you ever feel weird DMing someone, it\'s usually because there was no
signal. The timing was off. Or the conversation had no context.

This lesson fixes that.

Here\'s the big rule:

We do not DM for attention. You DM in response to intent.

When you DM from intent, people welcome the message. Selling feels like
help.

When you DM without intent, it feels spammy. Resistance shows up. You
hesitate to follow through.

The lesson breaks down the 3 green-light moments that tell you it\'s
time to DM. If none of them are present, don\'t DM.

It also covers how to open the convo cleanly. How to guide it toward a
sale without pressure. And how to follow up without chasing.

If DMing feels heavy, go upstream. Fix your positioning and your proof.
When those are right, DMs feel obvious and revenue stops feeling forced.
