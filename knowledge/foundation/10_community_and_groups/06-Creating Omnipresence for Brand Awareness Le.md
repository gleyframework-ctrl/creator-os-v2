ABOUT THIS LESSON
How you can start increasing your brand awareness with minimal work, as we mentioned in the beginning of the
document, every SINGLE platform now has their own version of short-form video content.
You can create a single piece of content and use it as:
      Tiktok
      Facebook reels on your profile
      Youtube shorts
      Facebook reels on a page
      Pinterest Idea
      Instagram Reels
      FB + IG story
      FB Reel inside community
      Soon To Be Twitter

There is also software out nowadays that lets you do this automatically Repurpose.io
Now, we’re not telling you to go out and master 5-different platforms all at once, if you’re just starting & even looking
to maximize your time & efficiency put your FOCUS on TIKTOK & FACEBOOK.
When repurposing to other platforms you can simply have your profile optimized with an option to join your group,
even to this day we’re not active on other platforms besides Facebook or Tiktok even though we’re repurposing
short-form content onto them.
