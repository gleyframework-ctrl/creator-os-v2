Welcome to our first Deep Dive (this will be – by far –the most ethically correct Deep Dive of them all
:-)!

In the first two Deep Dives we’ll explore the conscious, rational, logic, and rules based part of our
decision-making (Deep Dive 1: system 2) and the emotional, intuitive, and fast part of our decision-
making (Deep Dive 2: system 1).

After system 1 and system 2 we will Deep Dive into internal brain processes (attention & perception
and needs & motivations). The last Deep Dive will be about external factors that influence these brain
processes (persuasive marketing).

Our aim for this first deep dive is to learn you:
1. How we tend to exaggerate the influence of system 2 on our decision-making.
2. When system 2 is likely to become involved in a decision.
3. How system 2 is likely to be kept involved in a decision.
4. How these theories and insights are used to make your online dialogue more persuasive and/or
   ethical.
