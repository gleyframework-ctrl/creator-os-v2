ABOUT THIS LESSON
Start leveraging short-form video content like Tik-Tok Facebook reels, YouTube shorts or any other platform and
direct all of the traffic/all the content you're creating to your COMMUNITY.
If you’re new! YOUR CONTENT NEEDS TO SELL ON YOU AS A HUMAN NOT A PRODUCT

Content…
Content is going to be crucial right, nobody is going to pull out their credit card and buy anything from you if they
don't know who you are.
The point of your content is to establish trust with your audience to start building up that audience to build
connections through your content by sharing your story sharing your experiences sharing things that are going on in
your life so the potential of turning into a paid client becomes a reality.
there's a beautiful Synergy on Facebook where you can post content and directly move into messenger
conversations and sell products directly and Messenger to them.
And I'm not telling you to go out there and spam your link or post anything like that's not content.
Be yourself be a human being you know you're building a personal brand so injecting your personality and actually
being yourself in your content does a great job at building actual connections with people and the deeper the
connection you have with somebody the easier the conversation is when it comes time to present an offer or see if
they'd be a good fit to buy something from you in the first place.
You are going to be posting content likely every day, a mixture of long-form content & short.
& videos if you’re doing TikTok / FB reels (highly recommended)
The goal with a lot of your content is going to be establishing the relationships of people building connections and
building out some type of relationship with your audience and then mixing posts that are going to get them to raise
their hands and ask for help.
Once they've raised their hand or showed interest in what you're doing what you're offering something that you're
selling or even like a Lead Magnet or a free resource that you have you then going to follow up with them in
messenger and either a full-on the document or the resource that you were offering or move into a conversation
where he can present them with a paid opportunity.
