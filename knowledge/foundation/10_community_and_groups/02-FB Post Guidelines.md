**Facebook Post Guidelines**

**Worst Practices**

-   Include a link in your post (ask for your audience to engage to
    > receive the link) This tends to heavily reduce the reach. Try
    > adding them in the first comment - or ensure you use sparingly.

-   Using stock photos - Remember this is about building **YOUR** brand.

-   Avoid blocks of text. Be sure to break into bite-sized chunks so
    > it's easy to read

-   Asking complex questions. No one wants to feel like they're dumber
    > for having stumbled on your content

-   Using a GIF in a post when you can use an image. This is fine for
    > comments, but as the main post it won't get the same reach.

-   Sharing your posts in multiple places/groups. This just makes you
    > look lazy.

**Types of posts that tend to perform the best:**

**Text with an image**

-   A post with text and an image will almost always get the most
    > engagement.

-   Pathway posts that share a story are gold. Try write as many of
    > these as you can. The more pathways you take your reader on, the
    > more connected they will feel to you and the more money you are
    > likely to make!

**Text**

-   Simple text performs extremely well.

-   People love to talk about themselves, so posts that ask questions of
    > your readers work extremely well. (ie what's your favourite travel
    > destination?)

**Facebook LIVES**

-   Facebook lives show up in your friends' notifications. Lives are
    > great for adding value, trainings, lifestyle and interviews too.
    > Plus, you can get some solid reach on these too!

**Pre-recorded Videos**

-   Videos alone don't get as much engagement or reach as static posts -
    > the exception to this is a watch party that has a couple of active
    > engagers in it.

**Things To Remember**

-   Your first two lines are the most important and are the 'hook' (more
    > training on copywriting coming soon). This is your headline - use
    > it wisely. Ideally, this should captivate the reader enough for
    > them to want to click through and read the rest. Offer things such
    > as a benefit of reading, pain point, displaying vulnerability etc.

-   Post at [least every day]{.underline}

-   Respond to EVERY SINGLE PERSON that engages with you (it kicks the
    > algorithm into gear and your next post is more likely to show up
    > in that person's newsfeed)

-   Use REAL pictures of you that fit into the Facebook ethos (i.e
    > something showing your real life)

-   Use emojis to capture people's attention as they read through

-   Use line by line text - i.e break up your posts so they're readable.

-   **ASK FOR ENGAGEMENT** (ie, drop a gif if you want x, give me a #me
    > if you'd like y etc)

-   Be real - Document your journey. YOU as a person are relatable - not
    > some guru!

-   Do not follow into the trap of writing "I know better than you / you
    > suck at posts." Being humble is an attractive quality to have!
