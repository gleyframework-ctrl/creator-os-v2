ABOUT THIS LESSON
Welcome to the Group
       (Main goal) - Presell them on wants and desires with you and your journey
       Go them focused by hitting on what they want, why they are there
       What do they want to do and achieve?
       What are they going to get out of being here with you?
       Based on the above you want to lean into how you have achieved the things
       Enter (bit of your back story)
              how you got into the game
              How it was when you started
              Lessons, frustrations, aha moments & then the event that led to your success
Indoctrination - What's your model?
       Methodology
       Philosophy
       Strategy
       Plan
       Results (Yours and Clients + Communities you’re involved with)
       How you do things
Training
         Most important - Cover all the most repeated questions you get or see folks having
         Anything you already have assets for, Docs, resources, templates or other lead magnets
         Free strategies that will get them a quick win or wins
         Basic understanding of online business as a beginner
         Fundamentals they must understand, setting those lovely realistic expectations
         All of these should be pre-selling on the ‘indoctrination’ above & the ‘offers’ below setting the scene
Offers
       What do you have for folks and at what levels
       Offer 1- whos it for, how it helps, problem it solves, expected outcome & price(optional)
       Offer 2- (same as above)
       Offer 3- ……..
       So on and so forth
 Make it easy for them to reach out to you, steps to do that or best way you prefer
For each video be sure to ask them to do something, get a commitment from them
CTA ideas
       Be sure to head to the group and share your biggest takeaways from xyz
       To hold yourself accountable drop your biggest reason behind wanting to do this
       Share your experience and what you do now
       If you have any questions around anything give me a shout here (insert messenger link)

       For offers - If you’d like to grab this just head over here (link for offer) or redirect to you
Keep it simple, deliver that value sauce and make it easy for them to have a chat with you
