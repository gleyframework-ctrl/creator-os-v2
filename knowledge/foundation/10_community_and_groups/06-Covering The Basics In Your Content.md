ABOUT THIS LESSON
Looking at it from the consumer's perspective - folks seeing you on TikTok for the first time are further back on the
customer journey
They might not even know what you do, who you are, how you do things etc
It’s why we talk often of creating layers in your content (especially in the earlier stages / if you have no / very minimal
audience)
Eventually, you can pivot - to convert more of your pre-existing followers but to start - you will need to cast a wide net
- and go heavy as you as the brand owner.


These can be places like:
         Your profile / pinned posts
         Reels, Stories, TikToks
         Guide Section in your groups (leveraged assets to get folks up to speed)
         Inject yourself into other people groups via live interviews & joint training (audience swaps)
The ultimate goal is this realm is to cause as MUCH AWARENESS around you, yourself, your brand & your offers as
you can.
Why are we doing this on a deeper level? The more content folks have to consume from you, the longer they’re able
to see/acquire strategy & concepts from you that ACTUALLY help them attain a certain level of results (could be 1
lead, profile/group set up / funnel built, etc)
The resistance for them to purchase products from you will become reduced
Let’s look at where the bulk of your audience is at in this stage.
         They don’t really know who you are
         What you do
         How you do things
         Morals, Ethics
         Stances in the market
         Beliefs
Most important: WHY they should listen to you
It is far better to create content in this arena from the standpoint of folks who are COMPLETE strangers to you - don’t

make the assumption that they already know.
This also drives home the fact that “You can in fact talk about the same thing 7,000 different ways” over and over
and over again.
How do you keep the attention of the folks in your audience?
By keep talking about things they want and how to get them
Something Tdawg mentioned that will never get old
         Find out what they are sick of & then cut it open and pour salt in it
         Show them a different, desirable & new way out of it with the salt repellant juice
         Be the only muh fucka that can give it to them inside of your world
This goes for offers, assets, lead magnets, Lives etc
Give more to Get more is the way and also how you get more attention + retention
We mentioned Netflix a good while back and that's what you want to create online
A place or places that folks can get as much of you as possible
         New Eyeballs quick and easy (FB Group + Youtube)
         Get all your lives and other trainings downloaded and uploaded to YT
         Take old written content that performed well and put in other places
         Respin it, repost it, redo it, but just get it in front of new folks
Have ‘YOU’ plastered everywhere possible where folks can find you and binge on all your series of Content and
Trainings
If you don’t have a day of each week where you give something really fucking good away for free then you definitely

need to do it - Pick a day of the week and give away something bomb digity to folks
         Take shit that you have created and plan or schedule a color block post to drop
         Lives you’ve did on X topics
         Lead Magnets collecting dusk are assets waiting to produce $$
         Explain Breakthroughs & results of yours in a doc or short video
Plan and promote your own free shit because you know you are the shit
Run a training on something you really want to or have been wanting to but been putting it off
          Make it so good that you have to charge for it later
         Tell them you are charging for it after x people grab it/watch it
         Create a brand new pop up FB group where you teach xx folks something cool for free
Get Creative and as you are creating these things you throw them into the cycle of your ecosystem to keep attention
and gain new attention at the same time
