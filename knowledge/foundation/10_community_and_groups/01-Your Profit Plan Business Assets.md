ABOUT THIS LESSON
The Power Of Simplicity - The Key Elements To Having A Highly Profitable BUT SIMPLE AF Business Model
If you have a social media account, you have all the pieces to have a highly profitable business right NOW!

Our first $100k’s online was created by simply:

      Turning our Facebook profiles into billboards for our business.
      Creating written / video content documenting our process, journey, and story
      Talking to people off the tail end of the content

The Business Assets - What You Need
An attention medium - Think TikTok, fb reels, Instagram reels, YouTube shorts. These work well because they allow
you to tap into cold, new folks who have never seen you before
An Indoctrination Station - A community - over time this will become your hub, the centerpiece to your business &
where you send EVERYONE off of your short-form attraction content.
Recommended: Facebook To Start
Facebook is an AMAZING platform to build your brand on - there is this amazing synergy between having the ability
to post content, Get feedback from the people & make offers directly through Messenger.
The entire system/process becomes like a fire with gasoline being poured onto it when you mix in the low-hanging
fruit of viral traffic sources to get even more exposure to your FB profile & long-form content.
Understanding FB Layers: Branded Personal Profile - the billboard for your business - what you do - whom you help -
your story. (Not a business page)
Stories - 24-hour pieces of content - work for fun engagement & connecting with people through messenger
