ABOUT THIS LESSON

Pre-Client Flow
1. Traffic Sources / Content Creation / Tiktok / IG / FB Profile
2. Identification / Group Members Area - Outreach with the goal of gauging where someone is at
3a.Nurture Phase - Tagging newer (Green members on relevant content) Get them as active as possible in your
community & start punching them in the face with value
3b. Pushing To Offer - People coming into your community who’re struggling already problem/solution aware,
moving into offers/sales convos
4. Your Leveraged Content - Group Posts, Training, Offers, “Content Modes”
5. The Follow Up - Messaging & Sparking conversations with those who’re engaging on content, Commenting on
your posts / Taking action on your CTA’s
6. Making Offer & When Sale Happens Moving Into Buyers Flow


Post Client Flow -
1. Fulfillment - delivering on the product, Bonuses, Resources that were paid for.
2. Snagging wins for clients as fast as possible - (keeps them motivated & the earlier on you can get them a few wins
the better you’re testimonials will be)
(The longer people work with you, the less they’ll usually credit their success to you)
3. Identifying your rockstars - For potential ascension or back-end offers
Example Of Our Structure / Flow
        Traffic Sources (all feeding into Group) Everything Outside the Group is “bait” to kickstart this journey/cause
        FOMO for those who aren’t in
        Where We Cherry Pick People out of the member's area - Get a gauge on where they’re at through DM -
        Depending on the convo we’ll either move them into nurturing content in the group or present some type of
        offer
        Leveraged content inside of the group - Pre-selling, Offers, Lifestyle, Lead Gen, Wins.
        Continued Follow-up & respark convos off people who’ve shown interest on posts/content (With the main goal
        of identifying people for Group Juice or other offers)
        Move Buyers Into Group Juice (with the goal of replicating the same thing as above but with the core
        accelerator offer)
        Cherry-picking the rock stars - finding out what they’re missing next and facilitating what they need throughout
        the coaching program
Break down
Content > Group > Group Juice > Core Accelerator
