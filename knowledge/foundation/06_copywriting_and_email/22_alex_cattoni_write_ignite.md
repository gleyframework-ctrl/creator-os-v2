## 1. QUICKSTART COPYWRITING CHECKLIST

Answer these five questions before writing a single word.

### 1.1 The Five Questions

| Question | What It Uncovers |
|----------|------------------|
| WHO is your ideal customer avatar? | Target audience, desires, pain points |
| WHERE are they discovering your offer? | Campaign context, awareness level |
| WHAT does the product do? | Core problem and benefit |
| HOW does it deliver results? | Solution, mechanism, approach |
| WHY would someone buy from you over someone else? | Unique Selling Proposition |

### 1.2 Income Engine Example

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| WHO | CPA operators in MENA who burned domains, waste money on SMTP, spend hours manually extracting. Goal: scale to 200k emails/month. |
| WHERE | YouTube/TikTok, familiar with brand, on email list or social following. |
| WHAT | Problem: Domains keep burning. Benefit: Never worry about deliverability again. |
| HOW | Done-with-you private VPS setup with Postfix and Python automation. |
| WHY | You burned 17 domains yourself and built a system that works - plus you do it WITH them, not just sell a course. |

### 1.3 USP Statement Formula

[Product] helps [audience] with [problem] achieve [benefit] through [unique solution].

**Income Engine USP:**

Infrastructure Private Deployment helps Arabic CPA operators who keep burning domains achieve worry-free scaling to 200k emails/month through a done-with-you private VPS and automation system.

## 2. WHEEL OF PERSUASION

The emotional and logical flow that leads to your product.

### 2.1 The 6 P's

| P | Description | Example |
|---|-------------|---------|
| Problem | Core problem product solves | "My domains keep burning and I don't know why." |
| Pain | Negative feelings from the problem | Anxiety, frustration, embarrassment, fear of losing campaigns |
| Prescription | Solution/secret/discovery (product-agnostic) | Owning your own infrastructure instead of renting shared SMTP |
| Pivot | Your mission/reason for creating product | "I burned 17 domains before I figured this out." |
| Positioning | Why similar solutions haven't worked | Most operators try different SMTP providers, but real issue is shared reputation |
| Product | Seamless introduction of your offer | "That's why I created Infrastructure Private Deployment." |

### 2.2 How to Use

Fill out each section with key words and ideas.

When writing sales page, follow natural flow:

Problem → Pain → Prescription → Pivot → Positioning → Product

## 3. HOT OFFER MATRIX

Framework for constructing an irresistible offer.

### 3.1 Step 1: List Features

Everything the product includes.

**Income Engine Example:**
- Private VPS setup (Ubuntu 24.04)
- Postfix configuration
- Python automation scripts (extraction, validation, import)
- Telegram bot control
- 30-day priority mentorship
- Done-WITH-you live setup call

### 3.2 Step 2: Convert Features to Benefits

**Formula:** [Feature] so you can [Benefit]

| Feature | Benefit |
|---------|---------|
| Private VPS setup | so you can own your infrastructure and never share reputation |
| Postfix configuration | so you can ensure emails land in inboxes, not spam |
| Python automation scripts | so you can run your entire pipeline with one command |
| Telegram bot control | so you can manage everything from your phone |
| 30-day mentorship | so you can get your questions answered immediately |
| Done-WITH-you call | so you can learn while we set it up together |

### 3.3 Step 3: Juicy Bennies

Rewrite benefits with emotional, power-word-rich language.

**Example Juicy Bullets:**

- Never worry about deliverability again with your own private VPS – you own the reputation, not rent it
- Wake up to 5,000 fresh, verified emails every morning – fully automated, zero manual work
- Run your entire pipeline with one Telegram command from anywhere in the world
- Get direct access to me for 30 days – every question answered within hours
- Watch over my shoulder as we set everything up together – you learn, you own it

### 3.4 Step 4: Value Justification

**Question 1:** What is the highest value? What might someone pay elsewhere?

**Example:** Private infrastructure consultant = $2,000+. SMTP providers = $300+/month = $3,600+/year wasted.

**Question 2:** What is the regular price? (Should be much lower than value)

**Example:** Regular price $2,997. Beta price $497.

### 3.5 Step 5: Scarcity

Four types of scarcity:

| Type | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Price Scarcity | Discount for limited time | 50% off for first 10 clients |
| Quantity Scarcity | Limited quantity | Only 10 beta spots |
| Premium Scarcity | Limited-time bonuses | Free 30-day mentorship for first 10 |
| Offer Scarcity | Offer available for limited time | Beta pricing ends December 31st |

**Justify your scarcity** – give a real reason.

**Example:** "We're launching the beta program to get real-world feedback. That's why we're offering this exclusive price to the first 10 operators."

### 3.6 Step 6: Delivery Details

When, where, and how do they receive the product?

**Example:** "After you join, you'll receive a link to book your done-WITH-you setup call. We'll meet on Zoom, and within 48 hours, your private infrastructure will be running."

## 4. 16-STEP SALES PAGE FORMULA

Complete structure for any sales page.

| Step | Section | Purpose |
|------|---------|---------|
| 1 | Headline | Hook attention, let them know they're in right place |
| 2 | Indoctrination | Make them feel understood; create need for solution |
| 3 | The Discovery | Share the solution before presenting product |
| 4 | Pivot | Bridge solution to your product; share your story |
| 5 | The USP | Why your product is different; positioning |
| 6 | The Offer | Exactly what they get |
| 7 | Juicy Bennies | 5-8 emotional benefit bullets |
| 8 | Value | Communicate the product's worth |
| 9 | Regular Price | Show the normal price |
| 10 | Promotion | The discounted price |
| 11 | Reason Why | Justify the discount |
| 12 | Bonus Stacking | List bonuses with values |
| 13 | Scarcity | Why act now |
| 14 | Risk Reversal | Guarantee |
| 15 | Next Steps & Delivery | What happens after purchase |
| 16 | CTA | The buy button |

## 5. H.O.T. HEADLINE FORMULA

### 5.1 The H.O.T. Acronym

| Letter | Meaning | Description |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| H | Hook | Type: Benefit, Threat, or Mechanism |
| O | Open Loop | Curiosity gap: Specificity, Preview, Cliffhanger, Rhetorical Question |
| T | Target Audience | Who it's for (implicitly or explicitly) |

### 5.2 Three Types of Hooks

| Hook Type | Example |
|-----------|---------|
| Get, Achieve, Benefit | "How to Stop Burning Domains in 48 Hours" |
| Threat, Warning, Pain | "Warning: If You're Still Using Shared SMTP, You're Losing Money" |
| Mechanism | "The Postfix Setting 99% of Operators Ignore" |

### 5.3 Four Types of Open Loops

| Open Loop Type | Example |
|----------------|---------|
| Specificity | "The 3 Settings That End Domain Burns" |
| The Preview | "Here's what top CPA operators do differently..." |
| The Cliffhanger | "I was about to quit when I discovered..." |
| Rhetorical Question | "What if you could wake up to 5,000 emails every morning?" |

### 5.4 The Headline Set Format

**Line (pre-head) – Hook (main) – Stinger (sub)**

**Income Engine Example:**

Line: Stop Renting Your Deliverability

Hook: The 48-Hour Fix That CPA Operators Are Using to Scale to 200k Emails

Stinger: (Without Being a Tech Genius)

## 6. COPY GLOSS CHECKLIST

After writing your page:

- [ ] Read it out loud – check cadence and flow
- [ ] Add transitions and coherence markers
- [ ] Add section openers (e.g., "Here's the thing...")
- [ ] Proofread (use Grammarly)
- [ ] Check on mobile – is it readable?
- [ ] Check Hemingway Editor score – aim for Grade 7 or lower
