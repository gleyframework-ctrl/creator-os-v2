                               PART FOUR: VALIDATION QUESTIONS

     I’d like to ask you a few questions about what I just described

     1.     Does the problem resonate with you?
     2.     Would you invest to solve the problem and/or get the outcome I described?
     3.     Do the steps make sense? Can you see yourself going through this process?
     4.     Assuming the investment made sense, would you join a program like this?
     5.     How much would you invest?
     6.     Would you pay               (insert your price point here)?
     7.     Would you like me to contact you when the program launches?


          If YES to #7, Follow up in less than 5 days to schedule a session to discuss the program.
