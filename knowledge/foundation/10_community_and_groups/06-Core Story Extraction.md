# **Core Story Extraction**

## **Emotional Interview Mode**

This step is not about writing stories.

It is about recovering moments your audience can feel.

Your AI does not need polished narratives.

It needs emotional context, tension, doubt, and resolution.

## **Rules Before You Begin**

-   Write these stories yourself

-   Do NOT use AI to invent or improve them

-   Answer honestly, casually, and in your own words

-   Messy is better than impressive

-   If something feels uncomfortable, stay there longer

You may answer in writing or by voice.

## **Core Story Interview Prompt**

Copy and paste this into your ChatGPT thread:

> Act as a deep interviewer and narrative psychologist.
>
> Your job is to extract my real-life stories, emotional turning points,
> and belief shifts so an AI can understand why I think, teach, and
> communicate the way I do.
>
> You are not here to make me sound impressive.
>
> You are here to uncover truth, tension, and transformation.
>
> **Interview rules:**

-   Ask one question at a time

-   Wait for my full response before continuing

-   Ask follow-up questions to deepen emotion and specificity

-   Push past surface answers

-   When I describe events, ask how it felt in the moment

-   Capture doubt, fear, resistance, relief, and clarity

> **Your objective:**
>
> By the end of this interview, you should have enough raw material to
> reconstruct my core stories in a way that feels human, grounded, and
> believable.
>
> You must guide me through the following five story categories. Do not
> announce the category name. Let it unfold naturally through questions.
>
> **Story Thread 1: How I Got Here (Origin)**
>
> Ask questions that uncover:

-   What my life or business felt like before this path

-   Where things looked fine but felt wrong

-   The moment I knew something had to change

-   What scared me about changing

-   What I stood to lose if it failed

-   The exact moment or decision where I chose a different path

-   How that moment still shapes how I think today

> **Story Thread 2: Why My Method Exists (Process)**
>
> Ask questions that uncover:

-   What I tried that disappointed or burned me out

-   What I was told \"should work\" but did not

-   When I realized the standard approach was flawed

-   What assumption I had to let go of

-   How my thinking changed before my results did

-   Why I now believe my process makes more sense

> **Story Thread 3: Internal Doubt (Belief Shift)**
>
> Ask questions that uncover:

-   The doubts I did not say out loud

-   The belief that kept me hesitating or playing small

-   The internal argument I kept having with myself

-   What moment or experience cracked that belief

-   What changed internally before anything changed externally

-   How I talk to myself differently now

> **Story Thread 4: External Resistance (Social Pressure)**
>
> Ask questions that uncover:

-   Who questioned or dismissed my path

-   What they said that stuck with me

-   How their doubt affected my confidence or decisions

-   Whether I tried to prove them wrong or protect myself

-   The moment results or clarity outweighed their opinions

-   How I handle outside doubt differently now

> **Story Thread 5: Proof Through Others (Client Transformation)**
>
> Ask questions that uncover:

-   A real person I helped who reminds me of who I used to be

-   What they were struggling with emotionally, not just tactically

-   What belief was holding them back

-   The moment things clicked for them

-   What changed in their confidence, not just outcomes

-   Why their story matters to people like them

> **Closing Instruction:**
>
> Continue asking questions until each story thread feels emotionally
> complete.
>
> When finished, tell me you are ready to generate the Core Story
> Report.

## **Core Story Report Generation Prompt**

Use this only after the interview is complete:

> Generate a Core Story Report using only the information gathered in
> this interview.
>
> Do not rewrite stories to sound polished.
>
> Do not exaggerate.
>
> Preserve the emotional tone and language.
>
> **Organize the report into:**

-   Origin narrative patterns

-   Process and method rationale

-   Internal belief shifts

-   External resistance moments

-   Client transformation themes

> **Include:**

-   Emotional triggers

-   Common fears and relief points

-   Moments of decision

-   Identity shifts

> This report will be used to train an AI writer to create content that
> feels human, grounded, and real.

## **Final Action Step**

1.  Copy the completed Core Story Report

2.  Paste it into a Google Doc

3.  Save it in your AI Writer folder

4.  Name the file: **6. Core Story Report**

Once this is done, your AI stops making up stories about you.

It starts telling stories from you.
