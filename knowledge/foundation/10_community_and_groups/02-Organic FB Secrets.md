**Facebook Profile Set Up**

> **[Cover Photo]{.underline}**
>
> Your cover photo's sole purpose is to generate leads & build
> authority. Your cover photo should include a clear picture of yourself
> with your face.
>
> If you are building a personal brand and people want to know what you
> look like.
>
> Your cover photo should also include some short text with the name of
> your group, something about what it is that you do, and how they can
> join your group.
>
> I would highly recommend looking around throughout various different
> profiles and seeing what they're doing, the design and layout, and
> model one for yourself that you like.
>
> Below is an image of the cover photo I used for my personal profile
>
> ![](media/image2.png){width="6.5in" height="2.46875in"}When you're
> uploading your cover photo be sure to put a call to action and a link
> inside of the description.
>
> Example: Want to learn how to make money online with affiliate
> marketing? Join my free group here Example.com
>
> Alternatively, you can put an affiliate link here or a
> [linktree]{.underline} that allows you to put multiple links in one
> location.

> **[Profile Picture]{.underline}**
>
> Keep it clean and simple.
>
> A clear photo from the shoulders up is best.
>
> Again, people want to see your face.
>
> Otherwise, a picture in a nice location or something similar will
> work. It's surprising how many people don't have profile pictures,
> this is a MUST A picture of your pet or your favorite sports team
> isn\'t good enough either, it must be a picture of you!
>
> You want to look professional, when you're taking a profile picture,
> ask yourself.
>
> If I saw this person would I want to do business with them? If not,
> then you'll want to change your photo.
>
> **[BIO]{.underline}**
>
> Your bio should be a brief description of exactly what you do online
> and a call to action. Keep it short and sweet, don't overthink it.
>
> I like to use Link.tree in this section, as it allows you to put
> multiple links in one location Below is an image of my Bio.

![](media/image1.png){width="6.5in" height="2.5in"}

> **[Info And Links]{.underline}**
>
> Keep it simple here, I would highly recommend removing as many things
> that you can from this section of your profile.
>
> The fewer distractions the better.
>
> I don't have anything other than where I live and links to my private
> group ![](media/image4.png){width="3.84375in" height="6.0in"}
>
> **[Featured Image]{.underline}**
>
> The featured image is a powerful way to reinforce your call to action
> here. If you have a group or a funnel already, this is a great way to
> send people there.
>
> The featured image should have a picture of you again with some brief
> copy directing people to your group or funnel.
>
> Example: "Click the link above \^\^\^"
>
> If you don't have a group or a funnel, keep this section blank for now
> until you get either one set up.
>
> Alternatively, you could direct them to a lead magnet or an affiliate
> link if you don't have a group or a funnel.
>

> **[Cleaning Up Your Time Line]{.underline}**
>
> Remember you're now embarking on the journey of turning your Facebook
> profile into a money-making machine and in a sense you're turning it
> into a business.
>
> Anything that looks unprofessional should be removed, and if you're
> unsure about a certain post just delete it...
>
> You'd be surprised by how far people will go back and dig through your
> photos and posts. I've had people commenting and liking on posts that
> I did years ago.
>
> **[Activating The Follow Button]{.underline}**
>
> You can access this setting by viewing your profile and selecting
> timeline settings. That will bring you to this page.

![](media/image3.png){width="6.5in" height="3.5104166666666665in"}

> Set Who Can Follow Me to Public to allow people who aren't friends of
> yours follow your content.
>
> **[Timeline Settings]{.underline}**
>
> Change your settings for "Who can post on your timeline" to"Only Me".
> Also, change your setting for "Who can see what others post on your
> timeline" to "Only Me"

![](media/image5.png){width="6.5in" height="2.9895833333333335in"}

> You want it so that you're the only one that is allowed to post things
> to your timeline and it looks professional when someone you add lands
> on it.
