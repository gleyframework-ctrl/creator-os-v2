CHUNK: Pub Chat & The Origin Story Iceberg
CATEGORY: builder
SOURCE: TTB — Pub Chat Framework + Origin Story
---
These two frameworks dictate HOW you speak and WHAT you reveal to build deep, unshakeable connection with your audience.
---
THE PUB CHAT FRAMEWORK (How you speak)
"There is only one person on the other side of the screen. You are talking to thousands of screens, not thousands of people."
When you record a video or write a post, imagine you are sitting at a pub table or on FaceTime with exactly one person (your Dream Follower).
Rules of Pub Chat:
- Never say "Hello everyone," "Hi guys," or "Welcome back."
- Say "You" (singular). Speak as if looking them directly in the eyes.
- Do not script. Speak naturally, with pauses and real emotion.
---
THE ORIGIN STORY ICEBERG (What you reveal)
Most creators only show the tip of the iceberg: daily content and surface-level value. But true connection happens below the surface.
Your Origin Story is the massive iceberg under the water. It includes your deepest struggles, vulnerabilities, and the experiences that shaped your worldview.
Tom's Origin Story: $12,000 in credit card debt paying rent on a 23% interest card. Bullied in high school for being "dumb" because of dyslexia and ADD. Going to school every day thinking, "If I don't say anything, they won't have anything to make fun of."
Result: Sharing that origin story in just two sentences separated him from every other creator in his space.
---
DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION
What is the "below the surface" origin story from your life that you have been afraid to share — and how would you tell it to your Dream Follower using the Pub Chat framework?
