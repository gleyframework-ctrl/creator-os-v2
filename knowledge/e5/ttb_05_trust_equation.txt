CHUNK: The Trust Equation & the 7-11-4 Rule — Measuring Trust
CATEGORY: builder
SOURCE: TTB — Accumulated Trust + Google 7-11-4 + KPIs
---
Before anyone is ready to buy from you, they need accumulated trust. Mamba's equation: Average time per interaction x Frequency of interactions x Quality of each interaction (your TRUST score) = Minutes of Trust per month.
THE BENCHMARK: Google's 7-11-4 study found an average person needs about 7 hours of interaction (420 minutes), across 11 touchpoints, on 4 different platforms, before making a purchase decision.
---
THE MATH THAT CHANGES EVERYTHING
A 10-minute video x 4 posts per month x 50% quality = 20 minutes of trust per month. 420 / 20 = 21 months until a buyer. Now raise quality to 75% (better stories, more humanity, more service) with the SAME 4 posts: 30 minutes per month. 420 / 30 = 14 months. You just saved 7 months without creating one extra piece of content.
This is the core insight: QUALITY is the fastest lever. Most creators try to double frequency (burnout) or length (boredom). The Builder raises the TRUST quality of every interaction instead.
---
KPIS TO TRACK MONTHLY
- Minutes of trust per month (time x frequency x quality).
- Months-to-hero: 420 / your monthly minutes.
- Content consistency (posts per week — consistency beats quantity).
- Revenue per launch.
- Follower-to-buyer conversion rate (as trust rises, conversion rises).
---
EXAMPLE: Two creators post 4 times a month. One chases viral tricks (40% quality). One speaks to his Dream Follower with real stories (80% quality). Same effort — the second builds buyers in half the time.
---
DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION
Estimate your numbers honestly: average minutes per interaction, posts per month, and quality percentage. What is your months-to-hero today — and which variable will you raise first?
