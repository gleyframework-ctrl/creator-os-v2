ABOUT THIS LESSON
Now, not to confuse branding with color schemes, logos, or all of that jazz.. While they do have their time and place,
we’re talking about YOU, at its core.
A personal brand. And the one thing that will be the centerpiece of it as a whole: YOU.
You're the brand. You're what you're technically selling. And if you don't include this piece you will have a hard time
selling anything online.
Benefits:
        Creating content around yourself, your story, life, and journey are all very easy things to include because you've
        lived your life
        It is unique - no one Is you & gives you a competitive edge against similar folks in your market

        It's a breeding ground for deeper connections, longevity & security online

        It lets you build a business / crack profits online centered around something you ENJOY / passion

If you’re brand new, this has to be the heavier piece in your world right now, the opposite of what most people will tell

you..
If people aren’t sold, connected or have ANY type of trust built in YOU as the brand owner, they will not consistently

buy your offers
Things we can start doing NOW, that have big impacts down the line in your business.
For this training, when we’re talking about brand, on a deeper level we’re talking about YOU
        As a whole, a wife, a husband, a parent, A BBQ connoisseur, a car enthusiast, a wine drinker, a vacation taker, a
        hunter, a dog person, a chicken tender, who you are as a person beyond biz
I was asked a hard question the other night… (Nate)


“What type of lifestyle are you portraying? That folks will be stuck at home working all day?”
And we’re not saying to go out there and create a fictitious character of yourself to look good on the internet.
But instead, start looking deeper at the perception being created of yourself on the internet.
If we look at big brand owners, personal brands, etc - They lean into an identity

Which is usually tied to a desirable lifestyle.
Things that we regularly need to balance in our business/marketing (you’ll see us tweaking to this too)
        Personal
        Nonbusiness stuff
        Hobbies
        Passions
        Experiences
        Things you do
        Foods you love
        Just shit YOU enjoy
And these can be SO TINY - but powerful.
Ex:
        The freedom to go to lunch with your significant other whenever you want
        Taking your kids to school without stress
        Not sitting in traffic
Anything and everything that is YOU / who you are / where you’re at / what you currently do beyond a business
perspective If we can anchor these to “having things/freedoms/passions” that folks want but have / desire to do it
can be very compelling.
