## 3. THE 7-STEP INVISIBLE SALES FUNNEL

### Step 1: Problem Statement Ads

Run simple "ugly" image ads testing 30 different problem statements.

Spend $100 total to see which gets most clicks/engagement.

The winner tells you exactly what message resonates.

**Income Engine Hooks to Test:**

- "Why your CPA domains keep burning (even with good offers)"
- "The $13/month infrastructure secret 99% of operators ignore"
- "Stop losing $500/month to burned domains"
- "Your SMTP provider is lying to you. Here's why."
- "3 hours of manual extraction = 5 minutes with this"

---

### Step 2: Splinter Lead Magnet

Create a free resource that is USEFUL BUT INCOMPLETE.

It solves a specific sub-problem but points to your core offer.

Splinter it off your existing product - no need to create from scratch.

**Income Engine Example:**

"CPA Starter Blueprint" PDF:
- Explains why domains burn
- Gives basic setup
- But doesn't give full automation system

That's the "incomplete" part that leads to VIP/Infrastructure.

---

### Step 3: 100 Headline Test

Before building a landing page, test 100 different headlines.

Use a simple poll or cheap Facebook ads.

This alone can double opt-in rates.

**Income Engine Headlines to Test:**

- "The Domain Burn Audit"
- "CPA Infrastructure Starter Kit"
- "5-Day Email Extraction Blueprint"
- "Stop Burning Domains Checklist"
- "The Private VPS Setup Protocol"

Pick the winner for your landing page.

---

### Step 4: Quick-Cash Handraiser Campaign

Only 3-6% of leads are ready to buy right now. This campaign identifies them.

**The Formula:**

"I'm looking for X [ideal clients] who want Y [known desired result] in the next Z days, without/with [something they don't want / do want]. Just reply with [phrase] and I'll send details."

**Income Engine Example:**

"I'm looking for 5 Arabic CPA operators who want to stop burning domains in the next 7 days without needing any technical skills. Just reply with 'TELL ME MORE' and I'll send you the details."

Those who reply get your sales page or booking link.

---

### Step 5: First 30 Indoctrination Series

Automated email sequence that:

- Introduces new leads to your brand
- Builds trust
- Leads to Pillar Content
- Uses action-based tagging for relevant offers
- Includes "dual voice" (founder + support)

**Income Engine Application:**

Your A.I.M. 3-email sequence fits perfectly:

Email 1: ACKNOWLEDGE - Welcome, deliver promise
Email 2: INSPIRE - Your story, show what's possible
Email 3: MOVE - Invite to Pillar Content or Handraiser

---

### Step 6: Pillar Content (Gated & Ungated)

Create ONE piece of content you wish every prospect would see.

Make an UNGATED version (YouTube video) and a GATED version (webinar, PDF, assessment).

Both link to each other and to your core offer.

**Income Engine Pillar Content:**

**Ungated:**
YouTube video: "How I Burned 17 Domains and Built a 200k-Email/Month System"

**Gated:**
Webinar: "The Infrastructure Masterclass" (requires email)

Every marketing road points to this Pillar Content.

---

### Step 7: 10X for 10% Margin Maximizers

Create an upsell that is 10X more expensive than your core offer.

Appeals to top 10% of customers.

**Three Upsell Angles:**

| Angle | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| SPEED | Get results faster (priority setup, done-for-you) |
| AUTOMATION | Get results with less work (done-for-you, software) |
| ACCESS | Higher status experience (mastermind, direct access) |

**Income Engine Upsell Ladder:**

| Offer | Price | Position |
|-------|-------|----------|
| VIP | $197 | Core offer |
| Infrastructure | $497 | 2.5X upsell (SPEED + AUTOMATION) |
| Mastermind | $5,000+ | 10X upsell (ACCESS + SPEED + AUTOMATION) |

## 4. THE GROWTH FLYWHEEL

### 4.1 Growth Engine

Visual flowchart of your entire customer acquisition process.

Maps every step from traffic to sale to upsell.

Once mapped, you see where leads drop off.

**Income Engine Growth Engine Stages:**

1. Traffic (TikTok/YouTube)
2. CTA - Comment trigger word
3. DM - ManyChat sequence
4. Lead Magnet opt-in
5. Email nurture
6. Handraiser reply
7. Sales call
8. Sale
9. Upsell / Ascension

---

### 4.2 Growth Scorecard

Metrics dashboard tracking KPIs for each stage.

Use colors (red/yellow/green) to show performance against targets.

Tells you exactly where the bottleneck is.

**Income Engine KPIs:**

| Stage | Metric | Target | Status |
|-------|--------|--------|--------|
| Traffic | Views/mo | 20,000 | Yellow |
| Engagement | Comments | 200/mo | Red |
| DM Captures | Email subs | 100/mo | Red |
| Handraiser | Replies | 20/mo | Red |
| Calls | Booked | 10/mo | Red |
| Sales | Closed | 3/mo | Red |

---

### 4.3 Growth Plan

90-day plan of projects to move red metrics to green.

Focus on the biggest bottleneck first.

**Weekly Review Process:**

1. Check Scorecard every Monday
2. Identify the #1 red metric
3. Assign one project to fix it
4. Execute for the week
5. Review results
6. Repeat

## 6. PRE-MEETING ASSESSMENT

Send after they book a call.

**Income Engine Assessment Questions:**

1. What's your current monthly revenue from CPA?
2. How many emails are you sending per month?
3. How many domains have you burned in the last 6 months?
4. What's your biggest frustration with your current setup?
5. What have you tried before to fix this?
6. On a scale of 1-10, how urgent is this problem?
7. What would happen if you didn't solve this in the next 30 days?
8. Are you ready to invest in a solution if it's the right fit?
