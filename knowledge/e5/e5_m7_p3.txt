FRAMEWORK: التشخيص السريري للحملات وتعظيم الإيرادات (Clinical Campaign Diagnosis & Revenue Maximization)
WHEN TO USE: When a member's funnel is leaking money, when they don't know what to test, or when they want to maximize Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) through scientific follow-ups and upsells.

CORE METAPHORS & PRINCIPLES (Use these to teach members):
1. استعارة الفحص السريري (The Clinical Exam): Treat every campaign like a patient. You never prescribe medication (traffic/ads) before doing a full clinical exam (diagnosing the metrics). If you can't measure it, you can't improve it.
2. المؤشرات الحيوية الصارمة (Strict Vital Signs): The Minimum Performance Metrics are not suggestions; they are vital signs. Opt-in >20%, Sales Page >1.5%, Order Form >20%, Upsell >15%. If any metric drops below, a "red light" turns on. Stop guessing and fix the exact bottleneck.
3. تضخيم العواقب (Consequence Amplification): In Stage 2 follow-up, urgency isn't created by fake countdown timers, but by clearly illustrating the psychological and financial cost of inaction (تكلفة عدم اتخاذ إجراء).
4. تسلسل الإنقاذ المالي (The Financial Rescue Sequence): In Stage 4, if they don't buy the main offer, don't let them leave. Downsell to a payment plan, then to a lower-ticket core offer. The goal is to acquire the buyer and remove financial friction, not just extract maximum cash upfront.
5. فلسفة البيع الإضافي (Upsell Philosophy): Upsells are not greedy cash grabs. They are the logical answer to: "What does the client value next?" (Speed, ease, automation, error reduction). If framed as an extension of customer service, conversion skyrockets.

ADVANCED PRICING & TESTING TACTICS:
- The Hyper-Buyer Tier: Always include a Mid-Tier (2-3x) and a High-Tier (up to 10x) offer. A small percentage of buyers will always seek maximum support and are willing to pay for it. Don't leave this money on the table.
- Risk-Reversal Testing: Test a "14-Day Free Trial" (Reverse Add-On) against a "Thank You Page Continuity Gift" to see which model maximizes Long-Term Value (LTV) with the least friction.

DECISION RULES FOR THE COACH:
1. Member wants to scale ads but conversions are low? Stop them. Run the Clinical Exam. Identify the bottleneck (Constraint) using the Vital Signs. Fix the constraint before adding traffic.
2. Member's email follow-up is just "Hey, did you forget this?" Diagnose: They are missing the 4-stage psychological sequence. Force them to add Consequence Amplification and a Downsell rescue sequence.
3. Member only has one product and no upsells? Diagnose: They are leaving 70% of revenue on the table. Force them to build a Hyper-Buyer tier (10x) and a Speed/Ease upsell.

ARABIC TERMINOLOGY:
Clinical Diagnosis = التشخيص السريري | Vital Signs = المؤشرات الحيوية | Bottleneck/Constraint = نقطة الاختناق / القيد | Consequence Amplification = تضخيم العواقب | Cost of Inaction = تكلفة عدم اتخاذ إجراء | Financial Rescue Sequence = تسلسل الإنقاذ المالي | Hyper-Buyer = المشتري النهم / الشغوف | Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) = القيمة الدائمة للعميل.
