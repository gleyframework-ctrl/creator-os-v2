# DAN LOK - ULTRA HIGH TICKET CLOSING
## 50+ Must-Have Sales Questions

Source: Dan Lok Ultra High Ticket Closing Course
Category: Sales / Qualification
Last Updated: March 2026

## 1. REVENUE GOALS QUESTIONS

Use in Step 3 (Understand Motivation) and Step 7 (Money Stage) of Dan Lok's 9-Step Script.

| # | Question | Purpose |
|---|----------|---------|
| 1 | How did you arrive at that goal? | Understand their reasoning and commitment level |
| 2 | Who or what was involved in coming up with that goal? | Identify influencers, partners, or stakeholders |
| 3 | What's the upside (or downside) to missing, meeting or exceeding that goal? | Quantify emotional and financial impact |
| 4 | When do you need to achieve these goals by? | Establish timeline and urgency |

## 2. BUSINESS GOALS QUESTIONS

Use in Step 4 (Identify Pains) and Step 5 (Dream).

| # | Question | Purpose |
|---|----------|---------|
| 5 | What's at the top of your priority list for the rest of this year? | Uncover their immediate, real priorities |
| 6 | How did those things make it to the top of the list? | Understand their decision-making process |
| 7 | How do those priorities relate back to revenue? | Connect priorities to business outcomes |
| 8 | What problem are you trying to solve? | The core question |
| 9 | What metrics are you responsible for? | Understand how they measure success |
| 10 | Are there specific revenue goals you're working toward? | Validate if there's a concrete number |
| 11 | How did you or the business arrive at those goals? | Assess the rigor behind the goal |
| 12 | Would you say those goals are nice to have or need to have? | Differentiate between desire and necessity |
| 13 | If they're nice to have, is there a need to have a number? | Push for a concrete target |
| 14 | Why is that number a need to have? | Uncover the emotional driver |

## 3. PLANS AND PAST ATTEMPTS QUESTIONS

Use in Step 4 (Identify Pains) and Step 6 (Bridge the Gap).

| # | Question | Purpose |
|---|----------|---------|
| 15 | How long have you been working on getting to that goal? | Assess persistence and past effort |
| 16 | What have you done to achieve this goal in the past? | Understand their history and failed attempts |
| 17 | What worked well? What didn't work? Why? | Diagnose past successes and failures |
| 18 | What's your plan now to achieve the goals we discussed? | See if they have a plan, and if it's viable |
| 19 | Who else is working on it? | Identify team members or stakeholders |
| 20 | Is this at the top of their priority list? If not, what is? | Understand organizational priorities |
| 21 | How much time do you think they'll need to work on this? | Gauge their commitment and time allocation |
| 22 | Can they do this and keep up with other responsibilities? | Assess capacity and potential overwhelm |
| 23 | Do you think you'll need to hire someone? | Uncover need for additional resources |

## 4. BUDGET AND DECISION MAKERS QUESTIONS

Use in Step 7 (Money Stage) for qualification.

| # | Question | Purpose |
|---|----------|---------|
| 24 | Is this problem funded? | Direct qualification question |
| 25 | Whose budget does the funding come from? | Identify the true economic buyer |
| 26 | Is the budget owner an executive sponsor? | Determine the level of authority |
| 27 | Who else will be involved in choosing a vendor? | Map the decision-making committee |
| 28 | Do you have written decision criteria? | Understand formal selection process |
| 29 | What's the process for purchasing once you decide? | Understand buying process and roadblocks |
| 30 | How can I help make this easy? | Position yourself as a facilitator |

## 5. CHALLENGES AND OBSTACLES QUESTIONS

Use in Step 4 (Identify Pains) and Step 6 (Bridge the Gap).

| # | Question | Purpose |
|---|----------|---------|
| 31 | Why do you think it has been challenging to get there? | Uncover their perceived obstacles |
| 32 | Do you think you'll need to change your approach? | Gauge openness to a new solution |
| 33 | What do you anticipate being easy or difficult? | Uncover potential implementation fears |
| 34 | Is there anyone who might derail this work? | Identify internal skeptics |
| 35 | Why do you think they would derail this work? | Understand source of resistance |
| 36 | What specifically do you need to do differently? | Help them articulate the change needed |
| 37 | What are your primary roadblocks? | Directly ask for biggest obstacles |
| 38 | Are there parts of your plan you're unsure about? | Identify where your solution fits |

## 6. TIMELINE AND URGENCY QUESTIONS

Use in Step 8 (Moving to the Close).

| # | Question | Purpose |
|---|----------|---------|
| 39 | What's your timeline to achieve these goals? | Establish concrete deadline |
| 40 | How did you come up with the timeline? | Assess reasoning behind deadline |
| 41 | If it took an additional 1, 3, or 6 months, is that ok? | Test the true urgency |
| 42 | Why is delaying ok or not ok? | Uncover consequences of delay |
| 43 | When do you think you need to start? | Create sense of immediacy |
| 44 | What needs to happen before you can start? | Identify pre-conditions |

---

## 7. CONSEQUENCES QUESTIONS

Use in Step 5 (Dream) and Step 6 (Bridge the Gap) to create emotional urgency.

| # | Question | Purpose |
|---|----------|---------|
| 45 | If you exceed these goals by 20%, what happens? Dream out loud. | Help visualize positive outcome (creates desire) |
| 46 | If you miss these goals by 20%, what happens? Scenario-plan. | Help visualize negative consequences (creates urgency) |
| 47 | What would a successful outcome look like? | Get them to paint vivid picture |
| 48 | If you implement this, how do you hope things are different in one year? | Connect solution to long-term vision |
| 49 | How will this solution make your life better? | Uncover personal, emotional benefit |
