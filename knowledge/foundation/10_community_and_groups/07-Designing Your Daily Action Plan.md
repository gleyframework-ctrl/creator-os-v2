ABOUT THIS LESSON
Picking your non-negotiables based on what’s going on in your life & biz, the thing to note is that you can change/
pivot energy depending on the actual outcome you’re looking for / micro goal but as a whole, it comes down to a few
things
        Showing up for your audience / future audience publicly - Making it easy for people to:
               Know who you are, as an individual, a business owner
               Who do you help, and what types of problems do you help people solve
               What you don’t do / what kinds of things do you not enjoy/stand behind
               Understand what is inside your offers, where it is going to take them, what has it done for you, where it
               is going to help you go
Figure out Your OWN cadence that will allow you to show up more & consistently for LONGER periods of time (now
this doesn't mean you have to go hard every single day 24/7)
But figuring out a flow that you can maintain consistently for longer because the more you’re showing up &
consistently showing up for bigger pockets of time, the easier & easier sales will start to become
Whether this looks like:
        Going live a few times a week with shorter written content to fuel those lives
        Doing fewer lives & more written content
        Create leverage Where You Can To Maintain Your Energy + Minimizing What You Have To Do On A Daily
        Basis all while still providing value
THINK ABOUT IT - LEVERAGE - we went live in a facebook group for 5-days years ago - which has now produced
$1M+ In sales
Whether we’re talking about free training or paid products, look at ways that you can create micro assets & things
that will allow you to step away from the business in certain stages (This is an extremely important one the more
you grow & the more success you have)
Buckets to ideally cover in say Group Training + Guides sections
        Who you are
        What you’ve done, what you’re doing - community results/things you're a part of
        Everything someone would need to know before having a chat / to feel confident in singing up for one of your
        offers
               Things people are constantly asking you
               What affiliate marketing is
               What the business entails
               What they would have to actually be doing
               How you do things
This isn’t a “Daily action plan” per se - but this help maintains what you actually can start doing on a day-to-day
basis.
Instead of me having gone through convo after convo every single day about XYZ, and getting them to the point of
understanding how online business works
I can utilize the things I've created to do that for me, so I can put the time in DM & messages toward the people
who’re closer to the point of being ready to buy / problem or solution aware
Which all helps with maintaining that consistent flow day & day out
        DM’s + Making Offers
Having some form of convo daily & making some type of offer (these don’t have to always be monetary offers)
In order of how I personally prioritize my own inbox
        Buyers / Coaching clients - answering questions / helping them
        Offer Follow-Up - Comment Outreach/engagement
        Hotter conversations - people i’ve been chatting to about X thing
        Old Convos, People who’ve said no prior, you’ve chatted with before
        New followers, people who’re just coming into your world & wanna know what you’re about, funneling them
        into your leveraging this to build rapport & provide value in unison
        Outbound, off stories or “no offer content”
This doesn’t mean you need to check all these DM boxes every single day
If you have enough convos flowing & people you’re chatting to about XYZ, you don’t have to go out and start a new

convo or even respond to specific ones

“Not as far along as you’d like”
