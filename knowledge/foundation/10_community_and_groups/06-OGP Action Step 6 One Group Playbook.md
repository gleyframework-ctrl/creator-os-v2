Your job inside your community is simple.

Get people from \"I\'ll think about it\" to \"I need this now.\"

That\'s it.

Here\'s the thing most people miss:

Selling does not start when you pitch. Selling starts when someone moves
from watching → to identifying → to feeling like they need to act.

People do not buy because you explained better.

They buy because they heard it in a way that finally made sense to them.
Something clicks. They emotionally snap.

There are 4 core triggers that make that snap happen.

Your community activates these without pressure when it\'s built right.

The lesson breaks down all 4 triggers and how to spot the signals when
someone is ready.

Because buying triggers show up as behavior before they show up as
words.

When you know what to look for, selling becomes confirmation. Not
convincing.
