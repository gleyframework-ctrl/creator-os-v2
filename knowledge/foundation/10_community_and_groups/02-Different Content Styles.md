ABOUT THIS LESSON
Length - 5-180 seconds long (you can post longer videos but would usually recommend keeping them under a
minute so you can repurpose them on multiple platforms)
Styles -
Your Short Long Text Videos - Video Example - The short length videos with big bulky text on them - so the
consumers can read it - these can work in a pinch but wouldn’t make them the entire focus of your content strategy
The Talking Videos - Video Example - You Dropping knowledge & sharing views, experiences, and lessons - these
will usually get fewer views & engagement but they will DO AN AMAZING JOB at actually connecting with people
Trends - Video Example - These can work good for initial quick growth - but again wouldn’t turn them into your core
focus - we want to teach you how to build a brand & an engaged audience of your own - not become a carbon copy
of everyone else doing the same shit
Stitches & Duets - Stitch Example - Duet Example - Can work really well for piggybacking off of viral videos &
getting some exposure to yourself - spin them off into your own & add to the videos you’re creating
Responses - Video Example - Awesome way to build connections & respond to people who’re engaging with your
content - You will have the ability to get exposure to the new video & the old one
Series Videos - (Extremely Powerful) if you can find a style / angle of content that works well - doubling down on it
and turning it into a series that you can do on repeat over and over again - Did this for “exposing fake side hustle
videos”
Posting Guidelines
        2 hours between posts - 10 - 1 - 4 - 7 (would aim for 2-5 pieces of content per day)
        Volume 130 music 5 (if you use music - not required - benefits to using it & not using it)
        Hashtags - 2 niches 2 broad 1 trend - Aim for less than 1m per hashtag (not crucial - the biggest factor for
        growth will be the content) Or no hashtags.
