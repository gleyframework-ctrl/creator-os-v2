**What is a Launch?**

[A **launch** is a planned effort to bring a new product or service to
market. The goal is to make sure that everyone inside the company, your
partners, and ideal clients know about your new **offer**\...
**Launching** your **service** is just as important as developing a
great **service**.]{.mark}

**What type of launch?**

**5 Day Challenge**

**(It can have many names)**: This is a 5 day event where you create a
mini customer journey focused on providing the attendees with a new
exciting result in hopes of adding value to their life and getting them
on the phone or in the DMs with you to discuss your offer and how it is
the solution they need. Your goal is to create a journey that allows
your prospect to gain momentum and see the bigger picture of what will
come if they continue down this road.

**Webinar(for us it's a FB Live)**: This is typically an hour-long
presentation you give over the internet using Zoom, or Facebook LIVE.
You focus on providing the attendees with a new exciting result in hopes
of adding value to their lives and getting them on the phone or dms with
you to discuss your offer and how it is the solution they need.

**Get Started**

**1.) Develop a Title**: What result can you help them accomplish or get
closer to and what are some objections you can overcome with the
subtitle?

**Example 5 Day Challenge Title**: Idea to Income: No Tech, Sales, or
Marketing Experience Needed

**Example Live Title:** The 5 Steps to create an Income without a bunch
of Fancy shit.

**2.) Create Pre-Launch Email and FB Posts to check interest and
announce the thing**

**3.) Share your Plan for folks that attend the live as well as buy the
offer**

**For A 5 Day Challenge** (What will each day consist of? Topics and
homework.)

Day 1: Topic. Homework.

Day 2: Topic. Homework.

Day 3: Topic. Homework.

Day 4: Topic. Homework.

Day 5: Topic. Homework.

**For A Webinar or Live** (What is the result they can achieve and what
are the 3 KEY "Steps" or "Secrets" you will be uncovering?)

**Example**: 3 steps to land more sales, 3 steps to generate leads with
money

**4.) (Optional) Create Landing Page** Keep it super simple. What is it?
What will it help them do? And why would they want it?

Landing Page Example: [bit.ly/LandyourIdealClients]{.mark}

[Keep in mind youre just doing this to build a list of interested folks,
bonus points if you can give them something else that'll help them
before the live or to prep for it.]{.mark}

**5.) Develop a short and simple campaign for launch.**

Prelaunch: Curiosity and intrigue about whats coming to create a stir,
grab attention

1.  FB Posts

2.  Emails

3.  Tiktoks

4.  FB reels and stories

Launch: More Curiosity and intrigue + results from you, community and
others

5.  FB Posts

6.  Emails

7.  Tiktoks

8.  FB reels and stories

Everything should point to your Group where the live will be

**Plan your Live within the things above making sure folks know about
it**

**Go live and Over Deliver on the process, Plan, and the desired outcome
of what your offer will do for them.**

**Pre-sell and soft pitch the offer and make it all about them.**

**Now take the Live Content and let it fuel your content everywhere else
as you lead up to the end of pomo and close spots.**

**Share Feedback from live**

**Comments**

**Aha moments**

**Topics Covered**

**Lessons**

**etc**

Hit your posts and emails hard even after the Live to push folks back to
it

**6.) Have a Stripe or PayPal account or your Affiliate link handy to be
able to receive payments and Close sales**

**7.) Plan Bonus Q&A for those that couldn\'t make the live**

**8.) Follow up with folks that were on the live in the dms**
