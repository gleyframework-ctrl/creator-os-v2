# **[Launching A Community (Paid Or Free Group)]{.underline}** 

**Paid Group Launch)** Behind the scenes look at how we launched a brand
new group off of Facebook and added an additional 36k+ per year to our
income

We just used the group juice model to blow up a brand new group, which
reinforces what we talk about often - you can use these concepts to grow
whatever you want - a buyers group, a youtube channel, it's not platform
specific but the tactics can be the same.

Skool is in session, literally.

So we wanted to take a day to give you some raw tactics about how it
came to be, what we're going to be using it for & different ways that
you can use this concept in your own business.

**How it came to be:** A couple-minute conversation on the 3rd floor in
a beach house at Myrtle Beach - Flip showed me an email from Travis Sago
about how he was giving away bonuses for folks to sign up to a new
platform, and Skool was on my radar ever since taking Sam Ovens
Consulting accelerator.

We got back and started to brainstorm different places for this group to
live inside the business.

**Extra Tech (Anti "No upsells Ever")**

We added order bump (\$39)

And a one time offer for training we ran specifically for the folks in
the new group (we spun 100k game plan and ran a new pre-recorded
training with flip & nate)

Which is 1 x \$290 on the front and if they say no - it directs them to
a payment plan option of 4 x \$90

Which has been a big catalyst to pulling in big rev numbers with it
already (Think we're over 5k in the last 36 hours)

Low barrier of entry on front = more volume

Which in turn increases opportunity to get more purchase on upsells

This also opens up scope down the line to see the percentages of folks
buying & what average cart value is (so we could potentially run
re-targeting ads to this specific offer)

**Future Asset / Plans To Grow Group\
\
**A powerful angle that you can use - is talking about things you are
planning when you hit a certain milestone in the community.

Ex: Planning a free live event where i'm going to help you get clear on
X after we cross 100 members in the community

This can be an angle to get other people to help you talk about your
group & personally help you grow it.

You could give away:

-   Trainings

-   Assets

-   Calls / support

To the folks that help you grow your group or are active in your
communities themselves (think point system similar to skool)\
\
Which in turn fuels group growth & gives you more things to hit on
inside of your content because you can go speak on all of it externally.
