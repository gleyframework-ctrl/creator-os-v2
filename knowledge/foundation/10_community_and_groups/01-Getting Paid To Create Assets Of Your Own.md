Getting Paid To Create An Asset


A Couple of reasons you’d want to do this:


       Produce a chunk of change on the front end.
       Get paid to create an asset - Of your own or either one that you can bolt onto an affiliate product
       Increasing the perceived value of the affiliate product you’re selling because you’re giving more - relevant
       training while giving you the ability to step away/ reduce the time commitment

Things that will need to be in place prior -


       An offer/core product to sell.
       An audience - Even if it’s small - you can still do this
       Some goodwill built-up (If you’ve been selling hard for the next couple of weeks you don’t want to roll into this
       right away)
       Decide on a time frame/deadline - When you want to roll it out / when you’re closing the offer down / how
       many spots

Why this works -


       New
       Exclusivity
       Scarcity
              Time / Limited Spots
       Personal - Custom
       Bonuses / Extra Value

Things To Execute This:


1) A product/program to sell (a course or group coaching works best)
2) A place to run the back-end training (Can do zoom or via living in a pop-up FB group - The latter would look better
if you’re turning it into an evergreen asset)

3) A support structure - beyond the usual - Could be a Group chat - OR the pop-up group (You can only keep this
available for the time you’re running the thing)
4) A place to close (A Doc)


Action Plan
Step 1: Decide the core offer
It can be any of the following:

1) Course
2) Affiliate Product
Recommendations
Depending on the goal - it can change the pricing / what product you’re running this on the back end of.
       If the goal is to identify your “Buyers” people who could potentially ascend into premium-priced products - do
       this with something cheaper (Or if you don’t have a TON of results yet - can be easier to do it with something
       cheaper)

       To produce cash flow - Run it on the back end of a high ticket purchase

Decide On Length / Subject
You’ll want to keep the topic semi-relevant to what you’re selling / the product you’re promoting.
For example - I did this on the back end of SAA for 6 weeks covering FB Group growth - which then became an asset
that I created off the back end of making money.
Important: Put a length promised on delivery no MATTER the pricing - otherwise you’ll run into problems in the back
end for ascension - you’ll continually add more people & commit infinite times.
        1-6 Months for back end of high ticket.
List out things you’re confident in teaching/covering - These don’t have to BE HUGE breakthroughs..
        Sales - Conversions
        Content & Copy
        Tech & Set Up
Little Nuances that you’re confident in
Support & Structure -
      Pop Up FB Group
      Group Chat
      Messenger
Frequency -
      Are you connecting weekly - Bi-weekly
      Zoom / VIA live training with Q & A on the back end in a pop-up group
Bonuses -
      What things can you bolt onto this “New thing” to further increase the value

       Prioritize speed - look at things you have - things you’ve already done that are relevant & can be used

Steps -


       Put together your offer doc -

As we’ve touched on quite a few times - your stacked doc.
With this strategy, the core focus is going to be YOUR thing.
If you’re running it on the back end of affiliate - that’s icing on the cake / can use a bridge-type doc.




       Temp Checking In Your Content -

Start talking about X in your content - YOU DON’T have to mention the deadline right now or right away.
Do this later on in the promo - as you’re getting closer - you don’t want to say you’re running a promo for the next 3
days & then have it flop - because you now are at a deadline with no prospects
Posts like:
“Thinking about putting together a pop-up group for X”


Where we’ll dive into:
Solve Pain
Solve Pain
Who’d be interested?


       Move into FULL ON PROMO         -



Expect to be openly posting about your offer - DAILY. For X amount of time.
Note - This doesn’t mean just post - Here’s my offer - Here’s my offer.
Start attacking different angles & pain points in your content throughout the duration of the promo.
Ex - Im doing something for a group
       The importance of having a group
       What a group can do for your business
       How business changed after having a group
       Leaving money on the table not having a group
       A culture and community
       Eliminating the need to look for leads
       Leads coming to me
       A long-term play on business
       Your space/authority / the ability to control your own thing
       Pain points having a group can eliminate
               Cold outreach
               Calls or spending all day on zoom
               Adding friends
Look at the offer doc that you’ve crafted - look at all the problems that each portion of it solves and USE THAT FOR
CONTENT & ROTATE BETWEEN THEM
Running a 6-week thing - you now have 6 different pain points you’re solving & sub-sections that each one of those 6
things will help them achieve/eliminate
Embed Limited Availablity
X spots - Date Closed - If Available Price Increase
Countdown -
Post. Post. Post. About it everywhere & everywhere through the course of running a promo.
You will piss people off by running the offer. You will lose some people.
That’s fine.
When You Do Close it - MAKE IT KNOWN. This will help in the future.
Fulfillment -
DELIVER - To those who jump in - will drastically increase the chances of them jumping into the next thing or
purchasing something else.
       Can stack student wins -
       Testimonials
       More content - because you’re working with people who’re having wins too - can be little or big, monetary or
       non-monetary
Can do this in unison with moving into value mode with your public audience


Turning what you’ve done into AN ASSET -
You ran X for 6 weeks -
You now have 6 pieces of training that you can -
       Bolt onto the said product - letting you step away from the 1 to 1 / support
              Make your already stacked offer even better - because you’ve now got 6X relevant pieces of training + Q
              & A support structure

       Sell as a stand-alone product - introductory offer to identify buyers
